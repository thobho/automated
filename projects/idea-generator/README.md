# idea-generator

Phase 1 research to help a Polish solo developer pick one product to build and sell within a month. The work runs as a funnel: research through 9 lenses, ~200 one-line ideas, a scored shortlist of ~100, full write-ups of the top ~25, a skeptical review, and cheap validation plans for the top 3–5. Nothing gets built in this phase.

## How to read it
- **Results:** start with [reports/phase1-summary.md](reports/phase1-summary.md). Read it in this order: the bottom line at the top, section 4 (five validation plans, each ≤1 week and ≤200 PLN, with Go / No-go numbers), section 5 (order of the tests and the decision rule), then section 9 (open questions for you). Section 3 is the top 20 table.
- **Reviewed vs quick-scored ideas:** only the 25 write-ups in `ideas/<field>/NNN-*.md` were researched (≥5 sources) and reviewed (each ends with a **Devil's advocate** section and a **Review changes** line; rejected ones say so at the top and are listed in [rejected.md](rejected.md)). The other 81 rows of [ideas/shortlist.csv](ideas/shortlist.csv) carry quick scores only, which tended to fall by 0.0–0.6 when researched. [ideas/README.md](ideas/README.md) explains the CSV columns.
- **How ideas are scored:** [method/criteria.md](method/criteria.md) has the 1–5 rubric per lens, the weights, and how the weighted score maps to a chance band of reaching 10,000 PLN MRR.
- **Research behind the ideas:** `research/NN-<lens>/README.md`, one folder per lens.
- **Dropped ideas and later corrections:** [rejected.md](rejected.md) and [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).
- **Method and rules:** [BRIEF.md](BRIEF.md). Task instructions are in [tasks/](tasks/), and [RUNNER.md](RUNNER.md) describes the scheduled run.

## Link check
Every factual claim carries a source URL. Before a task is marked done, its changed files go through the link checker:
```
python3 tools/check_links.py <files>        # exit 1 only if a link is dead
cd tools && python3 -m unittest             # tests for the checker
```

## Phase 2: innovative LLM products to adapt for Poland
Phase 1 ideas were solid but not innovative. Phase 2 scans 51 developed countries for fast-growing, mostly LLM-native products, checks each theme against Polish demand, competition and demographic change, and keeps ideas where knowing Polish realities is the edge.
- **Brief:** [phase2/BRIEF.md](phase2/BRIEF.md) · **Status:** [phase2/PROGRESS.md](phase2/PROGRESS.md) · **Run procedure:** [phase2/RUNNER.md](phase2/RUNNER.md) (every task on opus, medium effort)
- **Results:** [phase2/reports/phase2-summary.md](phase2/reports/phase2-summary.md) (English) and [phase2/reports/podsumowanie-pl.md](phase2/reports/podsumowanie-pl.md) (Polish, for phone reading). Read the bottom line, then section 4 (five validation plans, each ≤1 week and ≤200 PLN, with go/no-go numbers) and section 9 (open questions).
- **Status:** research T01–T24 complete; T25 summary written. Coverage: 51 countries, 531 catalogued products, 69 themes (7 open, 30 partial, 32 taken in Poland). Funnel: 167 longlist ideas → 59 shortlisted → 24 written up → 14 still standing after the skeptical review (7 rejected, 3 merged). No reviewed idea stays in the `5–15%` band; all are `<5%`, mostly because a Polish incumbent or a free state service already covers the job. [011 OWU comparison agent](phase2/ideas/sme-ops/011-broker-owu-comparison.md) was corrected on 2026-11-06 (3.2 → 2.7) after Polish OWU tools were found. Next step for the owner: run the validation tests for 023, 002, 004, 001 and 011 in the order given in section 4.6 of the summary before writing any code.
- **Where things are:** reviewed write-ups in `phase2/ideas/<field>/NNN-*.md` (each has a **Devil's advocate** and a **Review changes** section), scores in [phase2/ideas/shortlist.csv](phase2/ideas/shortlist.csv), dropped ideas in [phase2/rejected.md](phase2/rejected.md), themes in [phase2/themes/THEMES.md](phase2/themes/THEMES.md), scoring rules in [phase2/method/criteria.md](phase2/method/criteria.md).

## Phase 3: removing friction in buying and ordering
Phase 3 drops the B2B SaaS question and looks for businesses that make a purchase easier (finding the right part, checking fit, a smoother order, one small physical step) without holding inventory, across five verticals: vehicle parts, home/climate/appliance parts, hobby niches, services with a logistics step, and small-business buying.
- **Brief:** [phase3/BRIEF.md](phase3/BRIEF.md) · **Status:** [phase3/PROGRESS.md](phase3/PROGRESS.md) · **Run procedure:** [phase3/RUNNER.md](phase3/RUNNER.md)
- **Results:** [phase3/reports/phase3-summary.md](phase3/reports/phase3-summary.md) (English) and [phase3/reports/podsumowanie-pl.md](phase3/reports/podsumowanie-pl.md) (Polish, with the top-5 table and a "co sprawdzić najpierw" section). Read the bottom line, then section 4 (validation plans) and section 6 (order of work).
- **Status:** T01–T15 complete; T16 summary written. Coverage: 201 catalogued businesses from 20 countries, 114 Polish demand signals, 87 items in a by-hand findability test (gap rate 55%, likely overstated because Allegro returned 403 and was never tested). Funnel: 95 longlist ideas → 26 shortlisted → 10 written up → 4 still standing after the sceptical review, **all `<5%`**: [009 fitment data for Allegro parts sellers](phase3/ideas/v1-vehicle/009-fitment-data-for-parts-sellers.md) (2.86), [001 catering-equipment parts](phase3/ideas/v5-small-business/001-catering-equipment-parts.md) (2.64), [005 camper habitation parts](phase3/ideas/v1-vehicle/005-camper-habitation-parts.md) (2.63), [006 heat-pump parts for installers](phase3/ideas/v2-home-appliance/006-heat-pump-parts-for-installers.md) (2.56). The main reasons: one-off consumer orders cannot pay back a Google Ads click, parcels from Germany remove the margin, and no supplier publishes dropship or reseller terms. Next step for the owner: send the free supplier e-mails and run the 009 seller pre-sale (section 6 of the summary) before writing any code.
- **Where things are:** write-ups in `phase3/ideas/<vertical>/NNN-*.md` (each with **Devil's advocate** and **Review changes**), scores in [phase3/ideas/shortlist.csv](phase3/ideas/shortlist.csv), dropped ideas in [phase3/rejected.md](phase3/rejected.md), findability tests in `phase3/findability/`, scoring rules in [phase3/method/criteria.md](phase3/method/criteria.md), corrections in [phase3/CHANGELOG.md](phase3/CHANGELOG.md).
