# 004 — "Podgląd KSeF": free no-login invoice viewer plus paid new-invoice watcher
**One-liner:** A free browser page that renders a KSeF invoice XML as a readable PDF/preview without sending it to a server, as the SEO entry point, and a paid watcher that emails or pushes new purchase invoices from KSeF to micro-firms and their accountants.
**Field:** accounting-invoicing-tax | **Type:** SaaS
**Target customer:** Owners of JDG/micro firms that receive invoices via KSeF but do not use an invoicing program that downloads them, and small accountants. Count: about 1.75 million active VAT payers are in scope of KSeF (L5-F03, official-data based); share without an integrated program unknown (unverified).
**Problem:** KSeF does not notify: "KSeF nie wysyła e-maili, SMS-ów ani powiadomień push o nowych dokumentach" (Fakturowo guide); the free state app requires login and has no notification feature; viewing a structured invoice needs a login or an integrated program.
**MVP scope (≤6 weeks):** (1) Static page: drop an FA(3) XML, get a rendered invoice and a PDF, all in the browser (no upload); SEO pages around "podgląd faktury KSeF". (2) Watcher: user adds a KSeF token/certificate, nightly polling of purchase invoices through the KSeF 2.0 API, email with PDF per new invoice, weekly digest. (3) Stripe subscription. Not in MVP: mobile app, issuing, accounting.
**Why now:** KSeF mandatory for all taxpayers since 1 Apr 2026, the smallest join on 1 Jan 2027 (official stage page); wFirma's help says KSeF tokens only work until the end of 2026, which complicates a token-based watcher (see risk).
**Foreign analogues:** None with traction found (searched, not exhaustively). Closest model: inbox-notification add-ons to government e-invoicing portals; none identified (unverified).
**Competition (PL / global):** Free state app (no notifications, login needed), the official mobile app 2.03/5 from 645 iOS ratings (L6-F01). Free hobby apps: "KSeF Mobile – powiadomienia" (iOS, free, 5.0 from 2 ratings, released 2026-10-01), "Monitor KSeF" (iOS, free, 5.0 from 3 ratings, 2026-03-13) (iTunes lookup API). Open-source: ksefwatch (MIT, self-hosted watcher that emails new invoices with a PDF, 4 stars, pushed 2026-10-21), KSeF_Monitor (MIT, 7 stars, multi-channel notifications, PDF), niutech/ksef-xml-pdf (GPL-3.0 browser XML-to-PDF viewer, 1 star), CIRFMF/ksef-pdf-generator (81 stars) (GitHub API). Commercial invoicing apps (inFakt, Fakturownia and others) download and notify inside the product (L6-F26). Gap: only for people with no integrated program; the no-login viewer exists in open source but with little visibility.
**Evidence of pain:** In a sample of 51 App Store reviews of the official app, 12 ask for push notifications of new purchase invoices; quotes: "Dodajcie możliwość włączenia powiadomień o nowych fakturach" (1 star, 2026-04-04) (L6-F01). Google autocomplete for "ksef podgląd faktur" suggests "bez logowania", "xml", "pdf" (L8-F16; autocomplete is not volume). Caveat: "bez logowania" may reflect a misunderstanding; one guide says it usually means background authorization by token or certificate (search summary). No Wykop/Facebook complaints read. Search volume: unverified.
**Monetization:** Free viewer; watcher 15–25 PLN per month per taxpayer, or 99 PLN/month for an accountant with 20 clients (estimate). Payer: the micro-firm or its accountant. Competitors are free, which caps price (L9-F22).
**Revenue sketch (estimate):** Assumes 19 PLN/month average. 6 months: 60 subscribers = about 1,100 PLN MRR. 12 months: 200 subscribers = about 3,800 PLN MRR. 10,000 PLN MRR needs about 530 subscribers (about 0.03% of 1.75 million payers), possible in count but not at a price that free apps tolerate.
**First 10 customers:** SEO landing pages for "podgląd faktury KSeF bez logowania" and "powiadomienia KSeF", answers in GOFIN forum and Polish JDG groups, a note to the 1-star reviewers' communities. Accountants: offer the watcher to 10 small offices found via the same forum.
**Build complexity:** low–medium. Risks: certificate/token custody, KSeF rate limits per context (L7-F03), PDF rendering fidelity (reuse the ministry's MIT generator), token sunset at end of 2026 (unverified secondary source) forcing certificate onboarding that non-technical users will find hard.
**Pros / Cons / Risks:** Pros: tiny build, SEO entry, low support. Cons: free hobby and commercial alternatives already solve notifications; the ministry may add push; users with an invoicing program do not need it; privacy concerns about uploading invoices. Risks: churn when the user switches to an integrated program, secret custody liability.
**Scores (1–5):** L1 1, L2 2, L3 2, L4 2, L5 4, L6 4, L7 5, L8 2, L9 2 | **Weighted:** 2.7 / 5 | **Chance band:** <5% (T16 review: L8 3→2, see Review changes; write-up value was 2.9, 5–15%; shortlist 3.1)
**Cheapest validation test (≤1 week, ≤200 PLN):** Publish the free viewer as a one-page site and a "powiadom mnie o nowych fakturach, 19 PLN/mies." waitlist with a Stripe pre-order link; spend 150 PLN on Google Ads for "podgląd faktury ksef bez logowania" and read the CPC and click volume in Keyword Planner. Success: at least 300 visitors, 20 waitlist sign-ups, 3 pre-orders.
**Kill criteria:** Fewer than 10 waitlist sign-ups from 300 visitors; or the ministry announces push/e-mail notifications in the state app; or Keyword Planner shows under 100 monthly searches for the target phrases.
**Confidence:** low
**Devil's advocate:** The pain is real (12 of 51 sampled reviews ask for notifications) but the people feeling it are the least likely to pay: micro-firms that refuse to use an invoicing program. Anyone who pays for software already gets downloads and alerts inside inFakt, Fakturownia, wFirma or similar, and those who don't pay have two free notifier apps and two MIT watchers. A second search found a further paid rival: firmly (e-file sp. z o.o.) downloads purchase invoices from KSeF automatically for status tracking, at $3.99/month or $39.99/year in-app ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/firmly/id6464039310), no rating shown yet) — i.e. below the write-up's 15–25 PLN price. The distribution channel is also compromised: CERT Polska warned on 18 Apr 2026 of a phishing campaign using fake "new KSeF invoice" emails and advised fetching invoices "wyłącznie po samodzielnym zalogowaniu bezpośrednio do KSeF" ([android.com.pl](https://android.com.pl/tech/1038580-phishing-faktury-ksef-ostrzezenie-cert/)). A product whose core output is an email saying "you have a new KSeF invoice", and whose onboarding asks a non-technical owner to hand over a KSeF certificate to an unknown site found through SEO, runs straight into that warning. The free viewer has the same trust problem, and its "bez logowania" keyword intent is partly served by the official QR-code verification on KSeF invoice visualisations (jpk.info.pl guide via search summary, unverified). At 19 PLN, 10,000 PLN MRR needs ~530 payers who would otherwise use a free app; there is no lock-in (L9 2) and the ministry can add push to its app at any time.
**Review changes:** L8 3→2 (CERT Polska phishing warning against exactly this kind of email undermines both the cold channel and the product's trust; SEO volume still unmeasured), weighted 2.9→2.7, band 5–15%→<5%. Also new rival firmly at ~$3.99/month caps price further (supports L3 2, L2 2 unchanged). Not rejected: the notification pain is the best-documented end-user pain in the set and a 1-week ads test is cheap, but it is now a candidate to fold into 001 as an office-side feature rather than a stand-alone product.
