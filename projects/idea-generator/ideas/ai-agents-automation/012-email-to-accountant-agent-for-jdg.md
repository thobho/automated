# 012 — "Wyślij do księgowej": e-mail and photo inbox agent that files cost documents for JDG clients
**One-liner:** A dedicated mailbox and WhatsApp/photo drop for sole traders (JDG): forward a receipt or invoice, an LLM extracts the fields, classifies the cost, and pushes a ready item into the accountant's system, with one reminder when something is missing.
**Field:** ai-agents-automation | **Type:** SaaS (merges I074 and I174)
**Target customer:** Small Polish bookkeeping offices (2–10 staff) with 30–150 JDG clients, and JDG owners who send documents by mail or phone. Count: unverified. About 1.75 million active VAT payers are in KSeF scope (L5-F03); offices and share of JDG clients still sending paper or PDFs are unverified.
**Problem:** Clients send documents irregularly, in many formats and messages. A trade article (infor.pl, vendor-linked, dated 22 Aug 2016) notes it is harder when a client sends 30 files across separate e-mails. The office retypes or chases, which is unpaid time against a flat 150–500 PLN/month JDG fee (L3-F25).
**MVP scope (≤6 weeks):** (1) Per-client inbound address and a Telegram/WhatsApp-free photo upload page (no WhatsApp Business approval needed); (2) LLM extraction of NIP, number, dates, amounts, VAT rates to a fixed JSON schema (L5-F27, L5-F28); (3) duplicate detection and "missing document" nudge; (4) office dashboard and CSV/JPK-friendly export for the main office tools (no deep integration with Saldeo/Comarch in v1); (5) Stripe per-client billing. Not in MVP: posting to the ledger, KSeF issuing, tax advice.
**Why now:** LLM-based OCR costs well under a cent per page (Mistral OCR launched at about 1,000 pages per dollar, 6 Mar 2025; Gemini Flash-Lite $0.10 input per 1M tokens) and schema-guaranteed JSON output is standard (L5-F27, F28). KSeF covers purchases from businesses, so the remaining paper-and-photo flow is receipts and foreign invoices; the share is unverified.
**Foreign analogues:** Dext (UK; formerly Receipt Bank): home page claims "over 700,000 businesses and 12,000 accounting and bookkeeping firms"; business plan $25.21/month for 250 documents and 5 users, accountant plans priced per client per month with a 10-client minimum (own page; claims self-reported). Hubdoc and AutoEntry are comparable (not opened).
**Competition (PL / global):** Polish offices already have OCR in their stack: SaldeoSMART (OCR, e-flow, KSeF, integrates with 40+ accounting programs per own page; free tier 5 invoices and 5 read credits per month), Fakturownia (OCR from scan or photo, 30-day trial, "Inbox" module), Comarch ERP XT (client adds documents from scan via app once the office enables it). Gap: none of the pages read describes an e-mail-forwarding inbox per client; unverified whether Saldeo has one (a search summary found no evidence). Global: Dext, Hubdoc, Parseur (generic e-mail parsing).
**Evidence of pain:** Weak. Vendor claims: SaldeoSMART analyst says OCR cut document-entry cost "nawet o 50%" (2016 article, vendor-linked). Comarch's help page and a poradnikprzedsiebiorcy.pl article describe the client-upload flow, which implies the problem is known and served. No complaints from offices or JDG clients (Wykop, Facebook groups, forums) were found; none read. Search-trend data: unreadable, unverified.
**Monetization:** 10–15 PLN per active JDG client per month, billed to the office (estimate; anchor: Dext per-client pricing, 150–500 PLN/month the office bills the client). 10 clients minimum (as Dext). Payer: the office, monthly.
**Revenue sketch (estimate):** Assumes 12 PLN per client, 60 clients per office. 6 months: 4 offices = 2,900 PLN MRR. 12 months: 12 offices = 8,600 PLN MRR. 10,000 PLN MRR needs about 830 clients, about 14 offices of 60.
**First 10 customers:** Small offices in Polish accountant groups on Facebook (existence unverified) and the GOFIN forum (cited in L6-F02); ifirma pays a PLN 100 bounty per referred office, showing that offices respond to referral money (L8-F08), so offer a referral bonus. Cold e-mail to 100 offices found via Google Maps.
**Build complexity:** medium. Mail ingestion (SES/Postmark), LLM schema extraction, accuracy and edge cases (paragony fiskalne, handwritten), GDPR processor role and EU-region model hosting, export formats.
**Pros / Cons / Risks:** Pros: cheap LLM costs; per-client revenue scales; testable with fixtures. Cons: incumbents bundle OCR and will add mail intake; offices tied to their ledger tool; accuracy of 95% still needs review; JDG pain unmeasured. Risks: personal data and invoices processed by third-party LLM APIs (GDPR), churn when offices switch tools, receipts being replaced by KSeF.
**Scores (1–5):** L1 1, L2 1, L3 3, L4 3, L5 3, L6 2, L7 4, L8 3, L9 2 | **Weighted:** 2.5 / 5 (computed 2.47) | **Chance band:** <5% (T16 review: L1 2→1 and L2 2→1, see Review changes; write-up value was 2.7 (computed 2.67), <5%; shortlist 2.9)
**Cheapest validation test (≤1 week, ≤200 PLN):** Run 20 real documents per volunteer office through a script (Mistral OCR or Gemini plus a JSON schema) and show field accuracy; ask 10 offices if they would pay 12 PLN per client per month. Success: ≥95% field accuracy on 100 documents and 3 offices agreeing to a paid pilot.
**Kill criteria:** Fewer than 3 of 20 contacted offices lack a client-upload flow today; or accuracy below 90% on typical receipts.
**Confidence:** low
**Devil's advocate:** The write-up's only gap was "none of the pages read describes an e-mail-forwarding inbox per client". A second search finds one at the best-known Polish online bookkeeping vendor: inFakt clients send cost invoices to a dedicated address of the form "imię.nazwisko@infaktpliki.pl", or photograph them with "Dodaj koszt" in the mobile app, and the document goes straight to their accountant (inFakt blog, published 24 Sep 2015, updated 18 Sep 2024). SaldeoSMART's own help material lists e-mail, interface and mobile app as client intake channels; wFirma has OCR in its online bookkeeping package and suggests the expense type from the second KSeF invoice per supplier; Fakturownia and Comarch ERP XT have photo/scan OCR. So the job is done in Polish, inside the tools offices already post from, and a separate inbox adds a second system plus an export step. Worse, the target volume is shrinking: since 1 Apr 2026 B2B purchase invoices from Polish sellers arrive in KSeF, and inFakt states it pulls them automatically every 15 minutes, leaving mostly fiscal receipts and foreign invoices for this product, a share nobody has measured. Cheap LLM OCR lowers the cost for incumbents as much as for a newcomer. Dext's scale is in markets without a national e-invoice clearing system. There is still no complaint from any office or JDG owner about the current intake flow.
**Review changes:** L1 2→1 (a Polish product, inFakt, already does the exact job: per-client forwarding address plus photo upload, live since 2015), L2 2→1 (inFakt, SaldeoSMART, wFirma, Fakturownia and Comarch all bundle intake/OCR in Polish; no reason to switch), weighted 2.7 (computed 2.67)→2.5, band stays <5%. Rejected: the gap claimed in the write-up does not exist and KSeF removes most of the remaining volume; logged in rejected.md.
