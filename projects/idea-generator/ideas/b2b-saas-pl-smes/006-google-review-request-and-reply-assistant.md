# 006 — Google review request and reply assistant
**One-liner:** A flat-PLN tool for local service businesses (dental and medical practices, car workshops, salons, restaurants) that sends a polite post-visit SMS or QR/link request pointing to the Google review form, tracks the rating trend and drafts replies in Polish with AI for the owner to approve.
**Field:** b2b-saas-pl-smes | **Type:** SaaS
**Target customer:** Owner or manager of a local service business with a Google Business Profile (clinic, workshop, salon, restaurant, trades). Count in Poland: not sourced (unverified). Booksy's salon-software base (3.97/5 from 3,892 iOS ratings, L6-F08) suggests a large listable beauty segment but is not a count.
**Problem:** Satisfied customers rarely post reviews unprompted; vendor blogs say "most satisfied customers won't write a review on their own" and cite Gatherup's claim that 51% would like to write more reviews but forget (vendor sources, treat with caution). Asking at scale needs a link, a QR code or an SMS, plus a consistent reply workflow.
**MVP scope (≤6 weeks):** (1) Onboarding: business name, Google review link (Place ID), SMS sender name. (2) Request channels: printable QR/poster, CSV or manual entry of customer phone numbers, SMS send via SMSAPI with consent checkbox and opt-out, quiet hours. (3) No review gating: every customer gets the same Google link (Google policy forbids encouraging only positive reviews). (4) Dashboard: requests sent, link clicks. (5) Reply drafts: owner pastes a review, gets 3 Polish reply drafts; automatic read/reply through the Business Profile API is v2 because API access needs approval (see Build complexity). Billing with Stripe in PLN.
**Why now:** Not a binding trigger (L5 3). LLMs make Polish reply drafting cheap; Google Business Profile reviews drive local choice (vendor claims, estimate).
**Foreign analogues:** NiceJob (US, home-service businesses): about 75 USD/month, up to 125 USD; Podium about 399 USD/month; Birdeye custom, reportedly from about 300 USD/month (hearsay in the source); GatherUp about 99 USD/month; Grade.us 60 USD per location (3 minimum) or 110 USD/month (conflicting) (all from a vendor-sponsored comparison blog, no official pages opened, treat as rough). Adjacent revenue proof: a Google Maps rank tracker for local-SEO agencies listed on Flippa with 19.1k USD monthly revenue, 522 subscribers, asking 575,000 USD (seller-reported, L4-F16). No public revenue for review-request tools found.
**Competition (PL / global):** meetergo (booking tool with a Polish page, Nuremberg hosting): automatic review-request email after each completed appointment, moderation, widget; free start, price not seen; targets medical practices, salons, coaches, trades. Free QR/link generators (meetergo, QR TIGER, Jotform). Polish e-commerce review platforms (Opineo, Trustmate) serve shops, not Maps; I found no Polish SMS-based Google-review tool aimed at local businesses in one search (not exhaustive; Trustmate and Opinie24 not checked). Global: the vendors above. Gap: Polish-language, flat PLN price, SMS-first for businesses without a booking system; unproven.
**Evidence of pain:** No forum or Reddit complaint found; evidence is vendor blogs (Harbingers, Blabla.ai, Napoleoncat) saying owners must ask proactively. Booksy reviews show salons pay commissions to get found (L6-F08), an indirect signal that discovery is paid for. Search trend: unverified. L6 stays at 2.
**Monetization:** 79–149 PLN net per month per location, annual discount; SMS pack included up to 150 per month (estimate). SMS cost: 0.16–0.17 PLN each on SMSAPI (price page; plus 49 PLN/month subscription for postpaid), so 150 SMS about 25 PLN. Payer: the business owner.
**Revenue sketch (estimate):** Assumptions: 99 PLN per month average, 70% gross margin after SMS. 6 months: 25 customers = about 2,500 PLN MRR. 12 months: 70 customers = about 6,900 PLN MRR. 10,000 PLN MRR needs about 100 customers; churn of small local businesses is likely 4–6% monthly (estimate), which makes 100 hard to hold.
**First 10 customers:** Walk-in/phone outreach to workshops and dental practices in one city (public listings on Google Maps with under 30 reviews are a listable filter), offer 60 days free; Polish Facebook groups for salon and practice owners; agencies that manage Google Business Profiles (resale).
**Build complexity:** low for the MVP above, medium with the Business Profile API. Google requires an access request: an organization account, a Business Profile verified and active for 60+ days and a website, then waiting for review (Google developer docs). Risks: SMS consent and GDPR/telecom rules for marketing-type messages (legal check needed), Google policies against incentives, gating and bulk patterns that can remove reviews, SMS deliverability and sender-name registration.
**Pros / Cons / Risks:** Pros: simple, testable, recurring, cheap SMS, large target base. Cons: low willingness to pay of micro-businesses against free QR tools, churn, pain unmeasured, policy constraints limit features (no gating). Risks: Google changes review rules; customers who spam requests get reviews removed; platform dependency on Google.
**Scores (1–5):** L1 3, L2 3, L3 3, L4 3, L5 3, L6 2, L7 3, L8 3, L9 3 | **Weighted:** 2.9 / 5 | **Chance band:** 5–15% (shortlist 3.1; L4 lowered 4 to 3 as the proof is a seller-reported adjacent listing, L7 lowered 4 to 3 because Business Profile API needs approval; low end of the band)
**Cheapest validation test (≤1 week, ≤200 PLN):** One-page offer "Więcej opinii w Google: SMS po wizycie, 99 PLN/mies." with a manual concierge pilot: ask 15 local practices/workshops (calls, walk-ins) to send you 30 customer numbers each, send the SMS via SMSAPI (about 5 PLN per 30) and report the new-review count after 7 days; success: 3 businesses get 3+ new reviews and agree to pay. Cost: SMS credit under 100 PLN.
**Kill criteria:** Fewer than 3 of 15 businesses willing to pay 99 PLN after seeing results; or response rate below 5% (fewer than 1.5 reviews per 30 SMS); or Google removes a pilot's reviews as manipulation.
**Confidence:** low
