# 022 — Field-service OS for cleaning firms
**One-liner:** One tool for Polish cleaning companies covering quote, job schedule per crew, a mobile checklist app for cleaners, client SMS and KSeF-ready invoicing, a Jobber/ZenMaid equivalent. Research found a Polish-language cleaning-software competitor with a free plan and a rating of 4.8 from 1,708 ratings, and no pain evidence, so the idea scores low.
**Field:** b2b-saas-pl-smes | **Type:** SaaS (web plus mobile web)
**Target customer:** Owners of cleaning companies with 3–50 cleaners (office, stairwell, post-renovation cleaning). Count: a trade-press article citing the BNF database (CEIDG/KRS/REGON) gives 48,726 registered cleaning firms, 4,150 of them new in 2025; the same article cites IBISWorld at about 23,690 active firms in 2024 (single secondary source, different definitions, via search summary of superauto.pl). Share that would pay for software: unknown (unverified).
**Problem:** Cleaning crews are scheduled in Excel and WhatsApp, quotes are typed by hand, and invoices go through a separate accounting tool (assumption: not confirmed by any quote found). Sector pain in Polish was searched for and not found.
**MVP scope (≤6 weeks):** (1) Client and site list with recurring job templates. (2) Weekly crew calendar with drag and drop. (3) Mobile web page for cleaners: today's jobs, checklist, photo, check-in. (4) Quote generator from a price list (m², frequency) to PDF. (5) Auto SMS reminders. (6) Invoice export to an invoicing tool (Fakturownia/wFirma/inFakt API) instead of building invoicing. Not in MVP: booking marketplace, payroll, payments, GPS tracking.
**Why now:** KSeF has been mandatory for all taxpayers' B2B invoices since 1 Apr 2026 (official KSeF pages, see 008), so recurring invoices for office-cleaning contracts must be sent through KSeF; this favours an integration with an invoicing tool, not a new invoicing engine. No other trigger found.
**Foreign analogues:** Jobber (Canada): plans 29–529 USD/month billed annually (first-year rates 21–371 USD), extra users 29 USD; page shows a 4.9 rating from 153 reviews and a "20,000+ home service pros" community (own page, self-reported). ZenMaid (USA, cleaning-specific): Starter 19 USD, Pro 39 USD, Pro Max 49 USD per month, "Used by 2,000+ owners" and "More Than 3,000 Cleaning Service Owners" on its page (self-reported). Housecall Pro: 59–299 USD/month annual (L3-F18). No public revenue figures for any of them (unverified).
**Competition (PL / global):** Anolla offers a Polish-language page "Oprogramowanie dla firm sprzątających": online booking, staff scheduling, CRM, automated messages, admin and client apps on iOS and Android, a free plan at 0 EUR, paid add-ons (Boost, integrations) unpriced on its page, a fee only after a service is confirmed (amount not stated), rating 4.8 from 1,708 ratings, "hundreds of cleaning companies" (own pages; company country not stated). Generic: ClickUp (free plan), Kadromierz, inEwi for schedules. Gap: not shown; invoicing and KSeF integration are the only open points and are not proven to matter.
**Evidence of pain:** None found. A Polish forum search (2026-10-29) for owners complaining about Excel/WhatsApp scheduling returned only vendor blogs and sponsored articles. An Ordio template page (vendor) says Excel stops being enough around 5 people. Search trend unreadable (unverified). Lens 6 scored 1 as a result.
**Monetization:** 99 PLN net/month for up to 5 cleaners, 249 PLN up to 20, 25 PLN per extra cleaner (estimate; compare ZenMaid 19–49 USD, Jobber 29–99 USD for small teams). Payer: owner. Recurring.
**Revenue sketch (estimate):** Assumes 150 PLN average. 6 months: 15 firms = 2,250 PLN MRR. 12 months: 45 firms = 6,750 PLN MRR. 10,000 PLN MRR needs about 67 firms, about 0.15% of 48,726 registered firms.
**First 10 customers:** Facebook groups for cleaning-firm owners (unverified, not readable), outbound to firms with PKD 81.21.Z from CEIDG (consent limits, L8-F13 to F15), Google Maps cleaning firms with 5+ staff, cleaning-trade fairs (unverified).
**Build complexity:** medium. Risks: wide scope, mobile polish for cleaners (web app only), SMS cost, invoicing via third-party APIs.
**Pros / Cons / Risks:** Pros: B2B recurring SaaS, understood model, clear price anchors, listable market. Cons: Polish competitor with a free plan and good ratings, no pain evidence, wide scope. Risks: free tier undercut, high churn among micro-firms, seasonal turnover of staff.
**Scores (1–5):** L1 2, L2 1, L3 3, L4 3, L5 2, L6 1, L7 3, L8 2, L9 3 | **Weighted:** 2.2 / 5 (computed 2.18) | **Chance band:** <5% (T16 review: L2 2→1, see Review changes; L6 = 1 knock-out; write-up value was 2.3 (computed 2.30), <5%; shortlist 2.3)
**Cheapest validation test (≤1 week, ≤200 PLN):** Call 15 cleaning firms (found on Google Maps) and ask how they schedule and quote and whether they know Anolla; offer a free setup of a crew calendar. Success: 5 firms use Excel/WhatsApp and 3 agree to a pilot at 99 PLN/month.
**Kill criteria:** Most firms already use Anolla or similar; or fewer than 3 of 15 say scheduling or quoting is a pain; or no one pays above 50 PLN/month.
**Confidence:** low
**Devil's advocate:** This is the widest MVP in the batch (quotes, crew calendar, mobile checklists, SMS, invoice export) for the market with the least evidence. Two searches in Polish (2026-10-29) found no cleaning-firm owner complaining about scheduling or quoting; the second one returned Anolla's two Polish pages for cleaning firms (calendar, job cards per site, staff schedules, pay-per-use, free plan, 4.8 from 1,708 ratings), ClickUp's free plan and ZenMaid in a Polish roundup, a gig platform (Stepapp) for cleaners, and freelancers building cleaning-order apps on AppSheet, i.e. owners who need something are either using a free tool or commissioning a cheap custom one. Most of the 48,726 registered cleaning firms are sole traders without crews; the 3–50-cleaner segment is uncounted. Micro-firms with high staff turnover churn, the free plan sets the price expectation at zero, and the only Polish-specific hook (KSeF invoicing) is already solved by the invoicing tools the MVP would export to. A newcomer would need field sales to cleaning-firm owners, which does not fit ~10 h/week.
**Review changes:** L2 2→1 (a well-rated Polish-language cleaning tool with a free plan, plus free generic schedulers; no reason to switch), weighted 2.3→2.2, band stays <5%. Rejected as the write-up recommended: no pain evidence (L6 = 1 knock-out), wide scope, free incumbent; logged in rejected.md.
