# 021 — KRS/CEIDG change monitor for B2B credit and KYC
**One-liner:** A subscription that watches a customer's list of counterparties in KRS and CEIDG and sends an alert (email, webhook, weekly digest) when management, address, capital, status or insolvency entries change, with a plain-language diff. Findings show a free official KRS newsletter and at least one cheap paid competitor already exist, so the wedge would have to be CEIDG coverage plus diff quality plus accounting-software integration.
**Field:** data-products | **Type:** SaaS (with API)
**Target customer:** Accounting offices, small distributors and B2B sellers who give trade credit, procurement and compliance staff at SMEs. Size: KRS covers 874,686 companies (Prześwietl.pl home page, database coverage); the number of firms that monitor counterparties: unknown (unverified).
**Problem:** Before extending credit or paying invoices, firms check whether a counterparty changed its board, address, capital or status. Checking manually is tedious; free tools alert only on the user's own entities or on limited lists (see Competition).
**MVP scope (≤6 weeks):** (1) Watchlist by NIP/KRS (CSV upload, API). (2) Daily poll of the public KRS API and CEIDG data warehouse API for changes; field-level diff. (3) Email and webhook alerts; weekly digest. (4) VAT white-list status check. (5) Stripe subscription by number of watched entities. Not in MVP: payment-delay data, scoring, KRD or BIK data (licensed).
**Why now:** From 1 Jun 2026 mObywatel shows KRS data and alerts, but only for entities where the user is a representative, procurator or supervisory board member (gov.pl), which does not serve counterparty monitoring. No binding deadline found. (Cuts both ways: the state is moving into alerts.)
**Foreign analogues:** North Data (Germany) and Creditsafe-type monitoring; North Data's pricing page returned 429 (unverified), so no traction figures were read. Revenue evidence: none found.
**Competition (PL / global):** Official, free: "Newsletter KRS" in Portal Rejestrów Sądowych, subscriptions up to 50 entities for 12 months with renewal; notices contain only the KRS number, case reference and date, not the content of the change (search summary of a consumer guide, with the 50-entity limit from a single secondary source, so unverified). Paid: Prześwietl.pl is reported to charge 59 PLN net/month for unlimited KRS-CEIDG monitoring of companies and links (single vendor blog, price not on the site's home page; the home page shows a free trial); BIK "Alerty o Kontrahencie" in its 19 Nov 2025 price list (amounts unclear in the search summary, PDF later returned 404); LEX Informator Gospodarczy and KRD offer e-mail alerts including debtor-register entries. rejestr.io blocked fetch (403). Gap: CEIDG change coverage and diffs are not clearly offered by the free tools; paid Polish aggregators were only partly checked.
**Evidence of pain:** No complaint or request for a counterparty-change monitor was found in forums or reviews (searches 2026-10-29). Indirect demand: several vendors (Prześwietl.pl, LEX, KRD, BIK) sell exactly this kind of alert, and the KRS newsletter exists because of company-theft concerns (KPMG and others wrote about it). Search trend unreadable (unverified).
**Monetization:** 49 PLN net/month for 100 watched entities, 149 PLN for 1,000, API plan 299 PLN (estimate, anchored to Prześwietl.pl at 59 PLN). Payer: accounting office or finance manager. Recurring monthly, with value rising with the list size.
**Revenue sketch (estimate):** At 90 PLN average. 6 months: 20 customers = 1,800 PLN MRR. 12 months: 60 customers = 5,400 PLN MRR. 10,000 PLN MRR needs about 110 customers; with a free official alternative, a conversion of that scale is uncertain.
**First 10 customers:** Accounting-office networks and KIDP / SKwP newsletters (L8-F12), outbound to firms from CEIDG by PKD (consent limits apply, L8-F13 to F15), integration listing in an accounting tool marketplace, a free 10-entity tier.
**Build complexity:** low–medium. Risks: KRS and CEIDG API limits and terms (L8-F10, F11), diffing XML or JSON documents, and the registers' own latency.
**Pros / Cons / Risks:** Pros: public data, easy to test, recurring, listable buyers. Cons: official free newsletter, cheap paid rival, product easy to copy, no pain evidence. Risks: registers add alerts; mObywatel feature extends; licence terms of data reuse (unverified).
**Scores (1–5):** L1 2, L2 1, L3 3, L4 2, L5 2, L6 2, L7 4, L8 3, L9 3 | **Weighted:** 2.5 / 5 (computed 2.49) | **Chance band:** <5% (T16 review: L2 2→1, see Review changes; write-up value was 2.6 (computed 2.61), <5%; shortlist 2.6)
**Cheapest validation test (≤1 week, ≤200 PLN):** Landing page "Alerts when your 100 counterparties change board, address or status" with a CSV upload that produces a sample change report from the last 30 days; send to 40 accounting offices by manual B2B email (needs a lawful basis) and post in accountant groups. Success: 10 uploads, 3 ask for ongoing alerts.
**Kill criteria:** Fewer than 5 uploads; or testers say the free PRS newsletter or Prześwietl.pl is enough; or the KRS/CEIDG API terms forbid commercial re-use.
**Confidence:** low
**Devil's advocate:** Counterparty monitoring in Poland is a crowded, tiered market, and the free tier is the state. The KRS newsletter in Portal Rejestrów Sądowych is free, takes any KRS number, covers up to 50 entities for up to a year and notifies when a case is registered or an entry is made (FakturaXL guide, a second source confirming the 50-entity limit the write-up marked unverified). From 31 May / 1 Jun 2026 registry alerts also reach mObywatel for one's own entities. Above it: Prześwietl.pl's reported 59 PLN net/month for unlimited KRS-CEIDG monitoring (which already covers the write-up's "CEIDG plus diffs" wedge, though the price is from a vendor blog), IMSiG's insolvency and restructuring notice monitoring at 120 PLN/month for 50 debtors up to 600 PLN for unlimited, with liquidation notices as an add-on, and debt-register alerts from KRD and BIG, BIK "Alerty o Kontrahencie", LEX Informator Gospodarczy. The changes a credit manager actually cares about (insolvency, debt-register entries, payment behaviour) live in the licensed registers this MVP excludes; board and address changes alone are weak credit signals. The product is easy to copy, uses the same free APIs as everyone else, and nobody was found asking for it. The state is moving towards alerts, not away from them.
**Review changes:** L2 2→1 (free official newsletter plus at least five paid Polish monitoring services, one covering KRS and CEIDG at 59 PLN; no reason to switch), weighted 2.6 (computed 2.61)→2.5, band stays <5%. IMSiG's 120–600 PLN/month plans show firms do pay for monitoring, so L3 stays 3. Rejected: the write-up's own wedge (CEIDG coverage and diffs) is already sold; logged in rejected.md.
