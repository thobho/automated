# 008 — WooCommerce → KSeF bridge (edge cases handled), with a Ceneo feed pack as a later add-on
**One-liner:** A free WordPress.org plugin plus a paid backend that sends WooCommerce B2B invoices to KSeF reliably, including the cases that break today's add-ons (foreign buyers without a Polish NIP, EUR invoices, OSS, B2C filtering, rejected-invoice handling, retries), with a status page per order. The Ceneo feed pack in the longlist entry is dropped from the MVP (not researched).
**Field:** ecommerce-sellers | **Type:** SaaS (WordPress plugin front end)
**Target customer:** Owner or operator of a Polish WooCommerce shop that sells to businesses (B2B invoices). Count: BuiltWith detects WooCommerce Checkout on 23,201 Polish sites (10.54% of tracked shop sites; Shoper 16,381, PrestaShop 16,033, IdoSell 4,419; snapshot date not given, counts are website detections, via a search summary because the BuiltWith page needed verification). The share issuing B2B invoices is unknown (unverified).
**Problem:** Only B2B invoices must go through KSeF; shops selling only to consumers need not use it (ksef.podatki.gov.pl training deck, Shoper guide); OSS, IOSS and proforma invoices cannot be issued in KSeF voluntarily (mBank article, per search summary). From 1 Jan 2027, a till receipt with a buyer NIP up to 450 zł stops counting as an invoice, so shops must issue KSeF invoices on request. Existing add-ons show edge-case failures (see Evidence).
**MVP scope (≤6 weeks):** (1) WooCommerce plugin (PHP, thin): order-to-invoice mapping for B2B orders, a flag to skip B2C. (2) Backend (Java or Node): FA(3) generation, KSeF 2.0 session handling per shop, idempotent send, retries, UPO retrieval, rejection reasons in plain Polish. (3) Edge rules: buyer without Polish NIP (foreign VAT ID or none), EUR invoices with VAT in PLN, advance invoices, corrections. (4) Order screen showing KSeF status and number, PDF visualization with QR. (5) Stripe subscription, plugin on WordPress.org (free front door, paid service per the directory's rules). Not in MVP: other shop platforms, Allegro, Ceneo feeds.
**Why now:** KSeF mandatory for all taxpayers from 1 Apr 2026 and the receipt-as-invoice rule ends 1 Jan 2027 (official stage page and training deck). Add-on market is young: 19 WordPress.org results for "ksef", only two with meaningful installs (L2-F06).
**Foreign analogues:** Italy's SdI e-invoicing created the same plugin niche: "POP – e-invoicing for WooCommerce: SdI, PEPPOL, KSeF (ex-WooPop)" lists 80+ installs and 15 ratings on WordPress.org, a small base, so the analogue shows existence not scale. Revenue evidence: none found (unverified).
**Competition (PL / global):** WP Desk "Flexible Invoices for WooCommerce – KSeF Add-on" (700+ installs, 1 rating, changelog version 2.4.11 dated 2026-08-19, requires Flexible Invoices 6.2.0+; PRO needed for automatic generation and OSS; support for PRO only, per search summary); Flexible PDF Invoices (6,000+ installs, 37 ratings); B2Brouter for WooCommerce (10+ installs); POP (80+); about 14 more plugins with fewer than 10 installs each (WordPress.org search, 2026-10-29); invoicing SaaS integrations (Fakturownia, wFirma, inFakt) bundle KSeF and connect to WooCommerce (L3-F26). Price of the WP Desk add-on not found. Gap: edge cases and reliability (below), not feature absence.
**Evidence of pain:** The WP Desk KSeF add-on's support forum lists 21 threads (last activity from 3 weeks to 9 months ago); about 15 concern KSeF failures: "Sending error for a customer from Greece" (10 replies), "Invoice sending error for IL and BR" (10), "EUR invoice: VAT wrongly sent in PLN to KSeF" (9), "XML schema validation failed, unknown error" (5), "Problem sending invoices to KSeF in production", "Stopping automatic emails when KSeF rejects invoices", "How to block sending B2C invoices to KSeF" (22 replies), "Filling field P_12_XII for OSS sellers" (14), "Automatic mapping of NP I and NP II" (14) (titles translated from Polish; resolution status not shown). Fakturownia on Trustpilot: 3.3 from 3 reviews, one 2-star "Bardzo często blokowane jest połączenie z Woocommerce." (n=3, L2-F14). Counter: support threads show an active vendor, and users of a plugin with 700+ installs are a small sample. Search trend: unverified.
**Monetization:** Free plugin, paid backend per shop: 29 PLN net/month up to 200 invoices, 59 PLN up to 1,000 (estimate; no competitor price found, so this is unanchored). Payer: shop owner. Stays within the 15–60 PLN/month range of KSeF-bundled invoicing apps (L3-F26: inFakt from 4.99, wFirma 19–54, Faktura.pl 18.99).
**Revenue sketch (estimate):** Assumes 35 PLN per month average. 6 months: 40 shops = 1,400 PLN MRR. 12 months: 150 shops = 5,250 PLN MRR. 10,000 PLN MRR needs about 285 shops, about 1.2% of the 23,201 detected WooCommerce sites (unknown B2B share, so reaching it is uncertain).
**First 10 customers:** Answer the unresolved threads in the WP Desk add-on forum and Polish WooCommerce groups with a fix offer; WordPress.org listing (search for "ksef"); SEO for "WooCommerce KSeF faktura obcokrajowiec/EUR/OSS"; direct outreach to shops whose pages show B2B ordering (NIP field at checkout, detectable).
**Build complexity:** medium. Risks: FA(3) mapping rules (OSS, NP I/NP II, advance invoices), WooCommerce ecosystem variety (different tax plugins), WordPress.org review (about four weeks, unnecessary external calls flagged, L7-F08), PHP plugin outside the founder's main stack (agent-built, and a community PHP client exists).
**Pros / Cons / Risks:** Pros: a marketplace where buyers search, documented edge-case failures, recurring need per invoice, free front door. Cons: B2C is out of scope so the addressable share is unknown; WooCommerce is only one of four large Polish platforms; incumbent invoicing SaaS connect to WooCommerce too. Risks: WordPress.org rules on paid features, platform dependency on WooCommerce, ministry rule changes.
**Scores (1–5):** L1 2, L2 3, L3 3, L4 3, L5 4, L6 4, L7 4, L8 3, L9 3 | **Weighted:** 3.3 / 5 | **Chance band:** 5–15% (shortlist 3.0; L6 raised 2 to 4 from the support-forum count, L8 stays 3 because install counts of the leaders are low)
**Cheapest validation test (≤1 week, ≤200 PLN):** Post in the WP Desk plugin forum threads and two Polish WooCommerce groups: "I fix KSeF sending for foreign buyers, EUR and OSS orders; send me a test order, free for 30 days"; collect 10 shops, then concierge-send their invoices from a script against the KSeF DEMO environment. Success: 5 shops submit orders, 3 would pay 35 PLN/month. Cost: under 100 PLN.
**Kill criteria:** Fewer than 5 of the 15 forum posters or group members reply; or most of the replying shops sell only to consumers; or WP Desk or an invoicing SaaS ships OSS/foreign-buyer handling first.
**Confidence:** low–medium
