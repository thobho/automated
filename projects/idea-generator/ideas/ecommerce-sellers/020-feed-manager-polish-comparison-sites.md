# 020 — Feed manager for Polish comparison sites and marketplaces
**One-liner:** A hosted tool that takes a Polish shop's product feed (WooCommerce, PrestaShop, Shopify, XML/CSV), fixes titles, EANs and categories, maps categories to Ceneo, Skąpiec and Allegro rules, validates before upload and alerts on rejected offers. The findings suggest the gap is narrow (Ceneo and Allegro quality checks), not "feed management" in general.
**Field:** ecommerce-sellers | **Type:** SaaS
**Target customer:** Owners and e-commerce managers of Polish online shops that list products on Ceneo or Google Shopping and sell on several channels. Ceneo says its database holds about 39 thousand stores, about 70 million monthly users and 349.5 million offers (Ceneo's 20th-anniversary figures as reported by Polish tech press, self-reported marketing, date of the store count not stated). Share of shops that actively manage feeds beyond a platform plugin: unknown (unverified).
**Problem:** Each channel wants its own XML format, category tree and attribute rules; platform plugins export a generic feed and shops fix errors by hand. Complaints found concern errors and stock mismatches in a Shopify Ceneo app (see Evidence), which is thin evidence.
**MVP scope (≤6 weeks):** (1) Import feeds by URL (Google, Ceneo, Allegro-compatible XML, CSV). (2) Rule editor for titles, prices, stock and exclusions (no-code rules, preview). (3) Export templates for Ceneo, Skąpiec and Google Merchant Center. (4) Validator with plain-Polish error messages and a daily health check. (5) Stripe subscription by SKU count. Not in MVP: bidding, image editing, scraping, Allegro listing creation.
**Why now:** No dated trigger found; Ceneo and Google formats change slowly (unverified). LLMs make title rewriting and category mapping cheaper, but nothing forces shops to act.
**Foreign analogues:** Mergado (Czechia): edits and exports e-shop feeds to comparison sites and marketplaces, 30-day free trial, extensions Feed Image Editor 79 EUR/month, Marketplaces 11 EUR, Translate 18 EUR, Bidding Fox 79 EUR; no customer count or revenue found (L1-F22). DataFeedWatch (Netherlands): Shop 64 USD/month (1k SKUs, 3 feeds), Merchant 84 USD, Agency 239 USD; its pricing page does not list Ceneo; it claims 2000+ channel integrations. Channable's pricing page returned 429 (unverified). Revenue evidence for any of them: none found.
**Competition (PL / global):** BaseLinker generates a "Ceneo – XML" export from any warehouse, which Ceneo reads every few hours; up to 20 full exports a day (BaseLinker help); this gives many multichannel Polish sellers a built-in solution. Witly feed app for Shoper (Google Merchant Center feed, daily refresh). WooCommerce: CTX Feed has 90,000+ installs and 838 ratings; a search for "ceneo" on WordPress.org returns only generic feed plugins (4 results, L2-F20). Shopify: Ceneo Feed XML from 4.99 USD/month, Komnata Feed 30 USD/month. Mergado and DataFeedWatch above. Gap: Ceneo-specific validation and monitoring with Polish messages; unproven.
**Evidence of pain:** Shopify app "Ceneo Feed XML": 1.3 of 5 from 3 reviews; 1★ 2026-10-12 "Cały czas robi błędy 500"; 1★ 2025-03-14 "Problemy w synchronizacji stanów magazynowych" (selling items already out of stock). A March 2025 forum thread shows a Ceneo XML import failing in HikaShop, a configuration issue. A web search for Ceneo rejected-offer threads returned none (searches 2026-10-29), so pain is unmeasured beyond this; Google Trends unreadable (unverified).
**Monetization:** Subscription by SKU count: 49 PLN up to 1,000 SKUs, 99 PLN up to 10,000, 199 PLN up to 50,000 (estimate, between the 5–30 USD Shopify apps and DataFeedWatch's 64 USD). Payer: shop owner or e-commerce manager. Recurring while the shop sells on comparison sites.
**Revenue sketch (estimate):** Assumes 90 PLN average. 6 months: 25 shops = 2,250 PLN MRR. 12 months: 70 shops = 6,300 PLN MRR. 10,000 PLN MRR needs about 110 shops, about 0.3% of Ceneo's 39 thousand stores.
**First 10 customers:** WordPress.org and Shopify App Store listings; answering "Ceneo feed" threads in PrestaShop/WooCommerce groups (unverified); outbound to small shops listed on Ceneo with a free feed audit ("we found 37 offers Ceneo would reject"); agencies that run Ceneo for clients.
**Build complexity:** low–medium. Risks: Ceneo's feed spec and the rejection reasons need hands-on shop access; Allegro rules need API access; large feeds need streaming.
**Pros / Cons / Risks:** Pros: pure data transformation, easy to test, recurring need, B2B payer, listable market. Cons: BaseLinker, Shoper and WooCommerce plugins cover the basics; pain not measured; Mergado already reaches Poland. Risks: platform-embedded feeds improving, churn when a shop stops using comparison sites.
**Scores (1–5):** L1 2, L2 2, L3 3, L4 3, L5 1, L6 2, L7 4, L8 3, L9 3 | **Weighted:** 2.6 / 5 (computed 2.61) | **Chance band:** <5% (T16 review: L1 3→2, see Review changes; write-up value was 2.7 (computed 2.69), <5%; shortlist 2.7)
**Cheapest validation test (≤1 week, ≤200 PLN):** Free "feed audit for Ceneo" landing page: shops paste their feed URL, a script returns a report of missing EANs, duplicate titles and category issues. Post in PrestaShop/WooCommerce Polish groups. Success: 20 feeds submitted, 5 of 20 ask for continuous monitoring and 3 accept 49 PLN/month.
**Kill criteria:** Fewer than 10 feeds submitted; or audits show nearly no Ceneo-specific errors; or respondents say BaseLinker/plugin export is enough.
**Confidence:** low
**Devil's advocate:** The product is a "Mergado for Poland", but Poland already has a home-grown feed optimiser and Mergado already sells here. Sembot (Rzeszów) lists a free Shopify app in Polish for editing and optimising thousands of products "za pomocą filtrów i reguł" with AI product descriptions (0 reviews on the listing), and a competitor's comparison describes Sembot Suite as a feed optimiser plus Google Ads generator and price monitor that is strongest in Poland with Ceneo and Allegro integrations (Dealavo blog, a rival vendor, so treat with care). BaseLinker, which most multichannel Polish sellers already pay for, generates the Ceneo XML that Ceneo reads every few hours; Polish agencies (e.g. Harbingers in Kraków) sell feed optimisation as a service on top of DataFeedWatch, Channable or Feedonomics. That leaves "Ceneo-specific validation with Polish error messages", a feature narrow enough that BaseLinker or Sembot can add it, and for which the only pain found is two 1★ reviews of one Shopify app (whose problems are 500 errors and stock sync, i.e. that app's bugs). There is no trigger (L5 = 1) and comparison-site traffic is a mature channel; Ceneo's 39 thousand stores are mostly served by their shop platform's built-in export. The 49 PLN entry price sits above free and 5 USD alternatives with no evidence that shops pay more for validation.
**Review changes:** L1 3→2 (a Polish feed-rules tool, Sembot, exists alongside Mergado's Polish presence, so "missing in Poland" no longer holds), weighted 2.7 (computed 2.69)→2.6, band stays <5%. Not rejected: the free "Ceneo feed audit" test in the write-up costs almost nothing and would show quickly whether Ceneo-specific errors are common, but the idea is a feature-sized wedge.
