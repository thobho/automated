# 010 — Tutoring and language-school manager (Polish: grafik, rozliczenia lektorów, płatności rodziców)
**One-liner:** A Polish-language admin tool for small tutoring centres and language schools: group and one-to-one schedule, attendance, monthly parent invoices with reminders, and automatic lecturer pay statements, priced per active student or per teacher.
**Field:** education-exams | **Type:** SaaS (merges I128 and I129)
**Target customer:** Owners of private language schools, tutoring centres and music or hobby schools with 3–30 teachers in Poland. Count: unverified; searches for a GUS/REGON count of PKD 85.59 entities returned none (web search 2026-10-29). Only company-reported figure found: Leader School franchise claims 28 sites (not a market count).
**Problem:** The owner schedules groups, tracks who attended, chases monthly fees and works out what each lecturer is owed from attendance, often in spreadsheets (assumption, not measured; see Evidence of pain). TutorCruncher's pitch is exactly this: lesson reports, invoicing and automatic tutor payroll.
**MVP scope (≤6 weeks):** (1) Groups, students, teachers, rooms, recurring lessons with a calendar; (2) attendance marking and lesson reports; (3) monthly fee schedule per student with a payment link (Stripe/Przelewy24) and automatic reminders; (4) lecturer settlement sheet (hours x rate, CSV/PDF); (5) parent portal. Not in MVP: e-learning, KSeF-grade invoicing (export to the school's invoicing tool instead), native mobile apps.
**Why now:** No regulatory trigger (L5 = 1). Only enabler: cheap AI agents lower build cost, which also lowers it for the incumbents.
**Foreign analogues:** TutorCruncher (UK): pay-as-you-go from £25/mo, Startup £60, Enterprise £200; own page claims "more than 10 years" and structured data 5.0 from 575 ratings (self-reported). Teach 'n Go (pricing in EUR/USD): €69/mo for up to 100 live students, €269 at 1,000, "schools in 55+ countries" (own claim). Optimate: per active teacher, USD 14.98/mo for 1 teacher up to USD 98.98 for 21–30; claims 370+ schools (inconsistent with 290 on the same page); its Polish branch and Europe focus are inferred, not stated.
**Competition (PL / global):** Not an empty niche. LangLion (Polish page; privacy notice names Primeon sp. z o.o., Warsaw): minimum 115 PLN/month, pay for active students, 14-day trial, modules for payments, finance and CRM. Teach 'n Go has a Polish-language page. Optimate targets language schools and tutoring centres. Anolla (free plan at 0 EUR, hundreds of tutors claimed, owner not named) has a Polish page for tutors. "Program Szkoła" is a legacy Windows programme listed on instalki.pl. Odoo is used as a generic option. Gap: none proven; a Polish-first product with lecturer-contract (umowa zlecenie) settlements might be sharper, but that is unverified.
**Evidence of pain:** None found in Polish. Searched: Polish queries on programs for language schools and tutoring centres (web search, 2026-10-29) returned vendor and catalogue pages, no complaints or forum threads. One job ad (via search summary) says a school uses LangLion for groups, calendar and lecturer-hour settlements, which shows the workflow exists, not that it hurts. Search-trend data: Google Trends not readable by the tools used (same limitation as in T13); unverified.
**Monetization:** Per active student (as LangLion) or per teacher (as Optimate). Suggested: 99 PLN/month for up to 50 students, 199 PLN for up to 150, 299 PLN above (estimate, in line with the 115 PLN LangLion floor and €69 Teach 'n Go). Payer: school owner, monthly, no commitment (competitors offer no lock-in).
**Revenue sketch (estimate):** Assumes 150 PLN average. 6 months: 15 schools = 2,250 PLN MRR. 12 months: 40 schools = 6,000 PLN MRR. 10,000 PLN MRR needs about 67 schools at 150 PLN; at 99 PLN about 101. The size of the reachable market is unverified, so the 20x sanity check from criteria.md cannot be passed.
**First 10 customers:** List 200 Polish language schools and tutoring centres from Google Maps and Facebook pages (a listable audience), cold e-mail/DM with a free migration of their spreadsheet and 3 months at half price; post in Facebook groups for school owners (not read, existence unverified).
**Build complexity:** low–medium. Calendar with recurring lessons and exceptions, billing and settlements are standard CRUD plus Stripe. Risks: payment collection from parents (Stripe/P24 fees), polish invoice export, GDPR for children's data.
**Pros / Cons / Risks:** Pros: recurring B2B revenue, easy to specify and test, owner-operator buyers who decide fast. Cons: four or more existing Polish-capable tools, no pain evidence, no trigger, switching cost for a school is moderate and then sticky. Risks: price war from tools with free tiers (Anolla), seasonality (September start, summer churn), children's data under GDPR.
**Scores (1–5):** L1 2, L2 1, L3 3, L4 3, L5 1, L6 2, L7 4, L8 3, L9 2 | **Weighted:** 2.4 / 5 (computed 2.39) | **Chance band:** <5% (T16 review: L2 2→1, L3 4→3, L9 3→2, see Review changes; write-up value was 2.8 (computed 2.76), <5%; shortlist 3.0 / 5–15%)
**Cheapest validation test (≤1 week, ≤200 PLN):** Landing page "Grafik, płatności i rozliczenia lektorów w jednym miejscu, od 99 zł/mies." with a waitlist; DM 60 schools from Google Maps with one question: "How do you compute lecturer pay and chase fees now?" Success: 5 replies describing a manual process and 3 agree to a 20-minute call. Spend ≤150 PLN on Google Ads for "program do zarządzania szkołą językową".
**Kill criteria:** Fewer than 3 of 60 contacted schools reply with a concrete pain; or most replies say they already use LangLion/Teach 'n Go and are satisfied.
**Confidence:** low
**Devil's advocate:** The write-up already found four Polish-capable tools; a second search (Polish query "program do zarządzania szkołą językową rozliczenia lektorów", 2026-10-29) found at least seven more that ship the exact MVP. Vibeclass computes lecturer hours from the schedule with individual rates, exports monthly PDF/CSV for accounting, takes card/BLIK/transfer from parents, sends reminders and has a parent app, from 49 PLN net/month for 20 active users. LingoDesk advertises automatic lecturer payouts, student balances, hour packages, instalments, a debtor list, Stripe and Fakturownia integration, priced per active student with a 30-day free trial and free migration (the same "free migration" hook this write-up planned for its first customers). ActiveNow totals lecturer hours into monthly pay, sends automatic payment reminders and claims to serve "ponad 2500 placówek" (self-reported). Luppo, Lingwista, Tuntievo and TenvirkCM also appear (search-summary level, not opened). The only gap the write-up suggested, umowa-zlecenie lecturer settlements, is already marketed as automatic lecturer payouts by three of them. A new entrant would be the twelfth Polish option, without a regulatory trigger (L5 = 1), with no pain heard, into a price range that now starts at 49 PLN, half the planned 99 PLN floor. At a realistic 99 PLN it needs ~101 schools, and the reachable market is still uncounted, so the 20× check cannot be passed. The validation test would most likely end at the write-up's own kill criterion ("already use … and are satisfied").
**Review changes:** L2 2→1 (at least eleven Polish-language competitors offering the full MVP, one claiming 2,500+ institutions), L3 4→3 (recurring payment is proven, but the planned 99 PLN floor sits above Vibeclass at 49 PLN and per-active-student pricing at LangLion/LingoDesk, so price power is weak), L9 3→2 (price war from 49 PLN tools and free trials with free migration; switching cost protects incumbents, not a newcomer), weighted 2.8 (computed 2.76)→2.4, band stays <5%. Rejected: no product gap, no trigger and no pain evidence; logged in rejected.md.
