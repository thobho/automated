# 019 — Feedback assistant for online language teachers
**One-liner:** A tool for independent online language teachers (Preply, italki and own-student tutors) that turns a lesson transcript or the teacher's quick bullet points into a polished post-lesson feedback note, homework and vocabulary list. The key finding is that Preply already ships this inside its own platform, which is why the idea scores low.
**Field:** education-exams | **Type:** SaaS (browser extension plus web app)
**Target customer:** Independent online language tutors. Preply alone says it connects more than 100,000 tutors in 180+ countries (OpenAI case-study page, via search summary because the page returned 403; platform figure). The number of tutors who teach on several platforms or privately: unknown (unverified). Most are outside Poland, so no Polish moat.
**Problem:** After each lesson a tutor writes notes and feedback for the student (grammar, vocabulary, homework). FeedbackPanda was built in 2017 to cut this chore with templates. The effort is real for a tutor teaching 20+ lessons a week (estimate), but no tutor complaint about it was found (see Evidence).
**MVP scope (≤6 weeks):** (1) Chrome extension or web upload for a lesson recording or a Zoom/Meet transcript. (2) LLM pass producing feedback in the teacher's own saved style (grammar errors, vocabulary, pronunciation hints, homework). (3) Student profiles with note history. (4) One-click copy to Preply/italki message box. (5) Stripe subscription. Not in MVP: video hosting, calendar, payments between student and tutor.
**Why now:** Cheap speech-to-text makes the core feature possible: OpenAI's gpt-4o-mini-transcribe is listed at USD 0.003 per minute (L5-F25, OpenAI pricing page). But the platforms see the same opportunity (below), so it is a trigger for competitors as much as for this idea.
**Foreign analogues:** FeedbackPanda (templates for online English teachers), founded 2017 by Arvid Kahl and Danielle Simpson; about USD 20,000 MRR within nine months, about USD 55,000 MRR and about 5,000 customers at its sale to SureSwift Capital in June 2019 for "seven figures" (founder interview on theygotacquired.com, self-reported). Today the feedbackpanda.com home page returned unrelated gambling content when fetched on 2026-10-29, so the product may be abandoned or the domain taken over (unverified; treat that page as untrusted). Other analogues: Audilingua (Chrome extension for English lessons, listing dated Feb 2026 with 5 users), Lesson Transcriber (first transcription free, then USD 7/month, per a search summary of its listing), Voilà (tutor tool for Google Meet and Zoom, price not found).
**Competition (PL / global):** Preply's own Lesson Insights: after each 1:1 lesson an AI analyses the transcript and produces summary, feedback on grammar, vocabulary and pronunciation, speaking time and key vocabulary; Preply/OpenAI state that 70%+ of tutors actively use it with a 4.7/5 satisfaction rating (self-reported marketing numbers). italki Premium plan USD 5.99/month (USD 4.99 annual) lists an "Advanced Lesson Summary" for students (toolradar summary). General transcribers (NoteGPT, AI Class & Meeting Assistant) cost little. No Polish product surfaced for tutors. Gap: tutors who work off-platform or on italki, and style personalisation; both small.
**Evidence of pain:** No tutor complaint about feedback or notes workload was found in a web search (2026-10-29); the r/PreplyTutors (about complaints on cancellations and commissions) and r/iTalki (about 9k members) subreddits were not readable, so this lens stays at "unverified". Indirect demand signal: 70%+ of Preply tutors use the platform's own feature.
**Monetization:** Individual tutor subscription of USD 9–15/month (estimate), anchored by Lesson Transcriber at USD 7. Payer: the tutor (a low-income self-employed person, price sensitive).
**Revenue sketch (estimate):** At USD 12 (about 48 PLN): 6 months 60 tutors = 2,900 PLN MRR; 12 months 200 tutors = 9,600 PLN MRR. 10,000 PLN MRR needs about 210 tutors. Unit cost: 100 lessons a month of 50 min at USD 0.003/min is USD 15 in transcription alone (arithmetic), which wipes out the margin at this price unless the tutor dictates short notes instead of uploading audio.
**First 10 customers:** Teacher groups for online English tutors (Facebook, Reddit; not readable, so unverified), Chrome Web Store, a free tier with five notes per month. FeedbackPanda's launch story gives word of mouth in a niche profession as the channel, with a co-founder who teaches.
**Build complexity:** low. Risks: recording audio of lessons needs student consent under GDPR; capture of audio from Preply's classroom is a platform terms question (unverified); transcription model retirements.
**Pros / Cons / Risks:** Pros: easy to build and test, global market, English-language product, a proven analogue. Cons: the biggest platform already built the feature; payers are low-income; no Polish advantage; unit costs high with audio. Risks: platform dependency (Preply or italki can ban or copy it), churn when tutors leave platforms, GDPR for audio of minors.
**Scores (1–5):** L1 2, L2 2, L3 2, L4 3, L5 3, L6 2, L7 4, L8 3, L9 2 | **Weighted:** 2.5 / 5 | **Chance band:** <5% (shortlist 2.8; L1 falls 3 to 2 since the analogue's site looks defunct and the market is not Polish; L2 falls 3 to 2 because Preply ships Lesson Insights to 70%+ of its tutors; L7 falls 5 to 4 because lesson audio comes from third-party platforms)
**Verdict note:** Likely to be rejected in the review (T16). Revive only for tutors outside Preply, or as a text-only note generator.
**Cheapest validation test (≤1 week, ≤200 PLN):** Post a "write your lesson notes in 30 seconds" demo with a waitlist form in r/iTalki and tutor Facebook groups; ask 10 tutors whether they pay for Lesson Insights alternatives. Success: 30 sign-ups and 5 tutors who say they would pay USD 10.
**Kill criteria:** Fewer than 15 sign-ups; or most respondents say Preply's Lesson Insights is enough; or tutors do not want to upload lesson audio.
**Confidence:** low
