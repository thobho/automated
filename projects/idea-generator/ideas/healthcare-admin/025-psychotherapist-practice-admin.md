# 025 — Psychotherapist practice admin
**One-liner:** A lightweight practice tool for self-employed psychotherapists and psychologists in Poland: online booking with recurring sessions, SMS/e-mail reminders, BLIK payments and invoices (KSeF-ready), a client list and short encrypted session notes, with no clinical or NFZ integration. It is the Calendesk model aimed at one profession. Research found established Polish and global rivals and no complaint evidence.
**Field:** healthcare-admin | **Type:** SaaS
**Target customer:** Private-practice psychotherapists, psychologists and coaches working alone or in small offices. Count: no official figure. A February 2025 Ministry of Health answer to an interpellation says it holds no data on the number of psychotherapists, other than 402 certified ones employed in hospitals (via search summary of api.sejm.gov.pl); the profession is not regulated (no state licence, certificates issued by associations such as PTP and societies under Polska Rada Psychoterapii). Market size therefore unverified.
**Problem:** A solo therapist runs bookings, cancellations, reminders, payments and notes in a calendar, a messenger and a spreadsheet or a general medical system. Session data are special-category health data under GDPR art. 9 (even the fact of attending therapy, per a Polish guide in a search summary), so tool choice carries compliance questions: DPA, EU servers, encryption, data export.
**MVP scope (≤6 weeks):** (1) Public booking page with recurring slots, buffers, online-session link. (2) SMS/e-mail reminders (SMS at cost). (3) BLIK/Przelewy24 payment for a session or deposit; invoice or receipt export to an invoicing tool. (4) Client list with consent record and short free-text notes, encrypted at rest. (5) DPA template, EU hosting, data export. (6) Subscription via Stripe. Not in MVP: AI note drafting, NFZ reporting, e-prescriptions, video calls.
**Why now:** No dated trigger found. Since early 2024 the mental health protection act defines "psychoterapia" and "psychoterapeuta" without regulating the profession (interpellation answer, search summary); no deadline affects tooling.
**Foreign analogues:** Calendesk (Poland, founder Maciej Cupial): booking SaaS for therapists, psychologists and service businesses; 20,804 USD MRR, 371 active subscriptions, Stripe-verified on TrustMRR; plans 79–147 USD/month (L4-F09, own pricing page). SimplePractice (USA): per-practitioner plans reported at 49 / 79 / 99 USD per month in a June 2026 comparison (another source lists a 29 USD starter; conflicting), AI note taker add-on 35 USD (search summary of comparison blogs). TherapyNotes about 69 USD solo and Jane App 54–99 USD (same source). Clinician counts and revenue: not found (unverified).
**Competition (PL / global):** Medfile (medical practice software for physiotherapists, psychologists): Free (1 user, 40 visits/month, 10 SMS), Plus 119 PLN monthly or 109 annual, Premium 179 / 164, Business 299 / 274.10, gross incl. 23% VAT, extra users 79–159 PLN; online payments and telemedicine from Premium (own page, L3-F33). Calendesk Standard 97 USD monthly (79 annual). meetergo has pages for therapists and psychiatrists (vendor). WOO PRO (WOA – Wellness Over All Sp. z o.o., first version 23 Feb 2025): AI clinical decision support for psychotherapists, price not found (App Store description via search summary). ZnanyLekarz profiles offer online payment and booking (search results). Gap: a cheap tool focused on therapists' booking-plus-payment-plus-notes in Polish at under 100 PLN; not shown to be unmet.
**Evidence of pain:** None found. A web search for Polish therapist-software discussions returned vendor pages only (searches 2026-10-29); Facebook groups for therapists and forums were not readable. Search trend unreadable (unverified). Lens 6 scored 1.
**Monetization:** 79 PLN net/month solo, 149 PLN up to 5 therapists (estimate; below Medfile Plus 109 gross-per-month annual and Calendesk 79 USD). Payer: the therapist. Monthly, sticky once client records are inside.
**Revenue sketch (estimate):** Assumes 85 PLN average. 6 months: 30 therapists = 2,550 PLN MRR. 12 months: 90 therapists = 7,650 PLN MRR. 10,000 PLN MRR needs about 120 therapists; the number of Polish private psychotherapists is unknown, so the 20× rule from the criteria cannot be checked (cap at 5–15% applies at most; band here is <5%).
**First 10 customers:** Calendesk's own approach is unknown; candidates: psychotherapist Facebook groups (unverified), training institutes' alumni newsletters, supervision groups, ZnanyLekarz-profile outreach by hand, content on "RODO w gabinecie psychoterapeuty".
**Build complexity:** low–medium. Risks: GDPR art. 9 exposure (DPA, breach duties, hosting in the EU), no medical-device or clinical features (stay out of AI diagnostics), SMS costs, handling of session notes liability. These sit close to the founder profile's "no medical licence" line, but an admin tool needs none; stay non-clinical.
**Pros / Cons / Risks:** Pros: Calendesk shows revenue for a similar model; low build complexity; sticky records. Cons: Medfile has a free tier and many features, Calendesk and meetergo exist, no pain evidence, unknown market size, hard-to-reach segment. Risks: health-data liability, price pressure from Medfile Free, a Polish regulation change (unverified).
**Scores (1–5):** L1 2, L2 1, L3 4, L4 4, L5 1, L6 1, L7 3, L8 2, L9 3 | **Weighted:** 2.3 / 5 (computed 2.33) | **Chance band:** <5% (T16 review: L2 2→1, see Review changes; L6 = 1 knock-out; write-up value was 2.5 (computed 2.45), <5%; shortlist 2.5)
**Cheapest validation test (≤1 week, ≤200 PLN):** Interview 10 private-practice therapists (via a LinkedIn or Facebook group post, 100 PLN of ads at most) on how they book and charge, which tool they use and what they pay; test a 79 PLN price. Success: 6 of 10 use a general calendar or messenger and 3 would pay 79 PLN for a booking-plus-payment page.
**Kill criteria:** Most interviewees already use Medfile Free, Calendesk or ZnanyLekarz; or none will pay above 49 PLN; or the DPA/GDPR work looks heavier than the product value.
**Confidence:** low
**Devil's advocate:** A second Polish search turned up more therapist tools, several free. Carepatron has Polish pages for therapy, counselling and mental-health practices: self-booking link, automatic reminders, client portal with forms and invoice payment via Stripe, clinical notes and an AI scribe that formats a session transcript into a note template, with a "get started for free" entry (prices on a separate page, not read). meetergo's guide (written by its co-founder) lists itself from 0 EUR/month with Frankfurt servers, recurring appointments and encrypted video. Anolla has Polish pages for psychologists, therapy and mental-health booking with usage-based pricing. These sit next to Medfile Free (40 visits/month), Calendesk and ZnanyLekarz's booking and online payments, which bring patients as well as software. The write-up's wedge, "booking plus payment plus notes under 100 PLN", is therefore available at 0 PLN from several vendors, some already with the AI-note feature the MVP excluded. No therapist was found complaining about the current tools; the number of private-practice therapists is unknown, so the 20× check cannot be done; and the product carries GDPR art. 9 health-data liability for a solo founder. Calendesk's 20.8k USD MRR shows the model can work, but across all service businesses, not one Polish profession.
**Review changes:** L2 2→1 (free and low-cost tools in Polish for therapists: Carepatron, meetergo, Anolla, Medfile Free, plus ZnanyLekarz; no reason to switch), weighted 2.5 (computed 2.45)→2.3, band stays <5%. Rejected: no gap, no pain (L6 = 1 knock-out), no trigger and health-data liability; logged in rejected.md.
