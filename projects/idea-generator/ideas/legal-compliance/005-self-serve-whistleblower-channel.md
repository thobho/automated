# 005 — Self-serve whistleblower channel for 50–249-employee firms
**One-liner:** A 10-minute-setup internal reporting channel (web form, anonymous two-way messaging, report register, 7-day acknowledgement timer, procedure template) priced at or below Polish incumbents and sold to HR or owners of mid-size employers. Absorbs I026 (white-label for resellers) and I164 (procedure and training pack) as upsells.
**Field:** legal-compliance | **Type:** SaaS
**Target customer:** HR manager, compliance officer or owner of a Polish employer with 50+ people doing paid work. Count: GUS Q4 2025: 2,909,645 active enterprises, of which medium (50–249) 0.6% (about 17,500) and large (250+) 0.1% (about 2,900) (podatki.biz summary of GUS; absolute counts computed from rounded percentages, so approximate). Also public bodies (gminy/powiaty above 10,000 residents per search summary, unverified).
**Problem:** Employers with 50+ people must run an internal reporting channel and procedure under the whistleblower act (Dz.U. 2024 poz. 928); the channel can be outsourced to an external provider. Failing to set up the procedure is a petty offence with a fine up to 5,000 zł (infor.pl), so enforcement pressure is modest and many firms buy the cheapest compliant tool.
**MVP scope (≤6 weeks):** (1) Multi-tenant app: per-employer subdomain, public report form with attachments and anonymous mailbox for follow-up. (2) Admin panel: report register, roles, status, audit log, 7-day acknowledgement and 3-month feedback timers (verify the exact deadlines in the act first). (3) Procedure template (Polish) generated from a questionnaire, plus employee notice text. (4) Stripe annual billing. (5) Encryption at rest, data processing agreement, retention settings. Not in MVP: hotline/voice, multi-language, training videos (upsell later).
**Why now:** The act is in force (the duty applies from 25 Sep 2024 per search summaries; the Dziennik Ustaw text is poz. 928), so demand is a backlog of employers who have not yet complied plus every firm crossing 50 employees (headcount is counted on 1 January and 1 July). It is not a new deadline, so urgency is low (L5 only 3).
**Foreign analogues:** Whistleblower Software (Germany/Europe): Core 99 EUR per month for 0–49 employees up to 349 EUR for 500–999, Advanced 149–529 EUR, annual prepayment, 12-month term (comparison listing); Whistlelink from 79 EUR/month; Vispato flat 79 EUR/month regardless of size; EQS Integrity Line quote-only (all from a search summary of a vendor-affiliated comparison, pages not individually opened). They prove a market with published tiered prices for SMEs; no public revenue figures (unverified).
**Competition (PL / global):** anonimowysygnalista.pl 199 / 269 / 389 / 600 PLN net per month on 12-month contracts (L3-F38); sygnalizuj.net.pl 229 PLN net/month for one organisation incl. form, hotline number, admin panel, 30-day trial (fetched); sygnalista.org.pl probably 150 / 190 / 390 PLN net/month (cents notation unverified, L3-F38); e-nform Silver 361–397 PLN/month plus setup 680 PLN, Gold 879, Platinum from 14,500 PLN/year (two conflicting price blocks, L3-F39); Sygnanet (Wolters Kluwer LEX Hub) quoted 335–835 PLN/month in a search summary, unverified; First Whistle (sponsored interview, no price). Global: the tools above and open-source GlobaLeaks. Gap: none documented. Incumbents already sell self-serve with trials, so "10-minute setup" is not a differentiator.
**Evidence of pain:** No complaint about incumbents found (Reddit/Facebook not readable, review sites not scanned). Cost signal: a PARP-listed whistleblower-protection course for employers costs 499 PLN net, showing employers spend on compliance support (search summary). Compliance rates unknown (infor.pl gives none). Search trend not checked: unverified. L6 stays low.
**Monetization:** Annual contract, 149–249 PLN net/month per employer (estimate, at the low end of the 150–600 PLN band); add-ons: hotline, training, procedure review. Payer: the employer (HR or owner budget). Resellers (accountants, HR consultancies, law firms) are a second payer at a margin.
**Revenue sketch (estimate):** Assumptions: 199 PLN/month average, annual billing. 6 months: 12 customers = about 2,400 PLN MRR. 12 months: 35 customers = about 7,000 PLN MRR. 10,000 PLN MRR needs about 50 customers, which is 0.25% of the roughly 20,000 firms with 50+ staff (sanity check passes: market well over 20 times 50).
**First 10 customers:** Outbound to HR directors of 50–249-employee firms found on LinkedIn and company career pages (listable); resell via 10 accounting offices and law firms (they already advise clients on the act, 20–30% margin); SEO for "kanał zgłoszeń sygnalistów cena"; Polish HR communities.
**Build complexity:** low–medium. Risks: data security and breach liability for confidential reports, correct legal deadlines and wording (needs a lawyer's review, cost estimate 1,000–3,000 PLN), support questions about legal interpretation (must be limited to product, not legal advice).
**Pros / Cons / Risks:** Pros: B2B recurring, published prices to benchmark, low infra cost, 12-month contracts common, reseller channel. Cons: at least five Polish incumbents, enforcement is weak (5,000 zł fine on individuals), price pressure from a 79 EUR flat-rate foreign tool, trust barrier for a new vendor handling sensitive reports. Risks: legal liability, a leak ends the business, EU/ministry rule changes.
**Scores (1–5):** L1 2, L2 2, L3 4, L4 2, L5 3, L6 2, L7 4, L8 3, L9 4 | **Weighted:** 2.9 / 5 | **Chance band:** 5–15% (shortlist 3.1; L2 lowered 3 to 2 because incumbents already offer trials and sub-250 PLN plans; low end of the band)
**Cheapest validation test (≤1 week, ≤200 PLN):** Landing page with a clickable demo and "od 149 PLN/mies., roczna umowa", outreach to 40 HR managers and 10 accounting offices by email/LinkedIn; success: 8 conversations, 3 who ask for a pilot or reseller terms. Spend: domain plus 100 PLN for a lawyer-reviewed one-page procedure checklist as a lead magnet.
**Kill criteria:** Fewer than 3 of 40 contacted HR managers say they have not yet bought a channel; or resellers say their clients already use a cheaper provider; or no one accepts a price above 150 PLN/month.
**Confidence:** low
