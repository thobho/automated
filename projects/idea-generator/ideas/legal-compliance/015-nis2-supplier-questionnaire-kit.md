# 015 — NIS2/KSC supplier-questionnaire kit ("Ankieta bezpieczeństwa dostawcy")
**One-liner:** A hosted kit where a Polish company covered by the KSC/NIS2 act sends a ready, Polish-language security questionnaire to its IT and service suppliers, collects answers and evidence, scores them and keeps an audit trail; suppliers answer once and reuse the answers for every customer.
**Field:** legal-compliance | **Type:** SaaS (merges I027 and I163)
**Target customer:** First: medium companies (about 50–250 staff, manufacturing, food, logistics, digital services) newly covered by KSC2 that must show supplier-risk control; second: their small IT suppliers who receive questionnaires. Count: disputed. The Ministry of Digital Affairs estimate cited in L5-F11 is more than 10,000 entities; a sponsored article (Vendigo) says "ponad 40 000" (poradnikprzedsiebiorcy.pl, promotional, lower reliability). Supplier count unverified.
**Problem:** TTMS, a Polish integrator, says from its 2025–2026 audits that "zdecydowana większość" of the organisations analysed had no systematic risk assessment of service providers with access to their systems, and lists it as one of five common gaps (the firm says its percentages are qualitative, not representative). Supply-chain security is a required measure under NIS2/KSC.
**MVP scope (≤6 weeks):** (1) Questionnaire template library in Polish (about 40 questions mapped to NIS2 art. 21 supply-chain items; legal review needed, not by me); (2) supplier portal (magic link, answer once, upload evidence, versions); (3) scoring and "gaps" report for the customer; (4) reminders, expiry (annual re-attestation), audit log, PDF export for auditors; (5) per-customer subscription with unlimited suppliers. Not in MVP: continuous scanning, ISO 27001 platform, legal advice.
**Why now:** KSC amendment in force from 3 Apr 2026; register application by 3 Oct 2026 (passed); security duties by 3 Apr 2027; first audit for key entities by 3 Apr 2028; fines mostly only after two years, i.e. from about April 2028 (itwiz.pl; L5-F11). Parts of the act were sent to the Constitutional Tribunal (search summary), so scope may shift. Penalties reported by TTMS: up to 100 million PLN, personal fines for managers up to 300% of salary.
**Foreign analogues:** Secfix (Germany): NIS2 and ISO 27001 automation for small firms, "90% faster" claim, customers Workmotion, Tanso, Zapnito, Velaris, 30+ success stories (ISO-focused); no price listed; its separate vendor-management page was not described. Vanta and Drata dominate SOC 2/ISO tooling at about $10,000–$18,000/year for small firms (aggregator estimates, unverified), which leaves a cheap, narrow supplier-questionnaire tool uncontested at the low end.
**Competition (PL / global):** Vendigo Compliance (Polish self-assessment platform, found via a sponsored article; prices and supplier module unverified). Sii Polska offers ServiceNow NIS2 with a third-party-risk module (enterprise, no price). T-Mobile Business sells KSC packages (Ready, Secure+, Pro) and a one-off Cyber Check; no prices on the one-pager. Consultancies (TTMS and many others) sell audits. No Polish self-serve, cheap supplier-questionnaire product was found in searches on 2026-10-29 (weak evidence).
**Evidence of pain:** Practitioner evidence only. TTMS (above). DAGMA/ESET report, cited by itwiz.pl: 36% of cybersecurity experts do not know whether their company is covered by NIS2 (a different problem: scope uncertainty). No buyers or suppliers asking for such a tool on forums were read. Search-volume: Polish NIS2 queries have almost no long tail (lens 8 finding, per shortlist reason); trend data unreadable.
**Monetization:** 199–499 PLN/month per covered company (unlimited suppliers), supplier side free (estimates). Anchor: Polish whistleblower channels sell at 199–880 PLN/month on 12-month contracts (L3-F38/39), showing the compliance-tool price band; Vanta/Drata are far above that. Payer: the covered company (IT/security or compliance manager).
**Revenue sketch (estimate):** Assumes 299 PLN average. 6 months: 8 customers = 2,400 PLN MRR. 12 months: 25 customers = 7,500 PLN MRR. 10,000 PLN MRR needs about 33 customers. A covered-company count above 10,000 makes this feasible in size; sales cycles to compliance managers are slow and each lead is individual.
**First 10 customers:** LinkedIn outbound to compliance/IT managers of 50–250-employee manufacturers and logistics firms (listable from KRS/GUS data); Polish NIS2 webinars and groups; partnerships with IT-service companies that need to answer questionnaires from customers and could push the kit upstream.
**Build complexity:** medium. Content correctness (needs a lawyer/CISO reviewer), supplier auth and evidence storage, GDPR/NDA handling, and keeping up with Constitutional Tribunal changes.
**Pros / Cons / Risks:** Pros: dated trigger, narrow wedge, B2B recurring, both sides of the market in one product. Cons: legal-content burden, long sales cycles, fines delayed to 2028 reduce urgency, large players and consultancies already sell NIS2 packages. Risks: Constitutional Tribunal referral may change scope; customers buy a consultancy report instead of a tool; two-sided cold start.
**Scores (1–5):** L1 2, L2 2, L3 3, L4 2, L5 3, L6 3, L7 4, L8 2, L9 2 | **Weighted:** 2.6 / 5 (computed 2.62) | **Chance band:** <5% (T16 review: L1 3→2, L2 3→2, L9 3→2, see Review changes; write-up value was 2.9 (computed 2.92), 5–15%)
**Cheapest validation test (≤1 week, ≤200 PLN):** Write the 40-question Polish questionnaire as a Google Form/PDF and a one-page landing page; contact 40 compliance or IT managers at covered-sector companies on LinkedIn offering a free pilot with 5 of their suppliers. Success: 5 replies, 2 pilots, 1 willing to pre-pay 299 PLN/month.
**Kill criteria:** Fewer than 3 of 40 contacts reply with interest; or most say suppliers answer in free-form PDFs from their consultants and they will not change that before 2028.
**Confidence:** low
**Devil's advocate:** The write-up's central feature, "suppliers answer once and reuse the answers for every customer", already exists for free, in the EU, with a Polish-language front page. NISD2.eu runs a supplier portal where the supplier answers up to 53 questions once and "release[s] them to every customer who invites you"; customers get a private link without an account; certificates (ISO 27001, SOC 2) are uploaded once; the portal "costs nothing and the code is open" (hosted in Germany). Its customer platform has a Polish page (nisd2.eu/pl) listing a supplier register with per-supplier status and a security questionnaire under art. 21(2)(d), PDF/CSV export, reminders and an activity log, with a guided implementation at €4,800 net/year and free self-hosting under AGPL-3.0. Its questionnaire is published as an open standard (version 3.1.0, 15 May 2026, 59 fields, MIT schema + CC BY 4.0 content, per search summary), so the "Polish template library" is a translation job anyone can do. Orbiq sells security-questionnaire software from €299/month and gives away a 67-question EU supplier questionnaire in Excel. So the cheap end is not uncontested, and a free open-source option sets the floor. Demand timing is also soft: security duties apply from 3 Apr 2027 but fines mostly from about April 2028, parts of the act were sent to the Constitutional Tribunal, and the only pain evidence is one integrator's qualitative audit note. Buyers are compliance managers with slow, individual sales cycles (L8 = 2), and many will receive a consultancy's questionnaire with their audit package instead. The one remaining edge is KSC-specific Polish wording and a Polish-speaking vendor, which a consultancy can bolt onto the free open questionnaire.
**Review changes:** L1 3→2 (the foreign analogue model now has a Polish-language page and a free supplier side, so "Polish alternatives weak" no longer holds cleanly; whether NISD2.eu's questionnaire itself is in Polish is unverified), L2 3→2 (a free, open-source, answer-once supplier portal plus a Polish-page platform at €4,800/year and Orbiq from €299/month cover the documented gap), L9 3→2 (AGPL self-hostable rival and openly licensed question set leave no content moat; supplier side must be free), weighted 2.9 (computed 2.92)→2.6, band 5–15%→<5%. Not rejected: covered-company count (over 10,000) and a dated 2027 duty remain, and a Polish KSC-mapped, Polish-supported version is still testable with the existing LinkedIn validation plan, but it must now beat a free option.
