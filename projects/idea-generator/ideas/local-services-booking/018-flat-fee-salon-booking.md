# 018 — Flat-fee salon booking with no first-visit commission
**One-liner:** A booking calendar for one-person and small hair and beauty salons with a single flat monthly price and no commission on any client, aimed at salons that left or avoid Booksy because of the Boost fee. Research shows the "no commission" gap is already filled by several cheap Polish tools, so the real wedge would have to be something narrower (see Verdict).
**Field:** local-services-booking | **Type:** SaaS
**Target customer:** Owner-operators of hairdressing and beauty salons in Poland. Dun & Bradstreet estimates over 132.8 thousand salons at end of 2025, about 14.7 thousand created in 2024 (via a rp.pl / trade-press search summary, commercial estimate). CEIDG shows about 70.5 thousand active entries with PKD 96.02.Z in early October 2026 (same summary); Scrap.io sees 47,279 salons on Google Maps (10 Aug 2026), most of them one-person businesses. Which share uses a booking system: unknown (unverified).
**Problem:** Booksy Biz charges 145 PLN net/month plus 35 PLN per extra user and a Boost commission of 45% net of the first visit of a new marketplace client (cap 250 PLN net); the commission is not charged on the salon's own profile link or on clients already in its contacts. Reviewers say the system also classifies referred or known clients as "Boost" (see Evidence).
**MVP scope (≤6 weeks):** (1) Public booking page and widget per salon (services, staff, opening hours, buffers). (2) Staff and owner calendar as a responsive web app (no native app). (3) Email and SMS reminders through an SMS gateway (SMS billed at cost). (4) Client list with CSV import from Booksy/Versum exports to cut switching time. (5) Optional deposit payment via Przelewy24/BLIK (later). (6) Stripe or Przelewy24 subscription. Not in MVP: marketplace, Instagram booking button, loyalty, inventory, payroll.
**Why now:** No dated trigger found. Booksy announced a 20% workforce cut in September 2026 (Wikipedia, secondary) and the Versum product was shut down on 31 Jan 2022 with users sent to Booksy Biz (search summary of a comparison page), so a pool of unhappy migrated users exists, but nothing forces a switch.
**Foreign analogues:** Fresha (UK) gives calendar software away or sells it cheaply and earns on payments and new-client fees; Calendesk (Poland, founder Maciej Cupial) shows 20,804 USD MRR and 371 subscriptions, Stripe-verified on TrustMRR, with plans from 79 USD/month annual; it sells to therapists and service businesses rather than only salons. Booksy itself is the proof of the category (about 350,000 business clients, about 70% of revenue from subscriptions, 25% from marketplace commissions, per Wikipedia citing the company).
**Competition (PL / global):** Booksy 145 PLN net + 35 per extra user, 7-day trial (own page). Fresha Poland: Independent 29 PLN/month, Team 19 PLN per bookable member, payments 1.29% + 0.49 PLN, BLIK 1.59% + 1 PLN, one-time fee on new marketplace clients (amount not stated on the page). Calendesk: Standard 97 USD monthly / 79 annual (5 employees, 750 bookings, 50 SMS), Pro 147 / 117; no commission mentioned. A competitor's comparison page (meetergo, a vendor, self-interested) lists Estetify 99 PLN gross unlimited staff, BeSkiz 35 PLN (Solo) and 79 PLN (Team) and states none of them take commission. Gap: not "no commission" (several tools already have it) but, at most, salon-specific onboarding in Polish and migration from Booksy; unproven.
**Evidence of pain:** Booksy Biz App Store listing, Polish store: 3.97/5 from 3,892 ratings. Of the 300 most recent Polish reviews (17 Jan 2024 to 27 Oct 2026), 130 are 1–2 stars and 44 mention commission or Boost (L6-F08, regex count). Quotes: "Klientów z polecenia i znajomych kwalifikuje jako boost przez co ściąga prowizję" (1★, 2026-10-12); "po wyłączeniu przez miesiąc ściąga 50% z usługi" (1★, 2026-08-18); "Gdyby istniała inna aplikacja do rezerwacji tak popularna jak Booksy, to chętnie bym zmieniła" (1★, 2025-02-23). Counter-evidence: 127 of the 300 reviews are 5★, and the same quote shows that the missing piece is the Booksy marketplace itself. Facebook groups of salon owners could not be read; Google Trends unreadable (unverified). Independent web search for forum complaints returned none, only vendor and affiliate pages.
**Monetization:** Flat subscription per salon: 49 PLN net/month solo, 99 PLN net/month up to 5 staff (estimate), SMS at cost plus margin. Payer: salon owner. Positioned between BeSkiz at 35–79 PLN and Booksy at 145+ PLN. Recurring monthly.
**Revenue sketch (estimate):** Assume 65 PLN average. 6 months: 40 salons = 2,600 PLN MRR. 12 months: 120 salons = 7,800 PLN MRR. 10,000 PLN MRR needs about 155 salons at 65 PLN, roughly 0.1–0.3% of the 47–133 thousand salons (market size is not the constraint, acquisition is).
**First 10 customers:** Reply in Booksy-hating threads and salon-owner Facebook groups (not readable by this research, so unverified); outbound to salons found on Google Maps whose Booksy profile shows many reviews (needs a business-contact basis under PKE art. 398 consent rule, L8-F13 to F15); offer free migration of the Booksy contact list for the first 20 salons.
**Build complexity:** low–medium. Risks: SMS deliverability and cost (0.14–0.17 PLN/SMS, L9 research), double-booking edge cases and time zones, Google/Instagram booking integrations need partner status, and migration since Booksy has no self-serve client export (competitor-reported, L9-F14).
**Pros / Cons / Risks:** Pros: huge listable market, simple product, clear pain on one fee, Polish proof that vertical booking pays (Calendesk). Cons: commodity feature set; at least five cheap Polish alternatives already advertise no commission; salons value marketplace discovery; Booksy tolerates its own-link clients without commission. Risks: price war with Fresha's 29 PLN anchor, churn of one-person salons (higher than 5%/month is plausible, estimate), SMS margin.
**Scores (1–5):** L1 1, L2 2, L3 4, L4 4, L5 1, L6 4, L7 3, L8 2, L9 2 | **Weighted:** 2.7 / 5 | **Chance band:** <5% (shortlist 2.8; L1 stays 1 and L2 falls 3 to 2 because Estetify, BeSkiz, Calendesk, Fresha and meetergo already sell "no commission"; L3 stays 4 as price anchors exist on 3 pages; L7 stays 3)
**Verdict note:** Score is below 2.8 (`<5%`). Keep only if validation shows salons choose the tool for a reason other than price, such as a Booksy-migration service or a narrower vertical (barbers, lash/brow, physio); otherwise reject in T16.
**Cheapest validation test (≤1 week, ≤200 PLN):** One-page Polish landing page "Zmień Booksy na stały abonament, migrujemy kontakty za darmo" with price 49/99 PLN; 150 PLN of Facebook/Instagram ads targeted at salon owners in two cities; count email sign-ups. Success: 15 sign-ups at under 10 PLN each and 3 who agree to a 15-minute call.
**Kill criteria:** Fewer than 10 sign-ups; or calls show the objection is losing Boost clients, not the fee; or interviewees already use Estetify/BeSkiz/Fresha and see no reason to move.
**Confidence:** low–medium
