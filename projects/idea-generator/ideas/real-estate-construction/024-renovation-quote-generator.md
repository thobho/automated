# 024 — Renovation quote (oferta) generator with a Polish unit catalogue
**One-liner:** A phone-first tool where a small renovation crew dictates or types a job description, picks from a catalogue of Polish renovation line items (m² of painting, tiling, plastering, with default unit prices the owner edits), and sends a client a quote link they can approve; merged with longlist idea I078. Research found direct low-priced Polish rivals and weak Polish demand signals.
**Field:** real-estate-construction | **Type:** SaaS (mobile web)
**Target customer:** One- to five-person renovation and finishing crews (tilers, painters, plasterers, general "remonty"). Count: not found; a search for PKD 43.3x counts returned no figure (unverified). The registers can be queried by PKD (dane.biznes.gov.pl, L8-F10).
**Problem:** Small crews quote verbally or in a notepad or WhatsApp, and disputes over extras follow. Formal estimating tools (WINBUD Kosztorys, Norma) are desktop programs based on norm catalogues (KNR, Sekocenbud) aimed at tenders and official estimates, not at a two-page quote for a bathroom.
**MVP scope (≤6 weeks):** (1) Line-item catalogue (own prices, per m², per piece, per hour) with Polish starter list. (2) Voice or text to draft quote via an LLM that picks items from the catalogue; owner edits. (3) PDF and a client link with e-approval and addendum ("aneks") flow. (4) Net/VAT/gross with 8% and 23% VAT presets (check VAT rules with an accountant, unverified). (5) Subscription via Stripe or BLIK. Not in MVP: KNR norms, drawings, scheduling, invoicing.
**Why now:** Voice input is now cheap: gpt-4o-mini-transcribe is listed at USD 0.003 per minute and speech models have a mini tier (L5-F25, OpenAI changelog and pricing). Jobber (below) added AI Voice for creating quotes in the field, which shows the pattern is practical. No law forces adoption.
**Foreign analogues:** Jobber (Canada): quote templates with signature approval, follow-ups, a "Draft for me" button that auto-drafts quotes, and Jobber AI Voice for creating quotes by voice; plans from 29 USD to 529 USD per month (own pricing page; rating 4.9 from 153 reviews, self-reported, L1-F01, L3-F17). No public revenue figures. Housecall Pro 59–299 USD/month (L3-F18).
**Competition (PL / global):** PodpiszWycenę.pl: quote on phone, client signs with a finger in the browser, PDF back, addendum quotes; free plan with 2 quotes a month, Pro 29 PLN gross for 30 days (does not auto-renew); no customer numbers (own page). Kalkulator Kosztorysu (Android, Buildora): own catalogue, sections, PDF export, sync; 13 downloads in total on the aggregator page, rated 5 from few ratings (instalki.pl). Remonix (iPad/iOS): job, cost and worker management with automatic import of cost invoices from KSeF, free, too few ratings to display (App Store). cenauslug.pl: price-range estimator with national average rates (search summary). WINBUD Kosztorys: desktop program with 350+ norm catalogues, Windows XP or newer, price not published (vendor page). Gap: a ready Polish unit catalogue with default prices and voice drafting is not offered by the tools found; low-priced competitors already cover signing and PDF.
**Evidence of pain:** A Wykop post (26 Aug 2026) by a developer who built a renovation-quote app asked whether tradespeople prefer notebook and WhatsApp: 0 upvotes, 8 downvotes, 5 comments; replies were dismissive ("ktoś jeszcze robi na gębę?", "Katalog Nakładów Rzeczowych, mówi to panu coś"); the author replied that KNR is for large tenders and "nikt nie ma czasu odpalać norm i szablonów" (Wykop, 2026-10-29 read). An older Wykop ask for free costing software got "Excel, ew. LibreOffice… Norma czy Zuzia… relikt przeszłości" (L6-F12, search summary). Three or four comments of this kind are low evidence; the audience on Wykop is not the tradespeople. Search trend unreadable (unverified).
**Monetization:** 39 PLN net/month for unlimited quotes, 79 PLN for a team of five (estimate; PodpiszWycenę is 29 PLN gross per 30 days, Jobber 29+ USD). Payer: the contractor. Usage is lumpy: crews need quotes when they get leads (risk of churn between jobs).
**Revenue sketch (estimate):** Assumes 40 PLN average. 6 months: 40 crews = 1,600 PLN MRR. 12 months: 120 crews = 4,800 PLN MRR. 10,000 PLN MRR needs 250 crews, achievable only with strong channel fit (not found).
**First 10 customers:** Facebook groups and forums for renovation crews (not readable by this research, unverified; elektroda.pl threads on "program do oferty cenowej" exist, per L6-F12), YouTube/TikTok content for tradespeople, supplier counters (building-materials stores). No registry-based outreach without consent (L8-F13 to F15).
**Build complexity:** low–medium. Risks: LLM line-item matching errors (a wrong price is the customer's liability, so owner review is required); catalogue defaults from own market research (no licensed price database such as Sekocenbud, which is out of scope).
**Pros / Cons / Risks:** Pros: simple to build and test, voice makes entry quick, clear niche. Cons: cheap competitors exist, willingness to pay is low (29 PLN/30 days anchor), poor distribution, lumpy usage. Risks: free tier pressure, price-data accuracy, churn.
**Scores (1–5):** L1 2, L2 2, L3 2, L4 2, L5 3, L6 2, L7 4, L8 2, L9 2 | **Weighted:** 2.3 / 5 (computed 2.32) | **Chance band:** <5% (T16 review: L2 3→2 and L9 3→2, see Review changes; write-up value was 2.5 (computed 2.54), <5%; shortlist 2.5)
**Cheapest validation test (≤1 week, ≤200 PLN):** Record a 60-second demo of "say the job, get a quote with prices", post it in 3 Facebook groups for renovation crews and a TikTok/Reels ad for 150 PLN aimed at tradespeople; collect phone numbers for a free pilot. Success: 20 sign-ups at under 8 PLN each, and 5 crews who send a real quote with the tool.
**Kill criteria:** Fewer than 10 sign-ups; or crews say Excel/WhatsApp is enough; or a pilot crew will not pay 39 PLN.
**Confidence:** low
**Devil's advocate:** The only open piece the write-up found, "a ready Polish unit catalogue with default prices and voice drafting", is the part least worth paying for. Default prices are what cenauslug.pl already publishes for free, and every crew overrides them with its own; voice-to-draft is what a free ChatGPT voice chat does with a short prompt, and AI quote apps for tradespeople already sell for USD 5.99/month (EverQuote: AI estimates from a job description, pricing templates, export; English and five other languages, not Polish). The rest of the MVP (catalogue, PDF, client signature, addendum) is sold in Polish by PodpiszWycenę.pl with a free plan and 29 PLN gross per 30 days that does not auto-renew, and by Kalkulator Kosztorysu (own catalogue, sections per room, net/VAT/gross, PDF). That 29 PLN non-renewing price is the market telling us usage is per job, not per month. The demand evidence is a Wykop post by a developer of the same kind of app that got 8 downvotes and replies asking who still quotes verbally or pointing to KNR. Crews get many leads through Oferteo and Fixly and answer there. At 40 PLN, 10,000 PLN MRR needs 250 paying crews that churn between jobs, reached through Facebook groups this research could not read.
**Review changes:** L2 3→2 (Polish tools with free tiers cover the quote, PDF and signature job; AI quote apps exist abroad at about USD 6/month; the voice gap is covered by free general chatbots), L9 3→2 (per-job usage and a non-renewing 29 PLN anchor imply high churn; no data moat in a price catalogue each crew rewrites), weighted 2.5 (computed 2.54)→2.3, band stays <5%. Rejected: no defensible gap and no channel; logged in rejected.md.
