# 018 — Done-for-you online accounting office for foreign sole traders

**One-liner:** A fixed-price online accounting office for foreigners running a JDG in Poland (Ukrainian, Belarusian, Indian and other IT contractors on B2B, drivers, builders), served in Ukrainian, Russian and English. AI does the work: onboarding interview and CEIDG registration help, choice of tax form and ZUS relief explained in the client's language, KSeF purchase-invoice booking, KPiR/ewidencja, JPK_V7 and ZUS DRA drafts, monthly "what you owe" message. A partner biuro rachunkowe with OC insurance reviews and signs; the founder runs the software and the client channel.
**Field:** accounting-tax | **Type:** service (tech-enabled) | **B2B** (sole traders) · shortlist id L001 (merges L002 onboarding for IT body-leasing firms, L004 drivers and couriers)

**Western analogues:**
- **Osome** (SG, UK, HK, UAE): AI-assisted incorporation, bookkeeping and filing with human accountants; **FY2025 revenue S$26.6M, CY2025 S$29M, EBITDA loss cut 50%** (company results, 2026-05-05) ([Osome](https://osome.com/sg/blog/osome-fy2025-results-ebitda-loss-cut-ai/), accessed 2026-11-01 in R7 catalogue).
- **Hnry** (NZ, AU since 2020, UK since early 2025): accountants + automation for sole traders, pricing a 1% fee on income, capped yearly (excerpt); **Series C of NZ$30M+ led by Movac, 2025-12**, almost NZ$100M raised since 2018 (company), revenue "roughly tripled" in a year (SmartCompany excerpt) ([Hnry news](https://hnry.co.nz/resources/news/hnry-raises-30-mil-to-accelerate-growth/); [RNZ 2025-12-10](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/top/581391/tech-firm-hnry-raises-more-than-30m-in-latest-funding-round), accessed 2026-11-06).
- **Accountable** (BE/DE): AI accountant for the self-employed, 50,000+ Belgian users (company, 2024-03), #1 top-grossing BE App Store Finance on 2026-11-01 ([Accountable press](https://www.accountable.eu/fr-be/press/accountable-lance-le-premier-comptable-ia/), via R3 catalogue).
- **Declaro** (RO): software + human accountants, ~200 clients five months after a 2026-05 launch, from 49 EUR/month ([Revista Biz 2026-10-05](https://www.revistabiz.ro/declaro-start-up-romanesc-de-contabilitate-digitala-investeste-1-milion-de-euro-in-dezvoltarea-rehnologica/), via R6 catalogue).

**Spread:** TH16 is the #1 theme: SG, HK, UK, NZ, AU, BE, DE, JP (AIBOS), RO, HU, HR (R1–R8 and R6), mostly 2023–2026 AI rebuilds of older services. The **foreigner-language** angle specifically was not found as a product anywhere; it is this idea's own wedge.

**Does the Western leader already work in Poland?** Osome, Hnry and Accountable do not serve Poland (country selectors, TH16 check). **Norman** (DE) does: Polish site in **Polish and English** (no Ukrainian), KSeF import, KPiR, JPK_V7 drafts, PLN prices **JDG Pro 29.25–39 PLN net/month**, states "software, not tax advice", no human accountant included, "8,000+ entrepreneurs" across markets ([norman.finance/pl](https://norman.finance/pl), fetched 2026-11-06). So self-service in English is taken cheaply; done-for-you in Ukrainian with a signing accountant is not offered by the Western players. Not tried hands-on (no signup).

**Polish adaptation, i.e. the edge:**
- Tax-form choice (skala / liniowy / ryczałt with PKD-dependent rates), ZUS reliefs (ulga na start, preferencyjny ZUS, mały ZUS plus), and ZUS DRA without an API ([local-moats](../../research/poland/local-moats.md) M07, M10).
- KSeF mandatory for VAT payers in 2026, JPK_V7 monthly (M01, M02).
- **Who may keep books for others:** since the 2014 deregulation no accountant certificate is needed for usługowe prowadzenie ksiąg, but mandatory OC insurance is (art. 76a/76h ustawy o rachunkowości per [Rzeczpospolita](https://pro.rp.pl/rachunkowosc/art11940011-biuro-rachunkowe-mozna-zalozyc-bez-uprawnien) and [INFOR](https://www.infor.pl/biura-rachunkowe/zakladam-biuro/740384,Obowiazkowe-ubezpieczenie-OC-biura-rachunkowego.html), search excerpts, accessed 2026-11-06; current wording of the act not read). So the founder could legally run the office, but the work and liability argue for a partner office.
- Clients' own situation: foreign residence status tied to the business (karta pobytu for JDG), Ukrainian FOP vs. Polish JDG questions, invoices to foreign clients (reverse charge). A foreign entrant would need Polish accountants anyway; translation is not the problem.

**Target customer in Poland:** foreign sole traders. **Ukrainians started ~31.4 thousand JDG in 2025, ~12% of all new JDG** (PIE, Tygodnik PIE 8/2026, search excerpt; another source gives 33.8k for 2025 and 31.3k for 2024); about one in seven new JDG belongs to a non-Polish citizen, mostly Ukrainian, Belarusian, German ([wartowiedziec.pl](https://wartowiedziec.pl/serwis-glowny/styl-zycia/79421-niemal-co-siodma-mala-firma-w-polsce-jest-juz-w-rekach-obcokrajowcow), excerpt, accessed 2026-11-06). The stock of active foreign-owned JDG was not found (unverified). Buyer: the contractor himself; channel partners: IT body-leasing firms that move staff to B2B (L002). CEE: the same model works in CZ (Ukrainian živnostníci) but needs a Czech partner office.

**Demand driver:** DD6 migrants (1.29M foreigners in ZUS, demography.md) plus the shift of Ukrainian businesses to Poland since 2022; KSeF 2026 adds complexity for self-filers; DD7 shortage of accountants (TH16 check).

**Evidence of Polish demand:** indirect. Autocomplete "biuro rachunkowe online" → "cennik", "ranking" (TH16); Ukrainian-language FOP vs. JDG guides exist from agencies (e.g. woborders.agency, search excerpt); one search for a Ukrainian-language online office found catalogue entries and Ukrainian-run offices, not a clear online brand (searched 2026-11-06). No forum or survey on language barriers with accountants found.

**MVP scope (≤6 weeks):**
1. Onboarding in UA/RU/EN: CEIDG wizard pre-fill, tax-form and ZUS-relief recommendation with a rules engine and a worked yearly comparison; the partner accountant confirms.
2. Monthly loop: KSeF purchase/sales invoices pulled (KSeF API), bank statement upload, AI categorisation into KPiR/ewidencja ryczałtu, VAT reverse-charge cases flagged.
3. Drafts for JPK_V7, PIT-5L/zaliczki, ZUS DRA amounts; the partner office files from its own software (Optima/Rewizor export).
4. Client message in the client's language each month: "pay X to US, Y to ZUS by the 20th", with mikrorachunek podatkowy.
5. Letter explainer for US/ZUS letters (photo → explanation + escalation to the partner).
6. Tests: golden month-end cases (ryczałt 12% IT, liniowy with VAT, reverse charge) with expected tax and ZUS amounts.

**Monetization:** 299 PLN net/month (ryczałt, ≤20 documents), 399 PLN with VAT; onboarding 199 PLN one-off; IT body-leasing firms pay onboarding per head. The partner office gets ~150 PLN per client per month (estimate, to negotiate). **Unit economics:** ~20 documents + a bank statement + 20 chat messages ≈ 80k input / 15k output tokens per client-month on Gemini 2.5 Flash (US$0.30 / US$2.50 per M, [OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/google/gemini-2.5-flash), accessed 2026-11-05) ≈ US$0.06 (~0.25 PLN); hosting ~5 PLN. Gross margin after the partner share ≈ 45–50%; tokens are irrelevant, the partner's and founder's hours decide it. **Customer count:** 10k PLN MRR ≈ 34 clients at 299 PLN; ~31k new Ukrainian JDG a year alone is far above 20×.

**Distribution, first 10 customers:** 2–3 IT body-leasing firms that hire Ukrainian/Belarusian developers on B2B (offer free onboarding for their next cohort); Ukrainian IT communities on Telegram and Facebook in Warsaw, Kraków, Wrocław; Ukrainian-language SEO on "ФОП чи JDG", "бухгалтер Польща онлайн".

**Regulation:** AI Act: bookkeeping support is not in Annex III ([Annex III](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/annex/3/)); limited-risk transparency only. Usługowe prowadzenie ksiąg: OC insurance mandatory for whoever provides the service (partner office); doradztwo podatkowe is a regulated activity, so the tax-form choice is framed as the partner's advice, not the software's (TH16 A26). RODO and AML: an accounting office is an obliged entity under the AML act (see 019); client KYC needed.

Durability score ~6 → **promising**.

- Polish adaptation can reproduce: replaced spend = the 150–450 PLN/month the contractor already pays a biuro; first channel = IT body-leasing firms and Ukrainian IT communities; retention driver = books, KSeF history and the partner relationship (switching an office is a hassle).

**Devil's advocate (T24, 2026-11-06):**
- *It already works in Ukrainian.* The wedge "no online office marketed in Ukrainian" does not hold. WoBorders, a Ukrainian business-services agency, sells bookkeeping for JDG and sp. z o.o. in Poland with Ukrainian-speaking accountants, documents sent electronically, chat over Telegram, Viber or WhatsApp, a team of 20+ Polish accountants and 1M PLN liability insurance, at **300 PLN gross/month with no invoices, 500 PLN up to 20, 800 PLN up to 40** ([WoBorders](https://woborders.agency/biznes-servisy/reyestracziya-biznesu-v-yevropi/buhgalterskyj-suprovid-v-polshhi/), fetched 2026-11-06; no AI mentioned). That is the proposed product without the AI, at a price the 299/399 PLN net plan does not undercut much. Ukrainian-run offices (UKDN, cited above) serve the same community by word of mouth.
- *A cheaper substitute removes the JDG altogether.* Bizky (Warsaw) invoices on the contractor's behalf "without ZUS, bookkeeping or opening a JDG", with a Ukrainian-language guide and RU/EN/PL site ([bizky.ai](https://bizky.ai/?p=9366), fetched 2026-11-06; commission not stated). For low-volume freelancers this competes with the whole idea; Useme does the same in Polish (not re-checked).
- *Polish customers won't pay this, at least not to a newcomer.* An IT contractor on ryczałt 12% with one foreign client already gets Norman in English at 29–39 PLN or inFakt with an accountant from ~60 PLN; the ones who need a Ukrainian-speaking human already have WoBorders-type agencies or a known accountant.
- *ChatGPT will absorb it.* The explanation layer (tax form choice, "what you owe" in Ukrainian) is exactly what a general assistant does well; the paid part is filing and liability, which belongs to the partner office, not the software.
- *The model collides with the founder profile.* The write-up itself scores L7 2: a service with a partner's margin share and multilingual support. Nothing found in this pass reduces that load; Osome, the best-documented analogue, is still loss-making (cited above).
- *Second competitor search (2026-11-06):* Ukrainian ("бухгалтерія онлайн Польща JDG українською") and Polish ("księgowość online dla obcokrajowców JDG po ukraińsku angielsku"): found WoBorders and Bizky (above); Polish price guides put online offices at 50–300 PLN/month and traditional ones at 200–900 PLN (freenance.io, kb.pl, search excerpts; secondary).
- *Claims re-checked.* Ukrainian JDG starts (~31k in 2025): unchanged, still a search excerpt of PIE. Norman PL pricing: not re-fetched.
- *Success signals.* Osome (revenue S$29M) and Hnry (Series C, revenue ~3×) are durable, not hype; L2 4 holds for the theme. The gap is in Poland, not abroad.
- *Opportunity/risk lists.* O1 rests on "no online office targets in Ukrainian", now false → M×M. R2 H×M confirmed by WoBorders. Lists otherwise independent.
