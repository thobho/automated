# Phase 2 longlist of adaptation ideas (T19)

One line per idea: a Western (or global) pattern × a Polish moat × a demand driver, for a specific Polish customer with a specific wedge. Ideas are **proposals**, not findings: the evidence behind each one (Polish players, Western leader's status, autocomplete demand, prices) is in the linked Poland-check file. Written 2026-11-05.

**Inputs:** seed ideas and residues from [poland-check/A](../poland-check/A/README.md), [B](../poland-check/B/README.md), [C](../poland-check/C/README.md) (open and partial verdicts first, taken themes only for named residues); demand drivers DD1–DD12 in [demography.md](../research/poland/demography.md#demand-drivers); moat IDs M01–M53 in [local-moats.md](../research/poland/local-moats.md); Western analogues from [THEMES.md](../themes/THEMES.md) (catalogue rows).

**Columns**
- **Type:** B2B = the payer is a business, office, practice, NGO or school; B2C = the payer is an individual (including a teacher paying from their own pocket).
- **What exactly is local:** the Polish law, system, data, industry structure or buying habit the product is built on. M-codes point to [local-moats.md](../research/poland/local-moats.md). A **†** marks a Polish rule or fact that comes from general knowledge and is **not yet sourced** in a Phase 2 file: verify before the idea is scored in T20.
- **Driver:** DD-codes from [demography.md](../research/poland/demography.md#demand-drivers), or a dated regulatory trigger named in the check file; "—" when none.
- **Check:** the Poland-check file holding the evidence (verdict in brackets: open / partial / taken).
- **Flags** in the idea name: *(seed)* = the check file's own seed idea; *(weak)* = the check file rates it weak or it overlaps a taken incumbent; *(licence?)* / *(AI Act?)* / *(MDR?)* = a regulatory line the founder profile must stay behind, to verify first.
- Founder profile filter applied: no idea needs the founder to hold a financial, medical or legal licence, hire staff or hold inventory. Where a licensed person must sign (accountant, sworn translator, engineer, notary), the product sells to that person or works through a partner.
