# Phase 2 countries: 51 markets × coverage

Coverage tracker for the regional scans (T05–T14). Region tasks update **status** (`todo` / `doing` / `done` / `thin`) and **products found** (rows in `catalogue/RN-<name>.csv` for that country) as each country is finished. The brief asks for ≥5 products per country or a reason why not; write the reason in the region's `README.md` and mark the country `thin`.

Media and chart links were checked on 2026-10-30 (home pages load and are the named outlet). "(403 to bots)" means the site exists but blocks the fetch tool; use a browser or search results for it. Local-language terms are search starting points (machine translation by the T01 author), not verified phrases; refine them per country and record what worked in the country file.

## How to use the columns

- **Search terms:** always also search in English and Polish. Combine the local terms with a vertical (e.g. German "KI-Agent für Steuerberater", Czech "AI asistent pro účetní") and with "2025"/"2026". Use site search on the local media (`site:<domain> <term>`).
- **App-store charts:** two free, no-login sources.
  - Apple's public RSS feed (JSON), pattern `https://itunes.apple.com/<cc>/rss/<feed>/limit=100/genre=<id>/json`, where `<feed>` is `topgrossingapplications`, `topfreeapplications` or `toppaidapplications`. Genre ids: 6000 Business, 6007 Productivity, 6017 Education, 6013 Health & Fitness, 6020 Medical, 6015 Finance, 6012 Lifestyle (checked 2026-10-30; all 51 country codes returned entries). Top-grossing is the better traction signal: it ranks by revenue, not downloads.
  - Similarweb's public top-apps pages for Google Play, pattern `https://www.similarweb.com/top-apps/google/<country>/<category>/`. No page was found for Luxembourg, Iceland, Estonia, Malta, Cyprus or South Korea (404 for the slugs tried); use the App Store feed there.
  - Sensor Tower and Appfigures public charts: see [sources.md](sources.md) (Appfigures blocks the fetch tool).
- **Local startup media:** see also the regional and global sources in [sources.md](sources.md); regional outlets (The Recursive for CEE, Wamda/MAGNiTT for the Gulf, Tech in Asia/e27 for Southeast Asia, Contxto/LatamList for Latin America) cover small countries better than local general news.
