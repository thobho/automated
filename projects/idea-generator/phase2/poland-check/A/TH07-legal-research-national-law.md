## 2. Does the Western leader already serve Poland?
- **Lexroom** (IT, theme traction leader): available in Italy, Spain and Germany; IT/EN/DE/ES sites; no Poland ([lexroom.ai](https://www.lexroom.ai/)). **Not in PL.**
- **Harvey**: firmwide deployment at WKB Lawyers (160+ Polish lawyers), June 2026 ([Harvey blog](https://www.harvey.ai/blog/wkb-lawyers-deploys-harvey-firmwide)) and a partnership with CEE firm Schoenherr. **Serves top Polish firms**, but as a general workspace, not grounded in Polish sources.
- **Wolters Kluwer and C.H.Beck** (the two Western publishers that own LEX and Legalis) both ship AI over Polish content: the "Western leader" is already the Polish incumbent.
- Could not check: answer quality on Polish questions (no trial accounts created).

## 3. Polish demand signals

## 4. What adaptation needs
M29 SAOS, M30 Portal Orzeczeń, M31 ISAP/ELI API (all open), M52 Polish legal language; plus commentary content, which is what LEX and Legalis own and a newcomer lacks. Niche public sources not bundled by publishers: KIO rulings (in SAOS), UOKiK and UODO decisions, KIS interpretations (M05 EUREKA), CBOSA administrative-court rulings.

## 7. Verdict: **taken**
General research over Polish law is held by the two publishers that own the commentary (LEX Expert AI, Beck-Noxtua/Legalis), Harvey already sells to large Polish firms, and several local startups compete. Only narrow, open-data niches remain, which belong more to TH54 (compliance) or TH20 (tax research) than to this theme.

**Seed idea (niche, weak):** monitoring + Q&A over **KIO public-procurement rulings** for bidders and their lawyers (SAOS has KIO data), sold per tender team; check overlap with batch B TH32 tender writing first.
