## 4. What adaptation needs
M52 Polish legal language and Kodeks cywilny clause logic; M31 ISAP for statute links; for SMEs, M50 VAT invoices and M32 BLIK. Polish specifics: umowa o dzieło vs zlecenie vs B2B (ZUS consequences, M07), kary umowne practice, KSeF invoicing clauses since 2026, RODO data-processing annexes (umowa powierzenia).

## 5. Demographic or social driver
Weak. Indirect: large B2B-contractor population (IT and other professionals on JDG, M10) who sign client contracts without a lawyer.

## 7. Verdict: **partial**
Global leaders are multilingual and already reach large Polish firms, so the in-house/law-firm segment is a marketing gap at best. What is not covered is a Polish-law, consumer-priced check for individuals and micro-firms signing standard Polish contracts, but search demand is thin and ChatGPT absorbs much of it.

**Seed idea:** "Sprawdź umowę B2B": a per-document (e.g. 49–99 PLN) review of B2B, zlecenie and dzieło contracts for IT and creative contractors, flagging ZUS/tax re-qualification risk (art. 22 §1¹ Kodeks pracy), IP transfer gaps (pola eksploatacji) and kary umowne, with an optional radca prawny check.
