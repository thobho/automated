## 2. Does the Western leader already serve Poland?
- **ElevenLabs Reception.ai**: Polish documentation, 70+ languages with auto-detect, own booking page and calendar, Google Calendar/Calendly/Cal.com ([docs PL](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/pl/reception-ai); [PL small-business page](https://elevenlabs.io/pl/agents/small-business-answering-service)). **Serves PL.**
- **fonio.ai** (AT, a theme example): Polish site, PLN pricing from 399–479 PLN/month (comparison above; [fonio PL](https://www.fonio.ai/pl/ki-telefonassistenten-archived/przychodnie)). **Serves PL.** Hanc.AI (AT) 49 EUR/month.
- **IVRy** (JP) and **Slang.ai** (US): not in PL (not checked further).
- Could not check: Booksy integration anywhere (none listed), call quality.

## 3. Polish demand signals
- Autocomplete: "ai sekretarka" → "automatyczna sekretarka ai", "wirtualna sekretarka ai", "ai jako sekretarka"; "wirtualna recepcjonistka" → "… praca" (human job searches). Moderate interest.
- Supply: 30+ vendors and price points down to 0–40 PLN show a commoditised market.

## 5. Demographic or social driver
DD7 labour shortage and DD12 rising wages make a human receptionist expensive; Polish callers phone rather than book online (THEMES Polish angle).

## 6. CEE check
Linca and Lorela (CZ) chart in Czech business app stores; Born Digital (CZ) in PL for enterprises ([R6 README](../../research/regions/R6-cee-peers/README.md)).

## 7. Verdict: **taken**
Dozens of Polish and foreign vendors sell Polish-speaking AI receptionists at 169–499 PLN/month, ElevenLabs and fonio included. Unless tied to a vertical system of record (see TH02 for clinics, TH27 trades in batch B), there is no defensible gap.

**Seed idea:** none for the horizontal product; the only residue is a **Booksy-native** receptionist for beauty salons (no vendor lists a Booksy integration), dependent on Booksy's partner terms (M48, unverified) — high platform risk.
