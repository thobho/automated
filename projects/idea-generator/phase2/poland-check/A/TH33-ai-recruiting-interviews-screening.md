## 2. Does the Western leader already serve Poland?
- **Maki** (FR; 50+ countries), **Hubert** (SE), **Alex** (US): no Polish page found in one search (not individually confirmed).
- **Hirevire** (one-way video interviews): Polish candidate interface, Polish transcription, AI scoring on Polish answers ([Hirevire Polish support](https://hirevire.com/helpdesk/polish-language-support-overview-5oeze)). **Polish works out of the box** for screening tools.
- Could not check: PLN pricing, Polish customer case studies of Maki/Hubert.

## 3. Polish demand signals
- Autocomplete: "ai rekrutacja" → "ai act rekrutacja", "bielik ai rekrutacja", "hrejterzy rekrutacja ai"; "rozmowa rekrutacyjna ai" → "rozmowa rekrutacyjna z ai". Interest exists, partly regulatory ("ai act") and partly candidate-side.
- Job posts: agencies hire "specjalista ds. rekrutacji z językiem ukraińskim" in Kraków and Wrocław ([pracuj.pl](https://www.pracuj.pl/praca/specjalista-specjalistka-ds-rekrutacji-z-jezykiem-ukrainskim-wroclaw,oferta,1004978828)): a manual step an agent could take over.

## 4. What adaptation needs
**AI Act high-risk (Annex III, hiring)**: obligations from 2 Dec 2027 after the Digital Omnibus (A23), plus Kodeks pracy anti-discrimination and RODO art. 22 (automated decisions). M08 praca.gov.pl and KRAZ (agency register) for temporary-work agencies; candidate languages UA/RU/PL/ka/hi; M40 OLX Praca and pracuj.pl as lead sources.

## 5. Demographic or social driver
DD6 (1.29M foreigners in ZUS, D33) and DD7 (half of firms cite worker availability, D39): high-volume blue-collar hiring with multilingual candidates.

## 6. CEE check
Talentiqa (CZ) blue-collar hiring chat agent; not seen in PL ([R6 README](../../research/regions/R6-cee-peers/README.md)).

## 7. Verdict: **partial**
Generic AI screening works in Polish already (Hirevire) and Polish bot vendors exist for corporates; the high-volume blue-collar and migrant segment run by agencje pracy tymczasowej has no visible AI product. The AI Act high-risk label raises compliance cost for anything that scores candidates.

**Seed idea:** multilingual **pre-screening and document-intake agent for staffing agencies** placing Ukrainian and other foreign workers: phone/WhatsApp/Viber conversation in the candidate's language, collects availability, skills and permit documents, checks legal-work status, books the agency interview; deliberately **does not score or rank** candidates, to stay out of the high-risk scope where possible (to verify with counsel).
