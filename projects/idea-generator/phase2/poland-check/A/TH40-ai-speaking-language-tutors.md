## 2. Does the Western leader already serve Poland?
- **Speak** (US): available to Polish learners of English, ~20 USD/month per a commercial guide ([speak.com](https://www.speak.com/); [Speakshark](https://speakshark.com/best-english-speaking-app-in-poland)). **Serves Poles learning English.**
- **Praktika** (UK), **Duolingo Max** video call, **ChatGPT voice**: work for English from Polish; free tiers.
- **Kielo** (FI, Finnish for immigrants) has no Polish equivalent from a foreign leader; Duolingo offers Polish as a course but not exam prep (not checked in-app).
- **Xeropan** (HU): Hungarian market; no Polish site found.
- Could not check: Speak's Polish UI (app install needed).

## 3. Polish demand signals
- Autocomplete: "angielski z ai" → "… za darmo", "… ranking", "… opinie", "… dla dzieci", "… cena", "… najlepsza aplikacja"; "rozmowy po angielsku ai" → "… z ai za darmo", "aplikacja do rozmowy po angielsku ai" (strong, but "za darmo" dominates).
- **Polish for foreigners:** "egzamin b1 polski" → "… przykładowe testy 2026", "… 2026 rejestracja", "… 2026 cena", "… przykładowe testy online"; "nauka polskiego dla ukraińców" → "… online", "… karty pracy", "… w szkole"; Ukrainian-language "вивчення польської мови" → "… безкоштовно", "… для українців".
- **Exam volume:** 21,781 candidates for state Polish certificate exams in 2024 vs 2,803 in 2017; demand driven by citizenship/long-term residence rules and a B2 requirement for non-EU university candidates (justification to Sejm print 2361: [docx](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/sejm/term10/prints/2361/2361-uzasadnienie.docx)).

## 4. What adaptation needs
Polish exam formats (Państwowa Komisja: B1/B2 parts, oral rubric), the 2026 regulation on certificates (Dz.U. 2026 poz. 676: [ELI](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2026/676/text/T/D20260676L.pdf)), Ukrainian/Russian/English UI, Polish pronunciation and case-ending feedback (model quality in Polish ASR is adequate per TH26; grammar feedback quality untested), M32 BLIK payments; employer channel via M08 foreign-worker permits and staffing agencies (TH33/TH34).

## 5. Demographic or social driver
**Strong for Polish-for-foreigners:** DD6 (1.56 million Ukrainians with permits, 1.29 million foreigners in ZUS; good Polish is the strongest predictor of staying, D34), D35 108.6k foreign students needing B2, DD11 migrant children in schools. Weak for English (shrinking cohorts).

## 6. CEE check
Xeropan (HU) is an English-for-Hungarians app; no CEE Polish-for-foreigners product found ([R6 catalogue](../../catalogue/R6-cee-peers.csv)).

## 7. Verdict: **partial**
English speaking practice for Poles is taken (Speak, Praktika, Duolingo, ChatGPT voice, free local apps) and rated hype. Polish for foreigners shows a clear driver (21,781 exam candidates in 2024, up eightfold since 2017) and visible search demand, with only small apps (Mówek, Univext) and human courses; no Kielo-style product aimed at the B1/B2 state exam was found.

**Seed idea:** "B1 bez stresu": AI speaking and writing coach in Ukrainian/English/Russian for the **state B1/B2 Polish exam**, with mock oral exams scored against the Komisja's published criteria, written tasks with case-ending corrections, and a countdown plan to the next session; 49–79 PLN/month or a 199 PLN exam pack, plus B2B sales to staffing agencies and universities with non-EU students.
