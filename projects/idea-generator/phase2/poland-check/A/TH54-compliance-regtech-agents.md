## 4. What adaptation needs
M31 ISAP/ELI API for change tracking of Polish acts, M13 CRBR and M09 KRS for AML/KYC, M20 BIP for regulator decisions, UODO and KRiBSI guidance (A24–A25), GIIF obligations for small obliged entities (biura rachunkowe, pośrednicy nieruchomości), M52 Polish legal language.

## 6. CEE check

## 7. Verdict: **partial**
A Polish AI compliance platform and many law-firm services cover large-firm NIS2 and AI Act work; small obliged entities with narrow, recurring Polish duties are not visibly served by an AI product.

**Seed idea:** AML-in-a-box for **small obliged entities under the Polish AML act** (biura rachunkowe, pośrednicy nieruchomości, dealers): agent that runs KYC from KRS/CRBR/CEIDG, keeps the required written risk assessment and procedures current, and prepares GIIF notifications; priced per office per month.
