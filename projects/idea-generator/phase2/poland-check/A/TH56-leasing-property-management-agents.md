## 2. Does the Western leader already serve Poland?
- **Re-Leased/Credia** (NZ), **Buena** (DE, Berlin property manager with AI), **RentBetter** (AU), **Dwelly** (UK), **Vessla** (SE): theme rows show home markets only; no Polish site or customer found. **Not in PL.**
- Could not check: each leader's signup flow (all are demo-led), Polish language.

## 3. Polish demand signals
- Autocomplete: "ai zarządzanie najmem" → none; "zarządca nieruchomości program" → none. Low search volume; buyers are a professional niche.
- Job posts for "księgowa ds. wspólnot mieszkaniowych" in Kraków, Warsaw, Wrocław, Sosnowiec (pracuj.pl search hits, e.g. [Kraków](https://www.pracuj.pl/praca/ksiegowy-ksiegowa-ds-wspolnot-mieszkaniowych-krakow,oferta,1004872413)) show labour-intensive back office.
- Paid AI courses for managers (above) show willingness to spend on AI skills.

## 4. What adaptation needs
Ustawa o własności lokali (uchwały, głosowanie indywidualne, zaliczki), media settlements (heat-cost allocators, water), M27 EKW for owner checks, M28 Geoportal, M14 TERYT, M32 BLIK/M33 Przelewy24 for owner payments, M40 OLX and Otodom for rental leads, licensed-manager practice (licencja zarządcy abolished, but liability insurance remains), RODO for residents' data.

## 5. Demographic or social driver
DD10: 208k dwellings completed in 2025 (D48), new blocks become new wspólnoty; DD9 suburban growth; institutional rental (PRS) growth (not quantified here).

## 6. CEE check
No CZ/SK/RO/HU property-management AI in R6 ([R6 catalogue](../../catalogue/R6-cee-peers.csv)).

## 7. Verdict: **open gap**
No Polish AI product and no Western leader present; incumbents are legacy desktop tools and managers improvise with ChatGPT. Demand evidence is thin (no search volume, only job ads and courses), so the gap needs interviews before it counts as strong.

**Seed idea:** "AI biuro zarządcy": inbox and phone agent for **zarządcy wspólnot mieszkaniowych** (firms managing 20–200 communities) that answers owners about balances and zaliczki from Lokale/S.Bit exports, files awarie to contractors, drafts uchwały and counts written votes (głosowanie indywidualne); priced per lokal (e.g. 1–2 PLN/lokal/month).
