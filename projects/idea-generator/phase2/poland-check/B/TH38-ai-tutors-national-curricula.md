## 4. What adaptation needs
Podstawa programowa and Kompas Jutra (new exams from 2030/31), CKE exam sheets and grading keys (egzamin ósmoklasisty, matura), Polish textbooks per publisher (Odrabiamy's moat), M32 BLIK for parents' payments, child data under RODO. Tutoring that scores pupils is closer to AI Act high-risk only when used by institutions; B2C self-study is not.

## 5. Demographic or social driver
DD11: new curriculum and later new exams; DD12 rising incomes and expensive services (private korepetycje) push parents to cheaper AI help; DD6 Ukrainian pupils need help in Polish.

## 7. Verdict: **taken**
Odrabiamy sells a curriculum- and textbook-matched AI tutor at 49 PLN/month, Astra AI (SI) ranks in the Polish store, Brainly and ChatGPT are free alternatives, and batch A found StudyFi and Knowunity with Polish sites. A solo entrant would fight funded brands on paid acquisition.

**Seed idea (residue):** Polish-language tutor for **Ukrainian pupils** in Polish schools (explains a Polish textbook task in Ukrainian, then trains the Polish subject vocabulary), sold to parents and to schools' oddziały przygotowawcze; overlaps with batch A TH40 seed (Polish for foreigners).
