Theme: [THEMES.md#th39](../../themes/THEMES.md#th39) · rank 42, batch B · sources accessed **2026-11-05** · method: [README](README.md#method).

## 2. Does the Western leader already serve Poland?
The theme's leaders are national by construction: Jurafuchs (DE law exams), MyMersi (KR), Mujtahid (SA), APEUni/PTE apps (AU/IL). None targets a Polish exam; the German B1 analogue (migrando) has a Polish-language blog page aimed at Poles in Germany, not the Polish exam ([migrando.de/pl](https://migrando.de/pl/blog/einbuergerung/b1-zertifikat/)). The relevant entrants are local apps above, several built by foreign solo developers.

## 4. What adaptation needs
- B1: exam format and scoring criteria of the Państwowa Komisja; the Polish Language Act was amended on 30 April 2026 (Dz.U. 2026 poz. 676), changes to certificate levels and commissions to be read before building ([ELI text](https://eli.gov.pl/api/acts/DU/2026/676/text/T/D20260676L.pdf)). Explanations in Ukrainian, Russian, English, plus Hindi/Belarusian/Georgian for newer migrant groups (DD6).
- Citizenship rules are in flux: a presidential bill (druk 1759) would raise residence from 3 to 10 years, and the MSWiA has announced a draft with a ~40-question civics test and B2 Polish, planned for adoption in Q4 2026; none was in force as of the sources found ([gazetaprawna.pl](https://www.gazetaprawna.pl/wiadomosci/kraj/artykuly/9898919,prezydent-proponuje-wydluzenie-czasu-oczekiwania-na-polskie-obywatelstwo.html); [forsal.pl](https://forsal.pl/kraj/aktualnosci/artykuly/11246150,dla-kogo-polskie-obywatelstwo-mswia-przygotowuje-zmiany-w-prawie.html); [Grant Thornton](https://grantthornton.pl/publikacja/zatrudnianie-cudzoziemcow-2026-sejm-odrzucil-zmiany-w-obywatelstwie/)). A civics test would be a new exam with no prep market yet.
- LEK/aplikacje: statutes from ISAP/ELI (M31), CEM question base; legal-exam questions test statute wording, so grounding in the act's current text matters (M52).

## 5. Demographic or social driver
DD6: 1.56 million Ukrainians with valid permits (D31) and 51% of pre-war Ukrainian migrants planning to stay (D34) feed B1 demand for long-term residence and citizenship; a B2 + civics requirement would raise it.

## 6. CEE check
No CZ/SK/RO/HU exam-prep product in the [R6 catalogue](../../catalogue/R6-cee-peers.csv). Mova, PolskiPro and other B1 apps appear to come from Ukrainian or foreign developers rather than CEE companies (developer origin not verified).

## 7. Verdict: **partial**
Driving theory, LEK and B1 are already served by paid local apps; Mova covers the AI-scored B1 writing and speaking niche with a Ukrainian interface, which was the obvious migrant wedge. What is not served is the civics test and B2 level that the MSWiA citizenship draft would add, and AI-tutored drilling for the legal entrance exams (only static past-question banks found); both are small or not yet law.

**Seed idea:** "obywatelstwo" prep pack ready for the day the citizenship amendment passes: civics question bank sourced from the act's annex once published, B2 writing/speaking scoring, explanations in Ukrainian/Russian/English, sold via migrant NGOs and legalisation agencies at 99–199 PLN one-off. Build only after the bill is adopted; until then, a waitlist page on "test na obywatelstwo" search terms is the validation.
