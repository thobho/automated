## 2. Does the Western leader already serve Poland?
- **Dust** (FR): self-serve, Pro **24 EUR/seat/month** (yearly), Max 120 EUR; EU data residency on Business; works through general LLMs, so Polish text is handled; no Polish customers on the pricing page ([dust.tt pricing](https://dust.tt/home/pricing)). **Usable from PL** today in EUR.
- **Relevance AI** (AU), **n8n**, Microsoft Copilot Studio, Google Agentspace, OpenAI's agent tools: global, self-serve, Polish-capable. **DRUID AI** (RO) and **Robotiq.ai** (HR) sell enterprise-wide across CEE.
- Could not check: Dust free-tier signup (not attempted; no account creation).

## 3. Polish demand signals
- Autocomplete (2026-11-04): "agenci ai dla firm" → 4 (firma, firmy, w firmie, automatyzacja); "automatyzacja ai dla firm" → 2; "wdrożenie ai w firmie", "n8n polska", "copilot dla firm cena" → echo only. Moderate, generic.
- Surveys: 85% of Polish organisations plan AI agents but only 5% are fully ready (Cisco, 2025-11) ([iMagazine](https://imagazine.pl/2025/11/02/raport-cisco-85-polskich-firm-chce-agentow-ai-tylko-5-jest-na-nie-gotowych/embed/)); 84% of Polish business leaders say they will deploy agents within a year (Microsoft, 2025-05) ([Microsoft PL](https://news.microsoft.com/pl-pl/2025/05/19/84-proc-liderow-biznesu-w-polsce-wdrozy-agentow-ai-w-ciagu-najblizszego-roku/)). The gap is skills, not tools.

## 4. What adaptation needs
Little for the platform itself. For services: RODO art. 28 processing agreements and art. 44 transfers (EU/PL hosting, Bielik/PLLuM as an option), M50 Polish VAT invoices, M41–M46 integrations (Comarch, Symfonia, InsERT, Fakturownia), M01 KSeF. The local value is in the integration and the implementer, not the builder.

## 6. CEE check

## 7. Verdict: **taken**
Horizontal agent builders are global, self-serve and Polish-capable (Dust at 24 EUR/seat, Copilot Studio, n8n), and CEE vendors plus Polish software houses sell implementations. A solo founder has no edge in a horizontal platform; the theme is only useful as a services wedge into a vertical found elsewhere.

**Seed idea (residue):** none as a product. As a channel: a fixed-price "agent setup for biuro rachunkowe" service (KSeF inbox triage, client document chasing) that later productises into the TH17 seed from batch A.
