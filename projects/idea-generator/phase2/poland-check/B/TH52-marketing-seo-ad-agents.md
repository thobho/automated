## 2. Does the Western leader already serve Poland?
- **Photoroom** (FR): the app works on Polish product photos without language dependency; global self-serve. **Serves PL** (no localisation needed).
- **Postiz** (HK, MRR USD 345k) and **SEOBOT** (TR): self-serve, USD pricing, content generation in any language the model writes. **Usable from PL**; no Polish platforms (Wykop, Allegro) integrated per catalogue notes.
- **AEO Engine** (US): agency-style service at USD 1,597–10,000/month, far above Polish SME budgets. **Not in PL.**
- **Uplane** (US, YC F25): ads and landing pages; Google/Meta only, no Allegro Ads. **Not in PL** (no Polish page found).
- Could not check: generation quality in Polish without accounts.

## 6. CEE check
No R6 marketing agent heading to Poland in the [R6 catalogue](../../catalogue/R6-cee-peers.csv); the main regional players (Surfer, Brand24, Senuto) are Polish and export.

## 7. Verdict: **partial**
SEO and AI-answer visibility tools for professionals are built in Poland and sold worldwide, and generic post schedulers and image tools already work in Polish. Not served: **local service firms** (dentists, installers, beauty salons, lawyers) who want to appear when Poles ask ChatGPT or Google AI Mode "who is a good X in my town", which depends on Google Business Profile, Polish review sites and booking platforms rather than on the firm's blog.

**Seed idea:** "Widoczny w AI" for local service firms: monthly check of 20 local prompts ("dentysta Wrocław Krzyki") in ChatGPT/Gemini/AI Overviews, then an agent that fixes the inputs: Google Business Profile posts and Q&A, review replies, ZnanyLekarz/Booksy profile text, schema on the site; 99–199 PLN/month with VAT invoice, sold through web agencies as white label. Absorption risk: Google may add this to Business Profile.
