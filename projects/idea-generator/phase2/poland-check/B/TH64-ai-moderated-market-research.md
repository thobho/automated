## 2. Does the Western leader already serve Poland?
- **Conveo** (GB per catalogue): AI moderator for voice and video interviews, "400+ enterprise teams" (Google, Canva, Orange, AB InBev); "dozens of markets", languages not listed on the home page, Poland not mentioned; free start ([conveo.ai](https://www.conveo.ai/)). Likely usable in Polish for multinationals' Polish samples (not verified).
- **Bolt Insight** (TR/UK): GBP 7M round 2025-12; serves global FMCG ([research-live.com](https://www.research-live.com/article/news/bolt-insight-secures-7m-investment/id/5145333)); Polish support not checked.
- **Usercall**: AI-moderated IDIs in "50+ languages" on every plan, self-serve from USD 89/month, recruitment quoted separately ([usercall.co](https://www.usercall.co/in-depth-interviews)). Polish not named on the page.
- Listen Labs, Outset, Koji: same model, English-first, multilingual ([outset.ai](https://outset.ai/almanac/ai-moderated-research-the-complete-guide-for-2026)).
- Conclusion: the software layer is multilingual and self-serve; the gap is **Polish respondents and a Polish-speaking buyer relationship**, not the model. This is the "it already works in Polish" trap.

- Industry: Polish research revenues grew about 4–5% nominal in 2024, flat in real terms; CATI needs 2–3x more call attempts than 3–4 years earlier and CAWI fights bot farms; OFBOR expects lower margins in 2025 ([Poland Insight](https://polandinsight.com/polish-research-industry-sees-revenue-growth-but-costs-rise-faster-63479/)). Latest OFBOR market value found is 637 PLN M for 2008 ([dbc.wroc.pl](https://dbc.wroc.pl//Content/120523/Wodkowski_Rynek_badan_marketingowych.pdf)); no current figure found.

## 4. What adaptation needs
Recruitment from Polish panels (incentives paid by BLIK or vouchers, M32), Polish voice quality (M53: no Polish consumer voice assistants, so voice interviews are unfamiliar to respondents), RODO consent and recording rules, PTBRiO/ESOMAR quality standards, and Polish-language reporting for local brand managers. Public-sector studies are tenders (excluded).

## 5. Demographic or social driver
None direct. Falling phone response rates (above) push fieldwork online; DD6 (two million foreigners) creates demand for studies in Ukrainian, which human moderators rarely cover.

## 6. CEE check
No CZ/SK/RO/HU AI-moderated research product in the [R6 catalogue](../../catalogue/R6-cee-peers.csv). Global self-serve tools are the entrants.

## 7. Verdict: **partial**
No Polish AI-moderated interview product exists, but global tools are self-serve and multilingual, so the software gap is thin; what they lack is Polish respondent recruitment and local sales. The Polish research market is small, flat in real terms and margin-squeezed, which cuts both ways: agencies need cheaper fieldwork but have little budget.

**Seed idea:** AI-moderated IDIs with Polish panel recruitment for SME brands and e-commerce: 20 voice/text interviews in Polish (or Ukrainian) in 72 hours, recruited from a partner panel, delivered as a Polish report with quotes; 2,500–6,000 PLN per study, white-labelled for small agencies.
