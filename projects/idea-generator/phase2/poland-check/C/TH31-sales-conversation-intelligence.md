## 4. What adaptation needs
Polish transcription with diarisation (solved by Whisper/Deepgram/ElevenLabs), M41–M45 CRM/ERP links (Polish distributors use Comarch, enova, Subiekt rather than Salesforce), call-recording consent under RODO and the Prawo komunikacji elektronicznej. Field sales (przedstawiciele handlowi) visiting shops would need in-person recording, which raises consent and works-council issues. Note: AI used to evaluate workers' performance can fall under AI Act Annex III (employment), a high-risk flag.

## 5. Demographic or social driver
DD7 labour scarcity raises the cost of ramping new reps (deelan's pitch: 30%+ shorter ramp, company claim). Weak.

## 6. CEE check
CloudTalk (SK) already bundles the feature with a Polish site. No HU/RO/CZ standalone product found in the [R6 catalogue](../../catalogue/R6-cee-peers.csv).

## 7. Verdict: **taken**
Conversation intelligence is a feature of the phone systems and meeting recorders Polish firms already use (CloudTalk, Teams, tl;dv), Gong supports Polish for enterprises, and Polish search demand is nearly absent. The AI Act employment flag makes rep scoring riskier.

**Seed idea:** (weak) voice-note visit report for field reps of Polish distributors: the rep dictates after a shop visit and the agent fills the visit report and order lines in Comarch/enova; 49–99 PLN/rep/month. This is order entry (batch A TH61), not call analysis.
