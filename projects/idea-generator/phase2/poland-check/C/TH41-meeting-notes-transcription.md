## 2. Does the Western leader already serve Poland?
Yes. Fyxer, Granola, Plaud, Fireflies and the notetakers above all handle Polish audio; Plaud is a known brand in Polish search. The Korean leader daglo and Taiwanese Atter are home-language plays whose Polish equivalent is the free Teams/Meet feature. Could not check: transcription accuracy on Polish audio (no account created).

Council sessions must be broadcast and recorded since 2018, but committees are not, and the Ombudsman asked the Sejm to regulate recording of committees ([RPO BIP](https://bip.brpo.gov.pl/pl/content/rpo-komisje-rady-gminy-nagrywanie-sejm)); a council protocol is not a verbatim record ([Prawo.pl](https://www.prawo.pl/samorzad/protokol-z-sesji-samorzadowej-rady-to-nie-stenogram,84169.html)).

## 4. What adaptation needs
For generic notes: nothing beyond Polish ASR (solved). For niche documents: the format of a *protokół* of a wspólnota mieszkaniowa meeting (uchwały, vote counts by udziały under the Act on ownership of premises), of a council session (ustawa o samorządzie gminnym, statut gminy, BIP publication, M20), and transcripts of recordings filed as court evidence (KPC rules on evidence).
