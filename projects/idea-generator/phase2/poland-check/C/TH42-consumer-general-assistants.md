## 2. Does the Western leader already serve Poland?
Yes, fully: ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Manus and Genspark work in Polish and sell through app stores. Autocomplete "chatgpt po polsku" → 10 suggestions ("bez logowania", "za darmo", "aplikacja", "premium"). Theme members Zapia (UY, WhatsApp assistant for LatAm), Wrtn (KR) and Codeway (TR) are home-market or global wrappers.

## 3. Polish demand signals
Autocomplete (2026-11-05): "chatgpt po polsku" → 10; "bielik ai" → 10 (opinie, chat, download, aplikacja, kurs, cena, api, bootcamp); "everbot" → 3 (ai, praca, s.r.o). Demand is for free access to the global leaders.

## 4. What adaptation needs
Nothing a foreign leader lacks: Polish works and app-store subscriptions are billed locally (BLIK availability in app-store billing not verified here). The only local lever is a Polish brand plus local invoices (M50), which Everbot is already using.
