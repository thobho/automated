## 2. Does the Western leader already serve Poland?
Mistral (FR, EU-hosted) and Tilde (LV, TildeOpen 30B for European languages incl. Polish) sell in the EU; the US frontier models handle Polish best per the test above. Arabic.AI, Fanar, HUMAIN and BgGPT are national plays for their own languages.
