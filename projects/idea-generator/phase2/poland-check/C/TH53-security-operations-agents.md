## 2. Does the Western leader already serve Poland?
The leaders (Tines IE, 7AI and Prophet Security US, Ethiack PT) and new European entrants (Qevlar AI, Paris, USD 30M in 2026-03; Fleuret AI, Paris, EUR 4M pre-seed for pentest agents) sell globally in English to security teams; security tooling is language-agnostic, so Polish SOCs can buy them directly ([Security Boulevard on Qevlar](https://securityboulevard.com/2026/03/news-alert-qevlar-ai-raises-30m-to-turn-security-alerts-into-actionable-defense-insights-across-socs); [Dealroom on Fleuret](https://dealroom.co/news/159780-fleuret-ai-raises-4m-to-put-ai-agents-to-work-on-pentesting/)). Not tried (enterprise demos only).

## 3. Polish demand signals
- Regulation: the amended KSC act implementing NIS2 applies from **2026-04-03**; entities had to apply for entry in the register by **2026-10-03**, must adapt systems and procedures by **2027-04-03**, and most fines apply after April 2028; a quoted survey says 36% of security experts did not know whether their firm is covered ([ITwiz, 2026-04-02](https://itwiz.pl/nowelizacja-ustawy-o-ksc-zaczyna-obowiazywac-rusza-harmonogram-wdrozenia-nis2-w-polsce/)). Entities self-identify; a commercial guide's figure of ~42,000 covered firms is unverified.
- Autocomplete not run for this theme (enterprise security buyers; low signal expected).

## 4. What adaptation needs
KSC act and its implementing regulations (incident reporting to CSIRT NASK/GOV/MON, register of entities), sector authorities, Polish-language policies and documentation for audits. SOC agents themselves need no localisation.

DD7: security staff are scarce; no demographic driver.

## 7. Verdict: **taken**
Security-operations agents are a global, English, enterprise product that Polish SOCs and MSSPs can buy as is; local knowledge adds nothing to alert triage, and the sales cycle and trust requirements do not fit a solo founder. The Polish angle is compliance paperwork under KSC (self-identification, policies, audit evidence), which belongs to batch A TH54 (compliance and regtech).

**Seed idea:** — (KSC/NIS2 self-assessment and documentation for mid-size "ważne" entities fits TH54; deadline 2027-04-03).
