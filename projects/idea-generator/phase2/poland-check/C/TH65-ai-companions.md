## 7. Verdict: **partial** (weak)
No Polish-language Catholic AI companion was found and Poland has a large practising Catholic population with known prayer-app habits, but the theme is hype-flagged (paid-ads growth, net losses at Bible Chat) and needs Church trust and content rights that a solo developer lacks.

**Seed idea:** (weak) Polish Catholic prayer companion: daily readings and reflections with an AI Q&A grounded only in licensed Polish texts, novena/rosary tracking; 19–29 PLN/month via BLIK; validate with one parish community first.
