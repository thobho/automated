## 2. Does the Western leader already serve Poland?
Yes. **Enhancv** (BG, theme leader) has a Polish-language AI CV generator page with "Polski (PL)" in the language selector and one-click tailoring of the CV to a job description; prices not shown on that page ([Enhancv PL](https://enhancv.com/pl/ai-cv-generator/)). LoopCV also has a Polish page ([LoopCV PL](https://www.loopcv.pro/pl/ai-cv-builder)). ChatGPT is a free alternative (usage not measured).

## 4. What adaptation needs
