## 2. Does the Western leader already serve Poland?
Yes. DeepL, Google Translate, phone makers' live translation and Owll-type apps translate Polish; Lokalise (LV) orchestrates LLM engines for software localisation and works for Polish ([R4 catalogue](../../catalogue/R4-nordics-baltics.csv)). Generic AI tools translate content but lose document layout, which makes them unfit for official documents ([Bluente, 2026](https://www.bluente.com/blog/translate-documents-to-ukrainian)).

"24h", "tanio", "niemiecki", "angielski", "cena", "szybko". Consumer demand for live translation is served by phones; paid demand is for certified translations.

## 4. What adaptation needs
Ustawa o zawodzie tłumacza przysięgłego (only a listed translator can certify), Polish civil-status and education document formats (akty stanu cywilnego, świadectwa), and Ukrainian/Belarusian/Indian document formats for migrants (DD6); layout-preserving output.

## 5. Demographic or social driver
DD6: 1.56 million Ukrainians and growing Asian migration need certified translations for MOS cases (see TH12) and for recognising diplomas.

## 6. CEE check
Lokalise (LV) is global; Tilde (LV) offers machine translation for European languages; neither targets certified translation in Poland.

## 7. Verdict: **partial**
General and live translation is commoditised by DeepL, Google and phones. A narrow tool is missing: layout-preserving AI drafts of foreign civil and education documents for sworn translators, who must still certify; the buyers are a small licensed profession, so the market is limited.

**Seed idea:** (weak) "Szkic tłumaczenia poświadczonego": upload a Ukrainian birth certificate, diploma or driving licence → Polish draft in the sworn-translation layout (with the required notes on stamps and signatures) for the translator to check and sign; 99–199 PLN/month per translator. Could be bundled with the TH12 case-file seed.
