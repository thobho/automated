# a16z Top 100 Gen AI Consumer Apps (T04)

Source family: Andreessen Horowitz's twice-yearly ranking of consumer gen-AI apps by web traffic (Similarweb) and mobile MAU (Sensor Tower). All sources accessed **2026-11-01**. Catalogue rows: `R0-US-001` … `R0-US-016` in [../../catalogue/R0-global.csv](../../catalogue/R0-global.csv).

**Choices**
- Read the [6th edition (2026-03-09)](https://a16z.com/100-gen-ai-apps-6/) and the [5th edition (2025-08-27)](https://a16z.com/100-gen-ai-apps-5/) in full; the [4th edition](https://a16z.com/100-gen-ai-apps-4/) was used only through the 5th edition's "all-star" list (apps on the web list in all five editions).
- Catalogued only products HQ'd in the 51 brief countries. Chinese and Russian apps (Doubao, Kimi, Quark, Kling, Hailuo, Pixverse, GigaChat, Yandex/Alice) are out of scope and listed below for context only. Big-tech assistants (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Copilot, Meta AI, Grok, NotebookLM, Google Labs) are not catalogued: no adaptation angle.
- The 6th edition widened scope to established apps with gen-AI features (Canva, CapCut, Notion, Picsart, Freepik, Grammarly); these are not LLM-native launches and were skipped.
- "All-star" status (on the list in every edition) is recorded as a *proxy* for durable use (signal 3), flagged as such: it is traffic, not paid cohort retention.

**Poland relevance.** The consumer list is dominated by general tools that already work in Polish (assistants, image, voice). ElevenLabs was founded by Poles (Staniszewski, Dąbkowski). Consumer creative tools give little localisation moat; the transferable patterns are (a) app builders with prebuilt local integrations and (b) e-commerce content (Photoroom) tied to Allegro/OLX listing rules.

**Out of scope (context only):** Doubao, Kimi, Quark, Qwen, DeepSeek, Kling AI, Hailuo, Pixverse, Cutout Pro, Meitu apps (China); GigaChat, Yandex Alice (Russia).
