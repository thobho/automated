# Funding rounds and AI rankings: Forbes AI 50, CB Insights AI 100, TechCrunch $100M+ trackers (T04)

Source family: annual AI rankings and big-round trackers for applied (not infrastructure) AI. All sources accessed **2026-11-01**. Catalogue rows: `R0-US-031` … `R0-US-054` in [../../catalogue/R0-global.csv](../../catalogue/R0-global.csv).

**Choices**
- **Forbes AI 50 2026** (published 2026-04-16): the [list page](https://www.forbes.com/lists/ai50/) renders only 4 profiles to the fetch tool (Anthropic, EliseAI, Gamma, Reflection). Other names came from a [MarketScale summary](https://www.marketscale.com/industries/software-and-technology/the-2026-forbes-ai-50-reveals-3056-billion-raised-consolidating-fast-around-a-handful-of-giants.md) (Rogo, Cursor, Gamma, Chai Discovery, Cognition, Mistral…). The full 50 were not recovered.
- **CB Insights AI 100 2026** (published 2026-05-05): the [report page](https://www.cbinsights.com/research/report/artificial-intelligence-top-startups-2026/) names 54 winners in enterprise and industry applications, without HQ or funding. A name on the list alone is not counted as traction; only entries where the page or a funding source gives a number were catalogued (7AI, Prophet Security, Bretton AI). The rest are listed below for T05–T14 to pick up.
- **TechCrunch trackers**: [55 US AI startups with $100M+ rounds in 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/11/26/here-are-the-49-us-ai-startups-that-have-raised-100m-or-more-in-2025/) and [17 in early 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/17/here-are-the-17-us-based-ai-companies-that-have-raised-100m-or-more-in-2026/). Model labs, chips, compute and data-labelling firms skipped; applied products catalogued with the round as traction.
- European applied-AI leaders (Legora, Fyxer, Granola, Synthesia, Light) were added from targeted searches because the TechCrunch trackers are US-only.
- A funding round is traction of investor belief, not of customer retention; `durable_signals` stays `unknown` unless a source shows retention or expansion.

**Poland relevance.** The regulated, document-heavy verticals funded here map onto Polish moats: personal-injury claims (EvenUp → Polish OC compensation market), AML for obliged entities (Bretton → ustawa AML, GIIF), property management (EliseAI → zarządcy nieruchomości and wspólnoty), AI-native accounting (Light → KSeF/JPK). US healthcare-admin products depend on US billing and fax workflows; Polish equivalents need NFZ/P1 integration and fall close to medical rules.
