# Y Combinator batches 2024–2026 (T04)

Source family: YC company directory, batches Winter 2024 to Fall 2026. All sources accessed **2026-11-01**. Catalogue rows: `R0-US-055` … `R0-DE-096` in [../../catalogue/R0-global.csv](../../catalogue/R0-global.csv) (42 rows; Legora, W24, is already `R0-SE-032`; Bretton AI, ex-Greenlite, is `R0-US-054`).

**Choices**
- The [YC directory](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies) renders with scripts, so the full company list was read from the open-source mirror of the directory's data, [yc-oss/api](https://github.com/yc-oss/api) (`companies/all.json`, last updated 2026-10-31; 6,358 launched companies). Each row cites the company's own YC page.
- **2,025 companies** are in batches W24–F26 (W24 248, S24 248, F24 94, W25 165, Sp25 144, S25 166, F25 145, W26 197, Sp26 193, S26 228, F26 197).
- YC does not publish revenue. The traction measure used is **team size** from the directory, a "fast hiring" proxy the brief allows (a company two years out of YC with 30–250 staff has customers paying for it). It is weak evidence and recorded as such; `durable_signals` stays `unknown` unless the YC page states customers or usage.
- Selected: LLM-native applied companies by team size (≥11) across 2024–2026 batches, plus every **non-US** applied company with ≥10 staff, because those are built around one country's rules (the adaptation pattern the brief wants). Skipped: AI-lab data vendors (Human Archive, Hub, Perit, AfterQuery, Datacurve, HUD), hardware/defence/robotics, fintech for emerging markets (Brazil, India, Kenya, Jordan — outside the 51 countries), non-AI marketplaces.
- Two directory entries did not match the expected company (slug "alex" is an Xcode sidebar app, not the AI recruiter on the CB Insights list; slug "tandem" is a 2019 remote-office app) and were dropped.

## Gaps
- Team size is self-reported on YC and can be stale; no revenue or retention evidence for these rows.
- Companies with <11 staff (most of the 2025–2026 batches) were not screened one by one; the 2026 batches will be under-represented until they hire.
- YC "Launch YC" posts and Requests for Startups not read.
