# Poland baseline: AI landscape (T03, part 1)

How far Polish firms and households have adopted AI, who builds AI in Poland, how well models handle Polish, which Western products serve Poland, and the rules. Finding IDs (A01…) let later tasks cite items. All sources accessed **2026-11-01** unless stated otherwise.

**Notes on method and choices**
- Eurostat figures were pulled directly from the Eurostat dissemination API (datasets `isoc_eb_ai` for enterprises and `isoc_ai_iaiu` for individuals), not from press reports. The URL cited is the API query that returns the numbers.
- Survey firms (EY, Microsoft, KPMG) use different samples; their numbers are not comparable with Eurostat and are kept apart.
- "via" marks a press report quoting a primary source that could not be read directly.
- The Polish AI act text (Dz.U. 2026 poz. 1003) PDF could not be converted to text in this environment; dates come from the Sejm ELI API metadata (primary), content details from secondary sources (marked).

| A07 | EY's 3rd *Jak polskie firmy wdrażają AI?* (497 medium and large firms, April 2026): firms that implemented AI report cost reduction (53%), better service quality (52%) and revenue growth (49%); **77% expect to raise AI spending within 18 months**; 28% are "stuck" with many point solutions. Barriers named: data quality, culture, strategy–results gap. | [EY Polska, 2026-04-16](https://www.ey.com/pl_pl/bmc/2026/raport-ey-jak-polskie-firmy-wdrazaj-ai) |
| A08 | KPMG survey of the largest Polish firms: the largest group expects ROI from generative AI only **after five years or more**. | [KPMG Polska press release](https://kpmg.com/pl/pl/media/media-press-najwieksze-firmy-w-polsce-spodziewaja-sie-zwrotu-z-inwestycji-w-generatywna-sztuczna-inteligencje-po-5-latach.html) (date not shown on snippet; unverified year) |
| A09 | GUS's own ICT survey measured only **3.7%** of enterprises using AI in 2023; PIE measured 7.2% and AI Chamber about 16% with other methods (non-comparable). PARP with Jagiellonian University (BKL) calls AI use in firms "rather niche" (report *Sztuczna inteligencja w firmach: gotowość do adopcji, kompetencje i potrzeby*). | GUS/PIE/AI Chamber figures via [EFL](https://efl.pl/pl/biznes-i-ty/artykuly/korzysci-koszty-a-moze-konkurencja-jak-polskie-firmy-podchodza-do-sztucznej-inteligencji); [PARP publication page](https://www.parp.gov.pl/component/publications/publication/sztuczna-inteligencja-w-firmach-gotowosc-do-adopcji-kompetencje-i-potrzeby-raport-z-badan-bkl) (page content not readable by tool) |
| A10 | NASK published a 2025 report on generative AI and the Polish labour market (exposure of occupations). | [NASK PDF, 2025-06](https://nask.pl/media/2025/06/Generatywna-sztuczna-inteligencja-a-polski-rynek-pracy.pdf) (not read in full) |

**Reading for the research:** Polish SMEs are 2–3× behind the EU on AI adoption, while large firms are close to the EU. Polish people use chat assistants privately but much less at work. That is a market of SMEs that have not yet bought AI tools, i.e. room for vertical, done-for-you products, but also a sign that SMEs need a concrete workflow, not a generic assistant ("no need" is the top reason for non-use).

## B. Polish-language model quality

| ID | Finding | Source |
|---|---|---|
| A11 | **Bielik** (SpeakLeash foundation + ACK Cyfronet AGH, trained on the Helios and Athena supercomputers): open-weight Polish models; v3 family (1.5B, 4.5B, 11B) released in 2025, 11B v3 paper January 2026. Bielik-11B-v3.0-Instruct scores 64.8 on INCLUDE-base-44, ahead of Qwen2.5-14B and phi-4; Open PL LLM Leaderboard 5-shot average ~64. Free to use; the foundation does not charge for deployments. | [arXiv 2601.11579 (Bielik 11B v3)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.11579); [arXiv 2604.10799 (tokenizer, v3 7B/11B)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.10799); [Cyfronet project page](https://www.cyfronet.pl/en/projects/initiatives/llm); business model comment via [Antyweb](https://antyweb.pl/bielik-ai-polska-biznes-opinia) |
| A12 | **PLLuM** (government-funded, HIVE AI consortium led by NASK-PIB): 11 open PLLuM models released; **since 31 Dec 2025 the mObywatel app has a virtual assistant built on PLLuM** (RAG over public-administration content, no personal data); also used as an internal assistant at the Ministry of Digital Affairs and in Gdynia and Poznań city halls. Budget raised from 14 to 19 million PLN in 2025. | [PAP](https://www.pap.pl/aktualnosci/polska-ai-juz-dostepna-dla-wszystkich-co-potrafi); [imagazine, 2026-01-02](https://imagazine.pl/2026/01/02/mobywatel-wchodzi-w-2026-rok-z-polskim-ai/); [ITwiz](https://itwiz.pl/polski-rzad-udostepnia-polski-model-jezykowy-pllum/) |
| A13 | **In head-to-head tests, global models beat the Polish ones on Polish tasks.** Oxido's "first Polish LLM test" (12 models, 20 tasks in 10 categories incl. Polish email writing, language correction, history and culture; reported by *Rzeczpospolita*, March 2026): Google's model first, Qwen and Llama on the podium, **Bielik and PLLuM at the bottom**. | [Bankier.pl / PAP, 2026-03-17](https://www.bankier.pl/wiadomosc/Polska-AI-przegrala-z-gigantami-Bielik-i-PLLuM-wypadly-slabo-w-testach-9099328.html) |
| A14 | **Polish is a strong language for frontier models.** In the OneRuler long-context benchmark (26 languages, COLM 2025) Polish ranked **first** at 64k–128k tokens (88% average accuracy across models; English sixth at 83.9%). | [arXiv 2503.01996](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.01996); summary at [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/polish-emerges-as-top-language-in-multilingual-ai-benchmark-testing/) |
| A15 | Vendor multilingual tables usually **omit Polish**: Anthropic's table (MMLU translated into 14 languages via OpenAI's MMMLU set) lists Spanish, German, Indonesian, Swahili etc. but not Polish, and says Claude "is capable in many languages beyond those benchmarked". Builders must run their own Polish evals. | [Claude docs: multilingual support](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/multilingual-support); [OpenAI simple-evals MMMLU](https://github.com/openai/simple-evals/blob/main/multilingual_mmlu_benchmark_results.md) |
| A16 | Polish-specific benchmarks exist for evaluation: Open PL LLM Leaderboard, PLCC (Polish linguistic and cultural competency), INCLUDE; an aggregator lists GPT-4-turbo at 67 and Bielik 3.0 11B at 71.8 PLCC, but mixes versions and settings (low reliability). | [Codesota Polish LLM benchmarks](https://codesota.com/polish-llm) (undated aggregator) |

**Reading:** For text quality, frontier models (Gemini, GPT, Claude) are good enough in Polish and better than local models; Polish models matter for **data residency, on-prem and public-sector** buyers, and as cheap self-hosted options, not for quality. Language alone is not a moat (brief rule 1).

| A26 | **Sector rules that bite AI products in Poland** (from local-moats.md and Phase 1): KNF for financial advice and credit intermediation; medical-device rules (MDR) for anything that triages or diagnoses; *tajemnica adwokacka/radcowska* and bar rules for legal advice; tax-advice monopoly (*doradztwo podatkowe*) limits automated tax advice; KSeF mandatory e-invoicing (2026); employment AI is high-risk under Annex III from Dec 2027 (A23). | Phase 1 notes in `../../../research/05-why-now/` (read-only); A23 sources |

**Reading:** For a solo founder, avoid Annex III uses (hiring scores, pupil grading, credit) or plan for compliance by Dec 2027; transparency duties (disclose bots, label synthetic content) already apply. KRiBSI is new, so enforcement practice is unknown in 2026.
