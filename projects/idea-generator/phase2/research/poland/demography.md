# Poland baseline: demography and social change (T02)

How Polish society is changing and what new demand that creates for 2026–2035. Each finding has an ID (D01…) so the **Demand drivers** section can point to it. All sources accessed **2026-11-01** unless stated otherwise.

**Notes on method and choices**
- Primary sources first: GUS publications (*Polska w liczbach 2026*, *Sytuacja demograficzna Polski do 2025 r.*), ZUS, NFZ, UdSC, NBP, Eurostat. Where the primary page could not be read by the tools (bot-blocked or PDF too large), a news article quoting the primary source is cited and marked "via".
- GUS PDFs were downloaded and converted to text locally to read the tables; the URL given is the PDF itself.
- "Estimate" or "unverified" marks numbers that are not official statistics.
- GUS "working age" (*wiek produkcyjny*) is 18–59 for women and 18–64 for men; Eurostat uses 15–64 and 65+. The two are not mixed in one finding.

## Findings

### A. Population, ageing and longevity

| ID | Finding | Source |
|---|---|---|
| D01 | Population at 31 Dec 2025: **37,333 thousand**, down more than 157 thousand in one year (−0.42%). Population has fallen every year since 2012 except 2017. | GUS, *Sytuacja demograficzna Polski do 2025 r.* (2026-09-29), p. 12–13: [PDF](https://stat.gov.pl/download/gfx/portalinformacyjny/pl/defaultaktualnosci/5468/40/6/1/sytuacja_demograficzna_polski_do_2025_2.pdf) · [publication page](https://stat.gov.pl/obszary-tematyczne/ludnosc/ludnosc/sytuacja-demograficzna-polski-do-2025-r-,40,6.html) |
| D02 | Natural decrease: **238 thousand births vs. ~406 thousand deaths** in 2025, a gap of 167.5 thousand. Births were three times lower than in the baby boom 40 years earlier. | GUS, *Sytuacja demograficzna… do 2025 r.*, p. 12–13 ([PDF](https://stat.gov.pl/download/gfx/portalinformacyjny/pl/defaultaktualnosci/5468/40/6/1/sytuacja_demograficzna_polski_do_2025_2.pdf)) |
| D03 | **21.1% of the population is 65+ (7.9 million)** at end-2025, vs. 14.2% aged 0–14 (5.3 million). Ageing index 148: 148 "grandparents" per 100 "grandchildren". Median age almost 44, eight years higher than in 2000. | GUS, *Sytuacja demograficzna… do 2025 r.*, p. 12 ([PDF](https://stat.gov.pl/download/gfx/portalinformacyjny/pl/defaultaktualnosci/5468/40/6/1/sytuacja_demograficzna_polski_do_2025_2.pdf)) |
| D04 | Median age rose from **32.3 (1990) to 43.8 (2025)**; women 45.4, men 42.3. Urban share of the population fell from 62% to 59% over the same period. | GUS, *Polska w liczbach 2026*, p. 2: [PDF](https://plwliczbach.stat.gov.pl/pl/assets/pdf/polska-w-liczbach-2026.pdf) |
| D05 | GUS projects **60+ population of 10.3 million in 2030, 11.4 million in 2040, 12.4 million in 2050**; in 2060 11.9 million, i.e. **38.3% of the population**. The 85+ group more than doubles vs. 2023, the fastest-growing age group. Women outnumber men 138:100 among seniors in 2030. | GUS projection via [Bankier.pl / PAP Biznes, 2024-12-24](https://www.bankier.pl/wiadomosc/Polskie-spoleczenstwo-sie-starzeje-a-trend-ten-bedzie-sie-tylko-poglebial-8866254.html) |
| D06 | GUS 2025 simulations: population in 2060 of **30.9 million** (official 2023 forecast, −17.6% vs. 2024) or **28.4 million** if fertility stays at 1.10 (−24.3%); the 90+ population roughly triples; in 2060 there is more than one non-working-age person per working-age person. | GUS simulations via [Analizy.pl, 2025-11-06](https://www.analizy.pl/puls-rynku/38718/polska-kurczy-sie-i-starzeje-szybciej-niz-zakladala-oficjalna-prognoza) |
| D07 | Eurostat: Poland's old-age dependency ratio (65+ per 15–64) rises from **under 30% (2022) to over 50% by 2050** and ~60% by 2100, among the highest in the EU. | Eurostat EUROPOP2023 via [Notes from Poland, 2023-04-20](https://notesfrompoland.com/2023/04/20/polands-population-to-shrink-23-by-2060-and-old-age-dependency-ratio-to-double/); dataset [tps00200 mirror on DBnomics](https://db.nomics.world/Eurostat/tps00200) |
| D08 | Healthy life expectancy at birth (2024): **women 65.3 years (79.4% of life), men 61.6 years**. A 60-year-old woman lives only **50.4%** of her remaining life in good health, a man 56.1%. Lubelskie has the shortest healthy life. | GUS, *Sytuacja demograficzna… do 2025 r.*, ch. 2.2 ([PDF](https://stat.gov.pl/download/gfx/portalinformacyjny/pl/defaultaktualnosci/5468/40/6/1/sytuacja_demograficzna_polski_do_2025_2.pdf)) |

| ID | Finding | Source |
|---|---|---|

Today's workers will get a much smaller pension relative to their wage. | ZUS, *Prognoza wpływów i wydatków Funduszu Emerytalnego do 2080 roku*, via [Interia](https://wydarzenia.interia.pl/kraj/news-przyszle-emerytury-moga-mocno-rozczarowac-zus-pokazuje-skale,nId,23488841) |
| D12 | Average gross monthly wage **8,903.56 PLN in 2025** (+9.1% nominal, +5.5% real); average non-farm pension and disability benefit 4,170.43 PLN, farmers' (KRUS) 2,228.07 PLN. | GUS, *Polska w liczbach 2026*, p. 8 ([PDF](https://plwliczbach.stat.gov.pl/pl/assets/pdf/polska-w-liczbach-2026.pdf)) |
