# Poland baseline: demography and social change (T02)

How Polish society is changing and what new demand that creates for 2026–2035. Each finding has an ID (D01…) so the **Demand drivers** section can point to it. All sources accessed **2026-11-01** unless stated otherwise.

**Notes on method and choices**
- Primary sources first: GUS publications (*Polska w liczbach 2026*, *Sytuacja demograficzna Polski do 2025 r.*), ZUS, NFZ, UdSC, NBP, Eurostat. Where the primary page could not be read by the tools (bot-blocked or PDF too large), a news article quoting the primary source is cited and marked "via".
- GUS PDFs were downloaded and converted to text locally to read the tables; the URL given is the PDF itself.
- "Estimate" or "unverified" marks numbers that are not official statistics.
- GUS "working age" (*wiek produkcyjny*) is 18–59 for women and 18–64 for men; Eurostat uses 15–64 and 65+. The two are not mixed in one finding.

## Gaps

- **Informal carers:** no official statistic; D16 is an online survey (n=810). PolSenior2 microdata or EHIS would give a better number.
- **Bon senioralny details:** media disagree on the eligibility age (65+ vs. 80+ in 2026–2028) and the roll-out pace; read the act in ISAP/Dziennik Ustaw before building on DD2.
- **Health queues:** WHC *Barometr* 2025 read only through Demagog; NFZ's own waiting-time data (*Terminy leczenia*) not analysed.
- **Mental health:** NFZ figures are from 2023; no 2025–2026 update found.
- **Private healthcare market:** size of medical-subscription and insurance market (PIU, PMR) not verified.
- **Migration:** sector breakdown of foreign workers (by PKD) and Polish-language proficiency levels were not found in a primary source; Belarusian, Georgian, Indian and Colombian communities not studied separately.
- **Return migration:** only the stock of Poles abroad; no data on how many return each year or what they need on return.
- **Regions:** suburban growth figures are from the 2021 census (via PIE, 2022); a 2025 gmina-level BDL query was not run.
- **Education:** content of the *Kompas Jutra* curriculum (what changes in subjects, homework, digital skills) not read; numbers of Ukrainian pupils in Polish schools not found.
- **Housing:** the "1–2 million dwellings" shortage is a contested estimate; rents not covered.
- **Several findings rely on press reports quoting primary sources** (marked "via"); spot-check before using any single number in a write-up.
