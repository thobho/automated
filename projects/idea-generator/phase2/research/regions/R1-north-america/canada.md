## Patterns
1. **Canada's LLM champions are legal and tax research firms selling into the US** (Blue J, Spellbook, Alexi, Clio). Blue J and Spellbook have the best durable evidence in R1: multi-year contracts (>50% of ARR) and NRR 130% — signals 3/4. A Polish tax-research tool over KIS/NSA is the closest adaptation.
2. **The state as distribution channel**: Infoway's national AI Scribe Program funds up to 10,000 one-year licences from 9 pre-qualified Canadian vendors; Québec certifies scribes (Santé Québec) and the Collège des médecins asks for patient consent. Poland has no such programme; NFZ/CeZ could become one (watch item).
3. **Civil-law notarial work** (Paraito, Québec) is the most Poland-like problem found in R1: title searches on old handwritten land records, French-only, priced per search. Polish notaries and księgi wieczyste are structurally similar.
4. **EMR vendors buy scribes** (MEDFAR–CareWay; Mutuo acquired by Healwell): an exit path for a Polish scribe to Polish EDM vendors.
