## Patterns
1. **Two channels for AI scribes**: enterprise (Abridge, Ambience via health systems) and **direct to clinician** (Freed: 17,000+ paying, self-serve, App Store grossing chart). The second is the founder-feasible template; Poland's private-practice market and the Canadian state-funded scribe programme (see [canada.md](canada.md)) show two routes.
2. **Front-desk voice agents for specialty practices** (Assort, Hello Patient) are the next wave after scribes. Assort's ~3M USD ARR at a ~750M valuation is a hype-ratio warning (valuation ≫ revenue).
3. **Home care and post-acute admin** (Zingage, Arya, Axle) grow on staff shortages — the same driver as Poland's ageing and carer shortage (see `research/poland/demography.md`).
4. US prior-auth and claims tools (Anterior, Candid, Tennr) depend on the US payer system and transfer poorly.
