## Patterns
1. **Teacher-side AI spread fast through free, bottom-up channels**: Brisk (Chrome extension in 1 in 5 US K-12 teachers' browsers), MagicSchool (6M+ sign-ups, then district contracts). Both are durable-signal 5 (owned distribution). The Polish counterpart needs podstawa programowa, CKE criteria and e-dziennik links; scoring students is AI Act high-risk, lesson-prep is not.
2. **Voice recruiters for high-volume and staffing hiring** (Alex, ConverzAI) are well funded; in Poland the staffing sector placing foreign workers is the buyer, but interviewing/scoring is AI Act high-risk.
3. **Consumer chart winners show hype markers**: Cal AI grew on paid influencer retainers with no renewal data yet (hype warning: growth from influencers), then sold to MyFitnessPal (excerpt). Speak sustains a top-grossing rank two years after its round (proxy for durability).
4. **US-specific consumer finance apps** (Dovly AI credit disputes; settlement-claim apps such as Settlemate at #20 Finance) rely on US credit-bureau and class-action systems; the Polish analogue would be BIK/KRD disputes.
