## Patterns (Chile)
1. **Regional legal AI on national corpora** (Magnar, Lemontech LIA, Jurispeed): Magnar reached 350 firms in seven countries in a year by loading each country's law.
2. **SME accounting connected to the tax authority** (Cenit: SII F29; Mexico RESICO). Software plus a human expert, priced as a subscription. Direct analogue of a KSeF/JPK-connected service for JDG/small sp. z o.o.
3. **WhatsApp agents for health-centre scheduling** (Cero, Eniax): the moat is the hospital system integration and no-show economics.
4. **WhatsApp CX/sales agents** (Vambe, Chattigo, Wots): Chattigo's exit (~USD 60M at >USD 10M ARR) is one of the few LatAm revenue figures in the theme.
