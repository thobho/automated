## Patterns (Colombia)
1. **Clinical documentation + decision support** (Telepatia): the fastest-funded LatAm vertical AI found; sells to institutions, not doctors. Same theme as Abridge (R1), Heidi (R8), Huli (CR).
2. **Pay-per-outcome sales agents** (Dapta's per-qualified-meeting pricing): durable signal 7.
3. **Tax-authority-connected accounting agents** (Cifrato on DIAN; R0).
4. **A wave of solo-developer "AI finance assistant" apps** in the Finance chart: low barriers, hype risk.
