**Why fewer than 5:** Costa Rica (~5M people) leads Latin America in generative-AI *use* (28.5% of working-age adults in Q1 2026, Microsoft data via [CR Hoy](https://crhoy.com/tecnologia/pais-mantiene-primer-lugar-regional-en-apropiacion-de-ia-generativa/), excerpt), but it has almost no AI *startup investment* (near-zero AI investment in the 2024 Latin American AI Index, [DPL News](https://dplnews.com/?p=259569), excerpt). The local tech economy is mostly nearshore services and multinational delivery centres. No Costa Rican LLM app appears in any of the 5 App Store top-grossing charts, and no 2025–2026 AI funding round for a Costa Rican startup was found. Four rows are all the evidence supports, and two of them are weak.

## Patterns (Costa Rica)
1. **AI scribe inside an existing EMR** (Huli): the same theme as Telepatia (CO) and Heidi/Abridge, sold through an installed base of doctors.
2. **High AI use, little AI building**: users adopt global tools; the market for local LLM products is small.
