## Patterns (Mexico)
1. **Tax-authority-connected filing for small taxpayers** (Heru, Cenit's RESICO product): SAT's AI-driven audits (Plan Maestro 2026) push demand; experts say AI reads data but accountants interpret it ([Expansión](https://expansion.mx/tecnologia/2026/06/29/ia-ya-puede-hacer-tu-declaracion-ante-el-sat)).
2. **Mexican-law legal AI** (LEXIMEX, AIJusticia) with no public traction, while the Chilean Magnar is about to open a Mexico office. National legal AI is crowded and regional players arrive fast.
3. **Agents inside a vertical workflow**: insurance brokers (Handle), freight (Nuvo AI), SME POS (Pulpos, Órale AI). Repeats R8/R9 "agent in a system of record".
4. **WhatsApp commerce agents** (Yavendió, Darwin AI, Vambe): heavy VC interest; WhatsApp-specific.
