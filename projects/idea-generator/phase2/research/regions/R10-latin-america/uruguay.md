## Patterns (Uruguay)
1. **Export-first vertical agents** built by small Uruguayan teams for foreign markets (Flai US dealers, Foliume EU insurance, Mozart collections in 15 countries).
2. **Consumer super-assistant on WhatsApp** (Zapia, 6M users): an LLM-native consumer pattern that only works where WhatsApp is the operating system of daily life, and it is exposed to Meta platform policy.
3. **Collections agents** (Mozart): the debt-collection job step is replaced by negotiation agents; same theme as Skit.ai (US/IN).
