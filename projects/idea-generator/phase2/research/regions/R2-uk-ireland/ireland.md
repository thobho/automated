**Market context.** PitchBook (via Silicon Republic) counts a record €691.3M of VC into Irish AI firms across 107 deals in 2025 and €256M across 75 deals in 2026 to 12 Oct; median deal €2.4M in 2025, €1.6M in 2026; Ireland is 9th in Europe by AI deal count and 2nd per capita ([Silicon Republic](https://www.siliconrepublic.com/business/pitchbook-ai-funding-ireland-sme-ivca-enterprise-ireland-2025-2026), read). Most of the money goes to late-stage horizontal companies (Wayflyer, Tines, Fonoa); Irish country-specific LLM products are small, pre-seed and seed.

**Country choice:** Intercom was founded in Dublin and keeps a large Dublin office but is headquartered in San Francisco; it is listed under Ireland because R0/R1 do not hold it and the brief asks where a product "first grew". Belfast-based Eolas Medical is in the UK file (Northern Ireland is UK). Newcode (legal agents, Dublin office, ARR tripled in 12 months, 60+ customers, Series A to €17M total, 2026-08, [Silicon Republic](https://www.siliconrepublic.com/start-ups/ai-start-up-newcode-adds-series-a-funding-grows-dublin-headcount), read) is Norwegian and left for R4.

## Patterns (Ireland)
1. **Ireland's LLM champions are horizontal and global** (Intercom Fin, Tines). Country-specific products are seed-stage: tax research over Revenue sources (Leapifai), Leaving Cert revision (SimpleStudy), dental voice (Linda AI).
2. **Tax research on national sources repeats across regions**: Leapifai (IE) joins Blue J (CA), Accordance and TaxGPT (US/R0). Leapifai is raising specifically to add UK codes, so national tax codes are treated as a moat to be built one country at a time.
3. **Accounting-firm automation sold through firms** (Outmin: 11 firms, 350 businesses) mirrors UK PeTAR and US Basis: partner channel, replaced bookkeeper hours.
4. **Fin's outcome-based pricing** (pay per resolution) is the clearest value-based pricing example in R2 (durable signal 7).
