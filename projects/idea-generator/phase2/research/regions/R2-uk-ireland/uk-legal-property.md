## Patterns
1. **The UK regulator licenses AI-run legal services.** Garfield.Law (SRA, 2025) shows an outcome-priced, narrow, high-volume legal process (small debt claims) can be productised. Poland has no ABS regime, but the pre-court part (wezwania, EPU filing by the creditor) needs no lawyer.
2. **Property transactions are the UK's deepest LLM-plus-registry theme**: Orbital (title review), Conveyd and Farringdon (AI-native conveyancers), Moverly (seller packs), Dwelly (lettings). All lean on HM Land Registry and government data. Poland's equivalents (EKW, EGiB, Geoportal, MPZP) are in [local-moats.md](../../poland/local-moats.md).
3. **Horizontal contract AI is crowded and has its first UK failure** (Robin AI, excerpt), while vertical players with a registry or court workflow keep raising.
