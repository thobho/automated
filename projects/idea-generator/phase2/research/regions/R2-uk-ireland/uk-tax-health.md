## Tax and accounting: Making Tax Digital is the trigger

**Context.** MTD for Income Tax became mandatory on 6 April 2026 for sole traders and landlords with qualifying income above £50,000; the threshold falls to £30,000 in April 2027 and £20,000 in April 2028 ([Property Investor Today](https://www.propertyinvestortoday.co.uk/breaking-news/2026/06/new-ai-tool-to-help-landlords-with-mtd/), read). UKTN puts the 2026 cohort at ~850,000 self-employed people and landlords ([UKTN](https://www.uktech.news/fintech/anna-money-secures-10m-injection-ahead-of-uk-tax-policy-shift-20251218), read). This is the closest UK parallel to KSeF/JPK: a dated mandate that forces digital records and quarterly filings on micro-businesses.

## Patterns
1. **MTD ITSA (April 2026) created a burst of AI tax tools** (ANNA, Record OS, FreeAgent, banks), most of them aimed at landlords and sole traders. The mandate's staged thresholds (2026/2027/2028) are a timing window (durable signal 10). Poland's KSeF rollout for JDG is the same shape, but Phase 1 found it crowded.
2. **The NHS is both a buyer and a channel**: Accurx reached most GP practices by shipping a scribe into a platform they already use; TORTUS went through a hospital pilot then a telephony partner. AI phone agents for waiting lists (Dora) attack no-shows, a problem NFZ also has.
3. **Clinical delivery models (Flok, Limbic, Cera) raise big rounds but need licences or staff**: catalogued for theme spread, flagged L7.
