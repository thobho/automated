## Patterns (UK)
1. **Regulated, registry-backed processes are where UK LLM startups cluster**: debt claims (Garfield), conveyancing and title (Orbital, Conveyd, Moverly), MTD tax (ANNA, Record OS, PeTAR), NHS admin (Accurx, Dora), employment tribunals (Valla). Each maps onto a Polish registry or procedure.
2. **Dated mandates create windows**: MTD ITSA (2026–2028) for tax; SRA authorisation (2025) for AI law firms; NHS AVT guidance for scribes.
3. **Hybrid AI + licensed human is the dominant UK model** (Lawhive, Conveyd, Garfield, Record OS, ANNA): AI does the volume, a regulated professional signs off.
4. **Few UK rows show retention**: durable signals are mostly replaced spend (signal 1) and timing (10). Moverly (Connells cohort) and Dora (pilot KPIs) are the only outcome data found.
