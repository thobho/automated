## Why only 2 rows (status `thin`)
- Luxembourg has ~670,000 residents; its AI startups build for the financial centre (funds, regtech) or for export, not for a domestic problem that a Polish counterpart could copy.
- The searches found few LLM products with any traction figure. Luxinnovation keeps a list of [Luxembourg AI startups and scaleups](https://luxinnovation.lu/resources/luxembourg-ai-startups-and-scaleups) (not screened one by one: time).
- Products found but not catalogued: **Tadaweb** (OSINT analytics, 20M USD round, date not shown; excerpt, not clearly LLM-based), **Wizata** (industrial AI, 2.7M EUR, undated; excerpt), **Aptus.AI** (Italian regtech that opened in Luxembourg in 2025-09; belongs to R5 Italy), **Algoreg** (regtech, ~1M USD; AI focus unconfirmed).
