## Patterns (Netherlands)
1. **Healthcare speech is the Dutch breakout**: one scribe reached a third of all GP practices in about three years, then sold to a Nordic consolidator. National-language clinical speech plus the national GP system is a real moat, but only until a well-funded European player buys the local leader.
2. **Accounting AI targets the firm, not the zzp'er**: Autoboeker and DataSnipper sell to accountants; the zzp'er self-service apps in the charts (Tellow, Dyme) are not LLM products.
3. **The Dutch AI startup pipeline is thin for applied B2B**: Techleap reports 27% of Dutch VC goes to AI vs. 32% in the EU ([accountant.nl](https://www.accountant.nl/nieuws/2026/2/ai-sector-in-nederland-blijft-achter-bij-andere-eu-landen/), excerpt); the large 2026 rounds were deep tech (CuspAI, Axelera, QuantWare; [ioplus](https://ioplus.nl/nl/posts/grootste-nederlandse-investeringsrondes-van-2026-draaien-om-ai), excerpt).
