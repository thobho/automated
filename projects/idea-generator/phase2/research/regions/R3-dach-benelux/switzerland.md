## Patterns (Switzerland)
1. **Sovereign hosting is a product feature in regulated professions**: TreuhandGPT (Swiss-hosted for fiduciaries), Langdock in Germany, Saipient's hosting partner. Polish biura and kancelarie have the same reluctance to paste client data into ChatGPT; Polish hosting plus Bielik/PLLuM is a credible selling point.
2. **Distribution through the incumbent's channel**: an association (TREUHAND|SUISSE) and a hospital-IT vendor (Cistec) carry the AI to thousands of users. For a solo founder in PL this is the realistic path to the first 100 customers.
3. **National billing codes** (Tarmed/Tardoc) are a recurring health-admin wedge (R1: US coding startups; R3: Saipient).
