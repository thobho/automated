## Patterns (Estonia)
1. **The state builds the public-services assistant** (Bürokratt): private LLM players for agency Q&A have no room. For Poland this is both a signal (citizens want plain-language answers about procedures) and a risk (mObywatel could absorb it).
2. **Language apps are a local paid category** (Speakly, Lingvist), as in Finland (Kielo, WordDive); none is yet a clear LLM-native "learn the language of where you live" product in Estonia.
3. **Horizontal B2B agents** (Pactum, Handhold) replace a procurement or sales role; little local knowledge involved.
