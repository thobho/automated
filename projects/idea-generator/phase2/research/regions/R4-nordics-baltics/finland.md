## Patterns (Finland)
1. **National-language learning for residents** (Kielo, WordDive) is the clearest LLM-native local consumer product. Poland has the same driver at larger scale (~1.5–2M Ukrainians).
2. **Care providers back scribes** (Cor Group → Gosta Labs; Terveystalo → Etsimo), matching Norway's municipalities buying for GPs.
3. Finnish applied-LLM rounds are horizontal (Zero, Comparables.ai); public-sector and legal LLM champions were not found.
