## Why Iceland is thin
Iceland has ~390,000 people. Its startup output is small, Northstack's funding reports found in search ended before 2025, and no 2025–2026 Icelandic LLM product except 50skills had public traction. Chart positions in Iceland need very little revenue, so Kvittun's rank is weak evidence.

## Patterns (Iceland)
1. **HR workflows with AI agents sold to large employers and the state** (50skills: City of Reykjavík, a ministry), parallel to Sweden's Intric for public bodies.
2. Paid local consumer apps are utility (receipts, family chores, finding certified tradespeople), not LLM-native.
