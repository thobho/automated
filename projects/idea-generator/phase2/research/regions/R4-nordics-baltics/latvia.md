## Patterns (Latvia)
1. **Local legal research with cited answers** (Lexu) mirrors Denmark's Pandektes and Sweden's legal tools: a national-case-law LLM layer is a recurring Nordic–Baltic product, sold through law-firm and bar partnerships.
2. **National-language LLM vendors** (Tilde) sell sovereignty to the public sector; Poland has the parallel in Bielik and PLLuM.
3. Most Latvian-founded AI companies target the US market (Trace.Space, Desktop Commander) rather than Latvian problems.
