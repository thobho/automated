## Patterns (Lithuania)
1. **Lithuanian AI companies expand to Poland first** (Sigvi live, Paysera planned): the Baltic-to-PL entry path is real; check it in every Poland check.
2. **Accounting as a tech-enabled service** (RoboLabs, Paysera) rather than software only, as in Denmark's Repodo: AI does the work, a human signs off.
3. **SMB "AI employees"** (Sintra) reached 12M USD ARR with self-serve pricing, a global, not local, play.
4. **Driving-test prep is a paid local category** (four KET apps in the Education top 30); a Polish LLM tutor for the prawo jazdy theory test is an untested idea worth noting.
