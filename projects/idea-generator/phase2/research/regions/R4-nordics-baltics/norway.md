## Patterns (Norway)
1. **GP scribes spread nationally in two years** (61% of GPs trying speech-to-note in 2025), with municipalities buying for GPs. This is the strongest adoption-speed data point in the region.
2. **AI-native engineering firms** (Solvera, and Endra in Sweden) sell outcomes instead of software, the "service-as-software" model from R0/R1.
3. Norway has fewer LLM startups with public traction than Sweden; incumbents (DNB, Compello, Tripletex-type ERPs) add assistants themselves.
