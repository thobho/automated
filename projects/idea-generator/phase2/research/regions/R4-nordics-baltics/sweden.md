## Patterns (Sweden)
1. **Stockholm produces vertical LLM firms with fast follow-on rounds**: Legora and Lovable (R0), then Tandem (health), Aloi (legal), Endra (engineering), Vessla (property). Most go pan-European quickly, so they are entry risks for Poland as much as templates.
2. **Sovereign AI for the public sector** (Intric) is a theme not seen in R1–R3: municipalities buy a shared assistant platform with DPIAs, not single apps.
3. **Accounting-firm agents** appear again (Bluebook), with a warning: its site is down 20 months after the pre-seed.
