## Patterns (France)
1. **Legal AI is the densest French vertical**: Doctrine (now LexisNexis), Haiku, Ordalie, Jimini, with a state programme backing the sector. Consolidation has begun (RELX–Doctrine, LegalPlace–Legalstart).
2. **Micro-business back office as one agent** (LegalPlace) builds on the incorporation funnel; same theme as R3's AI-native accounting firms.
3. **Healthcare front desk**: voice agents (Vocca) distributed through practice-software vendors (Cegedim), next to scribes (Nabla).
4. **Education**: grading assistant with a curriculum moat (Ed.ai) and paid revision apps in the chart; the state is building its own teacher assistant (tender), a Bürokratt-like risk.
