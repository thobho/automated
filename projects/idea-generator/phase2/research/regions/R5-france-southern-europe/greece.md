## Patterns (Greece)
1. **Greek AI founders build abroad** (Rillet, Saronic); at home, applied LLM products are small B2B agent vendors (Moveo, Helvia).
2. **National legal AI** appears again (dikaio.ai + three smaller ones) but with hundreds of users, not thousands.
3. **The state built the citizen assistant first** (mAigov, mAiGreece), on Azure OpenAI.
