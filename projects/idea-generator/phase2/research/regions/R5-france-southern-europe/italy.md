## Patterns (Italy)
1. **National legal AI scaling to Europe** (Lexroom: 8,000+ firms, very high daily use), repeating Doctrine (FR) and Pandektes/Lexu (R4).
2. **Procurement bid agent** (Cato) built on the complexity of national tender rules.
3. **Student study apps from Italy chart at home** (Kiwi AI, Algor), helped by local habits (sbobine) and law (DSA compensatory tools).
4. Commercialisti AI: no startup with traction found; the profession's council and incumbents (TeamSystem, Zucchetti) seem to own the channel.
