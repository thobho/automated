Context: the biggest Maltese AI story is not a startup. On 2026-05-16 the government and OpenAI agreed that every citizen and eID-holding resident aged 14+ who completes a free University of Malta "AI for All" course gets a year of ChatGPT Plus (or Microsoft 365 Personal with Copilot); the Malta Digital Innovation Authority checks eligibility via eID; financial terms undisclosed ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/malta-chatgpt-plus-partnership), excerpt; [Engadget](https://engadget.com/2174473/openai-is-offering-chatgpt-plus-to-citizens-of-malta-for-a-year), excerpt). The University of Malta is building a Maltese-centric LLM (post-doc call, excerpt). A 2024 report counted ~1 AI startup per 9,500 people and named Binderr and CIVIQUO ([HyScaler](https://hyscaler.com/insights/malta-ai-startups-race/), excerpt).

## Patterns (Malta)
1. **The state buys a general assistant for everyone** (ChatGPT Plus for all eID holders): the opposite of Estonia's or Portugal's build-your-own approach. It narrows the room for thin consumer-assistant products and leaves room for workflow products.
2. Malta's local specialities (corporate services, iGaming, financial licensing) produce compliance tooling (Binderr) rather than LLM-native products.
