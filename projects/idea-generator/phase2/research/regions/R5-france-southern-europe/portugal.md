Context: Portugal's applied-AI scene is dominated by horizontal B2B (customer-service agents, cybersecurity) and by a few unicorns (Sword Health, Feedzai, OutSystems). The state is building **Amália**, the first Portuguese-state LLM, framed as language and data sovereignty rather than a "Portuguese ChatGPT" ([Sábado](https://www.sabado.pt/sabermais/web-summit), read; no budget or dates on the page), the same pattern as Bielik/PLLuM and Estonia's Bürokratt (R4). ## Patterns (Portugal)
1. **Horizontal B2B agents** (Automaise, Visor.ai, Augusta Labs) sold to banks, insurers and telecoms; no country-specific LLM champion with traction was found.
2. **State sovereign LLM** (Amália), the same pattern as Bielik/PLLuM, Tilde (LV) and Bürokratt (EE).
3. **Graveyard signal:** Unbabel (an early AI-translation leader) was acquired and then declared insolvent in 2026, an example of an LLM-commoditised category.
