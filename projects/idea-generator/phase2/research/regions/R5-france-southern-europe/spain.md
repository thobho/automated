## Patterns (Spain)
1. **Consumer tax filing with AI at scale** (TaxDown: 3M+ users, >10M EUR revenue, profitable), stronger evidence than any other R5 consumer app.
2. **Voice agents for healthcare and SMB phones** (Tucuvi, HeyDiga), with Telefónica as incumbent competitor.
3. **Spanish HR suite becoming an agent platform** (Factorial) and moving into DE/FR/IT/PT: an entrant risk for Polish HR tools.
4. **Exam-prep for public jobs** (oposiciones) is a big paid niche without an LLM leader found; Poland has no equivalent mass civil-service exam, so the pattern transfers weakly (closest: aplikacje prawnicze, egzamin na doradcę podatkowego, driving theory).
