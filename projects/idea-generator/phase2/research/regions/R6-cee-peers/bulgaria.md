## Patterns (Bulgaria)
1. **The CEE company with the strongest agent traction is a Bulgarian finance platform** (Payhawk: >100M USD ARR, agents doing AP, travel and support). It already has a Warsaw office, so Polish AP/expense agents for mid-market firms face it directly; the open room is below its segment (micro-firms, biura rachunkowe) and Polish-specific closing (KSeF, JPK).
2. **Tax authority adopts an on-prem national LLM** (National Revenue Agency on BgGPT, 2026). If PLLuM follows in Polish public administration, thin citizen assistants lose room while integrators and workflow tools gain.
3. **Bulgarian AI startups target global B2B niches** (Quillon, 1club AI, Enhancv), not Bulgarian law.
