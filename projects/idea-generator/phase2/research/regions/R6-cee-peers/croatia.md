## Patterns (Croatia)
1. **E-invoicing mandate (2026) creates "explain my invoices" tools** (FiskAI), the same trigger as KSeF in Poland. Croatian products here are pre-traction, which says the AI layer on a mandate is easy to start and hard to scale.
2. **CEE incumbents own customer-service pipes** (Infobip, AgentOS): any Polish CX-agent idea competes with a regional platform that already sells in Poland.
3. **Regional RPA vendors exit to global IT firms at small multiples** (Robotiq.ai, EUR 9M EV on EUR 1.4M revenue), a realistic exit benchmark for a Polish B2B automation product.
4. **Exam-prep demand is strong** (Astra AI #2, EduHub #17), with Croatian matura and medical entrance exams as local content.
