## Patterns (Hungary)
1. **Accountants as human-in-the-loop for an AI tax bot** (Bookkeepie): the bot sits on a marketplace that already sells accounting at fixed prices, so it has a channel and a correction loop. Closest analogue to a Polish "biuro rachunkowe + AI" model.
2. **Legal AI is held by the incumbent publisher** (Wolters Kluwer's Jogtár and Libra), as in Poland (LEX + Libra) and Slovakia (ASPI AI).
3. **Funded Hungarian AI companies sell globally** (Colossyan, Kodesage, DiffuseDrive), not local-law products; a small-language market limits local legal and health products (WK podcast, [Wolters Kluwer HU](https://www.wolterskluwer.com/hu-hu/expert-insights/legaltech-ai-jogi-munka-jovoje-podcast), excerpt).
4. No Hungarian scribe or practice phone agent found; foreign tools (Soniox, Transkriptor) market Hungarian medical transcription.
