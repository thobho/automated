## Patterns (Romania)
1. **Tech-enabled accounting firm wired into national e-invoicing** (Declaro: e-Factura, SPV, SAF-T, Reges; 49 EUR/month; free company formation as funnel). With Bookkeepie (HU) and the R3/R4 AI-native accounting firms, this is the most repeated CEE SMB pattern. Its Polish twin needs KSeF, e-Urząd Skarbowy, JPK, ZUS PUE and S24/CEIDG.
2. **Consumer legal Q&A in three forms**: law-firm chatbot (Lawren.ai), free AI + paid lawyer check (AI+ Avocat), legal procedure automation (QuickLegal). None shows traction yet. The paid-human-check model is the most interesting for Poland because it keeps a licensed professional in the loop.
3. **Romanian consumer AI apps win in the US** (Bible Chat, ~EUR 14.5M revenue in 2025), not at home.
4. **Enterprise agent platforms** (Druid AI, UiPath) are global, not adaptation targets.
