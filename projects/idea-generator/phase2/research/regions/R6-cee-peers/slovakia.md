## Patterns (Slovakia)
1. **Slovak/Czech products treat Poland as the next market**: Nexana.ai is already active there; Sloneek is funding a Polish team. Together with Everbot (CZ) this makes CZ/SK the most direct entry risk for Polish SMB legal/accounting Q&A and HR-agent ideas.
2. **Incumbent legal publishers ship AI first** (Wolters Kluwer's ASPI AI and Praetor AI), so standalone legal AI in SK is thin, the same structure as Poland (Wolters Kluwer LEX, C.H. Beck Legalis).
3. **Local-language moderation** (elv.ai) is a small, publisher-funded niche.
4. Most funded Slovak AI companies are horizontal or deep-tech (Definic, PMcardio, AdSpawn), not local-law champions.
