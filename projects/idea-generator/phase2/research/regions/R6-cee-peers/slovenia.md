## Patterns (Slovenia)
1. **A national exam tutor became a pan-European top-grossing app** (Astra AI). The local start (matura papers, a known maths teacher, a telco bundle) gave it the first users; the product then generalised. Kuba (CZ) follows the same "local teacher → AI tutor" path. For Poland, this is a warning: Astra already sells in Polish, so a matura/E8 tutor must win on CKE-specific content and the Polish teacher-creator channel.
2. **Country-specific medical content scales across CEE**: Mediately keeps per-country drug databases (CZ, IT, HR, PL ...) and now adds an AI assistant on top, so a Polish "ChPL/drug Q&A for doctors" idea already has a CEE player in Poland.
3. **The rest of the scene is global B2B** (Katalist, Codeplain), funded partly by regional VCs (South Central Ventures, Romania's GapMinder).
