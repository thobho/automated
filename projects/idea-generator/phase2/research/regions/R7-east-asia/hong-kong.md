## Patterns (Hong Kong)
1. **Cantonese is the moat** (Fano Labs speech, dictation practice with Cantonese read-aloud): global models are weaker in Cantonese, so local language data matters, as Polish speech and legal language may for Polish products.
2. **Chat-app commerce agents** (Omnichat) follow messaging habits (WhatsApp in HK, LINE in TW). Polish analogue: Messenger/WhatsApp + Allegro/InPost order flows for small shops.
3. **Government LLM for civil servants** (HKGAI/HKPilot), like Pair (SG), Bürokratt (R4), BgGPT (R6).
4. **National curriculum micro-apps** (Education Bureau word list) chart without funding.
