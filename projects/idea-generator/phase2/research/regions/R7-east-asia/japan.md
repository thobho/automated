## Patterns (Japan)
1. **Incumbent-like vertical SaaS adds agents and reaches ¥10bn ARR**: LegalOn (legal) and LayerX (spend management) both claim ~¥10bn ARR; IVRy has 50,000 accounts. The durable path in Japan is a workflow product with a large installed base, then agents.
2. **AI phone answering is a mass SMB product in Japan** (IVRy), like Linca/Lorela in CZ (R6) and fonio (R3): phone-heavy cultures buy it.
3. **Hospital paperwork AI sold on measured time savings** (Ubie), with studies published by customers.
4. **AI-native tax/accounting firm** appears (AIBOS) with the same AI + licensed professional split as Declaro (R6) and Accountable/mika (R3).
5. **English speaking practice** is the top paid consumer AI use; local apps (SpeakBUDDY, Mochispi) hold ranks next to Speak.
