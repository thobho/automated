## Patterns (Korea)
1. **National-data champions add LLMs on top of a monopoly-like data asset**: 삼쩜삼 (tax filings of 24M people), LBox (case law), Law&Company (lawyer marketplace). The LLM is the newest layer, the moat is the data and the distribution.
2. **Marketplace -> AI SaaS for the same professionals**: Law&Company sells SuperLawyer to the lawyers it already lists; 2/3 of Korean lawyers are reachable through one company.
3. **Korean-language notetakers beat global ones in local charts** (daglo, Tiro above Otter/Notta), helped by Korean speech accuracy. Renewal of ~90% (Tiro) is a rare retention figure.
4. **Exam-driven paid AI**: TOEIC Speaking graders, nursing licensing exam AI, admissions AI.
5. **Patient-side medical AI linked to hospital records** (Persly) grows organically among cancer patients.
