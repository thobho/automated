## Patterns (Taiwan)
1. **Anti-fraud is the country-specific LLM use case** with real revenue (Gogolook); scams are a national issue in Taiwan and Thailand. Polish analogue: CERT Polska reports, BLIK and "na wnuczka" scams against seniors.
2. **Legal research incumbent fights a startup in court over data** (Lawsnote vs the legal database owner): data access is the moat and the risk. In Poland, SAOS and court portals are open, which lowers this risk.
3. **Voice-first bookkeeping for informal workers** (taxi drivers) and **voice baby logs** show micro-niche AI apps built by single developers making it into top-grossing lists.
4. Listed software firms (Appier, iKala) rebrand as "agentic"; they sell globally, not adaptation targets.
