## Patterns (Australia)
1. **Clinical documentation is the breakout category**: two scribes (Heidi, Lyrebird) with national GP EMR integrations and global expansion. Heidi's 70% non-AU usage shows a scribe built for one country's GP workflow travels once the EMR layer is rebuilt.
2. **Regulated care admin (NDIS, aged care)** is a distinct niche from clinical scribing: Minikai targets audit-ready notes for support workers. Same driver as Poland's ageing and DPS/home-care staffing gap.
3. **National legal and tax corpora as products** (Lawpath AI, MiAI, Isaacus, SavvyWise): small rounds, but accountants and lawyers invest as users (SavvyWise crowdfunding). Polish equivalent rests on SAOS, ISAP and interpretacje indywidualne.
4. **Migration creates paid consumer demand**: PTE apps and citizenship tests are top-grossing. Poland's migrant population needs B1 Polish certificates for residence/citizenship.
5. **Incorporation funnel + bank partner** (Lawpath + Westpac) is a distribution model worth copying for Polish SMEs.
