Context: Israel builds **global B2B AI companies with Israeli R&D and US go-to-market** (Navina, Hyro, Carefam, Chamelio, Qodo, Tavily all sell mainly in the US). The domestic market (~10M people) is small, so **Israel-specific LLM products are mostly consumer apps and public-interest services**: a state-funded rights chat (Kol Zchut), a ticket-appeal app (LoFrayer), exam-prep and Hebrew homework apps, a WhatsApp money coach (RiseUp). Wonderful is the exception that matters for Poland: an Israeli company whose whole thesis is **local-language customer-service agents for non-English countries**, already claimed in 30+ countries. Israeli startups raised $13.2bn in the first nine months of 2026 per CTech (excerpt), with AI security the largest category (out of scope).

## Patterns (Israel)
1. **Local-language agents as a global category**: Wonderful raised ~$284M in about a year on the thesis that support agents must be tuned per language, culture and regulation and deployed with local teams. This validates the R6 pattern (Born Digital, Linca) and is an **entry-risk signal** for any Polish voice/chat agent idea: check Wonderful's Polish presence in T16–T18.
2. **Healthcare front-office and back-office agents** (Hyro call centres, Navina pre-visit summaries, Carefam hiring): built in Israel, sold in the US. Hyro is the closest analogue to Polish przychodnia phone queues.
3. **Consumer "fight the bureaucracy" apps** (LoFrayer tickets, tax-refund services on success fees, Kol Zchut rights chat): Israeli users pay to beat municipal and tax rules. The Israel Bar Association's reaction to LoFrayer shows the main risk: unauthorised-practice rules. Poland has the same tension (radca prawny/adwokat monopoly on legal representation, while legal information is free to give).
4. **State-funded rights RAG**: Kol Zchut's chat, NSWEduChat (AU) and Pair/HKGAI (R7) show governments funding the "citizen question" layer. A Polish private product should sell to employers, gminy or NGOs rather than compete with a free state chat.
5. **Exam-driven consumer AI** again (Amirnet/psychometric, bar exam, driving theory) as in AU (PTE, citizenship) and R7.
