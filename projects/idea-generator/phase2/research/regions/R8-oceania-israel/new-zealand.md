## Patterns (New Zealand)
1. **Sole-trader tax as a service** (Hnry, Solo): the product owns the tax obligation, not just the ledger. Hnry charges a share of income (1%, unverified) and acts as tax agent (unverified). Polish analogue: JDG on ryczałt or skala, with ZUS and VAT; inFakt/ifirma sell bookkeeping, but nobody "owns" the money flow.
2. **SME software exporters add agents to existing ledgers** (Xero JAX, Re-Leased Credia): AI as a retention layer on a large installed base. Payment follow-up and document chasing are the first agent jobs.
3. **Small-market founders move to the US** (Ivo): NZ origin, US revenue. Not a Poland-specific signal.
4. **Meeting-to-document for operational industries** (Contented): minutes plus health-and-safety and compliance reports for meatworks, airports, dairy. Absorption risk from Copilot/Teams is high; the local template set is the only moat.
