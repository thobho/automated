## Patterns (Saudi Arabia)
1. **Teacher paperwork for a state school system is a paying B2C niche.** Four of the top 30 grossing Education apps (Shawahid #3, Al-Mu'allim #16, Al-Muwajjih #22, Taqrir #29) produce ministry-required evidence reports, counselling records and certificates, and post grades into Noor. With Öğretmen Evrak (TR) this is a 2-country pattern. Only Al-Muwajjih clearly uses an LLM; the others are templates. **That leaves room for an LLM-native version.** The Polish equivalent: documentation for ocena pracy nauczyciela and awans zawodowy, IPET/WOPFU, dziennik pedagoga, uzasadnienia ocen, plus e-dziennik exports. Polish teachers number ~700k (check in T16).
2. **Arabic speech and CX agents attract the most VC** (Lucidya USD 30M, Intella USD 12.5M, Wittify, Sawt, Velents): the dialect is the moat. The Polish analogue (Polish ASR for call centres) is real but not a solo-founder play.
3. **A national legal-AI wave, as in Turkey** (Mohami, Aqood, Malakah, Clauze): all pre-seed or seed, on Saudi law. Mohami's prepaid membership model is worth noting for Polish SMEs.
4. **Vertical POS with agents** (TabSense, 1,000+ restaurants in about a year via telco partners): the agent sits inside the system of record.
5. **National exam prep** (Mujtahid for Qiyas) repeats R8 (PTE, psychometric) and R7.
