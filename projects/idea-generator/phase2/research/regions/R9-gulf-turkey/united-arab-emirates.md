Context: UAE AI money is dominated by **state-linked sovereign AI** (G42, AI71, MGX). Startup rounds are mostly pre-seed to Series A, and many "UAE" startups are regional HQs of Egyptian, Jordanian or US teams. The [Wamda AI tag page](https://www.wamda.com/en/tag/AI) (read) listed 10 recent MENA AI rounds; only 2 were UAE-based (Ledgers USD 250k, AqlanX USD 10M from its parent group). Local champions solving a UAE-specific problem with LLMs are few. The clearest ones are the state's own TAMM agent and payroll/HR (Bayzat) built on WPS rules.

## Patterns (UAE)
1. **Arabic-language infrastructure gets the money** (CNTXT USD 60M, Arabic.AI, AqlanX), not vertical apps. Same as Bielik/PLLuM in Poland. The founder lesson: a language model is not a moat for an app builder.
2. **Legal AI for local law is appearing** (Qanooni, Legaline, Juriqa, oqood): all early. Legaline's mix of AI-scoped tasks and lawyer bidding for multilingual expats is a model for migrant legal help in Poland.
3. **Real-estate agency automation** (Coraly listings and leads, Prop-AI investor analytics) on top of portals. Repeats R8 Agentsy and R1 patterns.
4. **The state builds the citizen agent** (TAMM AutoGov). In Poland, mObywatel plays this role, so B2C govtech is closed to a solo founder; B2B deadline agents remain open.
