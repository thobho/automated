# T03 — Poland baseline: AI landscape and local moats

**Model:** opus (medium effort) · **Needs:** T01

Read `projects/idea-generator/phase2/BRIEF.md` first and follow its Rules. Commit and push after every small unit and keep your Resume line in `phase2/PROGRESS.md` current. Run the link check on changed files before reporting DONE.

Write two files.

`research/poland/ai-landscape.md`:
- AI and LLM adoption in Polish firms and households (Eurostat ICT surveys, GUS, PARP, PwC/Deloitte/EY surveys);
- Polish AI startups: at least 50, each with what it does, its customers and its stage (sources: Startup Poland reports, PFR Ventures and other VC portfolios, startup media, Crunchbase/Dealroom pages);
- Polish-language model quality (Bielik, PLLuM, benchmarks) and how well leading models handle Polish;
- which big Western AI products officially support Polish, and where they fall short;
- regulation: AI Act timeline and how Poland implements it, RODO practice, sector rules.

`research/poland/local-moats.md`: an inventory of Polish-specific building blocks a foreign product would struggle with. For each item: what it is, access (API, scraping, manual), cost, who needs it, and a link. Cover public systems and registries (KSeF, JPK, ZUS PUE/eZUS, KRS, CEIDG, GUS BIR/REGON, ePUAP, mObywatel, Profil Zaufany, NFZ/IKP/P1, e-Dziennik Librus/Vulcan, EKW księgi wieczyste, Geoportal, SAOS court rulings, ISAP legal acts, BIP), payment and commerce rails (BLIK, Przelewy24, PayU, Allegro, InPost, Ceneo), leading domestic software (Comarch, Symfonia, enova, Insert, Librus, Booksy, Znanylekarz) and anything else you find.
