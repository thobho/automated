## Most promising item groups
1. **Electronics and structural parts for discontinued or mid-age appliances (washer modules, drum spiders, hood boards):** 0 Ceneo results in every case, the forum answer is "buy a donor machine" (items 1–4). A fit-checked used-parts route (part number from the label, donor stock from repairers) is the clearest gap in V2.
2. **Heat-pump and AC parts for distributor brands (Kaisai):** neither parts nor remotes visible online; only the distributor (items 5, 7). Fit requires the unit's model and remote code. B2B angle: installers.
3. **Fridge door gaskets by model:** findable only by part number, no model lookup (item 15).
4. **Recurring consumables (Samsung, Xiaomi, MVHR filters):** easy to find, the open question is original-vs-clone trust and reminders (items 8, 16, 17): a replenishment, not a findability, play.

Not promising on this evidence: kitchen-robot and grinder parts (Lidlomix, Thermomix, Zelmer), Parkside and Dyson batteries, gas-cooker thermocouples, Defro controllers: **found easily** on Ceneo.
