T10 of Phase 3 ([task](../tasks/10-findability-test.md), [brief](../BRIEF.md)). Items from [demand/V3-hobby-niches.md](../demand/V3-hobby-niches.md) (IDs in brackets). All queries run by hand on **2026-11-06**.

## Method and limits
Same as [V1](V1-vehicle-parts.md#method-and-limits-same-for-v1v5): Allegro returned 403 and was **not tested**; "web" = the WebSearch tool in Polish (not google.pl); Ceneo and OLX public search pages read once per query; delivery times are **not shown** on these listing pages; at most 3 tries per item. Verdicts: **E** easy, **W** with effort, **N** not found / foreign only. Live animals (bees, shrimp) are not on Ceneo, so OLX was used for them.

## Most promising item groups
1. **Parts for imported used Dutch e-bikes (Gazelle, Sparta, Batavus):** no new batteries on Ceneo, used batteries of unknown health on OLX, a visible trade in imported bikes (item 9). Fit and battery health checking is the friction; shipping lithium batteries is a regulatory point.
2. **Fishing-reel and sewing-machine spare parts by part code (Shimano RD codes, Łucznik 4xx):** 0 Ceneo results, private sellers only, fit not stated (items 7, 8). A part-code lookup from the exploded diagram is the missing step.
3. **Single LEGO parts in Polish/PLN with local shipping:** demand exists (T09), supply sits on foreign-run Brick Owl/BrickLink and used ads (item 2).
4. **Live stock logistics (shrimp, queen bees):** easy to find, hard to ship or time (items 11, 14): a logistics, not findability, gap.

Not promising on this evidence: gunpla (named kits), keyboard switches, drones, valves, lures, yarn, 3D-printer parts, outboard impellers: **found easily**. Ham-radio and Unitra parts are findable but each has only 1–2 shops.
