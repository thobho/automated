# V4 — Services with a logistics step: findability test

T10 of Phase 3 ([task](../tasks/10-findability-test.md), [brief](../BRIEF.md)). Items from [demand/V4-services-with-logistics.md](../demand/V4-services-with-logistics.md) (IDs in brackets). All queries run by hand on **2026-11-06**.

## Method and limits
Same tools as [V1](V1-vehicle-parts.md#method-and-limits-same-for-v1v5) (Allegro 403, not tested; "web" = WebSearch tool in Polish, not google.pl; OLX public search). Ceneo does not list services, so V4 uses **web + OLX**. For a service, "found" means a **named Polish provider that offers this service** (ideally by post, since the buyer may not live in a big city), with a price or a way to order. Price guides without a provider count as price information only. "Delivery time" = turnaround, recorded where a source stated it. Fixly and Booksy were not searched. Web-search answers are summaries of the result pages; providers named below were seen in result titles or snippets, not visited.

Verdicts: **E** a provider found in 1 try, **W** 2–3 tries or only a single classified / brand channel / anecdote, **N** no Polish provider seen, or foreign only.

## Items

### 1. Garmin watch repair (V4-D01)
- Try 1, web `naprawa zegarka Garmin serwis wysyłkowy cena`: no provider, only general smartwatch-repair guides and a Garmin forum post asking where to repair in Europe.
- Try 2, OLX [`naprawa garmin`](https://www.olx.pl/oferty/q-naprawa-garmin/): 118 listings, mostly boats and navigators; **one** service "Wymiana baterii Garmin Instinct Fenix Venu Vivoactive Forerunner" (Łódź), no price, no shipping mentioned.

### 2. Battery replacement, Huawei Watch GT 2 (V4-D01)

## Most promising item groups
1. **Brand electronics repair after warranty (Garmin, Huawei watches, Roborock, Thermomix, Canon):** the only routes are the maker's channel (no prices) or a single small independent; no mail-in network (items 1, 2, 6, 8, 11). This is the densest T09 cluster and the test confirms the gap.
2. **Outdoor and technical clothing repair (down jackets):** no provider in two tries (item 4); a mail-in workshop would face little visible competition. Brand partnerships (outdoor shops) are the obvious channel.
3. **Car electronics modules by post (Bose/Harman amplifiers):** foreign-only repair; Poland's answer is a used module (item 10): a "send the module, get it back repaired" service fits the logistics step.
4. **E-bike battery cell replacement:** demand (T09) plus thin supply (item 13); the lithium-shipping rules decide whether a mail-in model is possible.
5. **Vintage audio and instrument repair (Unitra, brass):** single providers found by chance (items 9, 15); a directory plus courier pick-up could aggregate them.

Not promising on this evidence: PS5 pad repair (~30 OLX providers, some by post), AC installation pricing (well covered), tailoring price lists (kb.pl). Suitcase and pram repair have no providers, but T09 demand was weak (AC 10 and 4 suggestions) and DIY parts exist.
