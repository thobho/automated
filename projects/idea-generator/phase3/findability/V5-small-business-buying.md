# V5 — Small business and professional buying: findability test

T10 of Phase 3 ([task](../tasks/10-findability-test.md), [brief](../BRIEF.md)). Items from [demand/V5-small-business-buying.md](../demand/V5-small-business-buying.md) (IDs in brackets). All queries run by hand on **2026-11-06**.

## Method and limits
Same as [V1](V1-vehicle-parts.md#method-and-limits-same-for-v1v5): Allegro returned 403 and was **not tested**; "web" = the WebSearch tool in Polish (not google.pl); Ceneo public search; delivery times are **not shown** on these listing pages; at most 3 tries per item. Professional buyers mostly order from wholesalers by phone or B2B portals that are not indexed in Ceneo, so a "not found" here means **not findable by a buyer searching online**, not that no supplier exists. Verdicts: **E** easy, **W** with effort, **N** not found / foreign only.

## Most promising item groups
1. **Catering-equipment spare parts (Unox, Carpigiani, combi ovens):** no Polish online seller seen, only US/UK/Saudi shops (items 8, 9); restaurants lose money during downtime, which supports a fee or margin. Fits the V5 scan's B2B ordering idea.
2. **Parts for orphan or Chinese construction machinery (Kingway, KM 251, Balkancar, RAK):** 0 Ceneo results, private sellers or nothing (items 1–4). Same orphan-brand pattern as V1 and V2.
3. **Industrial sewing-machine parts (Juki):** whole machines are listed, parts are not (item 11); the Łódź/Pabianice textile cluster (T09) is a reachable first market.
4. **Hydraulic seals and wear parts by profile and size:** the size is findable, the correct profile is not (item 12): a classification problem that AI matching fits.
5. **Agricultural wear parts by type rather than brand (cultivator tines):** fit by dimension (item 17); old Polish machines with OE numbers (Ursus, Anna) are already well served (items 6, 7).

Not promising on this evidence: Ursus/Anna parts, V-belts, Kärcher and Wacker wear parts, online hairdressing wholesalers: **found easily**. Dental and fiscal-device parts are gated by authorised service and regulation.
