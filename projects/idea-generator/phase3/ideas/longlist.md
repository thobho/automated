# Phase 3 longlist: ideas for removing friction in buying

T11 of Phase 3 ([task](../tasks/11-longlist.md), [brief](../BRIEF.md)). One line per idea. Built only from files already on the branch: friction patterns (T03, [friction-patterns.md](../research/global/friction-patterns.md)), vertical scans and catalogues (T04–T08, `research/verticals/`, `catalogue/`), demand signals (T09, `demand/`) and the findability test (T10, `findability/`). No new web research was done for this file; every fact sits in the linked rows. 2026-11-06.

## How to read a line

- **Theme:** a tag for grouping write-ups (list at the end).
- **Friction:** the brief's six types: finding, fit, trust, logistics, coord (coordination), price (price discovery).
- **Model:** likely main model first, then others that fit: commission (incl. affiliate), margin (resale, supplier ships), fee (service fee), subscription, lead fee. A guess for T12 to test, not a finding.
- **Analogue:** the closest business from the catalogues (row id links to the catalogue file), with its T03 pattern id ([P1–P10](../research/global/friction-patterns.md#2-typology-of-winning-patterns)).
- **T10:** findability verdict from T10 for the closest tested item: **E** found easily, **W** found with effort, **N** not found / foreign only. "not tested" means T10 had no item for it (most service, B2B-workflow and data ideas); the reason is given. T10 did not test Allegro (HTTP 403), so gaps are likely overstated ([method](../findability/V1-vehicle-parts.md#method-and-limits-same-for-v1v5)).
- **PL supply / data:** Polish suppliers, distributors or data sources named in the research files. "unverified" means none was named or checked; nobody has been contacted.
- **Owner:** ideas inside the areas the owner suggested: **car** (car parts), **HVAC** (air-conditioning, heat-pump and ventilation parts), **hobby** (hobby niches). Blank = found by the research.
- **Evidence:** demand signal (T09 candidate row) and scan section with the analogue check.
