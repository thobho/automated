# 002 — Photo-based body-damage quote routed to partner body shops
**One-liner:** A driver with a dent or scratch uploads 4–6 photos; an AI model sizes the damage and a price range, and up to three nearby partner body shops send a binding quote within 24 hours.
**Vertical:** V1 | **Model:** lead fee paid by the body shop (main); success commission on a completed job (alternative) | **B2C** (B2B small fleets as a fallback)
**Friction removed:** price discovery (quotes for the same damage can differ by up to 100%) and coordination (driving to 2–3 shops for an inspection, phone calls). Today the driver phones or visits shops, asks on Facebook, or takes the insurer's partner network.
**Analogues:** Fixico (Netherlands, founded 2014): consumers uploaded damage photos and compared body-shop offers; 350+ shops within months, EUR 1 m of repairs in its first year; in 2016 it rebuilt the platform for fleet, rental and insurance customers; 3,000+ certified body shops and 200+ business partners in 10 countries by 2022; AI damage detection from photos launched 2024; US launch with Caliber in 2026 ([Fixico, our story](https://fixico.com/company/our-story), accessed 2026-11-06). Funding EUR 7 m Series A 2019, EUR 14.3 m total ([tech.eu](https://tech.eu/brief/amsterdam-based-car-repair-startup-fixico-lands-e7-million/), accessed 2026-11-06; [catalogue V1-NL-001](../../catalogue/V1-vehicle-parts.csv)). Revenue: third-party ranges only (unverified). Mazda UK runs a manufacturer photo-quote service through its repair centres ([Inside Mazda](https://www.insidemazda.co.uk/?p=11891), accessed 2026-11-06, via search summary). **Reading:** the consumer version was where Fixico started, not where it found its business.
**Findability gap in Poland:** not run in T10 (a service, not a product). T13 queries, 2026-11-06: `wycena naprawy blacharskiej ze zdjęcia online porównanie warsztatów lakierniczych` and `"wycena ze zdjęć" blacharz lakiernik serwis online wyślij zdjęcie szkody kilka warsztatów oferty`: no Polish service sending one set of photos to several shops; found price guides (cenauslug.pl, kb.pl), single-shop pages, job ads for body-shop estimators, and a forum warning that one shop's photo estimate ended up in an online vehicle-history report ([e-prawnik forum](https://e-prawnik.pl/forum/wycena-naprawy-samochodu-problem.html), accessed 2026-11-06). V1 scan §6 found the same ([V1 scan §6 #5](../../research/verticals/V1-vehicle-parts.md#6-ten-most-promising-analogues-for-poland-quick-polish-check)). Result: **gap exists for the multi-shop photo quote**; finding *a* body shop is easy (Panorama Firm, Google Maps).
**Supply side:** body shops do the work and are the paying side. Named candidates: (1) **Q Service Castrol** (Inter Cars + Castrol network, 500 workshops in 2022; general workshops, body-shop share unknown) ([Auto Świat](https://www.auto-swiat.pl/wiadomosci/wiadomosci-branzowe/siec-q-service-castrol-ma-juz-500-warsztatow/9f5lcnl), accessed 2026-11-06); (2) **Bosch Car Service** (body and paint at selected sites) ([Bosch Car Service fleet leaflet](https://www.boschcarservice.com/pl/media/images/16_9/about_bcs/bosch_car_service-obsuga_flot_samochodowych.pdf), accessed 2026-11-06); (3) **O.K. Serwis** (Inter-Team network of independent workshops) ([Inter-Team presentation](https://inter-team.com.pl/files/Rozne%20do%20newsow/Nowoczesny_Warsztat_OK_Serwis2.pdf), accessed 2026-11-06). Realistic first partners are single independent body shops in one city, signed one by one. Willingness to pay per lead: unverified; none contacted.
**Data:** photos from the driver (personal data: plates, location; must be blurred or consented). Price ranges from public guides (e.g. average rear-wing repair PLN 980 from 8 offers in 2026 ([cenauslug.pl](https://cenauslug.pl/przewodnik/naprawy-blacharskie-i-powypadkowe), accessed 2026-11-06)) and from shop quotes collected on the platform, which become the proprietary dataset. Professional estimating systems (Audatex, DAT) are licensed and not needed for a range.
**Polish competition:** insurers' partner networks for cashless repair (bezgotówkowa) take the insured claims ([otomoto, sieć partnerska](https://www.otomoto.pl/news/siec-partnerska-czy-warto-korzystac-z-takiego-warsztatu-przy-naprawie-z-oc), accessed 2026-11-06); Oferteo and Fixly sell general service leads (Oferteo packages PLN 259–7,199 net ([monetisation M13](../../research/poland/monetisation.md))); price-list sites (kb.pl, cenauslug.pl); Dobry Mechanik and similar workshop directories (body-shop coverage unverified). They leave a gap because none takes photos and returns comparable binding quotes.
**Demand evidence:** weak. Fleet size: about 21.1 m passenger cars registered at end-2025 after removing archived records (IBRM Samar, via [otomoto](https://www.otomoto.pl/news/samochody-na-polskich-drogach-ile), accessed 2026-11-06, search summary). Share with small self-paid damage per year: unverified. T09 did not mine body-damage phrases. The victim of a collision may choose a cash payout from the at-fault driver's OC instead of a cashless repair ([mubi](https://mubi.pl/poradniki/likwidacja-szkody-gotowkowa-a-bezgotowkowa/), accessed 2026-11-06), which creates buyers who want the cheapest good repair; their number is unverified.
**How AI is used:** classify the damage (panel, dent vs. scratch vs. crack, size) from photos, check photo quality, give a price range, route to shops by distance and specialism, blur plates. Cost per request: under PLN 1 in model calls (estimate). The shop still prices the job, so AI saves the inspection trip rather than replacing the estimator.
**Physical work:** none (the shop repairs the car; the driver drives there). By hand: signing and supporting shops (phone), handling disputes.

| Requests / month | Physical work | Shop onboarding and support (phone) | Driver disputes | Total h / month |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | 0 h | 6 h (sign the first 5 shops) | 0.5 h | 6.5 h |
| 50 | 0 h | 5 h | 2 h | 7 h |
| 200 | 0 h | 8 h | 6 h | 14 h (non-physical) |

(estimates)

**MVP (≤6 weeks):** one city (e.g. Warsaw or Wrocław), 5–8 independent body shops, a mobile web form with guided photos, AI damage summary and range, quotes sent to shops by e-mail/SMS with a reply link; the driver sees quotes side by side and books by phone. Lead fee charged by invoice monthly.
**Monetisation:** lead fee of PLN 25 per quote request a shop chooses to answer (estimate, benchmarked on Oferteo/Fixly pay-per-response, [monetisation M13–M14](../../research/poland/monetisation.md)); alternative 8% of the completed job, hard to track. **Unit economics per driver request (fees, estimate):**

| Item | PLN | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Lead fees: 2 shops answer × PLN 25 | 50 | estimate |
| AI and SMS cost | −2 | estimate |
| Support 10 min at PLN 60/h | −10 | estimate |
| Payment/invoice cost (monthly invoice per shop, transfer) | −1 | estimate |
| CAC per request: Google Ads CPC ~PLN 2, 8% click-to-request | −25 | estimate; CPC unverified |
| **Fee income after costs per request** | **12** | |

10,000 PLN monthly fees after costs needs ~830 requests a month (10,000 / 12), i.e. ~1,660 paid shop answers; at an assumed 30% booking rate shops would pay PLN 25 × 2 / 0.3 ≈ PLN 170 of fees per won job, against a PLN 980 average job: possible but a hard sell (estimate). Figures are **fees**, not turnover. Group: 21.1 m cars, so the 20× check (16,600 requests) is plausible on fleet size, but the self-paid share is unsourced.
**Distribution, first 100 buyers:** Google Ads and SEO on `lakierowanie zderzaka cena [miasto]`, `naprawa wgniecenia cena`; local Facebook city groups; the partner shops' own customers. First 100 requests in one city (estimate ~PLN 2,500 of ads at the CAC above, above the monthly budget, so organic pages must carry part).
**Regulation:** intermediary only; the body shop is the contractor and gives the warranty. RODO: photos with plates and location are personal data; consent text and retention limits needed; the forum case shows drivers fear estimates leaking into vehicle-history databases, so promise not to share with history services. Consumer rights for the repair sit with the shop. Unfair-competition risk if the AI range is presented as a binding price: label it as a range.
**Opportunities (szanse):**
1. Drivers who took a cash payout from the at-fault OC want the cheapest good repair, a ready-made buyer with money in hand. Likelihood M, impact H (6, major). True if: ≥20% of early requests mention an OC cash payout ([mubi](https://mubi.pl/poradniki/likwidacja-szkody-gotowkowa-a-bezgotowkowa/) shows the option exists; share unknown).
2. Body shops fill quiet weeks with small cash jobs and accept a per-lead fee. M / M (4). True if: 5 shops sign in the first month.
3. Small fleets (10–50 cars, firms without a fleet manager) take the same flow as a subscription, Fixico's path. L / M (2). True if: 2 firms ask for an account within 3 months.
4. The quote dataset becomes a price index that shops or media use. L / L (1).
**O = 13.**
**Risks (ryzyka):**
1. The consumer model does not hold: Fixico, the direct analogue, moved to fleets and insurers two years after launch. Likelihood H, impact H (9). Early sign: <30 requests a month after PLN 300 of ads. Mitigation in the MVP: the validation test also offers the flow to small fleets.
2. Insured damage goes through insurers' partner networks, leaving few self-paid jobs. M / H (6). Early sign: most enquiries ask "can you do it from OC?".
3. Body shops refuse to quote from photos or to pay per lead. M / H (6). Early sign: fewer than 5 of 15 shops phoned agree.
4. Drivers and shops take the deal off-platform after the first contact, so a success fee is uncollectable and lead fees feel unfair. H / M (6). Early sign: shops ask to stop paying after a month.
**R = 27.**
**Success signals:** Fixico shows durable signals only on the B2B side (expansion to 10 countries, 200+ business partners, signal 4) and none for consumers; no repeat rate is published. Hype warning: the consumer launch did not last (pivot in 2016). A Polish version can reproduce signal 1 (replaces trips for inspections) and possibly signal 5 (SEO on city price phrases); repeat purchase (signal 3) is weak because drivers have damage rarely.
**Graveyard:** Fixico's consumer marketplace (pivoted to B2B 2016, see above). Fixly (OLX services marketplace) reportedly agreed to be sold in 2025 (unverified, [monetisation M14](../../research/poland/monetisation.md)). Pay-per-lead service marketplaces in Poland survive on many categories, not one.
**Scores (1–5):** F1 3 (price discovery and trips; a shop is easy to find) · F2 3 (21 m cars sourced; self-paid damage frequency unknown; no T09 signals) · F3 4 (shops do all physical work) · F4 2 (~PLN 12 per request after CAC, 830 requests a month needed; estimates) · F5 3 (photo triage is real but the shop still prices) · F6 3 (no multi-shop photo quote; insurers and lead marketplaces nearby) · F7 3 (RODO on photos and plates) · F8 3 (shop sign-up is slow phone work) · F9 2 (shop list is copyable; quote data compounds slowly) | **Weighted:** 2.9 / 5 (2.88) | **Chance band:** `<5%`. Score gives `5–15%`; O 13, R 27, R − O = 14 → down one band (the H×H risk has a mitigation in the test, so no second step). The only direct analogue left this consumer model, and the fee per request after acquisition is thin.
**Cheapest validation test (≤2 weeks, ≤300 PLN):** phone 15 body shops in one city: will they answer photo requests and pay PLN 25 per answered request (0 PLN). Landing page "Wyślij zdjęcia szkody, dostaniesz 3 wyceny w 24 h" with PLN 250 of Google Ads on 20 local price phrases; forward photos by hand to the shops that agreed. In parallel e-mail 20 small firms with 5–30 cars offering the same flow free for a month. Measure requests, shop answer rate, bookings.
**Kill criteria:** fewer than 5 of 15 shops agree to pay; fewer than 15 requests from PLN 250 of ads; or fewer than 3 bookings from the first 20 requests.
**Confidence:** low (no Polish demand data; shop willingness untested).
