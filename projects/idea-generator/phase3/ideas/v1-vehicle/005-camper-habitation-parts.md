# 005 — Camper habitation parts by body brand, sourced from German suppliers and dealers
**One-liner:** A Polish-language parts desk for motorhome and caravan owners: give the body brand, model year and a photo (or the window label), get the exact seal, window, lock or panel with fit confirmed, shipped from a German wholesaler or brand dealer.
**Vertical:** V1 | **Model:** margin on resale (main); sourcing fee for dealer-only parts (secondary) | **B2C** (rental fleets as B2B later)
**Friction removed:** finding (part numbers sit in the body maker's dealer portal, not in shops) and fit (windows and seals fit by body brand, model, year and size). Today the owner searches OLX and sprzedajemy.pl, phones a Polish dealer, or orders from German shops in German.
**Analogues:** no direct parts-desk analogue in the catalogue; the pattern is a model-number parts shop (P1), e.g. PartSelect ([catalogue V2-US-001](../../catalogue/V2-home-climate-appliance.csv)). Supply-side players: Frankana Freiko (Germany), "Europe's leading wholesaler for camping and leisure accessories", supplying more than 3,000 specialist retailers, with a B2B login for dealers and its own consumer shops in DE/AT/NL/CH ([Frankana, about us](https://frankana.de/en/aboutus), accessed 2026-11-06); Reimo (Germany) runs a Polish shop with "over 15,000" camping accessories in stock ([Reimo PL, akcesoria kempingowe](https://www.reimo.com/pl/akcesoria-kempingowe/), accessed 2026-11-06, via search summary). Knaus Tabbert sells original parts to dealers through a dealer and aftersales portal (CAPP shop) ([Knaus Tabbert partners](https://knaustabbert.de/en/partners/), accessed 2026-11-06, via search summary). Repeat rate, margin and return rate: not published by any of them.
**Findability gap in Poland:** T10 (2026-11-06): Knaus entrance-door seal: web `uszczelka drzwi wejściowych Knaus kamper części sklep` and OLX `uszczelka drzwi knaus`: none seen, **N** ([V1 #7](../../findability/V1-vehicle-parts.md#7-entrance-door-seal-knaus-camper-v1-d07)); Hymer window: OLX `hymer okno`: most windows from **one seller in Borkowo** (Dometic S4/S7P, Polyplastic, 600–1,690 zł), fit by size and brand list, **W** ([V1 #17](../../findability/V1-vehicle-parts.md#17-window-glass-for-a-hymer-camper-v1-d07)); Niewiadów N126 window **W** (classifieds; maker shop by phone) ([V1 #8](../../findability/V1-vehicle-parts.md#8-window-niewiadów-n126-caravan-v1-d08)). T13 query `sklep części do kamperów i przyczep kempingowych Polska Knaus Hymer części zamienne nadwozie`: Reimo PL (accessories, not body parts), HYMER Poznań (dealer), classified ads; no Polish shop for brand-specific body parts. Allegro not tested (403), so the gap may be overstated.
**Supply side:** (1) **Frankana Freiko** (wholesale, B2B dealer accounts; Polish dealer terms and dropship unverified). (2) **Reimo** (retail and trade; already sells in Polish; reseller terms unverified). (3) **Knaus Tabbert / Hymer dealers** for original body parts: Knaus Tabbert dealer search ([Knaus Tabbert dealer search](https://www.knaustabbert.de/en/customer/dealer-search/), accessed 2026-11-06), HYMER original parts via dealers ([Hymer service and accessories](https://www.hymer.com/en/service-and-accessories/), accessed 2026-11-06); Polish dealer HYMER Poznań ([aplikuj.pl company profile](https://www.aplikuj.pl/pracodawca/364448/hymer-poznan-spolka-z-ograniczona-odpowiedzialnoscia/opinie), accessed 2026-11-06, via search summary). Will they work with a newcomer? Wholesalers serve "specialist retailers", so a registered Polish shop may qualify; brand dealers sell at retail and have no reason to give a reseller margin. None contacted.
**Data:** OEM part numbers live in dealer portals (closed). Usable sources: wholesaler catalogues (with an account), window makers' size tables (Dometic, Polyplastic labels), owner photos and the platform's own fit records. Scraping dealer portals is not allowed; build the fit table from confirmed orders.
**Polish competition:** Reimo PL (accessories, in Polish), one dominant OLX seller for windows, brand dealers (HYMER Poznań and others), German shops shipping to Poland (Fritz Berger, Frankana's consumer shops do not list Poland), private ads on sprzedajemy.pl. The gap: brand-specific body parts with confirmed fit, in Polish, without phoning a dealer.
**Demand evidence:** Google autocomplete `części do kampera` gives suggestions by body brand (Knaus, Rimor, Dethleffs, Hymer, Bürstner, LMC) ([V1 demand S10](../../demand/V1-vehicle-parts.md)). Market flow: PZPM/SPGC describe 2025 as a record year for camper and caravan registrations (search summary of the 2025 report; the PDF link returned 404 in the T13 link check, so the ~14,962 figure and its scope are **unverified**); Bankier reported Q2 and H1 2025 as the highest camper registrations in history ([Bankier, rynek kamperów z rekordowym wynikiem](https://www.bankier.pl/wiadomosc/Wakacje-na-czterech-kolach-w-cenie-Rynek-kamperow-z-rekordowym-wynikiem-8993664.html), accessed 2026-11-06, via search summary); IBRM Samar: 2,714 campers registered January–May 2026 (934 new, 1,780 used imports), +16.3% y/y ([Bankier, Q1 2026 record](https://www.bankier.pl/wiadomosc/Kampery-w-Polsce-Rekord-rejestracji-w-I-kw-2026-r-Ranking-modeli-9151858.html), accessed 2026-11-06, via search summary). Total fleet in Poland: unverified. Used imports dominate, so many owners have no Polish dealer relationship (inference).
**How AI is used:** identify body brand, model and year from photos (badges, window frames, door shape), read window labels (maker, type, size), map to wholesaler part numbers, draft German supplier e-mails. Cost per order under PLN 1 (estimate). Real edge only once the fit table from orders exists.
**Physical work:** none with goods if suppliers ship to the buyer; by hand: supplier e-mails/calls in German, damage claims on glass.

**MVP (≤6 weeks):** Polish site with a "find my part" form (brand, model, year, photos), 300 SEO pages for the most common parts (seals, windows, locks, skylights) by body brand, quotes by hand within 24 h from one wholesaler account and one German dealer; BLIK payment; seasonal launch in March.

10,000 PLN monthly gross profit needs ~200 orders a month (10,000 / 50), mostly in March–September. Figures are **gross profit**, not turnover (turnover ≈ PLN 140,000/month at 200 orders). The 20× check (4,000 buyers a month) cannot be shown: total fleet unsourced and parts purchases are rare per owner.
**Distribution, first 100 buyers:** SEO on `uszczelka drzwi knaus`, `okno do kampera hymer`, `części do kampera [brand]`; Polish camper Facebook groups and forums (names and sizes unverified); camper rental companies; spring caravan fairs.
**Regulation:** consumer sale with 14-day withdrawal ([logistics L13](../../research/poland/logistics-and-returns.md)) and rękojmia; the shop is the seller of record for imported goods (GPSR information from the EU supplier). Windows on motorhomes need E-marking for road use (flag, unverified per part). Gas parts (regulators, hoses) excluded from the MVP. Intra-EU purchase: VAT on resale; WEEE for electrical parts.
**Opportunities (szanse):**
1. The camper market grows at record pace (record 2025 registrations, +16% in early 2026), mostly used imports without a Polish dealer. Likelihood H, impact M (6, major, sourced: Samar and PZPM via Bankier). True if: 2026 full-year registrations again at a record.
2. High ticket: one window or seal-set order leaves PLN 50–150 gross profit, so ~100–200 orders a month suffice. M / M (4). True if: average order above PLN 600 in the first 20 orders.
3. Camper rental firms become repeat B2B buyers between seasons. L / M (2). True if: 2 rental firms order twice within the first season.
4. The same desk serves CZ/SK owners of the same German bodies. L / L (1).
**O = 13.**
**Risks (ryzyka):**
1. Brand-specific body parts are dealer-only and come at retail price, so there is no margin on the parts that are actually hard to find. Likelihood M, impact H (6). Early sign: the first German dealer quotes retail only.
2. Small, seasonal market: orders cluster in spring, and the total owner base is too small for 200 orders a month. H / M (6). Early sign: fewer than 20 enquiries in March–April from SEO and groups.
3. Oversized and fragile goods (windows, panels) shipped cross-border: breakage, high freight, returns borne by the shop. M / M (4). Early sign: one broken window in the first 10 shipments.
4. Reimo PL or the dominant OLX seller extends its range to body parts. M / M (4). Early sign: Reimo PL adds a "części do nadwozia" category.
**R = 20.**
**Success signals:** wholesalers (Frankana 3,000+ dealers) show steady specialist trade (signal 2 for dealers, unquantified); no repeat or margin figures (durability unproven). Hype warning: none found. A Polish version can reproduce signal 1 (replaces dealer phone calls and German shopping) and 5 (SEO on brand-part phrases); repeat purchase (signal 3) is weak.
**Graveyard:** no camper-parts marketplace closure found (one query, not exhaustive). The nearest lesson is eSpares (UK parts retail) going into administration in 2023 ([Kingfisher](https://www.kingfisher.com/media/news/2023/screwfix-acquires-assets-of-connect-distribution-services-limite), accessed 2026-11-06).
**Scores (1–5):** F1 4 (T10 N for the Knaus seal, W for windows with fit by size and brand; dealer phone calls) · F2 2 (was 3: record registrations sourced, but the group is small and the purchase rare and seasonal) · F3 3 (supplier shipping to buyers unverified; oversized glass) · F4 3 (PLN 50 per order after costs, ~200 orders/month needed; estimates) · F5 3 (photo-to-body identification helps; table must be built) · F6 3 (Reimo PL accessories, one OLX seller, dealers; body parts uncovered) · F7 3 (consumer sale, import, E-marking flag) · F8 3 (supplier work in German grows with orders) · F9 3 (fit table by body model compounds slowly) | **Weighted:** 3.0 / 5 (3.00) | **Chance band:** `<5%`. Score gives `5–15%`; O 13, R 20, R − O = 7 → down one band. The friction is real, but the hardest parts sit with brand dealers at retail price and the owner base is small and seasonal.
**Cheapest validation test (≤2 weeks, ≤300 PLN):** e-mail Frankana Freiko, Reimo and two German Knaus/Hymer dealers for trade terms and direct-to-buyer shipping to Poland (0 PLN). Post a free "find your part" offer in 3 Polish camper groups and run PLN 200 of Google Ads on 20 brand-part phrases; quote every request by hand. Measure requests, share of dealer-only parts, quoted margin, conversion.
**Kill criteria:** no supplier ships to Polish buyers at ≥20% margin; or fewer than 15 part requests in 2 weeks in season; or more than half of requested parts are dealer-only at retail.
**Confidence:** low (supplier terms, fleet size and Allegro coverage are unverified).
