# 008 — Collision-part sourcing desk for Chinese brands
**One-liner:** An owner or a body shop repairing an Omoda, Jaecoo, BAIC, BYD or MG after a collision sends the VIN and photos; the desk finds the fastest route for each part (importer warehouse, aftermarket wholesaler, a used part from a dismantler, an export seller in China), quotes a price and a date, and orders it; paid by a sourcing fee plus a margin or commission on the part.
**Vertical:** V1 | **Model:** service fee per sourcing case (PLN 99, estimate) + commission or margin on parts not from the dealer | **B2C and B2B (independent body shops)**
**Friction removed:** finding and logistics: body parts for new Chinese brands are not in aftermarket catalogues, so the owner waits for the dealer (ASO) and has no way to check another route. Today: wait for the dealer, post in brand Facebook groups, search OLX for used parts, or order from Chinese export platforms.
**Analogues:** **Ovoko** (Lithuania, ovoko.pl): used-parts marketplace for dismantlers, EUR 20 m Series B (Sep 2024), over 1 m parts sold, ~6 m monthly visitors, 17 countries ([catalogue V1-LT-001](../../catalogue/V1-vehicle-parts.csv); [Vestbee](https://www.vestbee.com/blog/articles/ovoko-raises-20-m), accessed 2026-11-06). **Opisto** (France): used-parts software for dismantlers, revenue EUR 9.74 m in 2024 ([catalogue V1-FR-006](../../catalogue/V1-vehicle-parts.csv)). **Carsub** (South Korea, 2023): used parts for imported cars, seed-funded ([catalogue V1-KR-002](../../catalogue/V1-vehicle-parts.csv)). None is a sourcing desk for one brand group; no analogue found for a paid "parts chaser".
**Findability gap in Poland:** T10 (2026-11-06): Omoda 5 rear bumper **W** (OLX only, used/new PLN 370–2,399); Jaecoo 7 front pads **N** ([V1 #1](../../findability/V1-vehicle-parts.md#1-front-brake-pads-jaecoo-7-v1-d02), [V1 #2](../../findability/V1-vehicle-parts.md#2-rear-bumper-omoda-5-v1-d01)). **T14 update (2026-11-06):** an Auto Świat check of 8 June 2026 found Jaecoo 7 front pads at Inter Cars (PLN 229, vs. more than PLN 800 at ASO) and Iparts.pl (PLN 119); body and lighting parts outside ASO only for MG HS (Polcar, Iparts.pl; Inter Cars has some body panels but no lamps), almost nothing outside ASO for BYD Seal U and Forthing ([Auto Świat](https://www.auto-swiat.pl/porady/czesci-do-chinskich-aut-jak-z-dostepnoscia-da-sie-cos-kupic-poza-aso/d2gg0fv), accessed 2026-11-06). Result: service parts are now findable; **collision body parts for Omoda/Jaecoo/BYD remain dealer-only**, which is the gap, but it is a supply gap, not a finding gap.
**Supply side:** (1) **Omoda & Jaecoo dealer network / central warehouse in Duchnice near Warsaw**: the importer claims 99.68% parts availability and, from 3 Oct 2025, a 20% rebate if a part ordered by an individual at ASO does not arrive within one working day; **warranty and insurance-claim repairs are excluded** ([Auto Świat, Oct 2025](https://www.auto-swiat.pl/wiadomosci/aktualnosci/omoda-i-jaecoo-wprowadzily-rabaty-za-opoznienia-zwroca-15-ceny-jesli-czesc-nie-dotrze/rqseq6p), accessed 2026-11-06). A desk buys at retail, like any customer. (2) **Polcar, Inter Cars, Iparts.pl** (aftermarket wholesalers and shops): MG HS body and lighting parts, service parts for Omoda/Jaecoo ([Auto Świat, June 2026](https://www.auto-swiat.pl/porady/czesci-do-chinskich-aut-jak-z-dostepnoscia-da-sie-cos-kupic-poza-aso/d2gg0fv)); wholesalers add body replacements for MG, BYD and Omoda per a leasing firm's blog ([Carlease Polska](https://carleasepolska.pl/blog/530/czy-czesci-do-chinskich-samochodow-beda-dostepne), accessed 2026-11-06, via search summary). Trade terms for a newcomer: unverified. (3) **Ovoko dismantlers** (used parts, commission model, already in Polish) and **AliExpress** export sellers (BYD owners post AliExpress listings as cheaper, per [Autoblog](https://autoblog.spidersweb.pl/ile-czeka-sie-na-czesci-do-chinskich-samochodow-klienci-rynek-polska), accessed 2026-11-06). None contacted.
**Data:** OE part numbers come from the brands' EPC catalogues (dealer-only access; unverified for third parties); aftermarket cross-references from wholesalers' TecDoc-based catalogues (licensed). The desk would build its own VIN → OE number → source table from cases; using dealer EPC data without a licence is not allowed (assumption, unverified).
**Polish competition:** dealers and importers (improving logistics: Duchnice warehouse, BYD's first Polish parts centre per Otomoto, [Otomoto news](https://www.otomoto.pl/news/dostepnosc-czesci-do-aut-z-chin-jest-problem), accessed 2026-11-06, via search summary); Inter Cars, Polcar, Iparts.pl for MG and service parts; Ovoko and OLX for used parts; body shops that already chase parts for insurers. Allegro: not tested (403).
**Demand evidence:** T09 S3–S5: Omoda Polska Facebook group with over 30,000 members; owners report ~8 months for an Omoda 9 rear bumper, 3–5 months for a front bumper, ~4 months for an Omoda 5 rear window; BAIC claims over a month; but the same article (3 Jun 2026) says newer posts show faster repairs thanks to a warehouse in Pomerania, MG waits are days to five weeks and Chery and BYD owners do not complain ([Autoblog](https://autoblog.spidersweb.pl/ile-czeka-sie-na-czesci-do-chinskich-samochodow-klienci-rynek-polska)). Fleet: Chinese brands took 12.69% of Polish new-car registrations in H1 2026 (5.82% in H1 2025); January–August 2026 MG 12,145, Omoda 9,201, BYD 8,054 registrations ([Interia](https://motoryzacja.interia.pl/wiadomosci/news-nowy-krol-chinskich-marek-w-polsce-juz-nie-mg-i-omoda,nId,23538854), accessed 2026-11-06, via search summary). Collision rate per car: unverified.
**How AI is used:** read VIN and damage photos, list likely parts, map to OE numbers learnt from earlier cases, search sources and draft supplier messages. Model cost under PLN 2 per case (estimate). The value is in the case history and supplier contacts, not in the matching; general assistants cannot see dealer stock.
**Physical work:** none with goods (suppliers ship to the owner or body shop). By hand: calls to dealers and dismantlers, chasing dates.

| Cases / month | Physical work | Supplier calls and chasing | Customer updates | Total h / month |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | 0 h | 6 h | 2 h | 8 h |
| 50 | 0 h | 25 h | 6 h | 31 h |
| 200 | 0 h | 90 h | 20 h | 110 h (far above 40 h) |

(estimates: ~35 minutes of calls per case, since most routes are phone-only)

**MVP (≤6 weeks):** a form (VIN, photos, insurer or cash), case tracker with status messages; manual sourcing across ASO, Polcar/Inter Cars/Iparts.pl, Ovoko and OLX; PLN 99 fee paid by BLIK only when a quote with a date is delivered; first brands Omoda, Jaecoo, BAIC.
**Monetisation:** PLN 99 sourcing fee (estimate) + 10% commission/margin on non-dealer parts (estimate). Who pays: cash-paying owners and independent body shops; insurance claims are handled by body shops that order from ASO. **Unit economics per case (gross profit, estimate):**

| Item | PLN | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Sourcing fee (paid in 70% of cases where a quote is found) | 69 | 0.7 × 99, estimate |
| Commission on non-dealer parts: 30% of cases × PLN 800 × 10% | 24 | estimate; used bumper PLN 370–2,399 on OLX (T10) |
| Payment fee (~1.5%) | −2 | [Freenance](https://freenance.io/przedsiebiorczosc/bramki-platnosci-2026-ranking-stripe-payu-przelewy24-tpay-bluemedia-porownanie/), accessed 2026-11-06, via search summary |
| Founder time 35 min at PLN 60/h | −35 | estimate |
| Wrong or damaged used part (5% × PLN 200) | −10 | estimate |
| **Gross profit per case before CAC** | **46** | |
| CAC (posts in owner groups, near zero; Facebook ads ~PLN 40 per paid case) | −20 | estimate, blend |
| **After acquisition** | **≈26** | |

10,000 PLN monthly gross profit needs ~220 cases a month before CAC (10,000 / 46): about 130 hours of calls, three times the founder's limit. Figures are **gross profit**, not turnover. The 20× check: 4,400 collision cases a month among Chinese-brand cars, unsourced; the fleet registered since 2024 is tens of thousands of cars (Interia above), so plausible only in a few years.
**Distribution, first 100 buyers:** Omoda Polska and other brand Facebook groups (posts where group rules allow), independent body shops that repair Chinese brands (found through the groups), SEO on `zderzak omoda 5`, `lampa tylna omoda 9`.
**Regulation:** body panels and lamps: road-approval marks on lamps (E-mark) must be checked for aftermarket lamps; used parts from dismantlers come with the dismantler's rights; imports from China make the buyer or desk the importer (customs, VAT, GPSR responsible person; [logistics L17](../../research/poland/logistics-and-returns.md#4-consumer-law-flag-not-legal-advice)), which the MVP avoids by not importing. Service fee contract: consumer withdrawal right for services until performed (flag). No licence needed.
**Opportunities (szanse):**
1. The Chinese-brand fleet keeps growing fast (12.69% share in H1 2026 vs 5.82% a year earlier), and each car is a future collision. Likelihood H, impact M (6, major). True if: share stays above 12% in 2027. Source: [Interia](https://motoryzacja.interia.pl/wiadomosci/news-nowy-krol-chinskich-marek-w-polsce-juz-nie-mg-i-omoda,nId,23538854).
2. Owners are already organised in large groups (Omoda Polska >30,000), so distribution costs little. H / M (6, major). True if: one group post brings 10 cases. Source: [Autoblog](https://autoblog.spidersweb.pl/ile-czeka-sie-na-czesci-do-chinskich-samochodow-klienci-rynek-polska).
3. Independent body shops pay per case to save their own calls. L / M (2). True if: 3 body shops send cases in a month.
4. The case history becomes a VIN→OE→source table worth licensing to wholesalers. L / M (2). True if: a wholesaler asks for the data.
**O = 16.**
**Risks (ryzyka):**
1. Importers hold all body-part stock for Omoda/Jaecoo/BYD, so a desk cannot shorten the wait; it only reports it. Likelihood H, impact H (9). Early sign: in the first 10 cases, the fastest route is the dealer in ≥7. Mitigation in the MVP: none; used parts help only for common panels.
2. The problem is fading: importers built warehouses (Duchnice, Pomerania, BYD centre) and promise one-day delivery with a 20% rebate. H / H (9). Early sign: brand groups stop posting waits in 2027. Mitigation: none.
3. Calls scale linearly; 50 cases a month already use 31 of 40 hours. H / M (6). Early sign: >30 minutes per case.
4. Most collision repairs go through insurers and their body shops, leaving few cash cases willing to pay a fee. M / H (6). Early sign: >60% of form starters say "insurance claim".
5. Aftermarket wholesalers (Polcar, Inter Cars) add body parts for these models, removing the need. M / M (4). Early sign: Omoda 5 bumpers listed at Polcar.
**R = 34.**
**Success signals:** Ovoko shows durable signals 5 (organic traffic, ~6 m visits) and 8 (dismantler software and logistics), with revenue doubling in H1 2024 (company claim, [Vestbee](https://www.vestbee.com/blog/articles/ovoko-raises-20-m)). A Polish desk could reproduce signal 9 (pull from owner groups) and 1 (replaces weeks of waiting) only while the dealer logistics are weak; signal 3 (repeat) is absent: an owner has a collision rarely.
**Graveyard:** no closed parts-sourcing desk found. Related: brands that leave the market strand owners (a leasing blog asks which Chinese brands may disappear from Poland, [Carlease](https://carleasepolska.pl/blog/527/czy-chinskie-marki-samochodow-znikna-z-polski-ktore-sa-zagrozone), accessed 2026-11-06, via search summary): that would be the real long-term need, for few cars.
**Scores (1–5):** F1 4 (was 5: real months-long waits for some Omoda/BAIC body parts, but improving and absent for MG, Chery, BYD) · F2 3 (was 2: sourced, fast-growing fleet; collision rate unsourced) · F3 3 (suppliers ship; every case relayed by phone) · F4 2 (was 3: ~PLN 46 per case before CAC, mostly paying the founder's calls) · F5 2 (matching helps; dealer stock is invisible to AI) · F6 3 (was 5: importers' own logistics and aftermarket wholesalers now address the gap) · F7 3 (lamps approval, used-part rights) · F8 1 (calls exceed 40 h/month before 70 cases; heavy coordination) · F9 2 (case data compounds slowly; groups can share sources for free) | **Weighted:** 2.7 / 5 (2.67) | **Chance band:** `<5%`, and **F8 = 1 is a knock-out** (criteria §3): the desk is phone work that grows with every case. O 16, R 34, two weight-9 risks with no mitigation. Also recorded in [rejected.md](../../rejected.md#t14--write-ups-batch-b-2026-11-06); the write-up is kept for T15.
**Cheapest validation test (≤2 weeks, ≤300 PLN):** one post in two brand groups (with admin consent) offering free sourcing for 10 cases in exchange for feedback; record for each case the dealer date, the best alternative found and the time spent; then ask whether the owner would have paid PLN 99. PLN 0–100 cost.
**Kill criteria:** in fewer than 4 of 10 cases an alternative route beats the dealer by ≥2 weeks; or average time per case above 45 minutes; or fewer than 3 owners say they would pay PLN 99.
**Confidence:** medium (press checks of availability are recent; insurance share and collision counts unverified).
