# 009 — Fitment data for Allegro and Shoper parts sellers
**One-liner:** A SaaS tool for car-parts sellers whose offers sit outside Allegro's VIN search: it reads their listings, part photos and OE numbers, proposes the vehicle compatibility list ("Pasuje do") using Allegro's vehicle IDs, and pushes it to Allegro (and to Shoper/WooCommerce shops) after the seller approves; paid monthly.
**Vertical:** V1 | **Model:** subscription (supplier pays: the seller) | **B2B**
**Friction removed:** fit, on the seller side: offers without compatibility data do not show as "fits your car" in Allegro's VIN search, so buyers skip them or buy and return. Today the seller links offers to vehicles by hand, relies on Allegro's catalogue product if the item is a TecDoc brand, or leaves the section empty. Seller forum posts complain that selling without technical parameters and vehicle links is hard and manual linking of many offers is tedious ([Allegro community thread](https://spolecznosc.allegro.pl/t5/zaawansowani-sprzedawcy/powi%C4%85%C5%BC-oferty-z-produktami/m-p/46021), accessed 2026-11-06, via search summary).
**Analogues:** **Partly** (New Zealand): AI model on licensed manufacturer data that maps parts to vehicles; USD 50 m Series B at USD 500 m valuation (June 2026), other sources say USD 90 m ([catalogue V1-NZ-001](../../catalogue/V1-vehicle-parts.csv); [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/06/23/partly-raises-50m-500m-valuation-crack-us-auto-parts-market/), accessed 2026-11-06). **Versable** (US, 2022): LLM with retrieval generates titles, specs and fitment from part numbers for sellers; raised USD 2.6–3.1 m per a third-party profile (unverified) ([catalogue V1-US-012](../../catalogue/V1-vehicle-parts.csv)). **M2E Pro** (eBay/Magento extension) offers eBay parts compatibility through TecAlliance data ([M2E Pro blog](https://blog.m2epro.com/?p=5090), accessed 2026-11-06, via search summary). Traction of the seller-side tools in revenue terms: unknown.
**Findability gap in Poland:** not a consumer query, so T10 did not test it ([longlist #19](../longlist.md#v1--vehicle-parts-and-car-related-buying)). Context: Allegro VIN search (Feb 2026) covers about 56 m of ~104 m motoring offers, leaving ~48 m without VIN fit (computed in [V1 scan §4](../../research/verticals/V1-vehicle-parts.md#4-what-the-numbers-say); [Instalki](https://www.instalki.pl/news/internet/allegro-wprowadza-wyszukiwanie-po-vin-dla-56-mln-ofert/), accessed 2026-11-06). T14 queries (2026-11-06) `automatyczne uzupełnianie kompatybilności pojazdów w ofertach Allegro … BaseLinker` and `Allegro tabela kompatybilności … TecDoc integracja narzędzie`: no Polish SaaS found that fills "Pasuje do" automatically for non-TecDoc items; found freelance job posts asking for exactly that ([Useme job, app listing car parts with "Pasuje do"](https://useme.com/pl/jobs/aplikacja-wystawiajaca-czesci-motoryzacyjne-samochodowe-na-allegro-pasuje-do-poj,90270/), accessed 2026-11-06, via search summary) and **Ovoko's SaaS for dismantlers, which connects to Allegro** and syncs listings ([Ovoko help, connect Allegro](https://help.ovoko.com/hc/lv/articles/6793042424477), accessed 2026-11-06, via search summary). Result: gap for non-dismantler sellers of non-TecDoc and used parts; dismantlers already have Ovoko.
**Supply side (data and channel partners):** (1) **Allegro API**: the "Pasuje do" section in car and motorcycle parts categories is integrated with the TecDoc vehicle database; sellers send compatible-product IDs found via `/sale/compatible-products` (by brand, TecDoc parameter or phrase); if an offer is linked to a catalogue product with a compatibility list, Allegro adds it automatically; up to 2,000 rows ([Allegro Developer, compatibility list](https://developer.allegro.pl/compatibility_list), accessed 2026-11-06). Free with the seller's API access; terms of Allegro's API apply. (2) **TecAlliance (TecDoc)** via its Polish partner **Cairo** (over 1,000 suppliers in the catalogue, price on request) ([Cairo TecDoc](https://www.cairo.pl/en/solutions/tecdoc/), accessed 2026-11-06): licence cost and whether derived fitment may be sold to third parties are **unverified**; a TecDoc integrator notes the licence is not worth it for a shop selling a few dozen universal items ([dev-opla](https://dev-opla.com/resources/what-is-tecdoc-auto-parts-ecommerce-guide), accessed 2026-11-06, via search summary). (3) **Ovoko** (possible partner or competitor: dismantler SaaS with Allegro sync, [catalogue V1-LT-001](../../catalogue/V1-vehicle-parts.csv)). Platforms to integrate with: **Base.com (BaseLinker)** and **Shoper** (app stores; terms unverified). None contacted.
**Data:** vehicle IDs come from Allegro (TecDoc-based) and are usable through the API for the seller's own offers. Mapping a part to vehicles needs either (a) the OE number → vehicles cross-reference (TecDoc, licensed; OE catalogues of carmakers, not licensed for this use), or (b) the donor car's VIN for used parts (the seller has it), or (c) AI extraction of fitment text already in the seller's titles and descriptions. Scraping parts sites (7zap and similar) for cross-references would breach their terms (assumption, unverified per site). The legal data path is narrow: (b) and (c), plus TecDoc if licensed.
**Polish competition:** Allegro itself (catalogue auto-fill, VIN search it can extend), Ovoko for dismantlers, TecDoc integrators (Cairo and others) for shops selling TecDoc brands, freelance developers building one-off integrations (Useme posts), BaseLinker add-ons (no automatic fitment feature found, unverified). Why a gap: non-TecDoc and used parts need per-item judgement that rules cannot do and Allegro has no incentive to do for sellers one by one.
**Demand evidence:** ~48 m motoring offers outside VIN search (computed, above); seller complaints and freelance job posts (above); buyer-side thread asking whether Allegro marks parts wrongly ([Allegro community](https://spolecznosc.allegro.pl/t5/dyskusje-kupuj%C4%85cych/czy-allegro-b%C5%82%C4%99dnie-oznacza-cz%C4%99%C5%9Bci-do-auta/td-p/1097722), accessed 2026-11-06, via search summary). Number of Allegro motoring sellers: **unverified**. Dismantlers: a press figure of 1,044 stations is undated (via search summary, not used for scoring); 585,562 cars went to dismantling stations in 2025 (CEP data via [Interia](https://motoryzacja.interia.pl/wiadomosci/news-te-samochody-polacy-najczesciej-oddawali-na-zlom-w-2025-roku,nId,22543896), accessed 2026-11-06, via search summary).
**How AI is used:** the product is the AI step: extract make, model, years, engine and OE numbers from messy titles, descriptions and label photos; propose matching Allegro vehicle IDs with confidence; flag doubtful items for the seller. Cost about PLN 0.01–0.05 per listing with a small model (estimate). A human doing this costs minutes per listing, so AI is the whole margin.
**Physical work:** none. By hand: onboarding calls, fixing wrong matches reported by sellers.

**MVP (≤6 weeks):** Allegro OAuth app; import a seller's motoring offers without compatibility; AI proposes the "Pasuje do" list from text and photos; seller approves in bulk; push via API; dashboard showing how many offers became VIN-visible. Free for the first 200 offers, then paid.

10,000 PLN monthly gross profit needs ~105 paying sellers before CAC (10,000 / 97), ~210 in year one after CAC. Figures are **recurring fees minus direct costs**, not turnover. The 20× check needs ~2,100–4,200 motoring sellers with uncovered offers: plausible for Allegro, but the count is unsourced.
**Distribution, first 100 buyers:** pull the list of Allegro sellers with many motoring offers lacking "Pasuje do" by browsing public category pages by hand (no crawling) and contact them; the Allegro community seller forum; BaseLinker/Shoper app marketplaces; Useme job posters looking for this exact tool.
**Regulation:** the seller stays responsible for "Pasuje do" (an Allegro moderator said so in a forum thread, via search summary); terms must say the tool proposes and the seller approves. Safety-critical parts (brakes, steering) carry consumer-claim risk for the seller if mis-matched; the tool should refuse auto-approval there. RODO minimal (business data). Data licences: TecDoc terms and Allegro API terms (flag, not advice).
**Opportunities (szanse):**
1. About 48 m motoring offers lack VIN fit, a large pool of sellers who lose visibility since Feb 2026. Likelihood H, impact M (6, major). True if: 10 of 30 contacted sellers say VIN visibility cut their sales. Source: [V1 scan §4](../../research/verticals/V1-vehicle-parts.md#4-what-the-numbers-say).
2. Sellers already pay freelancers for one-off "Pasuje do" tools, showing willingness to pay. M / M (4). True if: 3 Useme posters convert to trial users.
3. The same engine sells to Erli, eBay (EU parts compatibility) and Shoper shops. M / M (4). True if: one Shoper app listing gets 20 installs.
4. Allegro or Ovoko partners with or buys a tool that lifts VIN coverage. L / H (3). True if: Allegro's partner programme lists third-party compatibility apps.
**O = 17.**
**Risks (ryzyka):**
1. Allegro extends VIN coverage itself (catalogue matching, AI on titles) and makes the tool redundant. Likelihood H, impact H (9). Early sign: Allegro announces a coverage increase above 56 m offers. Mitigation in the MVP: none (the tool lives on Allegro's API).
2. Without a TecDoc licence, matching non-TecDoc parts from text alone is too inaccurate, and the licence is too costly or forbids derived use. M / H (6). Early sign: <80% of proposed matches accepted by sellers.
3. Dismantlers, the biggest group with uncovered used parts, already use Ovoko's Allegro sync. H / M (6). Early sign: ≥half of contacted high-volume sellers are Ovoko users.
4. Small sellers churn after the first bulk fix (one-off value). M / M (4). Early sign: >30% cancel after month 2.
5. Wrong fitment on brakes or steering leads to buyer claims and seller anger. L / M (2). Early sign: any complaint in the first 50 sellers.
**R = 27.**
**Success signals:** Partly and Versable show durable signals 8 (value sits in fitment data) and 7 (sold to businesses as software); no retention data published, and Partly's round size is reported inconsistently (a minor hype warning). A Polish version can reproduce signal 2 (sellers list weekly, so recurring use) and signal 1 (replaces manual linking hours); signal 8 is weak because Allegro owns the vehicle IDs and the VIN search.
**Graveyard:** no closed Polish fitment-data tool found. Pattern warning from the V1 scan: Allegro closed the mainstream fit gap in Feb 2026 itself ([V1 scan §4](../../research/verticals/V1-vehicle-parts.md#4-what-the-numbers-say)).
**Scores (1–5):** F1 3 (manual linking is real work; forum signals only) · F2 3 (large offer pool, sellers recur weekly; seller count unsourced) · F3 4 (pure software, no goods) · F4 3 (~PLN 97 per subscriber before CAC; data licence unknown) · F5 4 (AI extraction is the product; Partly and Versable sourced) · F6 2 (was 3: Allegro auto-fills from catalogue and owns VIN search; Ovoko serves dismantlers) · F7 3 (seller liability, data licences) · F8 3 (B2B sales calls) · F9 2 (was 3: Allegro holds the vehicle IDs; matches can be redone by others) | **Weighted:** 3.0 / 5 (3.01) | **Chance band:** `<5%`. Score gives `5–15%`; O 17, R 27, R − O = 10 and one H×H risk without mitigation → down one band. The tool is a feature of Allegro's own roadmap, and the data path without a TecDoc licence is narrow.
**Cheapest validation test (≤2 weeks, ≤300 PLN):** pick 30 Allegro sellers with many motoring offers lacking "Pasuje do" (by hand from public pages); send each a free report for 20 of their offers with proposed compatibility made by a prompted model; ask for a paid pilot at PLN 149/month. Cost ~PLN 50 in model calls; no ads.
**Kill criteria:** fewer than 3 of 30 agree to a paid pilot; or sellers accept fewer than 80% of proposed matches; or TecAlliance/Cairo confirm that derived fitment for third parties needs a licence costing more than PLN 2,000 a month.
**Confidence:** medium-low (seller counts, churn and data-licence terms unverified).
**Devil's advocate:** (T15 review, 2026-11-06; fresh checks by hand)
- *Findability (of a competing tool):* web search `Allegro "Pasuje do" automatyczne uzupełnianie kompatybilności AI narzędzie sprzedawcy części 2026`: still no third-party tool that fills "Pasuje do" for non-TecDoc items. What Allegro shipped in 2026 is buyer-side: an AI shopping assistant for all app users from May 2026 (recommends, compares specifications; no vehicle-fit function mentioned) ([Tabletowo, 8 May 2026](https://www.tabletowo.pl/nowa-funkcja-w-aplikacji-allegro-asystent-ai/)), a ChatGPT app ([Wiadomości Handlowe, 12 May 2026](https://www.wiadomoscihandlowe.pl/retailtech-i-nowe-technologie/big-data-i-sztuczna-inteligencja/allegro-odpala-aplikacje-na-chatgpt-zakupy-szybsze-a-oferty-lepiej-dopasowane-2535002), via search summary) and, per a search summary, an AI assistant for sellers in Sales Center from April 2026 (scope: promotions and tools; unverified). The gap holds today; the direction of Allegro's roadmap (AI on listings and search, VIN search since Feb 2026) is the threat the write-up already rates. Ceneo and Google: not relevant (B2B tool).
- *Suppliers (data and channel).* (1) **Allegro API compatibility list**: public documentation, free with API access ([Allegro Developer](https://developer.allegro.pl/compatibility_list), checked again by the link checker). (2) **Cairo / TecDoc**: price on request; no public licence terms for derived data (unchanged). (3) **Ovoko**: exists and syncs dismantler stock to Allegro; it is a competitor for dismantlers, not a partner. No affiliate or reseller programme with Allegro for tool vendors was found in this review.
- *Unit economics recomputed* (per subscriber, estimates): no parcels or returns (software). The weak line is CAC. B2B software clicks in Poland cost PLN 3–8 ("SaaS / IT" range in [Ardura Lab](https://arduralab.pages.dev/en/glossary/cpc), weak source); at 3% click-to-trial and 25% trial-to-paid (estimates; no Polish benchmark found) CAC is **PLN 400–1,070** per paying seller, against the write-up's PLN 600 by outreach. At PLN 97 gross profit per month, payback is 4–11 months, and churn is unknown; at the midpoint (PLN ~735) and a 12-month life the first year leaves ~PLN 36/month (write-up 47). 10,000 PLN/month would need ~100–280 paying sellers, which hits the 55 h support line at 200.
- *What incumbents would do:* Allegro adds AI-suggested compatibility in the listing form (it already auto-fills from catalogue products and runs AI projects across the platform); BaseLinker or Ovoko add the same as a module for their existing seller base. Amazon.pl uses its own fitment data and is not a channel.
- *Legal flags:* hold (seller remains responsible for "Pasuje do"; safety-critical parts not auto-approved; TecDoc and Allegro API terms). Added: Allegro's API terms must allow a third party to write compatibility on a seller's behalf at scale (flag, not read in this review).

**Review changes:** F4 3 → 2 (a click-based CAC for B2B software, PLN 400–1,070, against ~PLN 97 a month with unknown churn). Weighted 3.01 → **2.86**. Band `<5%`. Not dropped: still the highest-scoring written-up idea, with no parcels, returns or physical work; T16 should test it with a seller pre-sale before any build.
