# 004 — Video or photo diagnosis before the part, ending in a dropshipped part
**One-liner:** A household whose washer, dishwasher or hood has failed sends the model plate, error code and a short video; AI triage plus (when needed) a 15-minute paid video call with an independent technician names the faulty part, and a Polish parts wholesaler ships exactly that part.
**Vertical:** V2 | **Model:** service fee for the live call + margin (or commission) on the part | **B2C**
**Friction removed:** finding (the buyer knows "it leaks" or "E18", not which part) and fit (which of several modules fits the E-Nr); trust (is it worth repairing?). Today the buyer asks on elektroda.pl, watches YouTube, guesses and buys a part, or pays a technician's visit (from ~PLN 100 locally; BSH PLN 170 for travel + diagnosis + small repair in its 2021 terms).
**Analogues:** Spareka (France, launched 2012): revenue about EUR 10 m in 2024, nearly 1 m customers, ~100,000 video consultations in 2024 (about 10× 2023); in about 30% of cases guided customers buy the part on Spareka; EUR 16 m raised 2020–2023; video calls partly paid by France's bonus réparation (sponsored article, company figures) ([Maddyness](https://www.maddyness.com/2025/01/09/spareka-plateforme-leader-daide-a-la-reparation-leve-25-millions-deuros-et-ouvre-son-capital-au-grand-public/), accessed 2026-11-06; [catalogue V2-FR-001](../../catalogue/V2-home-climate-appliance.csv)). Miele USA sells a remote-assist session for USD 59, refunded if a technician visit is booked ([Miele Remote Assist](https://www.mieleusa.com/c/remote-assist-6278.htm), accessed 2026-11-06, via search summary). Manufacturers in Poland offer remote diagnosis only for connected appliances (Bosch Home Connect) ([Bosch kontakt](https://www.bosch-home.pl/serwis/kontakt-i-informacje/kontakt/kontakt), accessed 2026-11-06) or app-based Smart Diagnosis (LG) ([LG Polska](https://www.lg.com/pl/lg-experience/tech-hub/sprytne-diagnozowanie-usterek-w-pralce-twinwash-za-pomoc-smart-diagnosis), accessed 2026-11-06).
**Findability gap in Poland:** T10 (2026-11-06), indirect: Bosch WAW24540PL control module **W** (one plausible OLX listing, fit not stated), Electrolux EWT136551W drum spider **N** (foreign shop only), Electrolux EWC 1350 programmer **N** (donor machine only), Kernau hood board **N** ([V2 #1–#4](../../findability/V2-home-climate-appliance.md#1-control-module-bosch-waw24540pl-washer-v2-d01)). T13 query `zdalna diagnoza pralki wideorozmowa z serwisantem online porada naprawa AGD płatna konsultacja` (2026-11-06): no independent paid video-diagnosis service in Poland; only manufacturer remote diagnostics. Result: **gap for diagnosis**; the part itself is often not stocked in Poland for old or store-brand models.
**Supply side:** (1) **CzęściAGD.pl**: shop and wholesaler, matches parts to the model, states it sells certified parts ([catalogue V2-PL-001](../../catalogue/V2-home-climate-appliance.csv); [CzęściAGD.pl safety information](https://czesciagd.pl/images/UPLOAD/InformacjaBezpieczenstwa.pdf), accessed 2026-11-06); dropship or affiliate terms unverified. (2) **Popularna Popławscy**: wholesale and retail of large and small appliance parts ([pkt.pl](https://www.pkt.pl/firma/popularna-poplawscy-poprawa-s-k-a-122020751), accessed 2026-11-06, via search summary); terms unverified. (3) **BSH parts shop** (Bosch/Siemens parts by E-Nr in Polish) ([Bosch części zamienne](https://www.bosch-home.pl/sklep/czesci-zamienne/pralki), via [V2 scan §6](../../research/verticals/V2-home-climate-appliance.md#6-ten-most-promising-analogues-for-poland-quick-polish-check)); a consumer shop, so affiliate/referral at best. Technicians: independent repairers advertising on OLX and Oferteo ([Oferteo naprawa pralek](https://www.oferteo.pl/naprawa-pralek), accessed 2026-11-06); none contacted.
**Data:** model plates and error-code tables (manufacturer manuals and service notes, copyrighted; use for internal matching only), wholesalers' model-to-part lists (need a feed contract), and the platform's own record of symptom → confirmed part, which becomes the proprietary asset.
**Polish competition:** elektroda.pl forum (free expert help), YouTube repair videos, ChatGPT/Gemini reading an error-code photo, manufacturers' service (BSH PLN 170 diagnostic visit in its 2021 terms ([BSH regulamin](https://media3.bsh-group.com/Documents/19772027_211202_Regulamin_swiadczenia_napraw_serwisowych_przez_BSH.pdf), accessed 2026-11-06, via search summary)), local technicians (diagnosis with travel ~PLN 100 in an OLX listing, via search summary), parts shops with model search (CzęściAGD.pl). Gap: nobody sells "the right part for this symptom, guaranteed", remotely, in Polish.
**Demand evidence:** T09 signals V2-D01 to V2-D03 (forum threads on washer modules, autocomplete on hood parts) ([V2 demand](../../demand/V2-home-climate-appliance.md)); the elektroda thread on the Bosch module ([elektroda](https://www.elektroda.pl/rtvforum/topic3633231.html), accessed 2026-11-06 in T10). Household appliance ownership counts: not sourced here (unverified). EU right-to-repair rules are expected to push manufacturers to repair and offer parts ([cenauslug.pl, unijne prawo do naprawy](https://cenauslug.pl/poradniki/producenci-beda-musieli-naprawic-twoja-pralke-co-zmienia-unijne-prawo-do-naprawy), accessed 2026-11-06, via search summary).
**How AI is used:** first-line triage at near-zero cost: read the plate photo (E-Nr, PNC), map error code + symptom to likely parts, ask follow-up questions, check video for noises/leaks; only unresolved cases go to a paid technician call. Model cost under PLN 1 per case (estimate). The edge is the symptom-to-part history, not the chat, since ChatGPT already explains error codes.
**Physical work:** none with goods (wholesaler ships, buyer fits the part). By hand: scheduling technicians, part orders not covered by a feed, wrong-part claims.

**MVP (≤6 weeks):** washers only, 3 brands (Bosch/Siemens, Electrolux/AEG, Samsung); web form with plate photo, error code, video; AI triage with a suggested part and confidence; "Talk to a technician, 15 min, PLN 39" booked in fixed evening slots with 2 contracted technicians; part ordered by hand from one wholesaler; BLIK payment.
**Monetisation:** PLN 39 call fee (estimate, below the ~PLN 100 local visit) + ~20% margin on the part (estimate, wholesaler terms unverified). **Unit economics per guided customer (gross profit, estimate):**

| Item | PLN | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Call fee (60% of customers need the live call) | 23.4 | 0.6 × PLN 39, estimate |
| Technician pay (0.6 × PLN 25 per call) | −15 | estimate |
| Part margin: 30% buy a part × PLN 250 × 20% | 15 | conversion from Spareka (above); price and margin estimates |
| Payment fees (~1.5% on fee and part) | −1.5 | secondary source ([Freenance](https://freenance.io/przedsiebiorczosc/bramki-platnosci-2026-ranking-stripe-payu-przelewy24-tpay-bluemedia-porownanie/), accessed 2026-11-06) |
| Wrong-part cost (5% of part buyers × PLN 100) | −1.5 | estimate; fitted electrical parts are hard to return |
| Shipping | 0 | buyer pays InPost ([logistics L2](../../research/poland/logistics-and-returns.md)) |
| Support 5 min at PLN 60/h | −5 | estimate |
| **Gross profit per guided customer before CAC** | **15.4** | |
| CAC (Google Ads CPC ~PLN 1.5, 5% to a paid step) | −30 | estimate; CPC unverified |
| **After paid acquisition** | **−14.6** | |

10,000 PLN monthly gross profit needs ~650 guided customers a month (10,000 / 15.4), all from organic channels. Figures are **gross profit**, not turnover. Buyer group size unsourced, so the 20× check (13,000 a month) cannot be shown.
**Distribution, first 100 buyers:** SEO on error-code pages per brand (`pralka bosch E18 co to znaczy`, `pralka electrolux nie wiruje`), which cenauslug.pl already ranks for (competition), YouTube shorts per fault, answers on elektroda.pl where allowed by forum rules.
**Regulation:** advice liability (a wrong diagnosis leading to an electrical fault or flood); terms must say the buyer fits at own risk and must unplug and shut water; recommend a technician for gas or mains wiring. Part sales: 14-day withdrawal for unused parts ([logistics L13](../../research/poland/logistics-and-returns.md)), rękojmia, GPSR information from the supplier, WEEE for electrical parts. RODO on videos from inside homes.
**Opportunities (szanse):**
1. AI triage resolves most cases without a technician, so the live call becomes rare and the fee is mostly margin. Likelihood M, impact H (6, major). True if: ≥50% of test cases get a confirmed part from triage alone. No source yet.
2. EU right-to-repair rules raise interest in repair and parts availability. M / M (4). True if: Polish media and manufacturers announce longer parts availability in 2027 ([cenauslug.pl](https://cenauslug.pl/poradniki/producenci-beda-musieli-naprawic-twoja-pralke-co-zmienia-unijne-prawo-do-naprawy)).
3. A parts shop (CzęściAGD.pl) pays a referral or embeds the triage, giving a B2B exit. L / M (2). True if: a shop replies positively to one e-mail.
4. Independent technicians want paid remote calls in the evening. M / L (2). True if: 2 of 5 contacted agree at PLN 25 per call.
**O = 14.**
**Risks (ryzyka):**
1. Free help (elektroda.pl, YouTube, ChatGPT reading the error code) makes a paid diagnosis a hard sell. Likelihood H, impact H (9). Early sign: <3% of visitors pay PLN 39. Mitigation in the MVP: AI triage free, fee only for the live call.
2. No repair bonus in Poland (France subsidises Spareka's calls), so the buyer pays the full fee. H / M (6). Early sign: buyers compare the fee to a PLN 100 visit that includes the repair.
3. A wrong diagnosis leads to a fitted, non-returnable part and a dispute. M / M (4). Early sign: >5% of part buyers say the fault persists.
4. Technician availability in evening slots is unreliable, creating on-call duty for the founder. M / M (4). Early sign: missed slots in the first 2 weeks.
**R = 23.**
**Success signals:** Spareka shows signal 1 (replaces a technician visit), 9 (pull: video calls ×10 in a year) and a 30% conversion to parts; revenue flat around EUR 10–12 m since 2018 ([V2 scan graveyard note](../../research/verticals/V2-home-climate-appliance.md)) and a subsidy dependency (hype warning: subsidy). A Polish version can reproduce signal 1 and 5 (SEO on error codes); signal 8 only if the symptom-to-part data grows.
**Graveyard:** eSpares (UK appliance parts) went into administration; Screwfix bought its assets for GBP 3 m in 2023 ([Kingfisher](https://www.kingfisher.com/media/news/2023/screwfix-acquires-assets-of-connect-distribution-services-limite), accessed 2026-11-06): parts retail alone did not hold.
**Scores (1–5):** F1 3 (was 4: diagnosis friction is real, but free forums and AI answers cover part of it) · F2 3 (forum and autocomplete signals; size unsourced) · F3 3 (wholesaler ships; dropship terms unverified; fitted-part returns) · F4 2 (~PLN 15 per guided customer before CAC; negative with paid ads) · F5 3 (AI triage is a real step, but general assistants do much of it) · F6 3 (no paid remote diagnosis found; free alternatives strong) · F7 3 (advice liability, electrical parts) · F8 3 (technician scheduling) · F9 2 (symptom-to-part data compounds slowly) | **Weighted:** 2.8 / 5 (2.78) | **Chance band:** `<5%`. Score gives `5–15%`; O 14, R 23, R − O = 9 → down one band (H×H risk has a mitigation). Spareka's model leans on a French subsidy Poland lacks, and the economics only work on free traffic.
**Cheapest validation test (≤2 weeks, ≤300 PLN):** one landing page for 3 washer brands with free AI triage (prompted model behind a form) and a "15 min technician call PLN 39" button; PLN 250 of Google Ads on 20 error-code phrases; one technician paid per call; parts ordered by hand from CzęściAGD.pl. Measure triage completion, share who pay the call, share who buy the part.
**Kill criteria:** fewer than 3% of form starters pay for a call or a part; or triage confirmed by the technician in <40% of cases; or no wholesaler gives ≥15% margin.
**Confidence:** medium (analogue figures are company claims; Polish demand size unknown).
**Devil's advocate:** (T15 review, 2026-11-06; fresh checks by hand)
- *Findability: the diagnosis gap holds, but manufacturers fill it for new machines.* Web search `zdalna diagnoza pralki serwisant wideorozmowa płatna konsultacja AGD online`: still no independent paid video diagnosis in Poland. But Bosch runs "diagnostyka zdalna" through Home Connect, used so that the technician can bring the right part ([Bosch, kontakt](https://www.bosch-home.pl/serwis/kontakt-i-informacje/kontakt/kontakt); price not stated); LG offers Smart Diagnosis from a recording of the washer ([LG Polska](https://www.lg.com/pl/lg-experience/tech-hub/sprytne-diagnozowanie-usterek-w-pralce-twinwash-za-pomoc-smart-diagnosis), via search summary). So the gap is limited to older, non-connected and store-brand machines, which is also where parts are hardest to get (T10 N for Electrolux and Kernau). Allegro: 403, **unverifiable**; Ceneo not rerun (parts were tested in T10).
- *Suppliers.* (1) **CzęściAGD.pl**: exists (write-up); no partner, wholesale or dropship programme found in a search (2026-11-06). (2) **Popularna Popławscy**: wholesale and retail listed on pkt.pl; terms not public. (3) **BSH parts shop**: consumer shop; no affiliate programme found. A Shoper wholesaler catalogue lists dropship wholesalers, but the one appliance-named entry (AGDPartner) sells kitchen accessories, not spare parts ([Shoper, AGDPartner](https://www.shoper.pl/katalog-hurtowni/hurtownia/agdpartner)). No supplier with public dropship terms for appliance parts was found.
- *Unit economics recomputed* (per guided customer, estimates): call fee 23.4 − technician 15 + part margin 15 − payment 1.5 − support 5 − returns. Consumer part sales carry the 14-day withdrawal; at a 15% return rate (low end of the 15–25% auto-parts range, [Clerk.io](https://www.clerk.io/blog/5-ways-vehicles-parts-ecommerce-profitable), via search summary; no appliance figure found) and ~PLN 25 loss each (InPost business locker return PLN 12.84–18.55 ([logistics L2](../../research/poland/logistics-and-returns.md)) plus lost margin), returns cost 0.3 × 0.15 × 25 ≈ 1.1 → **PLN 15.8 before CAC**. CAC: a paid step after a free upload converts lower than a shop checkout; at CPC PLN 1.5–2.5 ([Ardura Lab](https://arduralab.pages.dev/en/glossary/cpc), weak source) and 3% to a paid step (estimate, below the 5% in the write-up) CAC is **PLN 50–83**. A failed appliance is a one-off event, so there is no second order to recover it: **−34 to −67 per customer**.
- *What incumbents would do:* CzęściAGD.pl or the BSH shop add "send a photo of the plate and error code" (the AI part is cheap for them); manufacturers extend remote diagnostics; ChatGPT and Gemini already read error codes from a photo. The founder's only asset would be the technician roster.
- *Legal flags:* hold (advice liability, fitting at own risk, WEEE, GPSR from the supplier, RODO on home videos). Added flag: a paid remote diagnosis is a distance service contract with a consumer, so the withdrawal-right rules for services performed at once must be checked before launch (flag, not checked, not legal advice).

**Review changes:** F4 2 → 1 (one-off purchase, PLN ~16 per customer before a CAC of PLN 50–83); F5 3 → 2 (manufacturers' remote diagnostics and general AI assistants cover the triage). Weighted 2.78 → **2.53**. Band `<5%`. **Dropped** and moved to [rejected.md](../../rejected.md): no repeat purchase to recover acquisition, no supplier with public dropship terms, and the AI step is free elsewhere.
