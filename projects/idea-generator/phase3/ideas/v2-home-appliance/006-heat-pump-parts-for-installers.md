# 006 — Spare parts for distributor-only heat-pump brands, for installers
**One-liner:** A heat-pump installer or service firm sends the rating plate and error code of a failed Kaisai, Haier, Gree or other distributor-sold unit; the service identifies the part number, gets a price and date from the brand's distributor (or a parts warehouse) and has the part shipped to the installer, with a VAT invoice.
**Vertical:** V2 | **Model:** margin on a resold part (supplier ships), optional monthly plan for service firms | **B2B**
**Friction removed:** finding (no public parts shop or price list for distributor-only brands) and logistics/coordination (the installer phones the distributor's service desk, waits for a quote and a date). Today the installer calls the distributor's service department, asks colleagues or buys a board on eBay.de from a German reseller; the end customer waits with no heating, which the installer page on Enerad lists as a drawback of the Kaisai brand ("longer warranty repair when spare parts are not available").
**Analogues:** Parts Town (US): OEM parts distributor for technicians, combined with Heritage Foodservice to stock parts from 800+ manufacturers and entered residential HVAC via Dayton (2021) and Encompass (2022) ([catalogue V2-US-005](../../catalogue/V2-home-climate-appliance.csv); [FER](https://www.fermag.com/articles/parts-town-finalizes-union-with-heritage), accessed 2026-11-06). Encompass Supply Chain Solutions (US): nearly USD 200 m annual revenue distributing OEM repair parts to servicers ([MDM](https://www.mdm.com/news/mergers-acquisitions/parts-town-acquires-encompass/), accessed 2026-11-06). Both **hold inventory**, which the founder cannot. A closer, no-stock analogue was not found. In Germany, eBay store "hvpa12" lists Midea main boards and inverter modules for heat pumps at roughly EUR 75–430 ([eBay.de hvpa12](https://www.ebay.de/str/hvpa12/Sonstige/_i.html), accessed 2026-11-06, via search summary; prices change often): a small reseller, traction unknown.
**Findability gap in Poland:** T10 (2026-11-06) [V2 #5](../../findability/V2-home-climate-appliance.md#5-spare-parts-main-board-kaisai-heat-pump-v2-d04): Ceneo `części pompa ciepła kaisai` gave 56 results, all complete units; a web query for a Kaisai main board found no part offers: **N**. T14 queries (2026-11-06): `części zamienne pompy ciepła hurtownia dla instalatorów Kaisai Haier Gree serwis` found **CNC Pompy Ciepła** (Starogard Gdański), which describes "one of the largest heat-pump spare-parts warehouses in Poland" with "over 1,000 products" shipped nationwide, for brands it services (Panasonic, Mitsubishi Electric, Daikin, LG, Vaillant) ([Oferteo profile](https://www.oferteo.pl/cnc-pompy-ciepla/firma/3846188), accessed 2026-11-06); `KLIMA-THERM części zamienne serwis Kaisai` found that Klima-Therm runs a service department where parts can be ordered in and after warranty, as a paid order ([Enerad Kaisai page](https://enerad.pl/producent/pompa-ciepla-kaisai-prezentacja-oferta-kontakt/), accessed 2026-11-06). Result: **gap is narrower than T10 said**: major brands have a parts warehouse; distributor-only brands have a parts route, but through one phone/e-mail desk with no public catalogue or prices.
**Supply side:** (1) **KLIMA-THERM** (Kaisai brand owner, Gdynia group): service department sells parts in and after warranty ([Enerad](https://enerad.pl/producent/pompa-ciepla-kaisai-prezentacja-oferta-kontakt/)); Kaisai's heat-pump catalogue speaks of an authorised installer network trained by Klima-Therm Academy ([Kaisai catalogue](https://kaisai.com/files/page_files/599/kaisai-heat-pumps-catalogue.pdf), accessed 2026-11-06, via search summary: "540 authorised installers", not read in the PDF itself). Reseller terms unverified; the distributor's own installers are its customers, so a middleman is a channel conflict. (2) **CNC Pompy Ciepła**: parts warehouse for major brands ([Oferteo](https://www.oferteo.pl/cnc-pompy-ciepla/firma/3846188)); a possible supplier or a direct competitor; terms unverified. (3) **Gree**: the catalogue names an exclusive Polish representative and an installer app (Strefa Instalatora Gree) for contact with brand engineers (Gree heat-pump catalogue 2022 hosted on enerad.pl, seen only as a search summary on 2026-11-06; the PDF link returned 404, so unverified); parts terms unverified. Fallback for out-of-production boards: German eBay resellers (hvpa12 above). None contacted.
**Data:** part numbers come from the brands' service manuals and exploded diagrams (copyright of the maker; Kaisai publishes installation manuals on kaisai.com, and a search summary says one has a spare-parts chapter without prices or sources; the PDF link found returned 404 on 2026-11-06, so unverified). Prices and stock exist only inside the distributors' systems; there is no feed. OEM cross-references (many store brands are made by large Chinese OEMs) are unverified: T14 found no source confirming which OEM makes Kaisai units, so cross-brand matching is a hypothesis.
**Polish competition:** each brand's distributor service desk (the default route), CNC Pompy Ciepła (major brands), general refrigeration wholesalers (e.g. Klimahurt, compressors and refrigerants, [aplikuj.pl profile](https://www.aplikuj.pl/pracodawca/375616/klimahurt-klimatyzacja-i-chlodnictwo/opinie), accessed 2026-11-06, via search summary), eBay.de resellers. Allegro: not tested (403). Why a gap remains: distributors serve installers they already know by phone; nobody publishes a searchable catalogue of parts for 20+ small brands, because volume per brand is small.
**Demand evidence:** T09 signals S6 (installer page on Kaisai parts delays) and S7 (autocomplete `części do pompy ciepła` with brand suggestions Kaisai, Haier, Panasonic, IVT, Stiebel Eltron, Buderus) ([V2 demand](../../demand/V2-home-climate-appliance.md)). Size: about **855,000 heat pumps** in Polish homes by mid-2026, up from 745,000 a year earlier; 72,000 sold in 2025 (PIE via [Gramwzielone, Aug 2026](https://www.gramwzielone.pl/?p=20372563), accessed 2026-11-06). Sales fell about 35% in 2024 to 80,600 units after Czyste Powietrze changes ([Gazeta Prawna](https://www.gazetaprawna.pl/biznes/srodowisko/artykuly/10910646,branza-pomp-ciepla-czeka-na-renesans-sprzedaz-w-polsce-spadla-o-35-pr.html), accessed 2026-11-06, via search summary). The share of the base from distributor-only brands and the yearly failure rate are **unverified**. The buyers are installers: their number in Poland is unverified (one brand claims 540 authorised installers).
**How AI is used:** read a rating-plate photo and an error code, map them to the service-manual part number (embedding search over manuals the installer or distributor provides), draft the quote request to the distributor, track the reply. Model cost under PLN 1 per request (estimate). A rules system plus a human could do this for a few requests a week; AI matters only if the catalogue spans many brands.
**Physical work:** none with goods (supplier ships to the installer). By hand: phoning distributors' service desks, chasing dates, handling wrong-part returns.

| 50 | 0 h | 15 h | 4 h | 19 h |

(estimates: ~20 minutes of calls per order while distributors have no portal)

**MVP (≤6 weeks):** a B2B form (plate photo, error code, symptom) and a part-request tracker; 3 brands (Kaisai, Haier, Gree); quotes sourced by hand from the distributors and German resellers; price shown with margin and date; payment by bank transfer or BLIK, VAT invoice; supplier ships to the installer.
**Monetisation:** margin on the part, ~15% on the distributor's price (estimate; distributor reseller discount unverified). Optional plan PLN 99/month for service firms (priority sourcing, no margin under PLN 500) as a test, not the base case. **Unit economics per order (gross profit, estimate):**

|---|---|---|

| **After acquisition (first year)** | **≈37** | |

10,000 PLN monthly gross profit needs ~115 orders a month before CAC (10,000 / 87), or ~270 a month in year one after CAC: about 45 active installer firms ordering every two months, all phoned by hand. Figures are **gross profit**, not turnover. The 20× check needs ~2,300–5,400 installer firms in the buyer group: plausible across all brands but unsourced; for distributor-only brands alone, unlikely.
**Distribution, first 100 buyers:** heat-pump installer groups on Facebook (names and sizes unverified), the installer lists published by brands (service-partner maps), Google Ads on `płyta główna pompa ciepła [brand]` (CPC unverified), the Warsaw HVAC Expo where Kaisai exhibits ([Klima-Therm news](https://www.klima-therm.com/kaisai-products-at-warsaw-hvac-expo,1885,en), accessed 2026-11-06, via search summary).
**Regulation:** B2B sale (no consumer withdrawal right, but civil-code warranty applies unless excluded in terms). Electrical parts: WEEE and GPSR information from the producer. Refrigerant-circuit parts (compressors, valves) need an F-gas-certified installer to fit: the buyer is certified, the founder only sells; do not sell refrigerant. No licence needed for the founder (flag, not advice).
**Opportunities (szanse):**
1. The 2021–2023 boom of cheap brands leaves a growing post-warranty base (855,000 units, +110,000 a year) whose owners need parts from 2026 on. Likelihood M, impact H (6, major). True if: installers in a test report post-warranty parts requests for brands with no public catalogue. Source: [Gramwzielone](https://www.gramwzielone.pl/?p=20372563).
2. A distributor outsources parts sales to an online partner to cut its own service-desk load. L / H (3). True if: one of three distributors agrees to a reseller price list.
3. A monthly plan for service firms that handle many brands creates recurring fees. L / M (2). True if: 3 firms pay PLN 99 in a test month.
4. Expansion to AC parts for the same brands (many split ACs on the same platforms). M / M (4). True if: AC requests reach 30% of the form's traffic.
**O = 15.**
**Risks (ryzyka):**
1. Distributors sell parts directly to installers already (Klima-Therm service desk), so the founder is a removable middleman paying retail. Likelihood H, impact H (9). Early sign: distributor quotes to the founder equal the price an installer gets by phone. Mitigation in the MVP: none that changes the structure; only the multi-brand convenience.
2. Margin too thin or nil: distributors give no reseller discount to a firm with no installer base. H / M (6). Early sign: two of three distributors refuse a discount.
3. The parts warehouse route (CNC Pompy Ciepła) already serves the high-volume brands, leaving only long-tail brands with tiny volume. M / H (6). Early sign: >50% of requests are for brands CNC lists.
4. Supplier calls grow linearly with orders and break the 40 h limit before 100 orders. H / M (6). Early sign: >20 minutes per order in the first month.
5. Heat-pump sales stay weak after subsidy changes, slowing the base growth. M / L (2). Early sign: PORT PC 2026 total below 72,000.
**R = 29.**
**Success signals:** Parts Town and Encompass show durable signals 1 (replaces a technician's lost day), 2 (professional buyers order weekly) and 7 (OEM parts margin held), but through inventory and exclusive distribution, which the founder cannot reproduce. A Polish version can reproduce signal 2 (repeat installer orders) and perhaps 6 (time to quote); signal 8 (hard to absorb) is weak because the distributor holds the stock and the data. No hype warnings found for the analogues.
**Graveyard:** Woltair (Polish heat-pump installer platform) closed when subsidy-driven demand fell ([V2 scan §5](../../research/verticals/V2-home-climate-appliance.md#5-graveyard-and-warnings)); different model (installation), same market risk.
**Scores (1–5):** F1 3 (was 4: a parts route exists via the distributor's desk; the friction is calls and waiting, not "not findable") · F2 3 (855,000-unit base with source, but distributor-only share and installer count unsourced) · F3 3 (supplier ships; every order relayed by phone) · F4 3 (~PLN 87 per order before CAC on estimated margin) · F5 2 (plate reading helps; the bottleneck is the distributor's phone, not matching) · F6 2 (was 4: the distributor itself sells parts; CNC Pompy Ciepła covers major brands) · F7 4 (B2B, no fitting, certified buyer) · F8 2 (calls exceed 40 h/month near 150 orders) · F9 2 (installer relations only; no data the distributor lacks) | **Weighted:** 2.7 / 5 (2.71) | **Chance band:** `<5%`. Score gives `<5%` already; O 15, R 29 and one H×H risk with no mitigation, so no upward move is possible. The friction is real for the installer, but the only supplier of each brand's parts is also the installer's existing contact, so a middleman adds a call, not a shortcut.
**Cheapest validation test (≤2 weeks, ≤300 PLN):** e-mail Klima-Therm, the Gree and Haier distributors and CNC Pompy Ciepła asking for reseller terms (0 PLN); in parallel, phone 15 installers from brands' service-partner lists and ask how they get parts today, how long it takes and whether they would pay a 15% premium for one multi-brand request form; PLN 250 of Google Ads on 10 `płyta pompa ciepła [brand]` phrases to a request form, sourcing quotes by hand.
**Kill criteria:** no distributor offers any reseller discount; or fewer than 5 of 15 installers say the distributor desk is a problem; or fewer than 5 qualified part requests from the ads.
**Confidence:** medium-low (supplier terms and installer counts unverified; the key fact, that distributors sell parts post-warranty, comes from one installer-comparison page).
**Devil's advocate:** (T15 review, 2026-11-06; fresh checks by hand)
- *Findability: N for an online shop holds; the route by phone is confirmed.* Web search `sklep internetowy części zamienne do pomp ciepła płyta główna sterownik Haier Gree Kaisai`: no Polish online shop listing boards for these brands. Found instead: the Kaisai distributor route again ([Enerad](https://enerad.pl/producent/pompa-ciepla-kaisai-prezentacja-oferta-kontakt/)); Refsystem named as the general Haier distributor for air-conditioning and heat pumps in Poland ([Architektura & Biznes](https://www.architekturaibiznes.pl/en/air-conditioners-brands-haier-most-advanced-technology-equipment,31380.html), 403 to the fetch tool, via search summary, **unverified**); a small shop selling controllers for other brands (Aquami, Heatmi) ([klimashop.beskidmaly.pl](https://klimashop.beskidmaly.pl/kategoria.php?id=6), via search summary); Gree boards only on a US site. So each brand has one Polish distributor that is the parts source. Allegro and Ceneo: Allegro 403 (**unverifiable**); Ceneo was run in T10 (only complete units).
- *Suppliers.* (1) **Klima-Therm** (Kaisai): sells parts in and after warranty through its service department; no reseller or dropship terms public. (2) **CNC Pompy Ciepła**: Oferteo profile exists (link in the write-up, checked again by the link checker); terms not public. (3) **Gree** representative: unverified (PDF 404). Added: **Refsystem** for Haier (unverified, above). None of the four publishes trade, dropship or affiliate terms. Each sells to installers directly, so a reseller sits between a distributor and the distributor's own customer.
- *Unit economics recomputed* (per order, estimates): the 15% margin is the weak point: a distributor that sells to installers at installer price has no reason to give a middleman a discount below that price, so the realistic margin on the first orders is 0–10% unless the shop charges a sourcing fee. At 10%: 1,000 × 10% = 100 − payment 15 − wrong part 20 (13% × PLN 150; 13% is the AWDA wrong-part incidence for professional counter sales, [Counterman](https://www.counterman.com/reeling-from-returns/), via search summary, unverified; the write-up used 5%) − support 20 = **PLN 45** per order before CAC (write-up: 87). Shipping stays with the installer (courier PLN 25–37 + 13% fuel, [logistics L3](../../research/poland/logistics-and-returns.md#1-parcel-network-and-prices)). CAC by direct outreach stays an estimate (no installer-level Google Ads CPC found; B2B search phrases for parts by board number have little volume). 10,000 PLN a month would need ~220 orders, far above the hours ceiling (62 h at 200 orders).
- *What incumbents would do:* if installers liked one-click parts, Klima-Therm or Refsystem would put their parts list in their installer apps or portals (Gree already has an installer app, unverified); CNC Pompy Ciepła would add the small brands. Allegro is not a factor in B2B heat-pump boards.
- *Legal flags:* hold (B2B; WEEE/GPSR from producer; F-gas: sell no refrigerant, buyer is the certified fitter). Nothing new found.

**Review changes:** F4 3 → 2 (a realistic 0–10% margin on a distributor's own installer price leaves ~PLN 45 per order before CAC). Weighted 2.71 → **2.56**. Band `<5%`. Not dropped: the validation step (asking Klima-Therm and Refsystem for reseller terms) is free and would settle the margin question at once.
