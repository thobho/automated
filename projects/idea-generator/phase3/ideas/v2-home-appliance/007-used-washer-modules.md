# 007 — Fit-checked used modules for discontinued washers from donor machines
**One-liner:** Independent repairers list control and programmer modules taken from donor washers, each tested and tagged with its exact part number and software version; a buyer whose washer is out of parts enters the model (E-Nr, PNC), gets only modules confirmed to fit, and the repairer ships with a 3-month warranty; the platform takes a commission.
**Vertical:** V2 | **Model:** commission on a marketplace sale (repairer is seller of record); margin variant rejected (would mean stock) | **B2C** (and repairers buying for their customers)
**Friction removed:** finding (new module discontinued, "product unavailable"), fit (a used module from another serial or software version may not work) and trust (is a used board from OLX working?). Today the buyer asks on elektroda.pl, buys a whole donor machine for PLN 100–400 on OLX, or a used module "fit not stated" for PLN 120–800, or scraps the washer.
**Analogues:** **Spareka** (France): parts marketplace that lists reconditioned boards from third parties next to new ones, e.g. a Whirlpool programmed board new at EUR 208.08 "sold new by Spareka" and reconditioned at EUR 147.00 "sold reconditioned by ENVIE Poitou-Charentes, Grade A" ([Spareka product page](https://www.spareka.fr/electromenager/spem4736962-electromenager-whirlpool-1898723.html), accessed 2026-11-06); other boards at EUR 50–100 reconditioned (via search summary). Spareka company figures: revenue about EUR 10 m in 2024 ([Maddyness](https://www.maddyness.com/2025/01/09/spareka-plateforme-leader-daide-a-la-reparation-leve-25-millions-deuros-et-ouvre-son-capital-au-grand-public/), accessed 2026-11-06; see [004](004-diagnosis-then-part.md)); share from used parts unverified. **eBay** US and EU sellers of used tested washer boards with 60-day to 1-year seller warranties, about EUR 25–400 ([eBay.de listing, Samsung DC92-01803D, "60 Tage Garantie"](https://www.ebay.de/itm/376242467625), accessed 2026-11-06, via search summary): the long tail exists, without fit checking.
**Findability gap in Poland:** T10 (2026-11-06): Bosch WAW24540PL control module **W** (one plausible OLX listing at PLN 699–799, fit not stated), Electrolux EWC 1350 programmer **N** (only donor machines PLN 100–400) ([V2 #1](../../findability/V2-home-climate-appliance.md#1-control-module-bosch-waw24540pl-washer-v2-d01), [V2 #3](../../findability/V2-home-climate-appliance.md#3-programmer-module-electrolux-ewc-1350-v2-d01)). T14 queries (2026-11-06): `używany moduł sterujący pralki sprawdzony testowany gwarancja sklep demontaż` and `sklep części AGD z demontażu używane moduły pralek firma hurtownia` found only private and small-business classifieds (sprzedajemy.pl: a "working and tested" programmer PLN 180, Bosch modules PLN 649 "some also from dismantling", a Samsung module PLN 300 ([sprzedajemy.pl](https://sprzedajemy.pl/dom-i-ogrod/agd/czesci-i-akcesoria/temat/agd+pralki+automatyczne), accessed 2026-11-06, via search summary)) and forum threads; **no Polish shop for tested used modules with fit data was found**. Module **repair** by post exists informally (a forum user paid PLN 110 including post to repair a programmer, against PLN 705 + PLN 150 for new parts, via search summary of elektroda threads).
**Supply side:** (1) **Independent repairers and dismantlers on OLX/sprzedajemy.pl** (none named; the sprzedajemy.pl seller offering Bosch modules "also from dismantling" is the type) — terms and willingness unverified. (2) **ENVIE** (French reuse network, reconditions boards and sells via Spareka, above): a cross-border source for EU-sold models; would it ship to Poland via a new platform: unverified. (3) **CzęściAGD.pl** (new parts, matches parts to models; [V2 catalogue V2-PL-001](../../catalogue/V2-home-climate-appliance.csv), [safety information](https://czesciagd.pl/images/UPLOAD/InformacjaBezpieczenstwa.pdf), accessed 2026-11-06): not a used-parts source but the fallback and price benchmark when a new module still exists. Foreign new-parts fallback: [ersatzteilshop.de](https://www.ersatzteilshop.de/ersatzteile-fuer/electrolux/ewt136551w-91321706400.html) (from T10). None contacted.
**Data:** module part numbers and the models they fit come from manufacturers' parts catalogues (BSH E-Nr, Electrolux PNC) seen through parts shops; used modules need the repairer to record part number, software/firmware version and the donor model. Many Electrolux and Bosch modules need programming for the specific model (a forum user notes an Electrolux service centre loads a configuration file), so "fits" means part number + programming state. Rights: part numbers are facts; copying shops' compatibility tables is not allowed without consent (unverified per shop terms).
**Polish competition:** OLX, sprzedajemy.pl and Allegro Lokalnie classifieds (no fit check, no warranty from private sellers); elektroda.pl forum classifieds; module repair services by post (informal); manufacturer service (new part if still made). Allegro: not tested (403). Why a gap: each module is a single item with an uncertain fit; volume per model is tiny, so shops do not stock used boards.
**Demand evidence:** T09 S1–S3 (elektroda threads: Bosch module unavailable; Electrolux spider "no parts any more"; EWC 1350 programmer bought used with fit doubt) ([V2 demand](../../demand/V2-home-climate-appliance.md)); forum buy requests "used, as long as it works" ([elektroda search, moduł sterować ariston](https://poszukaj.elektroda.pl/szukaj,modu%C5%82-sterowa%C4%87-ariston.html), accessed 2026-11-06, via search summary); advice to buy both modules from the same donor machine ([elektroda topic4130040](https://www.elektroda.pl/rtvforum/topic4130040.html), accessed 2026-11-06, via search summary). Number of washers in Poland and failure rates: unverified.
**How AI is used:** read rating-plate photos (buyer) and module label photos (repairer) to extract E-Nr/PNC, part number and software version; match them; flag "needs programming". Under PLN 0.50 per listing or request (estimate). Real but small: the hard part is getting tested stock listed, not matching.
**Physical work:** none by the founder (repairers test and ship). By hand: onboarding repairers, disputes over modules that fail.

**MVP (≤6 weeks):** listing tool for 5 repairers (photo of label → part number, software version, donor model, test video); buyer search by model; checkout via a payment gateway with split payout; 3-month repairer warranty; 3 brands (Bosch/Siemens, Electrolux/AEG, Whirlpool/Indesit).
**Monetisation:** 12% commission on the module price (estimate; Allegro's own electronics fees run 2.5–8%, [monetisation M7](../../research/poland/monetisation.md#2-marketplace-commissions-in-key-categories)). **Unit economics per order (gross profit, estimate):**

10,000 PLN monthly gross profit needs ~1,000 orders a month on free traffic (10,000 / 10), which would need hundreds of tested modules listed per month by repairers. Figures are **gross profit**, not turnover. Even a margin model (founder buys modules) would add inventory, a knock-out.
**Distribution, first 100 buyers:** SEO pages per module part number and washer model ("moduł [E-Nr] używany sprawdzony"), answers on elektroda.pl where forum rules allow, repairers sharing listings with their own customers.
**Regulation:** the repairer is the seller; a business seller of used goods owes the 14-day withdrawal right to consumers and 2-year non-conformity liability (may be shortened to 1 year for used goods only by agreement, unverified for this case) ([logistics L13–L16](../../research/poland/logistics-and-returns.md#4-consumer-law-flag-not-legal-advice)). The platform has marketplace duties under GPSR (Art. 22) and DSA trader checks. **WEEE law:** under the Polish ustawa o zużytym sprzęcie, dismantling waste equipment and preparing it for reuse happens only in a licensed treatment plant ("zakład przetwarzania") ([Standardy Prawa, ustawa o ZSEE art.](https://standardyprawa.pl/akt/416/art/48869), accessed 2026-11-06, via search summary): donor machines that are legally waste cannot be dismantled by a repairer; machines bought as used goods are a grey area (flag, not advice). Mains-voltage electronics: buyer fits at own risk.
**Opportunities (szanse):**
1. EU right-to-repair rules and repair culture raise demand for parts for older machines. M / M (4). True if: Polish media coverage and search interest for "naprawa pralki" parts grow in 2027 ([cenauslug.pl, see 004](004-diagnosis-then-part.md)).
2. Licensed WEEE treatment plants want a sales channel for reusable parts. L / H (3). True if: one plant replies that it harvests modules and would list them.
3. Repairers already sell modules on classifieds and would list on a fit-checked site to reach buyers nationwide. M / M (4). True if: 5 of 15 contacted repairers list ≥10 modules.
4. A tested-modules database becomes a B2B tool for repairers (search across colleagues' stock). L / M (2). True if: repairers search more than consumers in the test.
**O = 13.**
**Risks (ryzyka):**
1. Supply is too thin: few repairers keep and test modules, so most searches end with "not available". Likelihood H, impact H (9). Early sign: <50 modules listed after 4 weeks. Mitigation in the MVP: none beyond outreach.
2. Commission per order (~PLN 10 after costs) cannot pay for acquisition. H / H (9). Early sign: CAC above PLN 30 in the ads test. Mitigation: SEO only, slow.
3. Used modules fail or need programming, causing disputes the platform has to referee. M / M (4). Early sign: >10% disputes in the first 30 orders.
4. WEEE rules restrict harvesting parts from waste machines, limiting legal supply to licensed plants. M / M (4). Early sign: a repairer or lawyer says donor machines from collection points cannot be used.
5. Repairers and buyers bypass the platform after the first contact (phone number on the part). H / M (6). Early sign: repeat buyers contact repairers directly.
**R = 32.**
**Success signals:** Spareka shows that a parts marketplace can carry reconditioned boards beside new ones (signal 1: replaces a new washer; signal 8: data on part fit); ENVIE's supply comes from a subsidised social-enterprise network in France, which Poland lacks (structure, not a hype warning). A Polish version could reproduce signal 1 and signal 5 (SEO on part numbers) but not signal 3 (repeat purchase is rare: a household buys a module once in years).
**Graveyard:** no closed used-appliance-parts marketplace found; eSpares (UK parts retailer) went into administration and Screwfix bought its assets in 2023 ([Kingfisher](https://www.kingfisher.com/media/news/2023/screwfix-acquires-assets-of-connect-distribution-services-limite), accessed 2026-11-06): parts retail alone is thin.
**Scores (1–5):** F1 4 (T10 W/N; forum shows people scrapping or buying donors) · F2 2 (was 3: one-off purchase, no sourced size, rare per model) · F3 3 (repairer ships, but supply must be created and fails often) · F4 1 (was 3: ~PLN 10 per order before CAC; negative with ads) · F5 3 (label reading and fit match are real steps) · F6 4 (no Polish fit-checked used-module shop found) · F7 2 (was 3: used electronics, consumer rights on the repairer, WEEE dismantling rules) · F8 3 (no physical work; dispute handling) · F9 2 (fit data per used module is thin; repairers can leave) | **Weighted:** 2.7 / 5 (2.67) | **Chance band:** `<5%`. Score gives `<5%`; O 13, R 32 and two weight-9 risks, so no rule can move it up. The friction is real but rare per household, the supply does not exist in a listable form, and a commission on a PLN 250 module does not pay for finding the buyer.
**Cheapest validation test (≤2 weeks, ≤300 PLN):** phone or message 15 repairers who sell modules on OLX/sprzedajemy.pl: how many tested modules they hold, would they list with part number and software version for 12% commission; one licensed WEEE treatment plant asked whether it harvests modules. Then a static page per 20 commonly asked module part numbers with "notify me", PLN 200 of Google Ads on those numbers; count sign-ups.
**Kill criteria:** fewer than 5 repairers willing to list, or fewer than 100 modules offered in total; or under 20 "notify me" sign-ups from the ads.
**Confidence:** medium (the supply problem is clear; demand size and WEEE interpretation unverified).
**Devil's advocate:** (T15 review, 2026-11-06; fresh checks by hand)
- *Findability holds for a shop, but a cheaper substitute exists.* Web search `regeneracja modułu pralki wysyłka naprawa elektroniki pralki sklep moduły używane sprawdzone`: no shop for tested used modules with fit data; results were elektroda.pl threads, OLX and a price guide. The same threads show the substitute: sending the broken module to a repairer by post (PLN ~110 including post in one thread, against PLN 705 for a new module), and advice to match PNC/MCF/CCF and firmware from the donor plate ([elektroda search results](https://poszukaj.elektroda.pl/szukaj,indesit-modu%C5%82y.html), via search summary). Module repair keeps the buyer's own software version, so it beats a used module on fit, and it needs no supply of donors. Allegro: 403, **unverifiable**; Ceneo not rerun (used parts are not on Ceneo).
- *Suppliers.* (1) Independent dismantlers: still none named; no terms. (2) **ENVIE**: exists (write-up); no export or B2B resale terms found in this review. (3) **CzęściAGD.pl**: new parts only, no partner programme found (search 2026-11-06). No supplier of listable tested used modules exists to sign.
- *Unit economics recomputed* (per order, estimates): commission 30 − payment 4 − support 10 − disputes. Used electronics sold by a business to consumers carry the 14-day withdrawal; at 15–25% returns (auto-parts range from [Clerk.io](https://www.clerk.io/blog/5-ways-vehicles-parts-ecommerce-profitable), via search summary; no appliance figure found) with the commission refunded and 30 min handling, disputes cost ~PLN 9–15 → **PLN 1–7 per order before CAC** (write-up: 10). CAC at CPC PLN 1–2.5 ([Ardura Lab](https://arduralab.pages.dev/en/glossary/cpc), weak source) and 2–3% conversion: **PLN 33–125**. One-off purchase: never paid back.
- *What incumbents would do:* OLX or Allegro Lokalnie would not bother (tiny volume); module repairers would advertise by post more widely, which is cheaper for the buyer.
- *Legal flags:* hold (WEEE dismantling only in licensed plants; GPSR Art. 22 marketplace duties; DSA trader checks). No new flag.
