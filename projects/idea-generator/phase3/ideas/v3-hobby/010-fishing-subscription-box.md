# 010 — Fishing subscription box for the PZW audience
**One-liner:** A monthly box of lures and terminal tackle chosen for the subscriber's target species (perch, pike, zander, trout) and season, packed and shipped by a partner fishing shop from its own stock, sold by subscription with recurring BLIK or card payment.
**Vertical:** V3 | **Model:** subscription (margin between the box price and the partner shop's packed-box price) | **B2C**
**Friction removed:** finding and price discovery, weakly: the angler does not know which new lures are worth trying and wants a surprise without shop visits. Today anglers buy in specialist shops, on Allegro, at Decathlon or from forums and YouTube recommendations; no buyer is blocked from finding lures, so the friction is curiosity and convenience, not access.
**Analogues:** **Mystery Tackle Box / Catch Co.** (US, 2012): monthly lure box (about USD 14.99–24.99 per a gear site, [NW Yachting](https://www.nwyachting.com/?p=12426), accessed 2026-11-06, via search summary); about 55,000 subscribers in 2016 (via search summary, unverified); parent Catch Co. raised a USD 38 m Series B and USD 56 m in total ([catalogue V3-US-009](../../catalogue/V3-hobby-niches.csv); [Built In](https://builtin.com/articles/catch-co-38m-series-b-hiring), accessed 2026-11-06); revenue nearly doubled in 2020 (pandemic). In **April 2024 Gordon Brothers, an asset and liquidation firm, bought "all the assets of Catch Co."**, including Mystery Tackle Box, with "nearly $300 million in revenue" since 2012; the president spoke of "the challenges of our recent past" ([SGB Media, 3 Apr 2024](https://sgbonline.com/gordon-brothers-acquires-catch-co-assets/), accessed 2026-11-06). Other US boxes: Tackle Monkey (from USD 27.95), The Fly Crate (USD 24.95/month) ([My Subscription Addiction](https://www.mysubscriptionaddiction.com/b/tackle-monkey-bass-fishing-subscription-box), [Cratejoy](https://www.cratejoy.com/box-insider/best-fishing-subscription-boxes), accessed 2026-11-06, via search summary).
**Findability gap in Poland:** T10 did not test a box (no single item); Japanese lures were **E** ([V3 #10](../../findability/V3-hobby-niches.md#10-japanese-lures-jackall-v3-d14)). T06 found no active Polish fishing subscription, only SubClub "Fishbox" from 2014 ([V3 scan §7](../../research/verticals/V3-hobby-niches.md#7-ten-most-promising-analogues-for-poland-quick-polish-check); [Spider's Web 2014](https://spidersweb.pl/2014/10/subclub-zakupy-subskrypcyjne-w-polsce.html)). T14 query `box wędkarski subskrypcja co miesiąc przynęty 2026 paczka niespodzianka wędkarz abonament` (2026-11-06): only US boxes in results; **no Polish monthly box found**. Lures themselves: easy to find (Empik, Decathlon, Tradeinn list Mikado, Jaxon, Savage Gear: [Empik BigRiver store](https://www.empik.com/sklepy/sklep-bigriver,19914,m), [Decathlon Jaxon](https://www.decathlon.pl/sporty/wedkarstwo/wedki/f-brand_jaxon), accessed 2026-11-06, via search summary).
**Supply side:** a box needs goods bought ahead; to stay without inventory, a **partner shop must pack from its own stock**. Named possible suppliers: (1) **Mikado** (Polish fishing brand, founded 1989, makes and distributes tackle; sold at Decathlon and others; dealer terms unverified) ([Decathlon Mikado](https://www.decathlon.pl/sporty/wedkarstwo-spinningowe/wedki-spinningowe-i-castingowe/f-brand_mikado), accessed 2026-11-06, via search summary). (2) **Jaxon** (Polish manufacturer, products on the market since 1985; terms unverified) ([Decathlon Jaxon](https://www.decathlon.pl/sporty/wedkarstwo/wedki/f-brand_jaxon)). (3) **Konger** (Bogdan Gosztyła, Blizne Łaszczyńskiego; self-described leading fishing-equipment maker on a job-board page) ([aplikuj.pl](https://www.aplikuj.pl/pracodawca/387236/konger-bogdan-gosztyla/opinie), accessed 2026-11-06, via search summary). A packing partner (an online fishing shop such as BigRiver on Empik) is the real need: **none named as willing; unverified**. No dropshipping programme for lures was found.
**Data:** none licensed; the asset would be subscriber preferences (species, water type, region, method) and feedback on which lures caught fish. PZW fishery data could personalise by region (rights unverified).
**Polish competition:** specialist shops and Allegro sellers with one-off "mystery box" or lure sets (T06: one-off offers exist), Decathlon, Empik marketplace, Tradeinn (ES). Why a gap: Poland has had no subscription box culture for hobbies beyond a few attempts; subscription prices are anchored low (Allegro Smart! PLN 59.90 a year, [monetisation M9](../../research/poland/monetisation.md#3-what-poles-pay-subscriptions-for-today)).
**Demand evidence:** just over 500,000 PZW members paid dues in 2024, including 54,000 children ([xyz.pl](https://xyz.pl/potencjalnie-najwieksza-partia-w-kraju-poznajemy-polskie-wedkarstwo/), accessed 2026-11-06; [V3 scan §6](../../research/verticals/V3-hobby-niches.md#6-hobbies-in-poland-size-and-supply-gap)); a parliamentary question says about 600,000 (undated, [Sejm question 1063](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/sejm/term10/writtenQuestions/1063/body), accessed 2026-11-06, via search summary). Search or forum signals for "box wędkarski": none found in T09 (the V3 demand file has no fishing-box item). Polish spending on tackle: unverified.
**How AI is used:** choose box contents per subscriber from the partner's stock (species, season, region, past feedback), write the insert card, answer "how to fish this lure" questions. Under PLN 0.20 per box (estimate). A human curator could do it; AI adds little.
**Physical work:** none if the partner shop packs; otherwise the founder packs (knock-out). By hand: monthly box selection with the partner, complaints about contents.

| Boxes / month | Physical work (founder) | Curation with partner | Support and churn handling | Total h / month |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | 0 h (partner packs) | 3 h | 1 h | 4 h |
| 50 | 0 h | 4 h | 4 h | 8 h |
| 200 | 0 h | 5 h | 12 h | 17 h |

(estimates; if no partner packs, packing adds ~5 min per box: 17 h at 200 boxes, above the 8 h physical limit)

**MVP (≤6 weeks):** landing page with species quiz; 3 box types (spinning perch/zander, pike, trout); recurring payment (card or BLIK recurring via a gateway); one partner shop packs and ships by InPost; first 3 months as a pre-sale to test demand before any partner commitment.
**Monetisation:** PLN 79 a month (estimate; US boxes USD 15–28 ≈ PLN 60–110 at ~4 PLN/USD, estimate). **Unit economics per subscriber per month (gross profit, estimate):**

|---|---|---|

| Packed box from partner shop (contents + packing + its margin) | −50 | estimate; partner terms unverified |

| Support 2 min at PLN 60/h | −2 | estimate |
| **Gross profit per subscriber-month before CAC** | **9** | |
| CAC: Facebook/Instagram ads, ~PLN 60 per subscriber (estimate), 4-month average life (estimate) | −15 | estimate per month of life |

10,000 PLN monthly gross profit needs ~1,100 active subscribers before CAC (10,000 / 9); with acquisition the model loses money unless subscribers stay well beyond 7 months. Figures are **gross profit**, not turnover. The 20× check (22,000 anglers) passes against 500,000 PZW members, so size is not the problem; margin and churn are.
**Distribution, first 100 buyers:** fishing YouTube channels and Facebook groups (sizes unverified), PZW district events (access unverified), Instagram ads with unboxing videos; a gift-subscription angle for Christmas and Father's Day.
**Regulation:** standard consumer sale; for recurring deliveries the withdrawal period runs from the first delivery (L13); GPSR producer data for every item in the box (supplied by the partner); hooks are sharp but not regulated goods; recurring-payment consent and easy cancellation (UOKiK practice, flag). No licence.
**Opportunities (szanse):**
1. A large, organised hobby group (about 500,000 PZW members) with no Polish box. Likelihood H, impact M (6, major). True if: a pre-sale page gets 100 sign-ups from one channel. Source: [xyz.pl](https://xyz.pl/potencjalnie-najwieksza-partia-w-kraju-poznajemy-polskie-wedkarstwo/).
2. Gift subscriptions at Christmas give a seasonal spike with prepaid 3- or 6-month plans. M / M (4). True if: 30% of December sign-ups are gifts.
3. A Polish brand (Mikado, Jaxon) uses the box as a sampling channel and subsidises contents. L / H (3). True if: one brand offers samples at cost.
4. Feedback data on which lures catch fish becomes content (SEO, YouTube) that lowers CAC. L / M (2). True if: content brings 30% of sign-ups by month 6.
**O = 15.**
**Risks (ryzyka):**
1. Churn after a few boxes, as the analogue shows: Catch Co. raised USD 56 m and its assets were bought by a liquidation-type buyer in 2024. Likelihood H, impact H (9). Early sign: >20% monthly cancellations in the first 3 months. Mitigation in the MVP: prepaid 3-month plans only (partial).
2. No partner shop agrees to pack boxes from its stock at a price that leaves margin, so the founder must buy stock (F3 knock-out). M / H (6). Early sign: 3 of 3 shops ask for minimum orders.
3. Gross profit of ~PLN 9 per box does not cover paid acquisition. H / H (9). Early sign: CAC above PLN 40 in the pre-sale test. Mitigation: none beyond organic channels.
4. Winter seasonality: most anglers pause from November to March. H / M (6). Early sign: cancellations double in November.
5. Anglers prefer choosing lures themselves; a surprise box feels like paying for unwanted items. M / M (4). Early sign: low ratings of contents (<3/5).
**R = 34.**
**Success signals:** Mystery Tackle Box showed signal 2 (a hobby that recurs every season) and organic community reach, but its 2020 spike was a pandemic effect and the business ended in an asset sale (hype warnings: pandemic spike, heavy funding, exit through an asset buyer). A Polish version could reproduce signal 2 and signal 5 (community channels) but not signal 3 (retention) on the analogue's record.
**Graveyard:** Catch Co. assets sold to Gordon Brothers (2024, above); SubClub "Fishbox" (Poland, 2014) not found active ([Spider's Web 2014](https://spidersweb.pl/2014/10/subclub-zakupy-subskrypcyjne-w-polsce.html)).
**Scores (1–5):** F1 2 (lures are easy to find; the box sells discovery, not access) · F2 3 (500,000 PZW members sourced; no demand signal for a box) · F3 2 (was 3: needs a partner shop that packs from its stock, none named; otherwise inventory) · F4 1 (was 3: ~PLN 9 per box before CAC, negative after) · F5 2 (curation helps; a human could do it) · F6 4 (no Polish box found) · F7 4 (standard consumer sale) · F8 2 (was 3: depends on a packing partner; founder packing breaks the physical limit) · F9 2 (preference data is thin; boxes are easy to copy) | **Weighted:** 2.4 / 5 (2.40) | **Chance band:** `<5%`. Score gives `<5%`; O 15, R 34, two weight-9 risks → two bands down is already at the floor. The PZW audience is large, but the closest analogue failed after USD 56 m of funding, and the Polish margin per box cannot pay for acquisition.
**Cheapest validation test (≤2 weeks, ≤300 PLN):** a pre-sale landing page ("first box in March, PLN 79/month, cancel any time", deposit PLN 1 via BLIK) with PLN 250 of Facebook/Instagram ads targeted at fishing interests; in parallel ask 3 online fishing shops whether they would pack 100 boxes a month at PLN 50 including contents.
**Kill criteria:** CAC per deposit above PLN 40; or fewer than 50 deposits; or no shop agrees to pack without minimum orders.
**Confidence:** medium (analogue outcome sourced; Polish churn, partner terms and CAC unverified).
