# 001 — Catering-equipment spare parts for restaurants (Unox, Carpigiani and similar)
**One-liner:** A Polish-language shop that finds the exact OEM spare part for a restaurant's combi oven, soft-serve machine or dishwasher from a photo of the rating plate, and has an EU parts distributor ship it directly with a VAT invoice.
**Vertical:** V5 | **Model:** margin (main); service fee for urgent sourcing (secondary) | **B2B**
**Friction removed:** finding (part number hidden in an exploded diagram; Polish search returns generic gaskets) and logistics (downtime while the part comes from the US or Ireland). Today a kitchen calls its service technician, who orders the part, or the owner searches abroad (Parts Town, Nisbets, Ekuep) in English and pays foreign shipping.
**Analogues:** Parts Town (US), OEM parts from 800+ manufacturers, combined with Heritage Foodservice; parent PT Holdings bought Encompass (~USD 200 m revenue, 2022) and REPA, the European catering-parts distributor ([catalogue V2-US-005](../../catalogue/V2-home-climate-appliance.csv), [Dealroom on REPA](https://app.dealroom.co/companies/repa), accessed 2026-11-06). REPA Group (IT/DE/FR/ES/CH/AE) runs after-sales parts programmes for catering OEMs (MBM, Angelo Po, Welbilt, Convotherm) ([REPA](https://repagroup.com/), [REPA–MBM agreement](https://press.repagroup.com/repa-and-mbm-sign-global-spare-parts-supply-agreement), accessed 2026-11-06). GEV (Germany, founded 1991, REPA member) is a catering-spares wholesaler with a Polish-language site ([gev-online.com/pl](https://www.gev-online.com/pl/), 403 to the fetch tool, seen in search 2026-11-06). Traction figures for REPA and GEV: unverified. No repeat or return rate published by any of them.
**Findability gap in Poland:** T10 (2026-11-06): Ceneo `uszczelka drzwi piec unox` gave 8 generic Rational/GGM gaskets at 307–376 zł, no Unox; web search for the Unox XEVC gasket found only Ekuep (SA), Parts Town (US), Nisbets (IE): **N** ([V5 #8](../../findability/V5-small-business-buying.md#8-door-gasket-unox-combi-oven-xevc-v5-d08)). Ceneo `carpigiani części` 0 results; web only US/UK/DE shops: **N** ([V5 #9](../../findability/V5-small-business-buying.md#9-spare-parts-carpigiani-soft-serve-machine-v5-d08)). **T13 correction (2026-11-06):** a query `gastroparts.pl Toruń części do pieców konwekcyjno-parowych` found **Gastroparts sp. z o.o.** (Toruń, KRS 0001010864, registered January 2023), whose shop lists "over 80,000 spare parts", a Unox brand page, net prices and "in stock – ships within 24h" ([gastroparts.com](https://gastroparts.com/), accessed 2026-11-06), and GEV sells in Polish. The gap is therefore **"not found by a naive search"**, not "no Polish online seller": T10's N overstated it.
**Supply side:** (1) **Gastroparts** (Toruń): 80,000 parts, online account with discounts; whether it dropships for a reseller is unverified. (2) **GEV / REPA Group** (DE/IT): catering-spares wholesaler, trade focus; REPA is described as an official Unox distributor for DACH (search summary, unverified); reseller terms unverified. (3) **Parts Town** (US, also UK site): OEM list prices public (Unox KGN1659A gasket USD 143, 49 in stock) ([Parts Town](https://www.partstown.com/unox/unxkgn1659a), accessed 2026-11-06); shipping to Poland and trade terms unverified. Will they work with a newcomer? Unknown: none contacted. A wholesaler that already sells online in Polish has little reason to give margin to a front-end reseller.
**Data:** OEM exploded diagrams and part numbers (Unox runs a free 3D spare-parts tool in its service area ([NACC](https://www.thenacc.co.uk/news/home/unox-drives-service-standards-with-an-updated-digital-service-area-including-3d-spare-parts-tool), accessed 2026-11-06)); distributor catalogues. Rights: OEM diagrams are copyrighted; using part numbers for matching is normal practice, republishing diagrams is not (flag, not legal advice). A distributor feed would need a contract.
**Polish competition:** Gastroparts (online, 24 h shipping), GEV (Polish site), authorised service companies (Unox has field technicians in Poland, e.g. a Kielce job ad ([pracuj.pl](https://www.pracuj.pl/praca/technik-serwisu-serwisant-kielce,oferta,1004847750), accessed 2026-11-06)), equipment sellers such as expondo with spare-part request forms ([expondo spare parts](https://manuals.expondo.com/spare-parts/10013067), accessed 2026-11-06). Allegro was not tested (403). The gap left: no one matches a photo of the plate to the part in Polish, and the specialist shops are built for technicians who already know the number.
**Demand evidence:** Google autocomplete `części do pieca konwekcyjnego unox`, `części do maszyn do lodów` ([V5 demand S4, S5](../../demand/V5-small-business-buying.md)). Size: GUS via BOSBank, end of 2024: almost 30,000 restaurants (+13% y/y) and over 26,000 bars ([BOSBank Restauracje 2026.07](https://www.bosbank.pl/__data/assets/pdf_file/0024/76902/BOSBank_Restauracje_2026.07.pdf), accessed 2026-11-06, via search summary). Share with a combi oven or soft-serve machine: unverified. No forum thread with a buyer giving up was found.
**How AI is used:** reading the rating-plate photo (model, serial) and a photo of the broken part, matching to the OEM part number across distributor catalogues, drafting the order to the distributor. Cost per order: well under PLN 1 in model calls (estimate). A technician who knows the diagram does this in minutes, so the edge is for the owner without a technician.
**Physical work:** none with goods (distributor ships). By hand: phone or email the distributor for unlisted parts, chase late deliveries, handle a wrong-part claim.

| Orders / month | Supplier calls and chasing | Wrong-part claims | Total h / month |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | 2 h | 0.5 h | 2.5 h |
| 50 | 6 h | 2 h | 8 h |
| 200 | 20 h | 6 h | 26 h (above the ~8 h limit) |

(estimates: ~10 min per order on unlisted parts, 5% claims at 20–30 min each)

**MVP (≤6 weeks):** landing page in Polish for 3 brands (Unox, Carpigiani, Rational), photo upload of the plate, AI proposes the part number, owner confirms by hand and quotes a price with delivery date within 2 hours; payment by bank transfer or BLIK with a VAT invoice; supplier order placed by hand at one distributor.
**Monetisation:** margin on resale; optional PLN 49 urgent-sourcing fee for parts not in any catalogue (estimate). **Unit economics per order (gross profit, estimate):**

10,000 PLN monthly gross profit needs ~125 repeat-level orders a month (10,000 / 80). Group: ~56,000 restaurants and bars (above); 20× check (2,500) passes on outlets, but the share buying parts themselves rather than through a technician is unknown. Figures are **gross profit**, not turnover (turnover at 125 orders ≈ PLN 75,000).
**Distribution, first 100 buyers:** SEO pages per model and part (`uszczelka unox XEVC-0621`, `części carpigiani 191`), Google Ads on exact part numbers, and outreach to independent catering technicians who could order through the shop (they are also the competitor). Polish restaurant owner Facebook groups: names and sizes unverified.
**Regulation:** B2B sale, so no consumer 14-day withdrawal right for firms (only sole traders buying for their business get partial consumer protection; flag, not legal advice); rękojmia can be limited in B2B contracts. Gas and 400 V equipment must be installed by a qualified technician (a Polish seller page notes this ([Prime Gastro](https://primegastro.pl/en/products/piec-konwekcyjny-kompakt-manualny-wlacznie-z-4-blachami-429x345-mm-1), accessed 2026-11-06)); the shop must not sell gas-valve parts as "fit yourself". GPSR duties fall on the seller of record. RODO standard.
**Opportunities (szanse):**
1. Restaurants pay a premium for a part that arrives tomorrow instead of next week (downtime costs a service day). Likelihood M, impact H (6, major). True if: first 20 buyers accept a price 20% above Parts Town landed price. No source for the premium yet.
2. Multi-site chains open an account and reorder wear parts (gaskets, wash arms) on a schedule. L / M (2). True if: one chain reorders within 3 months.
3. Gastroparts or GEV agree to dropship with an API or e-mail feed, so the shop runs with no stock. M / M (4). True if: written terms within 4 weeks of asking.
4. Same model works in CZ/SK with the same distributors. L / L (1).
**O = 13.**
**Risks (ryzyka):**
1. Polish specialists already sell online (Gastroparts 80,000 parts with 24 h shipping, GEV in Polish), so the front end adds little. Likelihood H, impact H (9). Early sign: test buyers find Gastroparts themselves; Google Ads clicks go to them. No mitigation in the MVP beyond the photo match.
2. Distributors refuse trade terms to a newcomer or give only retail price. M / H (6). Early sign: no reply or retail-only offer from two of three suppliers.
3. Restaurants rely on their service technician, who brings the part, so the buyer is the technician, not the restaurant. H / M (6). Early sign: >50% of enquiries are from technicians asking for a discount.
4. Electrical parts ordered wrong are non-returnable at the distributor, so the shop eats the cost. M / M (4). Early sign: first wrong-part claim refused by the supplier.
**R = 25.**
**Success signals:** Parts Town and REPA show durable B2B parts distribution (roll-up by private equity, repeat buying by technicians, signal 2) but publish no repeat or margin figures (catalogue: durability unproven). Hype warning: none found. A Polish version could reproduce signal 1 (replaces a technician's markup) only for owners without a service contract; signal 8 (hard to absorb) is weak because distributors already sell online.
**Graveyard:** eSpares (UK appliance parts, 400+ staff) went into administration; Screwfix bought its assets for GBP 3 m in 2023 ([Kingfisher](https://www.kingfisher.com/media/news/2023/screwfix-acquires-assets-of-connect-distribution-services-limite), accessed 2026-11-06): stand-alone parts retail without its own distribution is fragile. merXu (Polish B2B marketplace) is likely closed (unverified, [V5 scan §5](../../research/verticals/V5-small-business-buying.md#5-graveyard-and-warnings)).
**Scores (1–5):** F1 3 (was 5: a Polish online specialist exists; friction is the part number, not availability) · F2 3 (~56,000 outlets sourced, buying share unknown) · F3 3 (distributor ships; dropship terms unverified) · F4 3 (estimate, CAC recovered on the second order) · F5 2 (plate reading helps owners; technicians do not need it) · F6 2 (was 4: Gastroparts and GEV serve the market online in Polish) · F7 4 (B2B, non-hazardous; gas parts flagged) · F8 3 (supplier deal is the hard part; hours exceed limit at 200 orders) · F9 2 (photo-to-part data is copyable) | **Weighted:** 2.8 / 5 (2.79) | **Chance band:** `<5%`. Score 2.79 gives `5–15%`; O 13, R 25, R − O = 12 and one H×H risk without mitigation → down one band to `<5%`. The T10 "foreign only" result did not survive one more search: the job is mostly done by Gastroparts and GEV, so the idea is a thin front end on someone else's catalogue.
**Cheapest validation test (≤2 weeks, ≤300 PLN):** (1) Ask Gastroparts, GEV and one REPA office for dropship/reseller terms by e-mail (0 PLN). (2) A one-page site "Wyślij zdjęcie tabliczki, podamy numer części i cenę w 2 godziny" with PLN 250 of Google Ads on 30 exact part-number and `części unox / carpigiani` queries; fulfil any order by hand through Gastroparts. Measure: enquiries, how many are technicians, and whether quoted buyers pay a 15–20% premium over Gastroparts' net price.
**Kill criteria:** no distributor offers a trade discount ≥15% within 3 weeks; or fewer than 5 photo enquiries from PLN 250 of ads; or quoted buyers mention Gastroparts/GEV and do not pay above their price.
**Confidence:** medium (the competition finding is solid; demand size and supplier terms are not).
**Devil's advocate:** (T15 review, 2026-11-06; fresh checks by hand)
- *Findability holds only in the narrow sense.* Ceneo `uszczelka unox` (2026-11-06): 30 products, none of them Unox (Corteco car gaskets, Simon frames, helmets) ([Ceneo](https://www.ceneo.pl/;szukaj-uszczelka+unox)). Allegro `uszczelka unox`: 403 to the fetch tool, **unverifiable**. Web search `uszczelka drzwi Unox sklep części zamienne piec konwekcyjno-parowy`: only foreign shops (FoodCraft HK with ~6-week delivery, Parts Town US), expondo's own part list and an elektroda thread advising to ask the brand distributor. So the T13 reading stands: the part is not found by a naive Polish search, but a buyer who types "Gastroparts" or asks their technician gets it.
- *Suppliers.* (1) **Gastroparts** exists and sells online, shows only "retail price net" and a consumer-style customer account; no reseller, partner or dropship programme on the home page ([gastroparts.com](https://gastroparts.com/)). Buying there to resell means no margin unless the shop marks up above a price the buyer can see. (2) **GEV is now REPA Deutschland GmbH** (rebranding press release, March 2024 ([REPA Deutschland rebranding PDF](https://cdn.uc.assets.prezly.com/1f944f5d-ceaf-4547-9cb6-cd73b78f59b3/-/inline/no/REPA%20Deutschland_Rebranding_Mar2024_DE.pdf))); REPA confirms it is "an official distributor for UNOX in Europe with focus on DACH regions", 1,500+ Unox parts ([REPA, Unox partnership](https://repagroup.com/partnership/unox/)), so the "unverified" in the write-up is now verified for DACH, not for Poland. Press material says REPA serves dealers and repairers; dropship and trade discounts: no public terms found. (3) **Parts Town**: retail prices public; no EU trade programme found. Net: none of the three publishes dropship or affiliate terms; the only one with trade focus (REPA) is also the supplier of the competitor.
- *Unit economics recomputed* (estimates, per repeat order, PLN): sale 600 net − part 450 − courier 36 (InPost 2–5 kg 32.07 + 13% fuel, [logistics L3](../../research/poland/logistics-and-returns.md); a DE→PL parcel would cost more, DHL EU tariff up to 5 kg EUR 16.49–20.49 ≈ PLN 70–87 at ~4.25 PLN/EUR, FX estimate ([DHL July 2025 list](https://group.dhl.com/content/dam/deutschepostdhl/en/media-center/media-relations/documents/2025/paketpreise-international-preisuebersicht-01072025.pdf), via search summary)) − payment 9 − wrong-part returns 13 (13% × ~PLN 100 for return freight and restocking; 13% is the AWDA wrong-part incidence for professional counter sales, search summary of [Counterman](https://www.counterman.com/reeling-from-returns/), unverified; the write-up used 5%) − support 20 = **PLN 72**. CAC: Google Ads CPC PLN 2.5 (top of the 0.80–2.50 PLN e-commerce range in [Ardura Lab](https://arduralab.pages.dev/en/glossary/cpc), a source without method) at 2% conversion (1.95% auto-parts e-commerce benchmark, [Scube Marketing](https://www.scubemarketing.com/blog/setup-for-google-ads-in-the-auto-parts-industry-2025), no primary source) = **PLN 125**. First order −53; ~139 repeat orders a month for 10,000 PLN gross profit. If the part ships from Germany the repeat order falls to ~PLN 30.
- *What incumbents would do:* if photo-to-part demand appeared, Gastroparts (already 24 h shipping, Unox brand page) or REPA would add a "send a photo of the plate" form in a week; they own the stock and the trade price. Allegro is not the threat here; the specialist wholesaler is.
- *Legal flags:* the write-up's flags hold (B2B, no 14-day withdrawal for firms; gas parts to be fitted by a qualified person). Added: a sole trader buying for the business has had selected consumer rights (unfair terms, rękojmia, distance-contract withdrawal) since 1 Jan 2021 when the contract is not of a professional nature for them, judged mainly by their CEIDG entry ([Taylor Wessing, 28 Dec 2020](https://www.taylorwessing.com/en/insights-and-events/insights/2020/12/entrepreneur-like-a-consumer), via search summary); a restaurant buying an oven part is likely professional (flag, not legal advice); GPSR applies to the seller of record for parts sold to consumers, not for pure B2B.

**Review changes:** F4 3 → 2 (the first order loses money at a sourced-range CPC and benchmark conversion, and a cross-border parcel removes most of the margin); other scores unchanged. Weighted 2.79 → **2.64**; band stays `<5%`. Not dropped: the validation test (ask REPA for trade terms) is free and decides it, but the idea now depends entirely on one wholesaler giving a ≥15% discount to a newcomer that would compete with its own Polish customers.
