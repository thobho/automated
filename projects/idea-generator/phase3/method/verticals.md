# Phase 3 verticals (V1–V5)

What each vertical covers, search terms to start from, and the marketplaces and sites to look at. Used by the vertical scans (T04–T08), demand mining (T09) and the findability test (T10). A starting list, not a limit: add terms and sites found during research at the end of each vertical, with the access date.

Each vertical has search terms in English, Polish, German and one more language chosen for where the strongest foreign players are: French for V1 and V4, Dutch for V2 and V5, Japanese for V3. Combine terms with words that reveal businesses and traction: EN "startup", "raises", "funding", "revenue", "marketplace"; PL "startup", "inwestycja", "przychody", "platforma"; DE "Startup", "Finanzierung", "Umsatz", "Plattform"; FR "levée de fonds", "chiffre d'affaires"; NL "investering", "omzet"; JA "資金調達" (funding), "売上" (sales).

Site links were checked on 2026-11-06 with `tools/check_links.py` and by reading the page title. "(blocks bots)" means the site returned 403, a timeout or a challenge page to the fetch tool; open it in a browser. Allegro blocks the fetch tool on every page: use a browser by hand, in small numbers (brief rules).

General marketplaces and price comparison to check in every vertical (Poland first, then neighbours): [Allegro](https://allegro.pl/) (blocks bots), [Allegro Lokalnie](https://www.allegrolokalnie.pl/), [Ceneo](https://www.ceneo.pl/), [OLX](https://www.olx.pl/), [Amazon.pl](https://www.amazon.pl/), [Vinted](https://www.vinted.pl/) (second-hand); abroad [eBay.de](https://www.ebay.de/), [eBay.com](https://www.ebay.com/), [Kleinanzeigen](https://www.kleinanzeigen.de/), [Marktplaats](https://www.marktplaats.nl/), [Leboncoin](https://www.leboncoin.fr/) (blocks bots), [idealo](https://www.idealo.de/) (blocks bots), [Geizhals](https://www.geizhals.de/).

## V1 — Vehicle parts and car-related buying

**Covers:** parts by VIN or registration, accessories, tyres and wheels, motorcycle parts and gear, camper and caravan equipment, EV charging gear, workshop booking with the right part, used and remanufactured parts.

**Typical frictions:** finding (OE vs. aftermarket part numbers), fit (engine code, year, trim), trust (fakes, used-part quality), logistics (fitting at a workshop, core returns), coordination (part arrives where the mechanic is).

## V5 — Small business and professional buying

**Covers:** spare parts for trades (HVAC installers, plumbers, electricians, mechanics), MRO (maintenance, repair and operations supplies), workshop and salon supplies, "one order for the whole job" for a contractor, B2B reorders.

**Typical frictions:** finding (parts spread across wholesalers with their own codes), price discovery (net prices behind trade accounts), logistics (delivery to site, same day), coordination (one job, many suppliers), invoicing (VAT invoices, deferred payment).
