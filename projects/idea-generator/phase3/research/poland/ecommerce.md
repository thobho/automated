# Poland: ecommerce size, structure and buying habits (T02, part 1)

Baseline for Phase 3. Each finding has a source URL and the access date. "Computed" means the arithmetic is mine from the cited numbers. "Unverified" means the claim comes from a secondary or vendor source I could not check against a primary one. Fees and rates change often: re-check before using a number in a write-up.

Related files: [logistics-and-returns.md](logistics-and-returns.md), [monetisation.md](monetisation.md).

## 1. Market size and reach

**E1. Allegro Polish GMV grew 12% in Q2 2026.** Allegro Polish Operations: GMV PLN 18,513.9 m in Q2 2026 (+12.0% YoY) and PLN 35,003.2 m in H1 2026 (+11.8%); Polish revenue PLN 3,166.5 m in Q2 (+14.4%). Preliminary, unaudited figures. Annualised, the Polish GMV is roughly PLN 70 bn (computed: H1 × 2, an estimate that ignores Q4 seasonality).
Source: [Allegro MAR attachment, 13 July 2026](https://about.allegro.eu/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/2026Q2_MAR_attachment_FINAL.pdf), accessed 2026-11-06.

**E2. Allegro has 15.5 m active buyers in Poland, spending about PLN 4,400 a year each.** Q1 2026: Polish active buyers 15.5 m (15.1 m a year earlier, +2.6%); GMV per active buyer in Polish Operations PLN 4,393.5 (+7.2%). Allegro International (Mall, CZ/SK and others) has 4.9 m buyers and falling (−7.5%). Growth now comes from spend per buyer, not from new buyers: the Polish buyer pool is close to saturated.
Source: [Allegro, Selected historical consolidated financial information Q1 2026](https://about.allegro.eu/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Selected-historical-consolidated-financial-information-Q1-2026.pdf), accessed 2026-11-06.

**E3. Allegro earns about 12.5% of Polish GMV from marketplace fees, and advertising is the fastest-growing line.** In the same Q1 2026 report, the segment table that appears to be Polish Operations shows marketplace revenue PLN 2,067.4 m, advertising revenue PLN 369.2 m (+24.8% YoY), logistics service revenue PLN 128.9 m (+110%) and price comparison revenue (Ceneo) PLN 63.7 m. Against Polish GMV of PLN 16,489.3 m, marketplace revenue is about 12.5% and advertising about 2.2% of GMV (computed; Allegro's own "take rate" definition may differ). For a seller on Allegro this means a total platform cost well above the headline commission once ads are counted.
Source: [Allegro Q1 2026 historical financial information](https://about.allegro.eu/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Selected-historical-consolidated-financial-information-Q1-2026.pdf), accessed 2026-11-06.

**E4. About 78% of Polish internet users (around 30 m people) shop online, and the share has plateaued.** Gemius "E-commerce w Polsce 2025": ~78% of internet users buy online; 41% bought from foreign sites (+5 pp YoY); average monthly online spend PLN 575; 82% shop on a smartphone. Read via the Przelewy24 summary; the Gemius PDF itself was not opened. A Gemius slide deck also notes no change in the online-shopper share year on year (via the same search; unverified).
Source: [Przelewy24, "Gemius E-commerce 2025. How Do Poles Shop"](https://www.przelewy24.pl/en/news/ecommerce-2025-online-shopping-trends-polish-consumers), accessed 2026-11-06.

**E5. Total market size estimates disagree by more than 50%.** Search results on 2026-11-06 gave about PLN 92 bn for 2025 (attributed to Krajowa Izba Gospodarcza), over PLN 150 bn of consumer online spending (Statista Market Insights) and a PwC forecast of PLN 192 bn by 2028. I could not open the PwC page (403) or a PMR 2025 figure. Definitions differ (retail sales vs. all online spend incl. services). Use Allegro's ~PLN 70 bn annualised Polish GMV (E1) as the one hard, audited-style anchor. All three market totals: **unverified**.
Source: search summary only; PwC page [Strategy& "E-commerce w Polsce"](https://www.strategyand.pwc.com/pl/pl/publikacje/2024/e-commerce-w-polsce.html) (blocks bots), accessed 2026-11-06.

## 2. Platforms and category leaders

**E6. Allegro is back ahead of Temu in reach; Temu is shrinking.** Mediapanel (Gemius/PBI), May 2026: Allegro 22.337 m real users, over 75% reach of Polish internet users, third publisher after Google and Meta; Temu (Whaleco) 17.364 m, down from 20.34 m in January 2026. Temu led the "zakupy online" category in several months of 2025 and in February 2026 (19.3 m), so the lead swaps. Figures for OLX, Amazon, Ceneo are behind the paywall of the article.
Source: [Wiadomości Handlowe, "Mediapanel maj 2026"](https://www.wiadomoscihandlowe.pl/e-commerce-i-e-grocery/rynek-e-commerce/mediapanel-maj-2026-allegro-wyprzedza-temu-a-biedronka-lidla-2535619), accessed 2026-11-06. The February 2026 figure is from a search summary of [Wiadomości Handlowe, "Temu znów liderem"](https://www.wiadomoscihandlowe.pl/e-commerce-i-e-grocery/rynek-e-commerce/temu-znow-liderem-e-commerce-w-polsce-2532200) (not opened; unverified), accessed 2026-11-06.

**E7. Allegro seller commission runs from 2.5% to 17% of the sale including shipping, with per-item caps in many categories.** Shoper Learn (March 2026): electronics 2.5–8%; motoryzacja 4.5% (truck tyres, cap PLN 120) to 16% (used engines, cap PLN 140); dom i ogród 4.5% (air conditioners, cap PLN 250) to 11%; any product not in the price list 17%. Plus PLN 1 per transaction without a subscription; subscriptions Basic PLN 49, Professional PLN 199, Expert PLN 3,000 per month. Secondary source (Allegro's own fee pages block the fetch tool); the Priceva calculator gives motoryzacja 4.5–16% too, a vendor summary gives car parts 5–12% (disagreement: see [monetisation.md](monetisation.md) M8).
Source: [Shoper Learn, "Prowizje Allegro – rodzaje i aktualne stawki"](https://www.shoper.pl/learn/?p=60750), accessed 2026-11-06.

**E8. Amazon.pl charges a lower commission than Allegro in car parts and tools.** Amazon.pl seller plans: Professional PLN 165.91/month net, Individual PLN 4 per item; referral fees: automotive 9% (7% for electronic devices, 8% for electronic accessories, cap PLN 120), tools and DIY 6%, home 8% up to PLN 85 then 10%, sports 10%, consumer electronics 5%, all others 15%; minimum PLN 1 per item. Amazon.pl reach is far smaller than Allegro's (E6 lists only Allegro and Temu; Amazon ranks lower in earlier Mediapanel lists per search, unverified).
Source: [sell.amazon.pl/oplaty](https://sell.amazon.pl/oplaty), accessed 2026-11-06.

**E9. Erli is the second Polish general marketplace; its "0% commission" claim is unverified.** An integration vendor states Erli charges 0% standard commission and earns from a premium subscription and CPC ads; Erli's earlier model was half of Allegro's commission. I could not reach Erli's seller fee page (erli.pl/sprzedawaj returned 404). **Unverified.**
Source: [Zunapro, Erli marketplace page](https://www.zunapro.com/poland/pl/marketplace/erli), accessed 2026-11-06.

**E10. Oponeo (tyres) is a PLN 2.4 bn business and its bike arm grows fastest.** Oponeo.pl Group 2025: revenue PLN 2,370.9 m (+12.1%), of which car accessories (tyres, rims) PLN 1,842.6 m (+6.7%) and bicycles (Dadelo) PLN 444.0 m (+58.2%); domestic sales PLN 2,061.5 m; group net profit PLN 58.4 m (−34.7%). Net margin about 2.5% (computed): a scale-and-logistics business, not a fat margin. Analogue for "Oponeo for X".
Source: [BiznesRadar, "Grupa Oponeo.pl – podsumowanie wyników za 2025 rok"](https://www.biznesradar.pl/a/146110,grupa-oponeo-pl-podsumowanie-wynikow-za-2025-rok), accessed 2026-11-06.

**E11. Autodoc (car parts) is a EUR 1.8 bn pan-European player with its largest logistics hub in Szczecin.** Autodoc 2025: revenue EUR 1,807.9 m (+16.3%), gross profit EUR 756.6 m (about 42% of revenue, computed), 9.3 m active customers, 18.9 m orders, shops in 27 countries; B2B arm AUTODOC PRO more than doubled to over EUR 140 m; a third-party marketplace in 10 countries with over 400 sellers. Poland-specific sales are not disclosed. The Szczecin hub is from search summaries (unverified).
Source: [AUTODOC 2025 results, EQS via Deutsche Börse](https://live.deutsche-boerse.com/news/EQS-News-AUTODOC-solidifies-European-leadership-with-strong-2025-performance-c76106a2-1307-4f01-9eb0-de73003f14c7), accessed 2026-11-06.

**E12. Ceneo (price comparison) belongs to Allegro and is small next to it.** Allegro reports Ceneo as "price comparison revenue": PLN 63.7 m in Q1 2026 (+4.2% YoY), under 3% of Allegro's Polish revenue (computed). Shops pay Ceneo per click; the CPC price list was not readable from the shop panel ([shops.ceneo.pl](https://shops.ceneo.pl/) shows only a log-in). Consequence: a Ceneo listing is one more Allegro-owned channel, not an independent one.
Source: [Allegro Q1 2026 historical financial information](https://about.allegro.eu/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Selected-historical-consolidated-financial-information-Q1-2026.pdf), accessed 2026-11-06.

## 3. Where search starts: Google vs. marketplace

**E13. 45% of Polish online buyers start the search on a marketplace (Allegro-commissioned study).** Minds&Roses "Customer Journey 2025" for Allegro (n=2,000, CAWI, April 2025): 45% start on a marketplace, +11 pp vs 2023; 43% of those who start on Allegro buy there. Sponsored content and commissioned by the incumbent: treat as an upper bound. A separate Danae study (n=1,000) found 13% of online buyers use ChatGPT or Copilot, mostly for product information (86%) and product search (73%).
Source: [Forbes.pl, "Raport Customer Journey 2025"](https://www.forbes.pl/biznes/raport-customer-journey-2025-o-zakupach-online-jak-kupuja-polacy/nd6m0my), accessed 2026-11-06.

**E14. In 2022 the split was 30% Allegro vs. 18% Google.** Allegro's ad director cited a 2022 study: 30% of online shoppers start on Allegro, 18% on Google; 91% of those who start on Allegro buy there. The study is not named in the article. The trend from E14 to E13 points one way: product search keeps moving from Google to the marketplace.
Source: [BiznesRadar, 3 Nov 2022](https://www.biznesradar.pl/a/113362,krzysztof-andrzejczak-allegro-30-konsumentow-robiacych-zakupy-w-internecie-poszukiwania-produktow-rozpoczyna-na-allegro), accessed 2026-11-06.

**E15. Google still has about 89% of general web search in Poland.** StatCounter, October 2026: Google 89.42%, Bing 7.66%, Yandex 1.37%, DuckDuckGo 0.75%. So SEO and Google Ads remain the main way for a new site to be found outside Allegro, even if product searches increasingly start on Allegro (E13).
Source: [StatCounter, search engine market share Poland](https://gs.statcounter.com/search-engine-market-share/all/poland), accessed 2026-11-06.

## 4. How Poles buy niche and second-hand items

**E16. Social media and second-hand are mainstream channels.** Gemius 2025: 19% of internet users bought via social media (about 7 times a year, average PLN 812 a year); 58% of e-consumers bought used products; among second-hand buyers, Vinted 48%, Allegro 46%, OLX 39%. I found no quantified source on forums and Facebook groups for niche hobby items; T09 should collect those signals directly (group names and member counts).
Source: [Przelewy24 summary of Gemius 2025](https://www.przelewy24.pl/en/news/ecommerce-2025-online-shopping-trends-polish-consumers), accessed 2026-11-06.

## 5. Payments, trust and invoices

**E17. BLIK is the default way to pay online.** Gemius 2025 (most-used methods): BLIK 72%, fast bank transfer (Przelewy24, PayU and others) 64%, card 43%; cash on delivery is used more by men (no share given). A new shop must offer BLIK and a pay-by-link gateway at launch.
Source: [Przelewy24 summary of Gemius 2025](https://www.przelewy24.pl/en/news/ecommerce-2025-online-shopping-trends-polish-consumers), accessed 2026-11-06.

**E18. BLIK processed 1.4 bn online payments worth PLN 219 bn in 2025.** BLIK 2025: 2.9 bn transactions in total (PLN 441.5 bn), online +20% YoY to 1.4 bn (PLN 219 bn), average ecommerce basket PLN 158, 20.7 m active accounts; BLIK Płacę Później (BNPL) only 235.5 k transactions (PLN 59.4 m). Deferred payment is still marginal.
Source: [ITwiz, "BLIK w 2025 roku"](https://itwiz.pl/blik-w-2025-roku-29-mld-transakcji-i-dynamiczny-rozwoj-e-commerce/), accessed 2026-11-06.

**E19. Business buyers on Allegro have a separate Allegro Biznes channel with net prices and deferred payment.** Allegro Biznes (February 2021 article) is for verified company-account holders: net prices and VAT rates on offers, wholesale price lists (discounts up to 55%), bulk-order discounts (up to 30%) and payment terms up to 60 days. Search summaries add that Allegro reported +36% buyers with a company account by April 2022; I could not open that source (comparic.pl, 403): **unverified**. Implication for V5: Allegro already serves small-business buying with invoices and terms; a newcomer needs a reason beyond "VAT invoice available".
Source: [Antyweb, "Nowy serwis Allegro Biznes już działa", 11 Feb 2021](https://antyweb.pl/nowy-serwis-allegro-biznes-juz-dziala-co-sie-zmienia-dla-sprzedajacych-i-kupujacych), accessed 2026-11-06.

## What this means for Phase 3 (my reading, not a sourced finding)
- **Allegro is the search engine for products** (E13, E14) and saturated in buyers (E2): an idea has to win on a step Allegro does not do (fit check, coordination, service), not on reach.
- **Allegro's cost to sellers is ~12–15% of GMV** (E3, E7). A specialist site whose supplier pays it a 3–8% affiliate or wholesale margin competes against that cost, which leaves room only if it brings buyers Allegro does not.
- **Oponeo and Autodoc show the pattern works in Poland at scale** but with thin net margins (E10) or heavy logistics (E11); both hold inventory, which the founder will not.
- **Payment and delivery are solved commodities** (E17, E18 and InPost in [logistics-and-returns.md](logistics-and-returns.md)): BLIK + parcel lockers are table stakes, not a differentiator.
