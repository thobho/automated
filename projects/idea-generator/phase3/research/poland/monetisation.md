# Poland: how a small site can earn (T02, part 3)

Baseline for Phase 3: affiliate and partner programmes, marketplace commissions, consumer subscriptions and lead fees. Each finding has a source URL and the access date. "Unverified" means a secondary source, a directory or a vendor that I could not confirm against the programme's own terms. **Most affiliate terms in Poland are visible only after signing up to a network; none of the rates below should go into a write-up without being re-read in the network panel.**

Related files: [ecommerce.md](ecommerce.md), [logistics-and-returns.md](logistics-and-returns.md).

## 1. Affiliate and partner programmes

**M1. Allegro's affiliate rates are not public, and third-party figures disagree.** BUY.BOX, a network that runs the Allegro programme, says partners earn a commission on the transaction value after a click, with links and product widgets, but shows the terms only after log-in to the publisher panel. A 2026 Polish affiliate guide states "Allegro Smart!: 1–12% by category (Plus typically 4–8%)", via Awin, 30-day cookie, PLN 50 minimum payout. The guide names a different network (Awin) from BUY.BOX, and T01 already found other summaries that disagree with each other ([sources.md](../../method/sources.md)). Treat any Allegro affiliate rate as **unverified**; the owner would need to register to see it. Also note: Allegro takes ~12.5% of GMV from sellers (E3 in [ecommerce.md](ecommerce.md)), so any affiliate payout is a fraction of its own take.
Source: [BUY.BOX, Allegro programme page](https://getbuybox.com/allegro/), accessed 2026-11-06; [Freenance, "Affiliate PL 2026"](https://freenance.io/pasywny-dochod/affiliate-marketing-pl-2026-top-programy-revenue-shopee-allegro-empik-amazon-revolut-prowizje/), accessed 2026-11-06.

**M2. Ceneo pays partners about PLN 0.35 per click-out plus a commission on "Kup teraz" sales, with a 90-day cookie.** Ceneo Partner Programme FAQ: about PLN 0.35 for each user who clicks "Idź do sklepu" from a Ceneo ad on the partner's site (varies by category, "often higher"); a commission on basket transactions bought on Ceneo, removed if the buyer returns the item; 90-day cookie; open to any adult, firm or private person, in Poland or abroad, with a site, blog, YouTube channel, Facebook page or group, or Instagram/Pinterest profile; payouts can be requested up to the 15th of each month. The basket commission rate is not published on the FAQ. Ceneo is Allegro-owned ([ecommerce.md](ecommerce.md) E12).
Source: [Ceneo Partner Programme FAQ](https://pp.ceneo.pl/najczesciej-zadawane-pytania), accessed 2026-11-06; [pp.ceneo.pl](https://pp.ceneo.pl/), accessed 2026-11-06.

**M3. Amazon Associates PL pays "up to 10%", by category; the rate card needs a log-in.** The programme's public page says advertising fees depend on the product category and reach up to 10%. The fee schedule page redirects to Amazon sign-in. A 2026 Polish guide claims 1–5% for PL, a 24-hour cookie and a PLN 50 minimum payout (**unverified**). Amazon.pl's reach in Poland is far below Allegro's ([ecommerce.md](ecommerce.md) E6, E8), so traffic sent there converts less often.
Source: [Amazon Associates Poland](https://affiliate-program.amazon.pl/), accessed 2026-11-06; [Freenance, "Affiliate PL 2026"](https://freenance.io/pasywny-dochod/affiliate-marketing-pl-2026-top-programy-revenue-shopee-allegro-empik-amazon-revolut-prowizje/), accessed 2026-11-06.

**M4. Autodoc PL runs an affiliate programme on five networks; directory figures say about 8% for new and 4% for returning customers, ~1% on tyres.** A programme directory lists Autodoc PL as active on Webgains (since May 2023), Tradedoubler (Jul 2023), CityAds (Oct 2024), Admitad and AWIN (Oct 2026). Rates from search summaries of directories (8% new customer, 4% existing customer, 1% tyres; Autodoc UK on Awin: 8% new, 4% returning, tyres 0% on Webgains) were not confirmed on a terms page I could open (Awin and Webgains pages returned 404): **unverified**. The pattern (lower rate on returning buyers and tyres) shows suppliers pay well only for **new** customers, which caps affiliate income from repeat buyers.
Source: [Affilitizer, Autodoc PL programmes](https://www.affilitizer.com/programs/autodoc.pl), accessed 2026-11-06.

**M5. Other Polish shop programmes: single-digit CPS is the norm outside fashion and beauty.** Search summaries of affiliate directories and guides give: 2407.pl (car parts and tools) CPS from 3.5%, 30-day cookie (affiliate directory, status unclear); Empik 5–10% via Awin, 30-day cookie; financial products pay fixed bounties (banks PLN 50–200 per account via webePartners). All **unverified**; I opened only the Freenance guide for Empik and the bank bounties. For Phase 3 unit economics assume **3–5% on parts and appliances** until a network panel says otherwise (estimate).
Source: [Freenance, "Affiliate PL 2026"](https://freenance.io/pasywny-dochod/affiliate-marketing-pl-2026-top-programy-revenue-shopee-allegro-empik-amazon-revolut-prowizje/), accessed 2026-11-06; [affi.io, 2407.pl](https://affi.io/m/2407-pl), accessed 2026-11-06 (not opened; via search summary).

## 2. Marketplace commissions in key categories

**M6. Amazon.pl referral fees by category (official).** Automotive 9% (electronic devices 7%, electronic accessories 8%, both capped at PLN 120); tools and DIY 6%; home 8% up to PLN 85 and 10% above; sports and outdoor 10%; consumer electronics 5%; electronic accessories 12% up to PLN 50 then 6%; computer accessories 8% up to PLN 50 then 4%; all other categories 15%; minimum PLN 1 per item; Professional plan PLN 165.91/month net. (Same data as [ecommerce.md](ecommerce.md) E8, counted once.)
Source: [sell.amazon.pl/oplaty](https://sell.amazon.pl/oplaty), accessed 2026-11-06.

**M7. Allegro commission ranges by category, with caps (secondary source).** Electronics 2.5–8%; motoryzacja 4.5% (truck tyres, cap PLN 120) to 16% (used engines, cap PLN 140); hand torches 9% (cap PLN 140); dom i ogród 4.5% (air conditioners, cap PLN 250) to 11%; professional cleaning equipment 4.5% (cap PLN 250); not in the price list 17%; plus Smart! delivery surcharge ([logistics-and-returns.md](logistics-and-returns.md) L4) and ads. (Same data as [ecommerce.md](ecommerce.md) E7, counted once.)
Source: [Shoper Learn, "Prowizje Allegro", March 2026](https://www.shoper.pl/learn/?p=60750), accessed 2026-11-06.

**M8. Sources disagree on Allegro's car-parts commission.** Shoper (M7) and the Priceva calculator give motoryzacja 4.5–16%; a Zunapro marketplaces guide (search summary only; the page now returns 410 Gone) put car parts in a 5–12% band; Zunapro's Erli page says Allegro charges "6–17%" overall. The differences come from caps, item condition (new vs used) and sub-category. Use the Allegro fee calculator per sub-category before modelling (Allegro blocks the fetch tool; check by hand).
Source: [Priceva, "Kalkulator prowizji Allegro"](https://priceva.com/pl/kalkulator-prowizji-allegro), accessed 2026-11-06 (via search summary); [Zunapro, Erli marketplace page](https://www.zunapro.com/poland/pl/marketplace/erli), accessed 2026-11-06.

## 3. What Poles pay subscriptions for today

**M9. Allegro Smart! (free delivery) costs PLN 59.90 a year or PLN 14.99 a month, and is often sold at half price.** Standard price PLN 59.90/year or PLN 14.99/month; promotion for new and returning users PLN 29.90 or 39.90 for a year until 15 January 2027 (requires marketing consents). Benchmark for the brief's "small subscription": the incumbent's flagship consumer subscription is **PLN 2.50–5 a month**, and it buys free delivery on everything.
Source: [Spider's Web, Allegro Smart promocja, Oct 2026](https://spidersweb.pl/2026/10/allegro-smart-promocja-pazdziernik-2026.html), accessed 2026-11-06.

**M10. Amazon Prime in Poland: PLN 69 a year or PLN 15.50 a month since January 2026.** Up from PLN 49 / 10.99; new prices for new customers from 13 January 2026, existing members from their next billing cycle (no earlier than 17 February 2026). Includes free delivery and Prime Video. A search result claims a later rollback to the old price (undated; **unverified**).
Source: [Chip.pl, "Amazon Prime drożeje w Polsce", 14 Jan 2026](https://www.chip.pl/2026/01/amazon-prime-drozeje-w-polsce-po-raz-pierwszy-od-lat), accessed 2026-11-06.

**M11. Consumer subscription prices are anchored very low; professionals pay ~PLN 150 a month.** Comparing M9, M10 and M12: consumers pay PLN 5–15 a month for broad bundles (delivery, video), while a one-person beauty salon pays PLN 145 net a month for booking software. A consumer subscription "to find a part" competes with PLN 59.90/year Allegro Smart!; a professional subscription (installers, workshops) sits in the PLN 100–200/month band that Booksy proves. (My synthesis of the cited prices.)
Sources: M9, M10, M12.

## 4. Lead fees and booking commissions

**M12. Booksy (beauty booking): PLN 145 net a month, plus 45% of a new client's first visit (capped at PLN 250) for marketplace leads.** Booksy Biz Poland: subscription PLN 145 net/month, PLN 35 net per extra staff member (no extra fee from the 10th); "Boost" marketplace promotion has no monthly fee but charges a one-off 45% of the net value of services in a new Boost client's first completed visit, capped at PLN 250; mobile payments 1.89% + PLN 0.49; gift cards 3%. This is the clearest Polish example of **subscription + success-based lead fee** for service providers.
Source: [Booksy Biz, cennik](https://biz.booksy.com/pl-pl/cennik), accessed 2026-11-06.

**M13. Oferteo (B2B and home services): providers buy points to unlock a client's contact, PLN 259–7,199 net per package.** Packages: 25 points PLN 259 net, 50 PLN 359, 125 PLN 799, 250 PLN 1,499, 500 PLN 2,999, 1,200 PLN 7,199 (−20% on the first purchase); points per contact vary by industry, job size and location, higher-value jobs cost more; packages valid 12 months; clients post requests for free. Cost per lead is therefore roughly PLN 6–10 per point times a variable number of points per contact (computed; points per contact not published).
Source: [Oferteo, cennik](https://www.oferteo.pl/cennik), accessed 2026-11-06.

**M14. Fixly (OLX's services marketplace) uses the same pay-per-response points model; prices are from an undated secondary source.** A Komputronik guide lists Fixly packages of 80 points PLN 119, 160 points PLN 249, 300 points PLN 399, unlimited PLN 999; up to five providers can respond to one job. Search summaries say OLX agreed to sell Fixly in October 2025 (not confirmed on an OLX page I opened). Both points: **unverified**. Worth a graveyard/pivot check in T07.
Source: [Komputronik Nano, "Aplikacja Fixly – opinie, cena"](https://nano.komputronik.pl/n/aplikacja-fixly-opinie-cena/), accessed 2026-11-06 (via search summary).

## Unit-economics anchors for later tasks (estimates from the findings above)
| Model | Typical Polish rate | What it means per PLN 300 order |
|---|---|---|
| Affiliate on parts or appliances (M4, M5) | ~3–8%, new customers only for the top rate | PLN 9–24 to the site, before CAC |
| Ceneo click-out (M2) | ~PLN 0.35 per click | 100 clicks ≈ PLN 35 |
| Marketplace seller fee (M6, M7) | 4.5–16% + delivery surcharge + ads | the cost a specialist channel can undercut |
| Lead fee to a service provider (M12, M13) | PLN ~10–250 per lead, or 45% of the first visit | works only if the provider earns more on the repeat customer |
| Consumer subscription (M9, M10) | PLN 2.5–15 a month for broad bundles | a niche "finding" subscription must beat this anchor |

**Reading (not a sourced finding):** affiliate income on a PLN 300 part is PLN 10–25 at best, and Google Ads clicks for commercial queries usually cost more than PLN 1 (unverified; T10/T11 should check in Google Keyword Planner). An affiliate-only model needs organic traffic (SEO, community). Margin (dropshipping, L11 in [logistics-and-returns.md](logistics-and-returns.md)) or a B2B subscription/lead fee gives more per transaction.
