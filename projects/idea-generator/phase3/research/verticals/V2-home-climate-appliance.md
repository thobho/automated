## 1. What the vertical is

Buying what keeps a home's machines running: spare parts for washing machines, dishwashers, fridges and ovens (found by model number); heating and cooling equipment and its parts (boilers, heat pumps, air conditioning); filters (HVAC, air purifier, water, fridge); consumables (vacuum bags, coffee-machine descaler, tool batteries); and the service or subscription around them. ## 2. Friction types present

| Friction | How it shows up in V2 | Who removes it (catalogue rows) |
|---|---|---|
| **Finding** | the buyer knows "my Bosch washer leaks", not the part number; the model code (E-Nr, 型式) hides on a sticker inside the door | PartSelect, Repair Clinic, eSpares, Spareka, BSH E-Nr search, Hitachi, andonya model-number guide |
| **Fit** | many near-identical variants; vacuum bags and filters have cross-brand codes; tool batteries by platform | Swirl type finder, Power for All (one battery standard across 10+ brands), Filterbuy (sizes), CzęściAGD.pl matching |
| **Trust** | is it the right fault? is the installer honest? will the purifier filter lock me in? | Spareka (video diagnosis by technicians), Kurashi no Market (reviews), Hello Watt (certified RGE installers), D&G (repair cover) |
| **Logistics** | heavy units must be delivered and installed; heat pumps and AC need certified fitters (refrigerant) | Coolblue (own vans and installers), Thermondo, Enpal, Aira, Quatt, BOXT, Coway (home visits) |
| **Coordination** | filters must be changed on a cycle; installers, paperwork and subsidy forms must line up | FilterEasy, Coway CODY visits, Amazon Dash Replenishment, Hello Watt (subsidy paperwork), Woltair (booking) |
| **Price** | installation quotes are not comparable; authorised service vs. generic part | BOXT (fixed price online), Pompy.app (device prices), Kurashi no Market (price lists per pro) |

**Finding and fit for appliance parts are already solved in Poland at catalogue level**: CzęściAGD.pl matches parts to the model, and BSH sells parts by E-Nr in Polish (see §6). The less-solved frictions are **diagnosis** (which part is broken, Spareka's video call), **installation price and trust** (heat pumps, AC) and **replenishment of the right filter** (no Polish filter subscription found).

## 4. What the numbers say

1. **Stand-alone parts retail is fragile.** eSpares, the UK's leading appliance-parts retailer with 400+ staff, went into administration and Screwfix bought its assets for GBP 3 m in March 2023 ([Kingfisher](https://www.kingfisher.com/media/news/2023/screwfix-acquires-assets-of-connect-distribution-services-limite)). Spareka's revenue has stayed around EUR 10–12 m since 2018 (company press releases and [Maddyness](https://www.maddyness.com/2025/01/09/spareka-plateforme-leader-daide-a-la-reparation-leve-25-millions-deuros-et-ouvre-son-capital-au-grand-public/)). Parts distribution is attractive only at scale and in B2B (Encompass ~USD 200 m, rolled up by Parts Town).
2. **The big money is in heating installation, and it is capital-heavy and subsidy-sensitive.** Enpal EUR 1.1 bn, Thermondo EUR 212 m, Aira EUR 200 m sales run-rate with EUR 150 m new equity ([Sifted](https://sifted.eu/articles/aira-e150m-heat-pump-rollout)). Thermondo expects its first operating profit only for 2026 ([Solarserver](https://www.solarserver.de/2026/08/28/thermondo-erwartet-fuer-2026-erstmals-operativ-einen-gewinn/)). Quatt's growth slowed when the Dutch government dropped the heat-pump mandate ([MT/Sprout](https://mtsprout.nl/groei/quatt-levert-na-warmtepomp-nu-ook-thuisbatterij-en-energie-wij-bouwen-wat-we-cool-vinden)). Woltair died (§5).
3. **Recurring revenue exists, but it rides on a service or an insurance product.** Coway (over 10.3 m rental accounts, FY2025 revenue about USD 3.3 bn, +15.2%, [Direct Selling News](https://www.directsellingnews.com/2026/02/06/coway-reports-q4-and-full-year-2025-financial-results/)) and Domestic & General (2.7 m repairs in FY25, adjusted EBITDA GBP 162 m on GBP 1.2 bn, about 13.5% computed; Asurion buying it for GBP 2.1 bn, [Insurance Business](https://www.insurancebusinessmag.com/uk/news/breaking-news/domestic-and-generals-us-turnaround-delivers-106-revenue-surge-ahead-of-2-1-billion-exit-565148.aspx)). Both need field staff or an insurance licence.
4. **Filter subscriptions alone are small.** FilterEasy/Second Nature raised nearly USD 35 m in total ([Global Venturing](https://globalventuring.com/mannhummel-second-nature-series-c)) and its "Second Nature" press page now redirects back to filtereasy.com (checked 2026-11-06, rebrand apparently reversed, inference). Filterbuy, the bigger US filter seller, won by owning factories ([Filterbuy](https://filterbuy.com/our-story/)), not by subscription. Amazon dropped physical Dash buttons in 2019 and kept only reordering built into appliances and Subscribe & Save ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2019/03/01/amazon-stops-selling-stick-on-dash-buttons/amp/)).
5. **No repeat rate, take rate or return rate was published** by any parts or filter business in the scan. The only conversion figure is Spareka's ~30% from video diagnosis to part purchase.
6. **AI roles found:** almost none. Model-number search, exploded diagrams and repair videos are rules and content; Spareka's diagnosis is done by human technicians by video; Amazon's reorder trigger sits in appliance firmware. A photo-of-the-rating-plate-to-part step (T03 AI section) was not seen live in any V2 business.

## 8. Takeaways for T09–T11

1. Do not build an appliance-parts shop or a model-number search: CzęściAGD.pl and BSH already do it in Polish, and eSpares failed in the UK.
2. The open friction in Poland is **diagnosis before the part** (Spareka pattern): which part is broken. A video or photo-based triage that ends in a dropshipped part is the most founder-shaped idea in V2, and the one where AI (reading the rating plate, matching the fault description) could replace part of the technician's time.
3. **Installer price, trust and subsidy paperwork** for heat pumps and AC (Hello Watt, BOXT, Kurashi no Market) is open in Poland at the comparison level, but subsidy-driven demand killed Woltair; T09 should check Czyste Powietrze demand and AC servicing demand.
4. **Replenishment** works when it rides on a service (Coway, D&G); a filter-only subscription is small and risks Molekule-style lock-in complaints. If tested, start with filters whose fit is confusing (purifier models, jug standards Maxtra vs. Unimax, rekuperacja filters).
5. T09 should mine Polish demand for: "jaka część do pralki [model]", "co się zepsuło w pralce", "filtr do rekuperatora [model]", "serwis klimatyzacji cena", "pompa ciepła Czyste Powietrze kto załatwi wniosek", "zamiennik filtra do oczyszczacza".
