T06 of Phase 3 ([task](../../tasks/06-scan-v3-hobby-niches.md), [brief](../../BRIEF.md)). Catalogue: [catalogue/V3-hobby-niches.csv](../../catalogue/V3-hobby-niches.csv) (39 businesses, 15 countries plus one of unknown base). All sources accessed **2026-11-06**. "Computed" marks my arithmetic; "unverified" marks what I could not confirm; "inference" marks my reading, not a source.

## 1. What the vertical is

Buying for a hobby: the kit, the spare part, the consumable and the second-hand item that a small group of enthusiasts needs and a general shop does not stock or describe well. The scan found six kinds of business:

| Type | Rows | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Collectibles and second-hand hobby marketplaces (commission) | 9 | Cardmarket, TCGplayer, BrickLink, Catawiki, Delcampe, Whatnot, Bunjang, Reverb, BikeExchange |
| Specialist shops that hold stock (margin) | 15 | Thomann, Muziker, bike-components, Wiggle CRC (dead), Bulk Reef Supply, Philibert, Rebel.pl, Botland, Gunfire, HobbyLink Japan, Yushakobo, KBDfans, 1001hobbies, WiMo, E.H. Thorne |
| Used or refurbished goods with a trust step | 4 | buycycle, Upway, The Pro's Closet (closed, reopened), Suruga-ya |
| Maker-owned communities (hardware plus content) | 4 | Prusa Research (Printables), Aquael, Hobbii, LoveCrafts |
| Import and coordination services | 4 | Buyee (proxy buying), Craftcloud (3D-print quotes), Shapeways (dead), Drop (group buys) |
| Data, subscription and app layers | 3 | Scalemates (kit database with shop prices), Fishbrain (app subscription), Mystery Tackle Box (lure subscription) |

## 2. Friction types present

| Friction | How it shows up in V3 | Who removes it (catalogue rows) |
|---|---|---|
| **Finding** | stock spread over small shops in several countries; Japanese shops and auctions need a Japanese address or phone ([Remambo/Japan Rabbit guides summarised in §8 searches; e.g. ZenMarket guide](https://zenmarket.jp/quickguide.aspx)) | Scalemates (prices from 30+ shops), Buyee, HobbyLink Japan, BrickLink, Cardmarket |
| **Fit** | standards that do not mix: bike axles and freehubs, keyboard switches and plates, model-railway gauges, filter cartridges by aquarium model, 3D-printer spare parts | bike-components (compatibility filters), Aquael (spare-parts catalogue by model), Prusa (own parts), Yushakobo |
| **Trust** | second-hand goods of uncertain condition; fakes in cards; unknown foreign shops | buycycle (escrow), Upway and The Pro's Closet (inspection), Catawiki (experts), Bunjang (safe payment), Cardmarket (trustee payment) |
| **Logistics** | bikes are bulky; imports from Japan, China and the US pay VAT and customs; printing a part needs a printer | buycycle (bike shipping), HLJ and Buyee (consolidation), Craftcloud (routes the print to a bureau) |
| **Coordination** | group buys of keyboard parts; preorders of model kits; monthly consumables | Drop (group buys), HLJ (preorders without prepayment), Mystery Tackle Box (monthly box) |
| **Price** | no comparable prices for used items or for print jobs | Reverb (price guide), Cardmarket (price trends), Craftcloud (instant multi-bureau quotes) |

**In Poland the big hobbies are already served in Polish by large foreign or domestic shops** (Thomann, Muziker, Prusa, Botland, Rebel.pl, Aquael, Gunfire). The open frictions are narrower: **instant quotes for print-on-demand**, **trust and shipping for used bikes**, **imports from Japan and China** for niche hobbies, and **price and stock aggregation across small shops** (§7).

## 4. What the numbers say

1. **The durable V3 winners are marketplaces with a low take rate and a long history.** Cardmarket runs on a 5% commission only, with a team of 50 and nearly 500,000 users (2021, [OMR](https://omr.com/en/cardmarket-trading-card-platform/)). BrickLink had 10,000+ stores before LEGO bought it ([Brickset](https://brickset.com/article/47293/the-lego-group-acquires-bricklink)). Catawiki reached EUR 105 m revenue but was still at about -0.4% adjusted EBITDA margin in 2024 ([Catawiki](https://www.catawiki.com/en/press/1860-catawiki-crosses-100m-revenue-mark)) and reports profitable growth in 2025 ([Catawiki](https://www.catawiki.com/en/press/1949-catawiki-delivers-profitable-growth-in-2025-driven-by-strong-business-performance)).
2. **Hobby marketplaces lose value when the hobby cools.** Etsy paid USD 275 m for Reverb ([Retail Dive](https://retaildive.com/news/etsy-to-acquire-music-gear-marketplace-reverb-for-275m/559314)) and sold it for USD 105 m in 2025 ([Etsy 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1370637/000137063725000040/etsy-20250331.htm)), about 38% of the price (computed).
3. **Bike retail is the clearest graveyard** (§5): Wiggle CRC, The Pro's Closet, BikeExchange troubles. The Polish bike market fell from 1.46 m units (2021) to an estimated 1.21 m (2024) while value rose to an estimated PLN 4.9 bn on e-bikes ([Forsal/PAP, VeloBank](https://forsal.pl/biznes/ekologia/artykuly/9563104,rynek-rowerowy-w-polsce-wart-jest-49-mld-zl-sprzedaz-spada-od-2021-r.html)). Venture-backed used-bike models still raise money (buycycle Series A about USD 22 m per an aggregator, [Seedtable](https://seedtable.com/startups/buycycle-de); the company timeline dates its Series A to 2023, [buycycle](https://buycycle.com/en-us/meet-the-team); Upway USD 60 m Series C, [Upway](https://upway.co/blogs/news/upway-raises-60-million-series-c)).
4. **Profitable hobby DTC exists in the Nordics and CEE without venture money**: Hobbii (DKK 21.6 m net 2024), Prusa (about CZK 3.7 bn revenue 2024, preliminary, [Forbes CZ](https://forbes.cz/lists/nc25/josef-prusa/)), Muziker, Gunfire. All of them own stock or make the product.
5. **Published repeat or take rates are rare.** Found: Cardmarket 5% commission; Fishbrain 15% commission on marketplace sales and 72% gross margin (2021, [Seedrs](https://www.seedrs.com/fishbrain), KingsCrowd); buycycle 3.95% seller + 4% buyer fee (US terms, [RoadBikeRider](https://www.roadbikerider.com/?p=77133)); Delcampe EUR 0.30 + 10% buyer fee from March 2024 ([EcommerceBytes](https://www.ecommercebytes.com/kvte)). No return rate was published by any V3 business.
6. **AI roles found: almost none.** Matching is done with databases (Scalemates, BrickLink part catalogue), rules-based quotes (Craftcloud) and human experts (Catawiki). Fishbrain's catch prediction is the only data-model role, and it is unverified whether it uses machine learning. A photo-to-part step (identify a model kit, a keyboard switch or an aquarium filter part from a photo) was not seen live.
