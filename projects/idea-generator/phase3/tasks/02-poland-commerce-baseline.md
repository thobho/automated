# T02 — Poland commerce baseline

**Model:** opus (medium effort) · **Needs:** T01

Read `projects/idea-generator/phase3/BRIEF.md` first and follow its Rules. Commit and push after every small unit and keep your Resume line in `phase3/PROGRESS.md` current. Run the link check on changed files before reporting DONE.

Write three files with 30–50 sourced findings in total.

1. `research/poland/ecommerce.md`: size and structure of Polish ecommerce; Allegro, Amazon.pl, OLX, Temu, Ceneo, Oponeo, Autodoc and the other category leaders (what they sell, seller fees, how a small seller gets listed, visible gaps); share of search starting at Google versus a marketplace; how Poles buy niche items (forums, Facebook groups, Allegro sellers, shops); payment habits (BLIK, Przelewy24, COD); trust and invoices for firms.
2. `research/poland/logistics-and-returns.md`: parcel costs for InPost, DPD, DHL, Orlen Paczka, Poczta; oversized and heavy goods; courier cost per kg; dropshipping and fulfilment partners in Poland (named, with terms if public); typical return rates by category; consumer law (14-day withdrawal, rękojmia, GPSR) and what it means for a reseller or a platform; VAT and OSS for cross-border supply.
3. `research/poland/monetisation.md`: affiliate and partner programmes available to a small site (Allegro Partner, Ceneo, Amazon Associates PL, supplier programmes) with rates and conditions; marketplace commission rates in key categories; what Poles pay subscriptions for today (examples with prices); lead-fee models used by installers and workshops.

**Done when:** all three files exist, every finding has a source and access date, and the link check passes.
