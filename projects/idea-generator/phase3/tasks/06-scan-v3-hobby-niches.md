# T06 — scan V3 hobby niches

**Model:** opus (medium effort) · **Needs:** T01

Read `projects/idea-generator/phase3/BRIEF.md` first and follow its Rules. Commit and push after every small unit and keep your Resume line in `phase3/PROGRESS.md` current. Run the link check on changed files before reporting DONE.

Scan vertical V3 (hobby niches with thin coverage) for businesses that remove friction when buying or ordering. Cover at least 8 countries (US, Germany, Netherlands, UK, France, Nordics, Japan or Korea, and 2+ CEE countries) and search in English, Polish and the local language. Use the examples in the brief as a start, then look for ones it does not name.

1. `research/verticals/V3-hobby-niches.md`: what the vertical is, the friction types present (use the brief's six), patterns, graveyard (businesses that failed or pivoted, and why), coverage in numbers (sources read, countries, searches, searches that found nothing).
2. `catalogue/V3-hobby-niches.csv`: 30–60 rows. Columns are in `method/criteria.md`.
3. For every business record: how it earns (commission, margin, subscription, service fee), what it does by hand versus with software or AI, and any public figure on revenue, GMV, repeat rate, take rate or funding (with URL and access date). Note the hype warnings (growth only from discounts, free delivery or ads).
4. For the 10 most promising: does it exist in Poland (a quick Polish search; the full check comes later), and what Polish thing would it need (catalogue data, suppliers, delivery, invoices, payment).

**Done when:** the research file and the catalogue exist with at least 30 rows, every figure has a source, and the link check passes.

Extra for V3: list at least 25 hobbies and for each give a rough size in Poland (club members, forum size, sales of the hobby's trade, with source) and whether supply is scattered over foreign shops (so a Polish aggregator or importer with fast delivery has a gap).
