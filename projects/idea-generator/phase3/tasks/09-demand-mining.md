# T09 — Polish demand mining

**Model:** opus (medium effort) · **Needs:** T02, T04, T05, T06, T07, T08

Read `projects/idea-generator/phase3/BRIEF.md` first and follow its Rules. Commit and push after every small unit and keep your Resume line in `phase3/PROGRESS.md` current. Run the link check on changed files before reporting DONE.

For each vertical V1–V5 write `demand/<vertical>.md` (same file stem as in `catalogue/`) with at least 15 concrete Polish signals that people cannot find or cannot order something: forum threads (elektroda.pl, forum.klimatyzacja-related forums, forum.motoryzacyjne, hobby forums), Reddit PL, autocomplete suggestions, Google Trends comparisons, Allegro and OLX "szukam" posts visible without login, comments under sellers, Facebook group names and sizes (only public counts).

For each signal: the query or link, date, what the person needed, what they were told ("nie ma w Polsce", "sprowadź z Niemiec"). Group the signals into **candidate hard-to-find items** with a rough monthly search volume or forum frequency (with source). This list feeds T10.

**Done when:** five files exist with ≥15 signals each, at least 60 candidate items in total, sources dated, and the link check passes.
