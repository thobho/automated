# T10 — findability test

**Model:** opus (medium effort) · **Needs:** T09

Read `projects/idea-generator/phase3/BRIEF.md` first and follow its Rules. Commit and push after every small unit and keep your Resume line in `phase3/PROGRESS.md` current. Run the link check on changed files before reporting DONE.

Run the findability test described in the brief. For each vertical write `findability/<vertical>.md` (same stems).

Protocol: pick ≥15 candidate items from T09 per vertical, mixing easy, medium and hard. For each, search the way a buyer would, in at most 3 attempts: Google (Polish), Allegro public search, Ceneo and OLX where relevant (by hand, no login, no crawling, no bulk). Record the exact queries, date, and a verdict:
- **Found easily** (item available in 1 try), **Found with effort** (needs 2–3 tries or guessing a name), **Not found / foreign only** (only abroad or none).
Also record the price range, the number of Polish sellers visible, delivery time and whether the seller is a small shop or a marketplace seller.

End each file with a table of verdicts and the **findability gap rate** (share of items not found or found with effort), and the list of the 5–10 most promising item groups with the reasons. Be honest: if everything is found easily, say so.

**Done when:** five files exist with ≥15 tested items each, queries and dates recorded, and the link check passes.
