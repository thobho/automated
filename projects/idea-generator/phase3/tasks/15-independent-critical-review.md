# T15 — independent critical review

**Model:** opus (medium effort) · **Needs:** T13, T14

Read `projects/idea-generator/phase3/BRIEF.md` first and follow its Rules. Commit and push after every small unit and keep your Resume line in `phase3/PROGRESS.md` current. Run the link check on changed files before reporting DONE.

Review the 10 write-ups as a sceptic, not as their author. For each add **Devil's advocate** and **Review changes** sections:
- try to find the buyer's item on Allegro, Google and Ceneo again (fresh queries, by hand) and say whether the findability result holds;
- check the 3 suppliers: do they exist, do they sell to resellers or ship directly, do they have a dropshipping or affiliate programme, are the terms public;
- recompute the unit economics with real parcel prices, a realistic return rate and a customer-acquisition cost from Google Ads CPC estimates (with sources);
- ask what Allegro, Amazon.pl, the producer or a bigger shop would do if the idea worked;
- check the legal flags (consumer law, product safety, F-gas, GPSR, data rights).
Change scores where evidence demands it, with a reason, log it in `CHANGELOG.md`, and move a dropped idea to `rejected.md`.

**Done when:** all 10 write-ups have both sections, the shortlist and changelog are updated, and the link check passes.
