# Lens 1 — Proven abroad, missing in Poland

Research for lens 1 of [BRIEF.md](../../BRIEF.md); scoring anchors in [method/criteria.md](../../method/criteria.md). All sources accessed 2026-10-29. Findings are listed in the topic files; IDs `L1-Fnn` are stable anchors.

**Headline:** 18 findings across 9 countries. Only a minority are real gaps. In most sectors (PV design, scheduling, HOA, whistleblower, fitness booking, freelancer marketplaces) Polish products already exist. The best remaining gaps are in narrow vertical software where the Polish web search surfaced no cloud, PLN-priced, self-serve product: field-service business management, tutoring-centre management, client photo galleries, software-only mileage logs, association/club management and NIS2/ISO tooling. All are "no equivalent found in a web search", which is weak evidence; verify before scoring L1 ≥ 4.
