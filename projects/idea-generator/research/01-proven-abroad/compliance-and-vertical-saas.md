# Compliance tools, niche vertical SaaS, and the reverse direction (PL to abroad)

Lens 1 findings. All pages accessed 2026-10-29. PL check = Polish-language web searches the same day (weak evidence of absence; see README Coverage).

## L1-F07 — iubenda (Italy): cookie banner + privacy-policy generator
- **Product:** iubenda, https://www.iubenda.com/en/pricing — consent banner, privacy/cookie policy generator, terms generator, accessibility add-on.
- **Pricing / traction (own page):** Essentials 4,99 EUR/site/month, Advanced 19,99 EUR, Ultimate 79,99 EUR (billed yearly, excl. VAT); claims "150,000+" customers (self-reported).
- **PL check:** searched `generator polityki prywatności i cookies RODO baner cookie narzędzie abonament polskie rozwiązanie iubenda alternatywa`. Only foreign tools surfaced (Termly, CookieYes with Polish docs: https://www.cookieyes.com/pl/documentation/instalacja-wtyczki-cookieyes-wordpress/); no Polish-built consent-management SaaS surfaced. **Not a gap in product availability** (iubenda/CookieYes already serve PL users); at most a Polish-language/PLN/UODO-specific wedge. Low differentiation.

## L1-F08 — HoneyBook (US): client CRM + proposals + contracts + invoices for creative freelancers
- **Product:** HoneyBook, https://www.honeybook.com/pricing
- **Pricing / traction (own page):** Starter $29/mo, Essentials $49/mo, Premium $109/mo (billed yearly); "100,000+ businesses"; G2 4.5 (158 reviews), Capterra 4.8 (598 reviews), App Store 4.8 (3.8k ratings) as stated on the page. Available only in US/Canada (UK/AU conflicting on the page).
- **PL check:** searched `CRM dla freelancerów i małych studiów kreatywnych umowy oferty faktury płatności formularze jedno narzędzie HoneyBook odpowiednik po polsku`. Results were English-only (Dubsado, 17hats, Tave, Bonsai). **No Polish-language equivalent found in this search**; PL freelancers mostly use invoicing tools (Fakturownia, inFakt) plus generic CRMs — not checked for proposal/contract flow (unverified).

## L1-F09 — Shepherd (US): cloud veterinary practice software
- **Product:** Shepherd Veterinary Software, https://www.shepherd.vet/pricing/ — records, scheduling, invoicing, inventory, client portal, AI note transcription.
- **Pricing (own page):** $349/month for one doctor ($299 with Shepherd Pay), extra doctors $99/$49; no clinic count given. SOC 2 stated.
- **PL check:** searched `oprogramowanie dla gabinetu weterynaryjnego ...` and `program dla lecznicy weterynaryjnej Polska ...`. Only a university syllabus mentioning a program called "Klinika XP" (https://sylabusy.pbs.edu.pl/pl/document/3f3b2c00-51d8-4086-8ee6-2ccc6b13026e.pdf) surfaced; **Polish vet software exists but could not be assessed** (pricing, cloud, KSeF). Regulatory hook: EU 2019/6 antimicrobial-use data collection, see PIWet material https://www.piwet.pulawy.pl/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/dr-Ilona-Materek-Oprogramowanie-informatyczne-umozliwiajace-gromadzenie-danych-na-temat-stosowania-PWPL-u-zwierzat-w-Polsce-Wyroznienie-Ministra-Rolnictwa-i-Rozwoju-Wsi.pdf. Medical-records data may touch founder "won't do" constraints only if it requires a medical licence (it does not for vets, but verify).

## L1-F11 — Secfix (Germany): ISO 27001 / NIS2 compliance automation for SMBs
- **Product:** Secfix, https://secfix.com/frameworks/nis2 — evidence collection, 20+ policy templates, 250+ automated ISO 27001 checks, risk management; claims "90% faster" compliance (own figure).
- **Traction (own page):** customer logos (Workmotion, Userlane, Kranus Health), 30+ success stories; languages DE/ES/EN/PT; pricing not found (the pricing page returned 404).
- **PL check:** searched `automatyzacja zgodności NIS2 ISO 27001 platforma SaaS dla małych firm ... narzędzie polskie`. Only foreign vendors (Kertos https://www.kertos.io/en/nis2-jetzt, Dataguard, Condignum); **no Polish-language NIS2/ISO tool found in this search.** Trigger: the Polish KSC amendment implementing NIS2 was reported as signed and in force from 3 April 2026 (itwiz.pl, unverified against Dziennik Ustaw): https://itwiz.pl/nowelizacja-ustawy-o-ksc-zaczyna-obowiazywac-rusza-harmonogram-wdrozenia-nis2-w-polsce/ ; fines up to 10 mln EUR reported at https://ksiegowosc.infor.pl/wiadomosci/7533605,nis2-wchodzi-do-polski-nawrocki-podpisal-ustawe-firmy-musza-dzialac-natychmiast-kary-siegna-10-mln-euro.html. Need the Dz.U. text and sector scope before use (L5).

## L1-F12 — Whistleblower channels (Poland): checked, PL vendors exist
- **Context:** The Polish whistleblower act requires internal channels for employers with 50+ people (counted at 1 January / 1 July) per https://kadry.infor.pl/wiadomosci/5542078,Czy-firmy-MSP-powinny-stosowac-procedury-whistleblowingowe.html.
- **PL check:** searched `system do zgłoszeń sygnalistów ustawa o ochronie sygnalistów ...`; found CRIDO "Sygnalizuj24h" (https://crido.pl/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/20240607_oferta_Sygnalizuj24h.pdf) and Wolters Kluwer LEX Sygnalista (listed in search results; its page URL returned 404 on check, so no link). **Equivalents exist; no pricing found. Likely not a gap** (recorded so later tasks don't re-research it). The foreign analogue (Whistleblower Software) page could not be fetched (404), so no foreign traction data.
