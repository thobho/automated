# Local-service and creator software (US / UK / CA)

Lens 1 findings. All pages accessed 2026-10-29. PL check = searches in Polish run via web search on the same date; a web-search miss is **weak evidence** of absence (see README Coverage).

## L1-F01 — Jobber (Canada): field-service software for home-service businesses
- **Product:** Jobber, https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/ — quotes, scheduling, invoicing, client messaging and payments for crews (cleaners, landscapers, HVAC).
- **Traction / pricing (own page):** Core $29/mo (1 user), Connect $99/mo, Grow $149/mo, Plus $399/mo (5 users) on annual billing; page cites "20,000+ home service pros" in its community and an aggregate rating of 4.9 from 153 reviews (self-reported). Payments/SMS are supported only in US, Canada, UK.
- **PL check:** searched `program dla firm usługowych zlecenia wyceny grafik ekip faktury aplikacja mobilna ... Jobber alternatywa Polska`. No Polish product surfaced; results were English (PocketSuite, Housecall Pro, Job Tracker Pro). **No equivalent found in this search** (weak evidence; Polish ERP/CRM such as Fakturownia are invoicing-only and were not checked for scheduling features — unverified).
- **Note for scoring:** the product needs PL invoice/KSeF handling and Polish VAT; no automatic payments outside US/CA per Jobber's page.

## L1-F02 — TutorCruncher (UK): management software for tutoring businesses
- **Product:** TutorCruncher, https://tutorcruncher.com/pricing/ — scheduling, lesson reports, invoicing and automatic tutor payroll, client portal.
- **Traction / pricing (own page):** Pay-as-you-go from £25/mo (2.65% card fee), Startup from £60/mo, Enterprise from £200/mo. Page structured data claims 5.0 from 575 ratings / 373 reviews and "more than 10 years" in market (self-reported, unverified; the page's structured data also contains an inconsistent £2,000 Enterprise price).
- **PL check:** searched `program do zarządzania szkołą korepetycji lekcje grafik rozliczenia korepetytorów oprogramowanie dla centrum korepetycji`. Surfaced Anolla (https://anolla.com/pl/oprogramowanie-dla-korepetytorow, a booking tool with Polish page; origin and depth not verified) and an Odoo blog article. **No dedicated Polish tutoring-centre management product found in this search**; Anolla is a partial competitor.
- **Why interesting:** niche vertical with recurring monthly fee and payroll/invoicing pain.

## L1-F03 — Pixieset (Canada): client galleries and proofing for photographers
- **Product:** Pixieset, https://pixieset.com/pricing/ — private client galleries, photo selection, print store.
- **Pricing (own page):** Free 3 GB (15% commission on store sales); Basic $8/mo annual, Plus $16, Pro $24, Ultimate $40 (annual). No user count on the page.
- **PL check:** searched `galeria dla klientów fotograf wybór zdjęć przez klienta platforma cena abonament polska alternatywa Pixieset`. Results were only English-language alternatives (Pixpa, Pic-Time, ShootProof, Picflow; source: https://alternativeto.net/software/pixieset). **No Polish-language gallery platform found.** Not checked: Polish wedding-photographer niche sites (unverified).
- **Caveat:** crowded globally (many alternatives); the gap would be Polish UI, PLN payments (BLIK/P24) and local print fulfilment.
