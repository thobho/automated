# Other markets: CZ, NL, FR, ES, Nordics, Asia, Brazil

Lens 1 findings added in T11 gap pass. All pages accessed 2026-10-29.

Method: for each candidate the vendor's own page (pricing or home) was fetched first, then one or two Polish-language web searches looked for a Polish equivalent. A search that finds nothing is weak evidence. Customer counts are the vendor's own claims (self-reported) unless stated otherwise.

## L1-F19 — Rentman (Netherlands): operations software for AV, event-production and equipment-rental companies
- **Product:** Rentman, https://rentman.io/pricing — inventory and equipment tracking (QR/barcode), crew and transport scheduling, project management, quoting and invoicing for AV/event production and party-rental firms.
- **Traction / pricing (own page):** €39/month platform fee plus modules per "Power User"/month: Inventory €14 (Standard) / €19 (Pro); Crew €14 / €19 / €24 (Essential / Standard / Pro); Organize €14; add-ons Quoting & Invoicing €9, History Logs €12, Equipment Tracking €9, extra warehouse €5. Basic users free; 8% off on annual billing. "50,000+ users" and Capterra 4.6/5 from 200+ reviews (self-reported). Copyright names "Rentman B.V."; Dutch origin is inferred from that, not stated on the page.
- **PL check:** searched `program do zarządzania wypożyczalnią sprzętu eventowego nagłośnienie oświetlenie` and `polski system dla wypożyczalni sprzętu scenicznego magazyn ekipa wycena`. Only foreign tools surfaced: HireTrack NX (https://www.capterra.pl/software/78309/hiretrack-nx), Rentopian, Rentman itself, IntelliEvent Lightning. The second search found a generic how-to article (https://appmaster.io/pl/blog/system-wypozyczania-sprzetu-skanowanie-mobilne) and trade news saying A-RENTAL is Poland's largest stage-equipment rental house (https://technologiesceny.substack.com/p/a-rental-inwestuje-w-creosy-od-glp), so the customer segment exists. **No Polish-built equivalent found in this search (weak evidence).**
- **Note for scoring:** A clear vertical with modular per-seat pricing, and the Polish customers (AV/stage rental houses, wedding/event rental) are easy to list. Rentman and HireTrack are already sold internationally. A Polish product would need a PLN/KSeF invoicing angle or a much simpler, cheaper tier for small rental shops to stand out.

## L1-F20 — AssoConnect (France): all-in-one admin for associations (members, dues, accounting, website)
- **Product:** AssoConnect, https://www.assoconnect.com/ — member CRM, membership/donation/ticketing forms, association accounting (chart of accounts, bank reconciliation, year-end closing), email, website builder, payments, and a business account with a French IBAN.
- **Traction / pricing (own page):** "Plus de 40 000 associations nous font confiance" (self-reported). Customers range from 8 to 800,000 members. A free "Offre Paiement" tier exists; the paid "Offre Sérénité" price is not shown on the home page. The /tarifs/ page returned empty content, so **paid pricing is unverified**.
- **PL check:** searched `program dla stowarzyszeń i fundacji ewidencja członków składki księgowość online` and `aplikacja do zarządzania stowarzyszeniem członkowie składki polska`. The first surfaced mostly generic or foreign content (ClickUp PL blog, Yapla). The second surfaced NGO Manager (Mconnect), which Capterra describes as built for associations and foundations in Poland, with dues and event management and a free version (https://www.capterra.co.nz/software/1237357/NGO-manager; search summary, not opened), plus ComUnity by the Polish firm EX-APP (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/comunity/id6502384322; search summary) and foreign tools that support PLN (VeryConnect, Raklet). **PL equivalents exist: NGO Manager (Mconnect), possibly ComUnity. Whether any of them covers Polish NGO accounting the way AssoConnect covers French accounting is unverified.**
- **Note for scoring:** The bundled bank account and payments part needs a licence and is out of scope. The accounting side (Polish NGO reporting) could be the angle if the existing tools don't cover it. Check NGO Manager's scope before scoring high.

## L1-F21 — Lステップ / L-Step (Japan): marketing automation on top of LINE official accounts
- **Product:** Lステップ, https://linestep.jp/lp/01/plan.html — segmentation, step messages, rich menus, click and conversion tracking, and booking calendars for SMBs running a LINE公式アカウント. Operated by ソーシャルデータバンク株式会社 (formerly 株式会社Maneql), Tokyo.
- **Traction / pricing (own page):** tax included, no setup fee. フリー ¥0 (~200 messages), スタート ¥5,000 (~5,000 msgs), スタンダード ¥21,780 (~30,000 msgs), プロ ¥32,780 (~50,000 msgs, includes 10 staff seats), Enterprise (Liny) not priced. Extra staff ¥1,100/month; webhook ¥5,500/month; webhook + API ¥11,000/month. The LINE account itself is billed separately by LINEヤフー. Group claims: 280 million messages sent per month, and Liny used by 184 municipalities, 38 prefectures and 7 ministries (self-reported, group-wide contracts). No SMB customer count is given.
- **PL check:** searched `automatyzacja marketingu WhatsApp Messenger dla małych firm polskie narzędzie segmentacja kampanie`. Only foreign platforms (ActiveCampaign WhatsApp, Delightchat, Deel Flows) and general explainers (https://clickup.com/pl/blog/512076/marketing-biznesowy-whatsapp) surfaced. **No Polish equivalent found in this search (weak evidence).** SMS and Messenger chatbot vendors were not searched.
- **Note for scoring:** The pattern is a niche CRM layered on a dominant chat channel, priced by message volume. LINE has no Polish counterpart, so this only transfers if a Polish SMB channel plays that role (WhatsApp, Messenger, or SMS). That demand is unproven. Treat it as a pattern, not a direct copy.

## L1-F22 — Mergado (Czechia): product-feed management for e-shops (comparison sites, marketplaces)
- **Product:** Mergado, https://www.mergado.com/pricing — edits and exports e-shop product feeds to comparison sites and marketplaces. Extensions cover image editing, marketplace listing (Allegro, Kaufland), translation, Heureka bidding, and scraping.
- **Traction / pricing (own page):** the plan-tier prices did not appear in the fetched text (unverified). Extension prices excl. VAT, monthly: Feed Image Editor €79 / 1,986 CZK, Marketplaces €11 / 286 CZK, Translate €18 / 486 CZK, Bidding Fox €79, Scraping Camel €37, Bidding Fox Elements €15. 30-day free trial; 20% off yearly. No customer count stated. Czech origin is inferred from the CZK pricing and Heureka focus (not stated on the page).
- **PL check:** searched `narzędzie do zarządzania feedem produktowym dla sklepów internetowych Google Shopping Ceneo polskie` and `Mergado feed Allegro polska klienci`. Results were platform plugins rather than standalone feed managers: Witly for Shoper (https://www.shoper.pl/aplikacje/integracja-z-google-merchant-google-shopping-feed-witly) and a Ceneo Feed XML Shopify app (https://open.store/shopify-apps/ceneo-feed-xml), plus explainer articles. Mergado itself already documents Allegro export (https://www.mergado.com/integration/allegro), so it already reaches the Polish marketplace. **Partial: plugin-level PL tools exist; no Polish standalone multi-channel feed manager surfaced in this search (weak evidence).**
- **Note for scoring:** A good fit for an API-minded solo dev (feeds are pure data transformation), but the space is crowded and Mergado already covers Allegro. The opening may be narrower: Ceneo- or Allegro-specific feed QA for WooCommerce/PrestaShop shops.

## L1-F23 — Twigsee (Czechia): kindergarten admin and parent communication app
- **Product:** Twigsee, https://www.twigsee.com/ — class management, tuition and meal fees, attendance, absence notes, the child register (matrika), admissions, and chat with parents. Serves public and private kindergartens and dětské skupiny. Part of the Bakaláři software family.
- **Traction / pricing (own page):** live counters show 309,633 users, 290,518 children, 30,934,400 photos and 10 language versions (self-reported). Prices are on a separate Ceník page, which was not fetched (unverified).
- **PL check:** searched `aplikacja dla przedszkoli komunikacja z rodzicami obecności opłaty za wyżywienie`. Several Polish systems surfaced: iPrzedszkole (parent guide at https://images.dlaprzedszkoli.eu/pp2korczak/iprzedszkole-instrukcja-dla-rodzicow(1).pdf), Obecności.pl by Itensity (https://apps.apple.com/pl/app/obecnosci-rodzice-opiekunowie/id6472890163), Inso, 4Parents and Pan Licznik (all from the search summary). **PL equivalents exist: iPrzedszkole, Obecności.pl, Inso, 4Parents, Pan Licznik.**
- **Note for scoring:** Not a gap. Publicly funded kindergartens buy through municipalities, which is a hard channel for a solo dev. Private żłobki/przedszkola might still be underserved on UX, but that is unverified.

## L1-F25 — TuriTop (Spain): booking system for tour and activity operators
- **Product:** TuriTop, https://www.turitop.com/pricing/ — booking calendar, real-time availability, online payments, channel manager for 70+ OTAs, resource manager, check-in app, API and webhooks, and a B2B reseller marketplace. TuriTop S.L., San Miguel de Abona, Tenerife.
- **Traction / pricing (own page):** Starter €29/month + 2% per online booking (min €0.10), Pro €99 + 1% (min €0.06), Enterprise €299 + 0.75% (min €0.03). Offline bookings carry no fee. 14-day free trial; a setup fee is mentioned but not quantified. ">1,000 active businesses", ">20 million processed bookings", Google 4.8 from 92 reviews (self-reported).
- **PL check:** searched `system rezerwacji online dla organizatorów wycieczek atrakcji turystycznych kalendarz bilety polski`. Foreign tools localised in Polish surfaced: Rezdy (Australia, https://www.capterra.pl/software/122690/rezdy), Anolla (https://anolla.com/pl/oprogramowanie-dla-ogrodow-zoologicznych) and PHPTRAVELS (self-hosted). The one Polish product, e-Rezerwacje24.pl by POP.PL (https://www.e-rezerwacje24.pl/docs/oferta_e-rezerwacje24.pl.pdf), targets accommodation. A Useme job post (https://useme.com/pl/jobs/rozwiazanie-do-zamawiania-biletow-grupowych-dla-atrakcji-turystycznej,136551/) shows a Polish attraction commissioning a custom group-booking tool. **No Polish-built tour/activity booking tool found in this search (weak evidence); foreign tools (Rezdy, Anolla) already market in Polish.**
- **Note for scoring:** The pricing (low monthly fee plus a per-booking cut) suits a solo dev. Polish customers would be escape rooms, kayak rentals, guided city tours, workshops and small attractions. Integrating with OTAs is a heavy build. A narrower MVP would be direct booking with Przelewy24/BLIK and group slots.

## L1-F26 — Z-API (Brazil): WhatsApp messaging REST API for SMB software and automations
- **Product:** Z-API, https://www.z-api.io/ — REST endpoints plus webhooks to send and receive WhatsApp messages from CRMs, ERPs, n8n, Make and Zapier. Use cases include sales follow-up, bots, and order or payment notifications. The fetched page does not say whether it uses Meta's official Business Platform (unverified). That matters for ToS risk.
- **Traction / pricing (own page):** Ultimate R$ 99,99 per instance/month (R$ 1.079,89/year, 10% off), "no message limit". Partner R$ 89,99 per instance/month for 10+ instances. 2-day free trial. "+100 mil clientes" (100k+), 99.98% uptime, "número 1 do Brasil" (all self-reported). Brazilian origin is inferred from the language, BRL and "suporte nacional".
- **PL check:** searched `WhatsApp API dla firm Polska wysyłka powiadomień integracja z CRM sklep cennik`. Only foreign providers and guides surfaced: Unipile's Polish-language pages (https://www.unipile.com/pl/whatsapp-api-kompletny-przewodnik-po-integracji/), mentions of Twilio and 360dialog as BSPs, Odoo's WhatsApp module, and an Elfsight overview. **No Polish-built equivalent found in this search (weak evidence).**
- **Note for scoring:** A pure API product with flat per-instance pricing fits a solo dev well, but in Poland SMS is the incumbent transactional channel and WhatsApp's SMB share is unverified. Unofficial session-based WhatsApp APIs carry account-ban and ToS risk. Score demand cautiously.
