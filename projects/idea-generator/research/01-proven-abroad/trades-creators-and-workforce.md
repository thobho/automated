Lens 1 findings. All pages accessed 2026-10-29. PL check = Polish-language web searches the same day (weak evidence of absence).

## L1-F15 — OpenSolar (Australia): free solar design and proposal software
- **Product:** OpenSolar, https://www.opensolar.com/ — solar design, quotes and proposals for installers; page says "100% Free of charge" (revenue from add-on services/marketplace not priced on the page).
- **Traction (own page):** "28,000+" active solar professionals, 185+ countries, 200+ partners (self-reported). Languages: English, Spanish, German, Italian, Hindi — **no Polish**.
- **PL check:** searched `program do projektowania i ofertowania instalacji fotowoltaicznych ...`. **Polish tools exist:** EasySolar (https://www.gramwzielone.pl/?p=101914), SOLCAD (https://www.gramwzielone.pl/?p=108901), BlueSol Design (https://www.gramwzielone.pl/energia-sloneczna/30355/bluesol-design-to-profesjonalne-oprogramowanie-do-projektowania-systemow-fotowoltaicznych-na-rynek-polski), PV BAKS. **Not a gap**; the foreign leader is free, which would make a paid Polish newcomer hard. Pricing of the Polish tools unverified.

## L1-F16 — plancraft (Germany): quotes, invoices and site management for tradespeople
- **Product:** plancraft, https://plancraft.com/ — browser + mobile app: quotes (AI drafts), invoices, time tracking, scheduling, DATEV export, e-invoicing, dunning for painters, roofers, electricians, etc.
- **Traction (own page):** "30,000+" tradespeople, Google 4.9 from 709 reviews, Capterra 5.0 (~30), App Store 4.2 (~90) (self-reported); versions for DE, AT, NL, UK, IT, ES. Prices not on the page (pricing URL 404).
- **PL check:** searched `program do wyceny remontów i kosztorysów dla małych firm wykonawczych ...`. Found Kalkulator Kosztorysu (mobile; https://www.instalki.pl/download/android/produktywnosc/kalkulator-kosztorysu/), Remonix (App Store; https://apps.apple.com/lc/app/remonix/id6670722907), WINBUD Kosztorys Start (desktop; https://inzynierbudownictwa.pl/?p=2544). **Partial equivalents exist**, mostly desktop estimating or small apps; a cloud all-in-one (quote + invoice + KSeF + crew scheduling) for small Polish ekipy remontowe was **not found in this search**. Needs a deeper review-based check (L2).

## L1-F17 — Topmate (India): bookable paid calls, products and memberships for experts
- **Product:** Topmate, https://topmate.io/ — one link page for booking paid 1:1 calls, webinars, courses, digital products; handles payments, calendar, reminders.
- **Traction / pricing (own page):** no monthly fee; takes 10% of sales via own link, 20% via marketplace; claims 4.8 million+ bookings and 100 crore INR paid to creators (self-reported, unverified). Buyers outside India pay in USD/EUR/GBP/CAD/SEK; payouts by PayPal or US bank — **no PLN or BLIK mentioned**.
- **PL check:** searched `płatne konsultacje 1:1 rezerwacja online dla ekspertów mentorów link do rezerwacji z płatnością BLIK platforma dla twórców Topmate odpowiednik`. Found only payment-gateway docs plus Calendesk (booking with BLIK, https://calendesk.com/glossary/what-is-blik) and naffy.io (Polish creator platform with BLIK, not verified for 1:1 booking). **No Polish Topmate-style marketplace found**; Calendesk and naffy.io are partial. Platform-fee-only model has low ARPU (L3) — flag.

## L1-F18 — Planday (Denmark): staff scheduling for hospitality and retail
- **Product:** Planday, https://www.planday.com/pricing/ — shift planning, time and attendance; from £2.99 per user/month (Starter, min. 5 users); 30-day free trial; UI in 8 languages (no Polish listed).
- **Traction (own page, testimonials only):** Domino's Denmark, Meininger Hotels, CEX (16-store franchise), Pasture Restaurants; no customer count.
- **PL check:** searched `grafik pracy pracowników restauracja sklep program do planowania zmian ...`. **Polish tools exist and are Labor-Code-aware:** inEwi (https://inewi.pl/blog/program-do-tworzenia-grafikow-pracy-ranking-najwazniejszych-funkcji), HRappka (https://hrappka.pl/?p=45735), Ordio (https://www.ordio.com/pl/branze/gastronomia), STAFF.IN (free app). **Not a gap**; recorded so later tasks skip it.
