# Allegro and e-commerce seller tools (PL)

Lens 2 findings. All pages accessed 2026-10-29. IDs `L2-Fnn` are stable anchors. Quotes are short and from the cited page. "Weak competition" here is relative: the Allegro integrator market is **crowded**; the findings below separate real gaps from price-driven churn.

## L2-F01 — BaseLinker/Base price hike pushed Allegro sellers to look for alternatives
- **Evidence:** Allegro Community thread, started 2024-10-04, replies to 2025-12-11: https://spolecznosc.allegro.pl/t5/zaawansowani-sprzedawcy/alternatywa-dla-baselinker-podwy%C5%BCka-od-stycznia-i-enterprise-od/m-p/883309. The opening poster expects costs to rise about 300%; other posters report +1,100%, +1,500% and one case of 3,000 to 45,000 (PLN, per the posters, unverified).
- **Quotes:** "koszty wzrosną o 300%"; a seller calls the tool "dość drogi i trochę przeładowany funkcjami" (fairly expensive, overloaded with features).
- **Second thread:** https://spolecznosc.allegro.pl/t5/zaawansowani-sprzedawcy/alternatywa-dla-baselinker/m-p/987268 (2021-03-13 to 2026-08-29): several users switched to Apilo for cost; one says the price dropped "from about 28,000 to 900 zł" (poster's claim, unverified).
- **Assessment:** an overpriced incumbent for large sellers, **but** at least 8 alternatives are named (Apilo, Sellasist, IDEAerp, xSale, Listerro, Pickgro, EasyUploader, Sello), so no empty market. The unfilled need named by posters is a cheap tool with stock sync **and** a simple warehouse/pick-and-pack assistant.
- **Evidence strength:** forum posts, self-reported; no independent price check.

## L2-F02 — Base (BaseLinker) pricing page: Business plan per-order fee, Enterprise above 5,000 orders
- **Evidence:** https://base.com/pl-PL/cennik/ — Freemium 0 zł (up to 100 orders/month); Business "279 zł + 0.99 zł per order" (net, shown for 1,000 orders/month); Enterprise by quote above 5,000 orders or 1 million PLN GMV per month, multiple accounts combined.
- **Why it matters:** confirms the pricing structure behind the complaints in L2-F01. The "Enterprise" threshold is what forum posters call the trigger.
- **Gap implied:** sellers at 1,000–5,000 orders/month pay per order; flat-fee rivals (see L2-F03) undercut them.

## L2-F03 — Apilo: flat plans, but ERP/stock-sync extras priced separately, support called slow
- **Evidence (pricing):** https://www.apilo.com/pl/cennik — Starter 39 zł (100 orders), Basic 199 zł (500), Professional 399 zł (1,500), Optimum 799 zł (5,000), Advanced 999 zł (10,000), net monthly. Extras: ERP integrations such as Subiekt GT from 1,788 zł/year and Symfonia 3,228 zł/year, enova365 149 zł/month, extra warehouse 59 zł/month, AI product descriptions from 0.50 zł per item. The page contradicts itself on some sync intervals and the Individual price (page text).
- **Evidence (complaint):** in the Allegro thread above, a user writes "support w apilo to żart XD" and another says support replies take 24–48 hours.
- **Assessment:** the cheaper challenger is already well-liked; weakness is support latency and integration add-on cost. Not an empty niche.

## L2-F04 — WooCommerce–Allegro plugins: few installs, thin ratings, one "dead plugin" review
- **Evidence:** WordPress.org search "allegro", https://wordpress.org/plugins/search/allegro/ — the leading free plugin "My auctions allegro" has 22 ratings and 500+ active installs; the other six listed have 0–1 ratings and 10–30 installs. Plugin page https://wordpress.org/plugins/my-auctions-allegro-free-edition/: 4.6 average (20 five-star, 2 one-star), updated October 2026.
- **Quotes from its two 1-star reviews:** "Dead plug-in do not use it" (2023-02-14, "Support is not answering since 9 month ago"); "Nie polecam tej wtyczki, skusiłem się ceną" (2021-12-08).
- **Assessment:** the WordPress Allegro-sync segment is small and fragmented; the paid pro edition's price is not stated on the page (unverified). Shops that run WooCommerce rather than Shoper/IdoSell are a niche that BaseLinker overshoots on price.

## L2-F05 — Booksy: 145 zł/month plus 45% first-visit commission, a recurring salon pain
- **Evidence:** https://jakdorobic.pl/ile-kosztuje-booksy/ (dated 2025-10-11, promotional site with a Booksy referral code, so treat as secondary): Booksy Biz 145 zł net/month for one user, +35 zł per extra staff; 45% net of the first visit for a client from Boost (minimum 25 zł, maximum 250 zł); online payments 1.89% + 0.49 zł; gift cards 3%. The page itself is internally inconsistent on extra-staff pricing.
- **Alternatives named in search results:** Koalendar, Versum (via https://craft.co/versum), Anolla (Polish page https://anolla.com/pl/oprogramowanie-dla-korepetytorow), eWAIT, Booklux — presence and depth **not verified**.
- **Assessment:** an expensive incumbent with commissions, but Polish challengers exist; the L2 gap would need a concrete salon-specific wedge. Salon complaints on Polish forums were **not found** in this session.
- **Primary source for current prices:** Booksy's own page was not read (unverified).

## L2-F06 — KSeF add-ons for WooCommerce: a market that is only now forming
- **Evidence:** WordPress.org search "ksef", https://wordpress.org/plugins/search/ksef/ — 19 results: only "Flexible Invoices for WooCommerce – KSeF Add-on" (wpdesk, 700+ installs, 1 rating) and "Flexible PDF Invoices" (6,000+ installs, 37 ratings) have meaningful installs. Fifteen of the 19 have 0–1 ratings and fewer than 10 installs.
- **Context:** an unrelated search result states KSeF became mandatory for micro, small and medium businesses on 1 April 2026 and penalties start 2027-01-01 (from a vendor-written snippet; confirm in the official text before use — see lens 5).
- **Assessment:** many fresh single-developer plugins and no clear leader outside wpdesk. This is a young market, not a neglected one. Do not read "0 ratings" as pain evidence.
