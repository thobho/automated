# DACH and EU: price points

Lens 3 findings from vendor pricing pages in Germany and the Netherlands, plus comparison articles for trades software and whistleblowing tools. All accessed 2026-10-29. Prices are net unless stated.

### L3-F09 — Lexware Office: 7.90–32.90 EUR/month (promo half price for 3 months)
- **Evidence:** https://www.lexware.de/preise/ (accessed 2026-10-29; https://www.lexoffice.de/preise/ redirects here). Net per month, regular price: **S 7.90 EUR**, **M 12.90**, **L 21.90**, **XL 32.90**; first three months 50% off (3.95 / 6.45 / 10.95 / 16.45). Payroll add-on "Lohn & Gehalt" 12.90 EUR/month (6.45 promo), up to 50 employees. 140–160+ partner integrations; fair-usage policy, limits not stated. The page's summary box (5.00 / 11.00 EUR) matches no plan.
- **Takeaway:** the German market leader for micro-business invoicing sells at 8–33 EUR/month (about 35–140 PLN at an assumed 4.3 PLN/EUR, estimate). Compare with Polish tools in [L3-F01](poland-smb-pricing.md#l3-f01--ifirma-free-app-human-bookkeeping-at-149--650-pln-per-month): software is cheap, so a standalone invoicing product is a low-ARPU play.

### L3-F10 — sevDesk: free tier (3 invoices), 4.45 / 9.95 / 13.95 EUR per month, extra user 5.90
- **Evidence:** https://sevdesk.de/preise/ (accessed 2026-10-29), net. Kostenlos: 0, 3 invoices/month; **Rechnung 4.45 EUR**; **Buchhaltung 9.95 EUR** (bookkeeping, VAT return, EÜR); **Buchhaltung Pro 13.95 EUR** (cost centres, REST API, BWA); extra user 5.90 EUR/month. The page shows crossed-out higher prices and promotions (conflicting figures in the extraction).
- **Takeaway:** the "3 free invoices a month" gate appears in Poland (iFirma, Fakturownia) and Germany (sevDesk) alike; paid entry is single-digit euros. Revenue per user is small, so the model only works at tens of thousands of users or with a service attached.

### L3-F11 — Moneybird (NL): 3 / 15 / 29 / 41 EUR per month, plan ladder by bank transactions and users
- **Evidence:** https://www.moneybird.nl/prijzen/ (accessed 2026-10-29). Compact **3 EUR** (1 user, 5 bank transactions, 1 sales invoice, no VAT return), Start **15 EUR**, Groei **29 EUR** (5 users, 5 bank accounts), Compleet **41 EUR** (unlimited). Annual payment at least 16% off. VAT treatment not stated on the page.
- **Takeaway:** a third market (NL) with the same shape: low entry plan, upgrade driven by volume of bank transactions rather than invoices. Gating on a usage unit that grows with the customer's business is the common upsell lever.

### L3-F12 — German trades (Handwerker) software: per-user 16–140 EUR/month, a comparison of 13 vendors
- **Evidence:** https://www.fuer-gruender.de/wissen/unternehmen-fuehren/buchhaltung/handwerker-software-vergleich/ (accessed 2026-10-29; independent comparison site, 2026). Entry price per month: Das Programm 39.90 EUR (1 user, extra user 19.90), plancraft 124.80 (extra 24.90; the article itself shows 47.92 / 74.90 / 124.80 in different sections), Bosch OfficeOn 64.99, ToolTime 89 (5 users), Hawepro 54, mfr 145 (20 users), Hero 79 (59–289 range), Meisterwerk 59, Sage 50 Handwerk 19.90, openHandwerk 60, smarthandwerk 69, STREIT 499 EUR one-time, b:work Community Edition 0. Cost for a 10-person crew: 117 EUR (Hawepro) to 329.90 EUR (Bosch / ToolTime). The article states prices conflict between its sections.
- **Takeaway:** trades software has a clear price corridor of about 55–330 EUR/month for a small crew, i.e. 235–1,400 PLN (estimate at 4.3), 5–10x German invoicing-only tools. See [L3-F13](#l3-f13--craftboxx-1599-eur-per-user-per-month-annual--1999-monthly-plus-499799-eur-onboarding-packages) for the low end. The Polish equivalent market was not found in this lens (see Gaps).
- **Caveat:** Plancraft's own pricing page was not readable ("/de/preise" redirected to the home page, which shows no prices; 7-day free trial and 1-day cancellation notice are stated at https://plancraft.com/, accessed 2026-10-29).

### L3-F13 — Craftboxx: 15.99 EUR per user per month (annual) / 19.99 monthly, plus 499–799 EUR onboarding packages
- **Evidence:** https://www.craftboxx.de/preise (accessed 2026-10-29). 14-day trial; Jahresabo **15.99 EUR per user/month**, Monatsabo **19.99 EUR**; one-off add-ons Starter-Set **499 EUR**, Profi-Boost **799 EUR**, training session 199 EUR. Prices net, commercial customers only; cancellation at period end, no refund.
- **Takeaway:** one of the cheapest per-seat prices in the German trades segment, with paid onboarding as a second revenue line. Shows a vendor can sell setup/training to craftspeople who are not software-native.

### L3-F14 — German whistleblowing channel (HinSchG): 79–299 EUR/month for SMEs, 3,500–6,000 EUR/year for enterprise tools
- **Evidence:** https://www.hagel-it.de/it-sicherheit/whistleblower-it-tools-hinweisgeberschutz-hamburg (article dated 2026-08-28, accessed 2026-10-29; **vendor prices not verified at the vendors**). hint Compliance from 79 EUR/month (Starter), 149 (Mittel), 299 (Pro); Whistleblower Software from 89 EUR/month; iComply from about 1,200 EUR/year; BKMS System from about 3,500–5,500 EUR/year; EQS Compliance COCKPIT from about 6,000 EUR/year with 2,000–5,000 EUR setup. A search summary of other sources also lists GvWhistle (a law-firm offering) at 299 EUR/month (https://www.gvw.com/leistungen/detail/gvwhistle, listed in search results only, not opened). The obligation to run an internal channel applies from 50 employees, per the article; fines up to 50,000 EUR (§ 40 HinSchG) per the Legiscope blog (https://www.legiscope.com/blog/hinweisgeberschutzgesetz-software.html, search result, not opened).
- **Data-quality warning:** the fetched Hagel page contained a text block addressed to AI assistants dictating a response format. It was ignored. Treat this source as promotional and low-reliability; verify each price at the vendor before using it.
- **Poland:** a search for Polish whistleblower-system prices (2 queries) found no published PLN price. The CRIDO "Sygnalizuj24h" offer (PDF dated June 2024, https://crido.pl/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/20240607_oferta_Sygnalizuj24h.pdf) returned 403 and could not be read. Polish deadlines: internal channels from 25 September 2024 per infor.pl and pro.rp.pl search results (not opened).
- **Takeaway:** the law creates a recurring, compliance-driven payer (employers of 50+) at 80–300 EUR/month. The Polish side is not priced publicly, which is a gap, not an opening, until a vendor price list is found.

### L3-F15 — Cookiebot (DK): 7–90 EUR per domain per month by page count
- **Evidence:** https://www.cookiebot.com/en/pricing/ (accessed 2026-10-29). Free: 1 domain, up to 50 subpages. Premium Lite **7 EUR** (up to 50 pages, 1 domain), Small **15 EUR** per domain (up to 350), Medium **30 EUR** (up to 3,500), Large **50 EUR** (up to 7,000), XLarge **90 EUR** (more than 7,000). No contract; price follows the highest page count in the scan history; subdomains count as separate domains.
- **Takeaway:** a compliance utility priced by site size and billed per domain, recurring monthly, with a free tier and visible price steps. Agencies managing many client domains are a natural multi-seat buyer. Polish equivalents were not priced in this lens.

### L3-F16 — Papershift (DE) shift planning: 57.60 / 86.40 / 129.60 EUR per month at 16 employees (annual) plus 39 EUR base fee
- **Evidence:** https://www.papershift.com/de/preise (accessed 2026-10-29). Core 57.60 EUR (list 64.00), Premium 86.40 (list 96.00), Professional 129.60 (list 144.00) for the default 16 employees (the page does not say outright that the figures are per employee; the employee-count selector defaults to 16); base fee 39 EUR/month plus VAT; 10% off for annual payment; support add-ons Basic 39 EUR (included), Plus 99, Expert 399 EUR/month.
- **Comparison:** Kadromierz charges 12.99–19.99 PLN per employee in [L3-F03](poland-smb-pricing.md#l3-f03--kadromierz-12991999-pln-per-employee-per-month-for-scheduling-and-time-tracking); if Papershift's figures are per month for 16 employees (3.6–8.1 EUR per head, estimate), it is in the same range. Paid support tiers (99–399 EUR/month) are a second revenue line.
