# Polish SMB tools: price points

Lens 3 findings. Every price below was read on the vendor's own page on 2026-10-29 (fetch tool, text extraction; a few pages render prices in images or JavaScript and are marked). Prices are **net (netto)** unless stated. "Anchor" means a number to compare a new product against, not proof that a product at that price is winning.

### L3-F01 — iFirma: free app, human bookkeeping at 149 / 650 PLN per month
- **Evidence:** https://www.ifirma.pl/cennik (accessed 2026-10-29). "Aplikacja" from 0 PLN/month (3 invoices free per month; Faktura+ add-on 12.50 PLN/month billed for 12 months; e-commerce integrator free up to 100 orders/month). Bookkeeping office plan for sole proprietors (ryczałt / VAT-exempt, up to 10 cost documents): **149 PLN/month**; for companies (full books, up to 10 documents): **650 PLN/month**; each extra 5 cost documents 33 PLN (sole proprietor) or 70 PLN (company); above 70 documents individual pricing. The page shows two effective dates (03/2026 and 09/2026) without saying which applies.
- **Who pays / how often:** every JDG and company pays monthly for bookkeeping; this is the largest recurring spend in the Polish micro-firm stack.
- **Comparison anchor:** the software layer (0–12.50 PLN) is priced near zero while the human layer costs 149–650 PLN. Value in this market sits in the *service outcome* (books done, tax filed), not in the tool.
- **Caveat:** it is a bookkeeping office price, not a software price; the 10-document limit makes it a "per-document" model.

### L3-F02 — Fakturownia: free Micro plan, paid tiers by users/companies, prices not machine-readable
- **Evidence:** https://www.fakturownia.pl/cennik (accessed 2026-10-29). Plans Micro, Start, Standard, Pro, Pro Plus. Micro: 0 PLN, 3 sales invoices/month, 1 user. Start and above: unlimited invoices; Standard: 3 users / 3 companies / 3 warehouses; Pro: 5 users, unlimited companies; Pro Plus: 10 users. Prices are "net for a 12-month plan; 20% discount with a promo code". Extra user packs: **49.99 PLN/month for 5 users, 83.46 PLN/month for 10 users**. The paid-plan prices were blank in the fetched text (the table is probably an image or script): **unverified**.
- **Takeaway:** the tier ladder is driven by users, companies and warehouses (accounting offices and multi-company owners pay more), not by invoice volume. A freemium wedge at 3 invoices/month is the market norm (iFirma, Fakturownia, sevDesk all use 3).

### L3-F03 — Kadromierz: 12.99–19.99 PLN per employee per month for scheduling and time tracking
- **Evidence:** https://www.kadromierz.pl/cennik (accessed 2026-10-29). Net per employee per month: "Planuję czas pracy" **12.99 PLN**, "Planuję i rozliczam" **15.99 PLN** (marked most popular), "Pakiet premium" **19.99 PLN** (sales call). Unlimited employees, locations and devices on all paid plans; annual payment gives "2 months free". The sign-up link on the page carries 12.99 for the middle plan, which conflicts with the displayed 15.99 (page inconsistency).
- **Who pays:** owners of shift-based businesses (retail, gastro, services). Recurring monthly, scales with headcount. A 20-person business pays roughly 260–320 PLN/month (arithmetic from the page, estimate).
- **Why it matters:** per-seat pricing at 13–20 PLN means 10,000 PLN MRR needs ~625–770 paying employees, i.e. ~30–40 businesses of 20 people. Per-employee pricing is a proven Polish SMB pattern.

### L3-F04 — Sellasist: order-volume ladder from 59 to 899 PLN per month, free up to 20 orders
- **Evidence:** https://www.sellasist.pl/cennik (accessed 2026-10-29). Free: 20 orders/month, 200 active offers, unlimited users. Paid ("abonament elastyczny", price set by last calendar month's volume): **59 PLN** (0–100 orders), **189** (101–500), **359** (501–1,000), **699** (1,001–5,000), **899** (5,001–10,000), then +449 PLN per extra 5,000 orders. Add-ons: Mini WMS 50 PLN per user/month, returns WMS mode 100 PLN/month, full WMS from 199 PLN per user/month.
- **Who pays:** Allegro / marketplace sellers; monthly; pay rises with their own revenue, so churn from low volume is automatic downward, upward expansion is built in.
- **Comparison:** compare with Base (BaseLinker) plans in [L2-F02](../02-weak-competition/allegro-and-ecommerce-tools.md#l2-f02--base-baselinker-pricing-page-business-plan-per-order-fee-enterprise-above-5000-orders). A new entrant would have to price below the 189–359 PLN band to attract 100–1,000-order sellers.

### L3-F05 — Shoper: shop platform at 299 PLN/month list, promotions of 29–33 PLN in year one
- **Evidence:** https://www.shoper.pl/cennik/ (accessed 2026-10-29). Standard / Standard+: list **299 PLN net/month** (3,249 PLN/year), promotional 29 PLN/month (339 PLN for first year when paid annually) and 33 PLN/month respectively; 50,000 products. Premium: from 729 PLN/month list (7,849 PLN/year), promotional 480 PLN/month, 500,000 products. Payment-provider commissions are charged per transaction (rates not on the page).
- **Takeaway:** the headline price is mostly a discount-led customer-acquisition device (about 10x year-one discount). Anyone building on top of Shoper sells to merchants who already pay ~300 PLN/month at renewal, so an add-on of 30–100 PLN/month sits below their platform bill.

### L3-F06 — SMSAPI: transactional SMS at 0.08–0.17 PLN per message plus a 49 PLN monthly fee
- **Evidence:** https://www.smsapi.pl/cennik (accessed 2026-10-29). Prepaid price by single top-up: 0.17 PLN (49–499.99 PLN) falling to 0.11 PLN (10,000 PLN+). Postpaid by monthly volume: 0.16/0.14 PLN (1–1,000 SMS) down to 0.10/0.08 PLN (50,001–100,000); the two columns are unlabelled in the extraction. Monthly fee **49 PLN net** is stated, unclear whether it applies to prepaid.
- **Takeaway:** a usage-priced API with a small fixed floor; resellers add booking/reminder products on top (see L3-F07 Booksy SMS at 0.10 PLN over a 100-SMS allowance, which prices SMS at roughly the wholesale rate).

### L3-F07 — Booksy Biz: 145 PLN + 35 PLN per extra calendar, ceiling 425 PLN, plus 45% on first visits
- **Evidence:** https://biz.booksy.com/pl-pl/blog/ile-kosztuje-program-dla-salonu-kosmetycznego-i-fryzjerskiego (accessed 2026-10-29), vendor's own article. Base 145 PLN net/month (first user); each extra staff calendar 35 PLN (users 2–9); from the 10th user no further fee, so the stated practical ceiling is **425 PLN net/month**. Free client app. Extra marketing SMS 0.10 PLN. Mobile payments 1.89% + 0.49 PLN; Tap to Pay 1.49%; gift cards 3%. "Booksy Boost": **45% of the net value of a new client's first visit**.
- **Who pays:** salon and barber owners, monthly; revenue also grows with transactions and commission. Complaints on this pricing: [L2-F05](../02-weak-competition/allegro-and-ecommerce-tools.md#l2-f05--booksy-145-złmonth-plus-45-first-visit-commission-a-recurring-salon-pain) and [L2-F15](../02-weak-competition/reviews-and-app-stores.md#l2-f15--booksy-29-on-trustpilot-16585-reviews-billing-and-cancellation-complaints) (not re-counted here).
- **Takeaway:** the vendor's own article gives no competitor prices (searched for them: none), so a cheaper rival has no public price benchmark except what the owner finds on each vendor's page. Versum's price was only "Custom" on a catalogue site, not read at the vendor.

### L3-F08 — Landingi (Polish-founded landing-page builder): 79 / 399 / 799+ PLN per month, annual billing
- **Evidence:** https://landingi.com/pl/cennik (accessed 2026-10-29; the old URL https://www.landingi.pl/cennik redirects here). Annual billing, net per month: **Build 79 PLN** (10 landing pages, 2,000 visits, 1 domain), **Optimize 399 PLN** (100 pages, 30,000 visits, 3 domains), **Scale from 799 PLN** (100k–500k visits tiers), **Enterprise from 4,400 PLN**. Annual billing gives two months free versus monthly; monthly prices are not shown in the extraction.
- **Takeaway:** a Polish-language, PLN-priced, usage-tiered marketing SaaS exists and sells at 5x–10x steps between plans; the ladder is by *visits*, so revenue grows with the customer's traffic. Shows B2B marketing tooling in Poland is sold in PLN at 79–800 PLN/month.
