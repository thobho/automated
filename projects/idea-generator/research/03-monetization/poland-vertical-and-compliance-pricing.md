# Polish vertical and compliance price points

Lens 3 findings added in T11 gap pass. Every price read on the vendor's own page on 2026-10-29 unless marked.

Prices are **net (netto, +23% VAT)** unless stated. Fetches used a text-extraction tool; where the extracted text was ambiguous this is marked in the caveat.

### L3-F32 — Weter.pl (vet clinic software): 899 PLN/year for 3 users (~74.92 PLN/month), +249 PLN/year per extra user
- **Evidence:** https://weter.pl/cennik.html (accessed 2026-10-29). "Pakiet dla lekarzy weterynarii" (farm and companion animals): **899 PLN net per year**, shown as **74.92 PLN net/month** equivalent with annual payment. Includes **3 users** with 3 separate warehouses and 3 treatment books. Each extra user **249 PLN net/year** (20.75 PLN/month equivalent). Worked example on the page: 5 users = 1,397 PLN net/year (116.42 PLN/month). **3 months free** trial. Above 20 users: individual terms. Standard data import free; custom work quoted separately.
- **Who pays / how often:** owner of a small vet practice; annual prepayment.
- **Comparison anchor:** Shepherd (US) costs 299–349 USD/month for one vet (L3-F19); Weter.pl is roughly 15–20x cheaper per month for 3 users. Polish vet software sits at Booksy's base price level (145 PLN/month, L3-F07) or below. A search summary also mentioned Lecznica.eu ("first year free, then 8 PLN/month") and Veterinaro ("from 56 PLN/month"): **unverified**, not fetched.
- **Caveat:** no monthly-billing option shown. The page does not say how many clinics use it. Gross prices not stated.

### L3-F33 — Medfile (physio / psychologist / medical practice EDM): free, then 109 / 164 / 274.10 PLN gross per month (annual), +79–159 PLN per extra user
- **Evidence:** https://medfile.pl/cennik (accessed 2026-10-29). Prices stated as **gross (incl. 23% VAT)**. Free: 0 PLN, 1 user, 40 visits/month, 10 SMS. Plus: **109 PLN/month on annual billing** (1,308 PLN/year), extra user +79 PLN/month. Premium (marked most popular): **164 PLN/month annual** (1,968 PLN/year), extra user +89, adds telemedicine, online payments, fiscal printer. Business: **274.10 PLN/month annual** (3,289.20 PLN/year), extra user +159, adds all AI modules, KSeF, patient portal, ZnanyLekarz integration. Cards also show 119 / 179 / 299 PLN, probably monthly-billing prices (not labelled). Add-ons: ZnanyLekarz integration 5.90–19.90 PLN per user/month, 1,000 SMS 99 PLN/month. 30-day Premium trial.
- **Who pays / how often:** solo physiotherapist, psychologist or small clinic; monthly or annual.
- **Comparison anchor:** at ~89–133 PLN net/month (gross ÷ 1.23, own arithmetic) the mid tiers sit just below Booksy (145 PLN net, L3-F07). The 40-visit free tier is the hook, as with the 3-invoice free tiers in L3-F01/F02.
- **Caveat:** no profession-specific pricing; physio and psychologist pages exist but use the same plans. Which of the two figures per card is monthly billing is inferred.

### L3-F34 — Moja Wspólnota (wspólnoty / property managers): 99–1,049 PLN/month base + 0.10–0.50 PLN per unit
- **Evidence:** https://www.moja-wspolnota.pl/oferta.php/cennik (accessed 2026-10-29). For **zarządcy** (from 01.01.2023, net): monthly fee + per-unit (lokal) fee: 1–200 units **99 PLN + 0.50 PLN/unit**; 201–500 **199 + 0.40**; 501–1,000 **249 + 0.25**; 1,001–2,000 **349 + 0.17**; 2,001–3,000 449 + 0.15; 3,001–5,000 649 + 0.13; 5,001–10,000 1,049 + 0.10; above 10,000 individual. For a **wspólnota** managing itself: up to 100 units, **1 PLN per unit**, billed once a year. Full function only with FN-Serwis's "Wspólnoty Mieszkaniowe" program; the page says it is not an offer.
- **Who pays / how often:** professional property manager (zarządca), monthly; or self-managed wspólnota, yearly.
- **Comparison anchor:** a zarządca with 500 units pays ~199 + 200 = ~399 PLN/month (own arithmetic, if the per-unit fee is monthly). Desktop alternative Pro Virtus (https://programy.nieruchomosci.pl/cennik/, fetched same day): one-off licence 2,098–2,798 PLN per workstation by unit count, a 199–1,498 PLN fee per extra ledger, and resident portal (IKM) **75–249 PLN/month**. Buildium (US) starts at 65 USD/month (L3-F21). Also seen: eMieszkaniec (https://emieszkaniec.pl/cennik/) 499 PLN/year (shown as 41.58 PLN/month) for a small wspólnota "program" package, and "from 200 PLN net/month" with bookkeeping included.
- **Caveat:** the page does not say whether the per-unit fee is monthly. Price list dated 2023; still published.

### L3-F35 — Portal OSK (driving school software): 129 / 299 / 599 PLN per month, −20% annual
- **Evidence:** https://portal-osk.pl/ (accessed 2026-10-29). Net, +23% VAT. Starter (1 instructor, up to 20 students) **129 PLN/month** or 103/month annual (1,236 PLN/year); Standard (up to 5 instructors, 100 students, marked most popular) **299** or 239 annual (2,868/year); Pro (unlimited) **599** or 479 annual (5,748/year). 14-day trial, no card. No setup fee mentioned.
- **Who pays / how often:** OSK owner; monthly or annual.
- **Comparison anchor:** two other Polish OSK tools fetched the same day: **OSKAdmin** (https://oskadmin.pl/cennik/) fixed fee 95 / 195 / 295 PLN + 35 / 45 / 50 PLN per instructor/month (annual billing; net/gross not stated); **Apkadoprawka** (https://apkadoprawka.com/pricing) **gross** 125 / 299 / 549 PLN monthly or 100 / 239 / 439 quarterly, by active instructors (1–3 / 4–7 / 8+). So the Polish OSK niche prices at ~100–600 PLN/month and charges by instructor, not student. Jobber's 29–399 USD (L3-F17) range is the closest foreign analogue.
- **Caveat:** at least three priced competitors already exist, so the OSK niche has public price competition. A search summary also listed OskSoft (149–299 PLN, licence 4,990 PLN) and eZapisOSK (290–490 PLN + 2,400–3,900 PLN setup): **unverified**, not fetched.

### L3-F36 — Mecenas.iT (law-firm software): 89 / 149 PLN per user per month, or 3,999 / 5,999 PLN perpetual

Wolters Kluwer LegalDesk returned 403; a search summary cited "START 85 PLN net/month" and "from 80.75 PLN": **unverified**.
- **Caveat:** perpetual licences still exist in this niche. Promo labels on kancelariaonline have no end date.

### L3-F37 — LangLion (language-school software): per active student, minimum 115 PLN per month
- **Evidence:** https://langlion.com/pl/cennik (accessed 2026-10-29). Monthly billing, "pay only for active students", no long-term commitment. **Minimum fee 115.00 PLN/month**. Core modules Administracja + E-dziennik; add-ons (online payments, finance, e-learning, CRM, registration, media library) are priced in a calculator. The per-student price and module prices did not render in the fetched text. 14-day trial.
- **Who pays / how often:** owner of a language or tutoring school; monthly, scales with enrolled students.
- **Comparison anchor:** per-active-student pricing is the education version of Kadromierz's per-employee model (L3-F03). The 115 PLN floor is below Booksy's 145 PLN base (L3-F07).
- **Caveat:** per-student rate **unverified** (calculator is JavaScript). A Capterra listing seen only in a search summary gave "from 32.63 USD per user": unverified.

### L3-F38 — anonimowysygnalista.pl (whistleblower channel): 199 / 269 / 389 / 600 PLN per month, 12-month contract
- **Evidence:** https://anonimowysygnalista.pl/ (accessed 2026-10-29). Net per month, **12-month contract**. Basic **199 PLN** (1 channel); Standard **269** (2 channels, template procedure); Gold **389** (3 channels, procedure + online training for all employees); Enterprise **600** (5+ channels, private subdomain; more channels individual). All include encrypted channel, unlimited database, operator training.
- **Who pays / how often:** employer with 50+ workers (the ustawa o ochronie sygnalistów obligation), or a public body; monthly over a fixed year.
- **Comparison anchor:** German HinSchG channels at 79–299 EUR/month (L3-F14), about 340–1,290 PLN; Polish prices are lower. Other Polish pages fetched the same day: **sygnalizuj.net.pl** (https://sygnalizuj.net.pl/cennik) one plan at **229 PLN net/month** (281.67 gross), per entity, 30-day trial; **sygnalista.org.pl** (https://sygnalista.org.pl/cennik/) Standard / Optimum / Premium, which most likely reads as **150 / 190 / 390 PLN net/month** (see caveat), the higher tiers adding document templates, 2–3 h/month consultations and legal phone support. The Polish SME whistleblowing price band is therefore **~150–600 PLN/month**.
- **Caveat:** no tier is tied to employee count. For sygnalista.org.pl the extracted text reads "15000PLNnetto miesięcznie"; a search summary rendered it "150^00PLN", so the cents are probably superscript and the price is 150.00 PLN. Treat the exact value as **unverified**.

### L3-F39 — e-nform (whistleblowing and reports system): Silver 361–397 PLN/month, Gold 879, Platinum from 14,500 PLN/year
- **Evidence:** https://enform.pl/o-e-nform/ (accessed 2026-10-29). Net. Silver (smaller organisations, 3 coordinators): **397 PLN/month + 680 PLN setup**, or 4,329 PLN/year; a second block on the same page shows **361 PLN/month / 3,936 PLN/year** (conflict). Gold (larger organisations, 10 coordinators, training included): **879 PLN/month**, 9,588 PLN/year, setup 1,000 PLN (another block says 1,200). Platinum (dedicated, on the client's server): **from 14,500 PLN/year**, perpetual licence available at an unstated price. 14-day trial for all tiers.
- **Who pays / how often:** mid-size and large employers and public bodies; monthly or annual, plus one-off setup.
- **Comparison anchor:** the upper end of the Polish band in L3-F38; Platinum matches the German enterprise tier of 3,500–6,000 EUR/year (L3-F14) in PLN terms. Tiers are set by the number of coordinator seats, not by employee headcount.
- **Caveat:** two inconsistent price blocks on one page; confirm the current figure. Sygnanet (Wolters Kluwer LEX Hub) returned 403; a search summary cited 335 / 585 / 835 PLN/month by number of recipients: **unverified**.

### L3-F40 — Lejdis Prawo (outsourced IOD / RODO): from 200 PLN/month for firms, from 450 PLN for medical
- **Evidence:** https://lejdis-prawo.pl/rodo/inspektor-ochrony-danych/ (accessed 2026-10-29). Net, monthly, +23% VAT. IOD subscription for firms without health data **from 200 PLN/month**; for medical facilities / sensitive data **from 450 PLN/month**. Consultation packs 190 (1 h) / 350 (2 h) / 600 (4 h) PLN/month; ad-hoc hour from 250 PLN; annual RODO audit from 150 PLN/month; UODO breach notification from 450 PLN. **12-month term**, then monthly notice.
- **Who pays / how often:** SMEs, clinics and practices that must or choose to appoint an IOD; monthly retainer.
- **Comparison anchor:** **DOIT** (https://doit.biz.pl/outsourcing-iod/, fetched same day): Start **from 1,200 PLN net/month** (up to 50 employees), Rekomendowany **from 2,400** (50–250), Enterprise individual. M3M's 4,000–5,000 PLN for mid-size firms (L3-F29). So outsourced IOD in Poland spans **~200 to 5,000+ PLN/month**, about 25x, depending on scope and response-time promises.
- **Caveat:** a search summary quoted Lejdis at "from 99 PLN net/month with annual prepayment"; the page fetched today says 200, so the search snippet is stale or from another page. The final price is set after a call.

### L3-F41 — codetech.dev (WCAG accessibility audit): from 1,500 PLN net for up to 10 subpages
- **Evidence:** https://codetech.dev/cennik/ (accessed 2026-10-29). Package "Audyt": **from 1,500 PLN net** (+23% VAT), **up to 10 subpages**, report with priorities and repair estimates, KPRM methodology, 60-minute post-audit consultation. It includes recommendations for the deklaracja dostępności, not the declaration itself. Turnaround not stated.
- **Who pays / how often:** public bodies (ustawa o dostępności cyfrowej) and, since the European Accessibility Act, e-commerce and service firms; **one-off**, possibly repeated after redesigns.
- **Comparison anchor:** EAA remediation quotes of 1,500–60,000 EUR (L3-F30) start far above this. One-off audits contrast with the recurring 150–600 PLN/month whistleblowing subscriptions (L3-F38). A search summary listed other Polish audit prices of 640–5,999 PLN net (crafton.pl, ks.pl, qualityisland.pl and others): **unverified**, not fetched.
- **Caveat:** it is a "from" price from a web agency that also sells WordPress sites, so the audit may lead into a rebuild sale. Netguru's audit page (fetched) shows no price, only a quote form.
