# US and global niche SaaS: price points

Lens 3 findings from vendor pricing pages in the US plus two EU/Polish-founded exporters. All accessed 2026-10-29, USD or EUR as listed, net unless stated. Purpose: show what vertical SaaS charges *per month per business*, to compare with Polish and DACH tools.

### L3-F17 — Jobber (CA/US field-service SaaS): 49–499 USD/month monthly, 21–280 annual (introductory)
- **Evidence:** https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/ (accessed 2026-10-29). Core 1 user: **49 USD/month** monthly or 21 annual; Connect 1 user 139 / 70; Grow 1 user 199 / 105 (5 users 299 / 161); Plus 5 users 499 / 280. Extra user **29 USD/month**. The page says annual rates are introductory and rise after 12 months (e.g. Core to 29 USD). Custom pricing for 16+ users and franchises.
- **Who pays:** owners of lawn, cleaning, HVAC and similar service crews, monthly. Price steps are by features and users, not by jobs.
- **Takeaway:** 5x spread between entry (49) and top (499) tiers on the same product; most revenue per customer comes from upgrading as the crew grows.

### L3-F18 — Housecall Pro: 59–299 USD/month (annual) / 79–329 (monthly), first-period promos
- **Evidence:** https://www.housecallpro.com/pricing/ (accessed 2026-10-29). Basic **59** (annual) / 79 (monthly), 1 user; Essentials **149** / 189, 5 users; Max **299** / 329, 8 users, extra user 35 USD/month. Promo first period: Basic and Essentials 26 USD for 1 month, Max 99 USD for 3 months. Some cards showed unreadable "$XX/mo" placeholders.
- **Comparison:** same band as Jobber, which gives a US price anchor of about 60–300 USD/month for a 1–8 person field-service business, against 55–330 EUR for the German trades tools in [L3-F12](dach-and-eu-pricing.md#l3-f12--german-trades-handwerker-software-per-user-16140-eurmonth-a-comparison-of-13-vendors).

### L3-F19 — Shepherd (vet practice software, US): 299–349 USD/month for one vet, +99 for the 2nd and 3rd, data migration 2,475 USD
- **Evidence:** https://www.shepherd.vet/pricing (accessed 2026-10-29). First doctor **349 USD/month** (299 with Shepherd Pay), doctors 2–3 at 99 each, further doctors 49 each; unlimited users, onboarding and support included. Unlimited relief vets 99 USD/month. Multi-location 249 per location. Non-profits 299 flat. Texting 99 USD per 5,000 messages. Data migration **2,475 USD** one-off (or 230 USD/month for 12 months). Billing starts 12 weeks after onboarding begins.
- **Takeaway:** a niche vertical charges per professional rather than per user, adds a one-off migration fee of roughly 7 months' subscription, and discounts for in-house payments (a payments-attach strategy). This is a US benchmark; the Polish vet-software market was not priced here (see Gaps).

### L3-F20 — Time To Pet (pet-sitting/dog-walking software, US): 25 / 50 / 72 / 79 USD per month
- **Evidence:** https://www.timetopet.com/pricing (accessed 2026-10-29). Lite **25 USD/month** (250/year), Solo **50** (500/year), Team 72 for 2 active staff (40 base + 32), Facility (daycare/boarding) **79**, Full Service custom. 14-day trial, no card; annual plans billed for 10 months (two free). Active staff = anyone with at least one scheduled service in the period.
- **Takeaway:** a very small micro-niche still has a four-step ladder starting at 25 USD; solo operators are the entry segment, facility owners the high-ARPU segment.

### L3-F21 — Buildium (property management, US): from 65 / 202 / 420 USD per month, unit limits not published
- **Evidence:** https://www.buildium.com/pricing/ (accessed 2026-10-29). Essential **65 USD/month**, Growth **202**, Premium **420** (all "starting at"); the page lists no unit limits or minimums and routes 5,000+ units to a phone number. Add-ons: Showings Coordinator from 41 USD/month; Property Inspections 99 USD setup plus 40–95 USD/month. Onboarding required for Growth and Premium.
- **Takeaway:** add-on modules priced at 40–100 USD on a 65–420 base show an "expand with modules" model. Polish landlords/administrators were not priced (a search for Polish wspólnota software prices returned nothing relevant).

### L3-F22 — Tidio (Polish-founded chat SaaS), priced in USD: free / 24.17 / 49.17 / 300 per month
- **Evidence:** https://www.tidio.com/pricing/ (accessed 2026-10-29). Free (50 billable conversations), Starter **24.17 USD**, Growth from **49.17**, Plus from **300** plus usage, Premium on request. Add-ons: Lyro AI Agent from 32.50 USD/month, Flows from 24.17. The page says annual billing gives 2 months free but does not state whether the listed prices are annual or monthly rates (the odd .17 suggests annual-equivalent, an inference).
- **Takeaway:** the price unit is *billable conversations*, i.e. usage of the customer's own business; the AI add-on is sold as a separate line. The vendor is based in Poland but prices in USD, so an entrant selling to Polish SMEs in PLN would be competing on price against a product that exports at USD levels.

### L3-F23 — Brand24 (Polish-founded media monitoring): 199–599 USD/month annual, 249–699 monthly
- **Evidence:** https://brand24.com/prices/ (accessed 2026-10-29). Individual **199 USD/month annual** (249 monthly), 2K mentions, 3 keywords; Team 299 / 349; Pro 399 / 499; Business 599 / 699 (100K mentions, 25 keywords); Enterprise from 1,499 annual. Overages are charged for mentions above the limit; extra keywords are not auto-charged.
- **Takeaway:** a Polish-founded niche B2B tool has an entry price around 800–1,000 PLN/month (USD→PLN at an assumed 4.0, estimate) and sells in USD; the market for mid-priced monitoring/marketing tools is clearly priced in the hundreds of dollars, well above any Polish invoicing tool.

### L3-F24 — iubenda (IT): privacy/cookie policy generator and consent from 4.99 EUR/site/month, accessibility add-on 6–50 EUR
- **Evidence:** https://www.iubenda.com/en/pricing (accessed 2026-10-29). Per site per month, billed yearly / monthly: Essentials **4.99 / 5.99 EUR** (25,000 pageviews, 20 clauses), Advanced **19.99 / 21.99** (50,000 pageviews), Ultimate **79.99 / 89.99** (150,000). Extra pageviews 0.05 EUR per 1,000. Accessibility plans (powered by AccessiWay): Lite **6 EUR**, Standard **50 / 55 EUR** (150,000 pageviews). 14-day trial, 14-day refund; prices exclude VAT.
- **Takeaway:** a compliance utility with a 16x price spread (5 to 90 EUR) driven by traffic; the accessibility line is the one linked to the European Accessibility Act. Together with Cookiebot ([L3-F15](dach-and-eu-pricing.md#l3-f15--cookiebot-dk-790-eur-per-domain-per-month-by-page-count)) it gives two EU pricing pages for site-compliance tools, both with a free or sub-10-EUR entry. Polish-language equivalents were not priced in this lens.
