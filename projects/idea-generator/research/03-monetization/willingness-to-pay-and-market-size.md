# Willingness to pay and market size

Lens 3 findings on what customers pay today for the *job* (not the tool), whether a feature is still sellable, and how many potential payers exist. Accessed 2026-10-29.

### L3-F25 — Polish bookkeeping offices charge 150–500 PLN/month for a JDG and 600–5,000+ PLN for a company
- **Evidence:** https://cenauslug.pl/poradniki/ile-kosztuje-prowadzenie-ksiegowosci-porownanie-biur-rachunkowych-i-programow-online (updated May 2026; accessed 2026-10-29; a price-comparison site, so commercially motivated). Net per month: JDG ryczałt up to 15 documents 150–350 PLN; KPiR non-VAT up to 20 documents 200–400; KPiR with VAT 250–500; sp. z o.o. full books up to 20 documents 600–1,500; 50–100 documents 2,000–5,000+. Payroll extra: 50–100 PLN per employee. Online programs: about 600 PLN/year self-managed and 2,400 PLN/year with accountant support (about 50 and 200 PLN/month, calculated).
- **Cross-check:** iFirma's own price list rises 12% for the standard package from September 2026 (211 PLN) while the ryczałt non-VAT package stays 149 PLN, per a search summary of https://www.ifirma.pl/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/RB_9_2026_zmiana_cennika_BR.pdf (PDF not opened); see also [L3-F01](poland-smb-pricing.md#l3-f01--ifirma-free-app-human-bookkeeping-at-149--650-pln-per-month).
- **Takeaway (willingness to pay):** Polish micro-firms demonstrably pay 150–500 PLN/month, every month, for the *outcome* of accounting. A product that automates part of an accountant's workload and sells to the **biuro rachunkowe** (many clients per office) rather than to the end client can charge on the same budget line.

### L3-F26 — KSeF connectivity is bundled free by the mainstream invoicing tools (counter-finding: not a standalone price point)
- **Evidence:** https://www.vatax.pl/blog/obsluga-ksef-porownanie-cen ("updated 15 October 2026", published by VATAX, which also sells Claro; accessed 2026-10-29) and https://fakturownia.pl/integracja-z-ksef (accessed 2026-10-29: "full, free integration", no extra fee on any plan). Per VATAX: Claro 0 / 5 PLN; inFakt from 4.99 PLN net with KSeF included; wFirma 19 PLN (invoicing) and 54 PLN (invoicing + accounting); Faktura.pl from 18.99 PLN; FakturaXL free up to 10 invoices; iFirma free up to 3 invoices. ERP side: Comarch Optima KSeF from 15 PLN/month cloud (20 invoices); Symfonia from 59 PLN for 120 operations and "from 2,340 PLN/year" for the API; InsERT 300 PLN per 1,000 points (1 point per document); enova365 and Fakt free. Some figures conflict inside the article (Symfonia 59 vs "38 per 100 e-invoices").
- **Takeaway:** the sending/receiving of KSeF invoices is priced at about zero for small firms. Willingness to pay appears only in (a) ERPs with per-document metering and (b) the layer on top (reconciliation, accountant workflow, AI classification). Do not count "KSeF connector" as a revenue idea; see also [L2-F10](../02-weak-competition/dev-tools-and-polish-apis.md#l2-f10--ksef-libraries-already-crowded-no-gap-in-the-basic-client-counter-finding).

### L3-F27 — Poland has ~2.9 million active firms, 95.9% micro; 15–17% are in construction and trade each
- **Evidence:** https://xyz.pl/dzieje-sie/gus-rosnie-liczba-firm-w-polsce-najwiecej-jest-mikroprzedsiebiorstw-1487 (media report citing GUS, Q3 2025: 2.875 million active firms, +5% year on year, micro 95.9%; the page does not name the GUS publication; accessed 2026-10-29). A search summary adds construction at 15.2% and trade/vehicle repair at 17.2% (same family of reports, not opened). Primary GUS release not read: **unverified at source**.
- **Use in sanity check:** at 99 PLN/month, 10,000 PLN MRR needs ~101 payers; against ~2.8 million micro firms that is 0.004%. Even a niche of 30,000 firms (estimate) passes the 20x rule in [method/criteria.md](../../method/criteria.md) easily. The constraint is not market size but reaching and converting payers.

### L3-F28 — Germany: 1,038,126 craft businesses (Handwerk) at end of 2025, 6.24 million employment relationships
- **Evidence:** https://www.zdh.de/daten-und-fakten/kennzahlen-des-handwerks/wirtschaftlicher-stellenwert-des-handwerks-2025/ (ZDH, accessed 2026-10-29). Betriebe 2025: 1,038,126 (change -0.0%); employment relationships 6,237 thousand (-1.3%, estimated; counting method changed in 2024).
- **Use:** at the German trades corridor of 55–330 EUR/month ([L3-F12](dach-and-eu-pricing.md#l3-f12--german-trades-handwerker-software-per-user-16140-eurmonth-a-comparison-of-13-vendors)), 1 million businesses is a ~1 billion EUR/year addressable pool for the software layer alone (estimate: 1M × 80 EUR × 12, assumes every business pays; real penetration is far lower and not sourced). The point is that the DACH niche-software market is an order of magnitude larger than the Polish equivalent, while a Polish-speaking solo founder has no language moat there.

### L3-F29 — Outsourced data-protection officer (IOD) in Poland: ~4,000–5,000 PLN/month for mid-size, from 5,000 for regulated firms (one provider, July 2026)
- **Evidence:** search summary of https://comparic.pl/outsourcing-iod-w-firmie-finansowej-ile-kosztuje-i-co-sprawdzic-przed-podpisaniem-umowy/ (provider M3M's comparison, July 2026; page **not opened**; accessed 2026-10-29 via search). No small-firm monthly rate was found (one query).
- **Takeaway:** compliance *services* are priced at thousands of PLN/month, while compliance *software* for small firms (iubenda 5–90 EUR, Cookiebot 7–90 EUR, whistleblower tools 79–299 EUR) sits two orders of magnitude lower. The gap suggests a "do part of the IOD's job" product could be priced at 150–500 PLN/month without being cheap relative to the service it replaces (idea-level inference, unvalidated).
