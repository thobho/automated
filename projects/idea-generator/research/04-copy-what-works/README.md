# Lens 4 — Copy what already makes money

Research for lens 4 of [BRIEF.md](../../BRIEF.md); scoring anchors in [method/criteria.md](../../method/criteria.md). All sources accessed 2026-10-29. Findings are in the topic files; IDs `L4-Fnn` are stable anchors. This lens is about **proof that a model makes money**, not about gaps in Poland (lens 1): where a Polish founder is the proof, that only shows the revenue is reachable from Poland.

**Headline:** 28 findings (F01–F28). 15 are **Stripe/RevenueCat/Paddle-verified revenue profiles on TrustMRR** (8 global, 7 Polish-based founders); 7 are marketplace listings (Flippa/Acquire, seller-reported); 6 are founder-published pages or benchmarks. Patterns:
1. **Polish-based founders reach 20k+ USD MRR in B2B and consumer niches.** Calendesk (vertical booking SaaS, 20,804 USD MRR, 371 subs, bootstrapped, since 2020) is the cleanest proof (F09); Visualizee.ai (profession-specific AI tool, 8,434 MRR, F13), Czat.ai (Polish-language GPT chat at 19 PLN/month, 7,534 MRR, F10), Zanfia (creator platform, F11) and OpenAlternative (directory, F12) are smaller. Two consumer/growth apps (F14, F15) show 19–29k USD MRR but the verification is stale (expired API keys).
2. **Niche developer APIs and self-hosted licences are the best fit for a solo developer with agents**: EmailEngine (237 annual licences at 1,450 USD, 19.4k MRR, Estonia, F03), Conductor (49 USD per QuickBooks Desktop file, 45k MRR, F07), Capgo (1,204 subscriptions, 33k MRR, F05), Image-Charts (50 subscriptions, 6.3k MRR over nine years, F04: the slow end).
3. **Small extensions and plugins mostly stay small.** Listings show 0.9–5k USD monthly revenue (F17, F20–F22), CodeCanyon and Shopify are power-law markets (F25, F26), and extension roundup claims are self-reported and years old (F24). The 10,000 PLN MRR bar (~2,500 USD) needs recurring prices and an audience, not a one-off tool.
4. **Evidence that no source is clean**: TrustMRR itself warns its numbers can differ from provider dashboards by up to 30%, lists only founders who opt in, and Flippa asking prices are not sale prices. L4 = 5 needs two close-model products with public revenue, one ≥ ~2,500 USD MRR: F03/F07/F09 each meet the bar alone, so a close-model pair exists for **vertical booking SaaS** (F08, F09), **developer API/licence** (F03, F07) and **AI utility with Polish-language moat** (F10 only: one product, so L4 ≤ 4 for that model).

## Seeds
Idea sketches for the longlist (T11). None is validated; each links to its finding. Overlap with seeds from lenses 1–3 is noted and must not be double-counted (lens 4 supplies only the "revenue proof").

1. **Vertical booking/CRM SaaS for one Polish professional niche (psychotherapists, physios, driving schools)**, 100–250 PLN/month, tied to Polish payments and RODO client records. Proof: Calendesk 20.8k USD MRR at 79–147 USD with a Polish founder; ConvertLabs 28k USD MRR. [L4-F09](poland-founders-trustmrr.md#l4-f09--calendesk-poland-20804-usd-mrr-371-subscriptions-bootstrapped-booking-saas-at-79147-usdmonth), [L4-F08](trustmrr-verified.md#l4-f08--convertlabs-us-28382-usd-mrr-472-subscriptions-at-67--197--397-usd-per-month). Overlaps lens 3 seeds 5, 6, 9; competition (Booksy, Versum, Calendesk itself) must be checked: **Calendesk is a direct Polish competitor to this seed**.
2. **Annual-licence, self-hosted developer component for a Polish/EU integration** (KSeF or ePUAP client, bank-statement parsing, JPK/e-declaration validation) sold as a licence at 1,000–2,000 USD/year. Proof: EmailEngine 237 licences at 1,450 USD. [L4-F03](trustmrr-verified.md#l4-f03--emailengine-estonia-19419-usd-mrr-237-subscriptions-at-1450-usdyear-self-hosted-developer-api). Overlaps lens 1/2 KSeF-SDK seeds; new here is the pricing/revenue proof. Free open-source competitors exist (lens 2).
3. **Per-company adapter API for a legacy Polish accounting/ERP desktop product** (Subiekt/Optima/Symfonia style), 20–50 USD per company per month. Proof: Conductor 45k USD MRR with 49 USD per QuickBooks Desktop file. [L4-F07](trustmrr-verified.md#l4-f07--conductor-us-45034-usd-mrr-299-subscriptions-at-49-usd-per-quickbooks-desktop-file-per-month). Risk: vendors' licence terms and an unknown number of integrators in PL (not measured).
4. **Polish registry/validation API with credit pricing** (NIP/REGON/KRS/VIES, IBAN, address). Proof: 1Lookup's usage-based credit model. [L4-F06](trustmrr-verified.md#l4-f06--1lookup-us-261262-usd-mrr-from-a-usage-based-phoneemailip-validation-api-listed-for-sale), [L4-F04](trustmrr-verified.md#l4-f04--image-charts-france-6261-usd-mrr-50-subscriptions-543011-usd-all-time-since-2017). Free official APIs (GUS, MF white list) compete, so it is a convenience wrapper only; 1Lookup's volume comes from a different market.
5. **Profession-specific AI tool for Polish architects/interior designers/real-estate agents** with a 3-step ladder (60 / 140 / 320 PLN/month, estimate). Proof: Visualizee.ai 8.4k USD MRR in about 2 years. [L4-F13](poland-founders-trustmrr.md#l4-f13--visualizeeai-poland-8434-usd-mrr-295-subscriptions-at-15--35--80-usd). Model-provider commoditisation and image-quality expectations are risks.
6. **Polish-language consumer assistant at 19–39 PLN/month** for one concrete job (letters to offices, tenant rights, school paperwork). Proof: Czat.ai 7.5k USD MRR, 955 subscribers. [L4-F10](poland-founders-trustmrr.md#l4-f10--czatai-poland-7534-usd-mrr-955-subscriptions-from-19-plnmonth-a-polish-language-gpt-chatbot-front-end). Low ARPU (~8 USD) and LLM cost per user unknown; consumer support load.
7. **Directory + sponsorship asset for regulated buyers** ("RODO-friendly / EU-hosted / KSeF-ready" tool directory) with a few high-priced vendor placements. Proof: OpenAlternative 6.2k USD MRR from 24 subscriptions. [L4-F12](poland-founders-trustmrr.md#l4-f12--openalternative-poland-6183-usd-mrr-24-subscriptions-a-directory-monetized-through-sponsorships-and-listings). Needs SEO traffic first; distribution (lens 8) decides it.
8. **Local-SEO / Google Business Profile rank-and-report SaaS for Polish agencies and franchises**, 100–300 PLN/month per client location. Proof: listed Maps rank tracker at 19.1k USD monthly revenue, 522 subscribers (seller-reported). [L4-F16](acquisition-listings.md#l4-f16--google-maps-rank-tracker-for-local-seo-agencies-us-listing-191k-usd-monthly-revenue-152k-profit-522-subscribers). Competition from global rank trackers not assessed.
9. **Seller-side extension/add-on for one marketplace workflow (Allegro/OLX/Vinted) priced at 29–99 PLN/month** with annual billing. Proof: small extensions run 0.9–5k USD monthly (F17, F21) and roundup claims of 3–42k (F24, self-reported). [L4-F17](acquisition-listings.md#l4-f17--recurring-email-service-over-gmail-chrome-extension--workspace-add-on-romania-508k-usd-monthly-revenue-503k-profit-5-years-old), [L4-F21](acquisition-listings.md#l4-f21--amazon-seller-chrome-extension-flippa-canada-881-usd-mrr-63-subscribers-169-usd-monthly-profit-12-churn--small-and-inconsistent), [L4-F24](benchmarks-and-founder-posts.md#l4-f24--eight-extension-revenue-claims-roundup-by-extensionpay-claims-by-founders-on-indie-hackers-years-20192021). Weak on its own; unofficial-UI and ToS risk. Overlaps lens 2 Allegro seeds.
10. **Software + advisory ESG/CSRD-reporting kit for Polish SME suppliers** (questionnaire, emission calculation, customer-ready report), 300–1,200 PLN/month (estimate). Proof: a Netherlands EU/UK sustainability platform listed at 19k USD monthly revenue, 41% margin. [L4-F18](acquisition-listings.md#l4-f18--eu--uk-sustainability-platform-for-smes-netherlands-listing-19k-usd-monthly-revenue-784k-profit-4-years). The regulatory deadline belongs to T06; margin is lower due to human advice.
11. **Open-revenue, EU-privacy-positioned utility** (analytics, consent, forms) with a public /open page as marketing. Proof: Simple Analytics ~49.6k USD MRR, 1,285 customers. [L4-F23](benchmarks-and-founder-posts.md#l4-f23--simple-analytics-nl-about-496k-usd-mrr-5957k-arr-1285-paying-customers-published-on-its-own-open-page). A crowded category, so only with a niche wedge.
12. **Polish templates/tools for JDG owners sold as digital products** (KSeF-ready invoice workbook, tax-calendar planner). Proof: Notion-template income claims (unverified, audience-dependent). [L4-F27](benchmarks-and-founder-posts.md#l4-f27--notion-template-creators-80k1m-usd-claims-no-verified-data-kajabi-article-2024-claims-from-20212022). Weak evidence; low ceiling without an audience.

## Gaps
1. **No German, Czech, Nordic or Spanish product with public revenue was found**; the DACH, CEE and Nordic proof sits only in Estonia/France/Netherlands items. Next: Indie Hackers location filters, TrustMRR country pages (`/country/de`, `/country/cz`), Acquire/Flippa searches with country filters, Czech/German founder blogs.
2. **Indie Hackers, the main self-reported source, was unreadable (403).** Next: a browser session, Wayback copies, or the IH data in newsletters (Indie MRR).
3. **Only ~100 of 182 Polish TrustMRR startups were visible**; ordering is unsorted. Next: the TrustMRR API or later pages of `/country/pl`; also check those with expired keys (F14, F15) after reconnection.
4. **Listing-level URLs for F16–F19 were not visible**, only search-table figures. Next: open each listing on Flippa and note date, verified status and profit calculation.
5. **Chrome Web Store, WordPress.org, Shopify App Store top lists and Product Hunt were not read**, so install and review counts as indirect traction (the L4 = 3 anchor) are missing. Next: fetch top-N lists in 2–3 categories and compare Polish equivalents.
6. **No cost or margin data for the TrustMRR products** (LLM/API cost of F10, F13, F15; third-party data cost of F06). Next: founder posts on X and IH on gross margin.
7. **Churn and age effects are unknown**: all-time revenue vs MRR suggests growth stages differ widely (F04: 9 years, 543k; F14: 17 months, 308k). Next: MRR history charts where TrustMRR shows them.
8. **Etsy/Gumroad bestseller evidence was not found as figures**; only unverified Notion-template claims (F27). Next: Gumroad creator pages that expose sales counts, Etsy shop sales counts for Polish-language digital products.
9. **FX rates are assumed (~4 PLN/USD)**; recheck with NBP before any score uses PLN conversions.
