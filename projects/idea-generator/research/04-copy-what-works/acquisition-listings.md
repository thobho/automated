# Marketplace listings (Flippa, Acquire.com)

Lens 4 findings from businesses **listed for sale**. Evidence tier: seller-reported numbers on a marketplace listing; Flippa marks some as "Vetted + Data Verified" (revenue, expenses, traffic reviewed by Flippa staff, Stripe connected) but asking prices are wishes, not sale prices. Listings are survivorship-biased the other way (sellers are exiting; some are inflated). Accessed 2026-10-29.

Source for F16–F19: one Flippa SaaS search result page (675 results; only ~24 visible in the fetch, so a partial, unsorted sample): https://flippa.com/search?filter%5Bproperty_type%5D=saas (accessed 2026-10-29). Listing-level URLs were not visible in the fetch; the figures are those shown in the search table. Re-check on the listing before relying on any number.

### L4-F16 — Google Maps rank tracker for local-SEO agencies (US listing): 19.1k USD monthly revenue, 15.2k profit, 522 subscribers
- **Claim / who / verifiability:** seller-reported: monthly revenue 19.1K, profit 15.2K, 273K ARR, 71% margin, 522 subscribers, age 1 year, asking 575,000 USD (Flippa SaaS search page above). Self-reported listing; verification status of this one not visible.
- **Model:** niche monitoring SaaS for agencies (local SEO rank tracking) with an AI "coach" add-on. Pays per client location; B2B recurring. About 37 USD per subscriber per month (computed).
- **PL/EU angle:** Polish local-SEO agencies and Google Business Profile owners are a listable audience; Polish-language report + pricing in PLN. Local-search rank tracking competitors were not assessed here (lens 2).

### L4-F17 — Recurring-email service over Gmail (Chrome extension + Workspace add-on, Romania): 5.08k USD monthly revenue, 5.03k profit, 5 years old
- **Claim / who / verifiability:** seller-reported: monthly revenue 5.08K, profit 5.03K (almost no costs), age 5 years, asking 350,000 USD, "highly rated extension, no maintenance" (Flippa SaaS search page, accessed 2026-10-29). Asking multiple about 5.7x annual profit (computed from the listing).
- **Model:** a single-feature extension inside a platform people already live in (Gmail), paid subscriptions. Evidence that a Chrome extension/add-on can run at near-100% margin for years; also a platform dependency (Google can change add-on rules).
- **PL/EU angle:** extension/add-on for a Polish daily-use web app (Allegro seller panel, BaseLinker, iFirma/wFirma web UI, KSeF portal). Platform risk is high; see the unofficial-UI caveat from lens 2.

### L4-F18 — EU & UK sustainability platform for SMEs (Netherlands listing): 19k USD monthly revenue, 7.84k profit, 4 years
- **Claim / who / verifiability:** seller-reported: monthly revenue 19K, profit 7.84K, age 4 years, asking 448,220 USD; assessments, carbon-impact reporting and advisory (Flippa "First Access" listing, Flippa SaaS search page above, accessed 2026-10-29). Mix of software and advisory revenue; the split is not visible.
- **Model:** compliance/ESG reporting for SMEs sold as software plus human advice. Margin about 41% of revenue (computed), lower than pure SaaS, which supports "software + services" as the revenue shape.
- **PL/EU angle:** CSRD/ESG value-chain reporting for Polish SME suppliers pressed by large customers; regulatory timeline belongs to lens 5 (T06), not counted here.

### L4-F19 — AI captioning SaaS (France listing): 5.83k USD monthly revenue, 3k profit, 5.5k MRR, 100% organic
- **Claim / who / verifiability:** seller-reported: monthly revenue 5.83K, profit 3K, MRR 5.5K, age 3 years, asking 110,000 USD (reduced from 175,000), "100% organic, zero management" (Flippa SaaS search page above, accessed 2026-10-29). Price cut of 37% from the first ask suggests the first ask was too high (computed).
- **Model:** single-purpose AI utility (video captions) selling by SEO. Exposed to price pressure from free alternatives and platform-built captions.
- **PL/EU angle:** Polish-language captioning/transcription (Polish speech models are weaker, so quality is a possible edge); lens 2 should check competitors such as existing Polish transcription tools.

### L4-F20 — WordPress ratings plugin (Acquire.com listing): 6.1k USD ARR, 9k USD TTM profit, 251–500 customers, one person
- **Claim / who / verifiability:** seller-reported on Acquire: ARR 6.1K (last month x 12), TTM profit 9K on TTM gross revenue 8.9K, last month revenue 737 and profit 700, +10% annual growth, churn 3–5%, asking 31,500 USD (3.5x profit), founded January 2023, sole founder, freemium open-source with a premium plugin and two linked free plugins of about 70K and 20K active installs. https://app.acquire.com/public/vvc1ctl45x-profitable-wordpress-ratings-plugin-9k-ttm-profit-250-customers (accessed 2026-10-29). Note: TTM profit 9K exceeds TTM revenue 8.9K on the page; the figures are not internally consistent.
- **Model:** freemium WordPress plugin with a premium upsell; a realistic outcome for a niche plugin: hundreds of customers, ~10k USD/year. Well under the 10,000 PLN/month target: that is ~120,000 PLN/year, while this listing's ~9k USD/year is ~36,000 PLN/year at an assumed ~4 PLN/USD (estimate).
- **PL/EU angle:** WooCommerce plugins for Polish needs (invoices, shipping, payment, Allegro sync). Several Polish ones exist (lens 2, F-items on WooCommerce); here the lesson is the scale of a single small plugin.

### L4-F21 — Amazon-seller Chrome extension (Flippa, Canada): 881 USD MRR, 63 subscribers, 169 USD monthly profit, 12% churn — small and inconsistent
- **Claim / who / verifiability:** Flippa marks it "Vetted + Data Verified" (Stripe connected): MRR 881 (headline says 1.1K), 63 subscribers (headline 65), monthly profit 169, site age 2 years, overall churn 12%, asking 26,500 USD, listing ended. The headline claims 70% margins while profit/MRR is about 19% (computed). https://flippa.com/12164346 (accessed 2026-10-29).
- **Model:** niche extension for Amazon sellers (listing/profitability evaluation) with a monthly subscription; the example shows how small a niche extension stays without distribution.
- **PL/EU angle:** Amazon.pl/Allegro/Temu seller analytics extensions; low ceiling per product, so it needs a bundle or a distribution channel. Counter-evidence for a quick 10k PLN/month.

### L4-F22 — Bulk file downloader Chrome extension (Flippa): 10.5k active users, one-time 9.99 USD upgrade, 322 sales — counter-finding
- **Claim / who / verifiability:** seller-reported: yearly profit about 8.4K USD (stats say 361 USD/month; description says about 700/month: conflicting), 10.5K active users in the past month, about 30K installs in the last year, store rating 2.7/5 from 31 reviews, monetization from April 2025, 322 sales (419 USD in April to 799 in July), asking 10,000 USD, listing ended (outcome unknown). https://flippa.com/12085118 (accessed 2026-10-29).
- **Model:** one-time paid upgrade, no recurring revenue; implied conversion about 1.1% of 30K yearly installs (322/30,000, computed).
- **Takeaway:** shows realistic extension conversion at a one-time 9.99 USD price: low three-digit sales for ~10k active users. Recurring pricing would be needed for 10,000 PLN MRR.
