# Founder-published numbers and store benchmarks

Lens 4 findings from founders' own open pages, roundups of self-reported extension/plugin revenue, and distribution statistics for app stores and marketplaces. These show **how the model pays and how skewed it is**; several are dated or estimated, which each finding states. Accessed 2026-10-29. Indie Hackers product/interview pages returned 403 to the fetch tool, so Indie Hackers claims here are quoted via third-party roundups (marked) or search snippets (marked "search summary, unverified").

### L4-F23 — Simple Analytics (NL): about 49.6k USD MRR, 595.7k ARR, 1,285 paying customers, published on its own /open page
- **Claim / who / verifiability:** the company's own live revenue page shows MRR ~49.6k USD, ARR 595.7k, 1,285 paying customers, "expect ~44k by the end of this month", NPS 57 all-time (20 in the last 30 days), 22B pageviews since September 2018; monthly expense and revenue reports since October 2019 are said to exist but costs and profit were not extractable, and the profit chart is labelled stale by 941 days. https://dashboard.simpleanalytics.com/open (accessed 2026-10-29). Self-published; the MRR may be connected to the payment provider, unverified. TrustMRR lists it at 38k MRR (category page https://trustmrr.com/category/saas, accessed 2026-10-29), a different number; the gap is unexplained.
- **Model:** privacy-first web analytics, EU-hosted, a crowded category (vs Plausible, Fathom, Google Analytics), reaching about 1,285 customers (~39 USD per customer per month, computed) through a "privacy" position and open-startup transparency.
- **PL/EU angle:** an EU-privacy-first alternative to a US tool in a regulated function (analytics, consent, forms, e-signature, backup) with the Polish/UODO angle; open revenue as a marketing tool. Overlaps lens 5 only through GDPR enforcement.

### L4-F24 — Eight extension revenue claims (roundup by ExtensionPay; claims by founders on Indie Hackers; years 2019–2021)
- **Claim / who / verifiability:** the roundup lists: GMass (Gmail email campaigns, 130k USD/month as of 2019, developer interview, 8–20 USD/month), Closet Tools (Poshmark automation, ~42k USD/month "verified" by the creator, 30 USD/month), CSS Scan (100k+ USD revenue, one-time 69 USD, screenshot August 2020), GoFullPage (10k USD/month, last listed 2021), Spider (10k USD in two months, 38 USD), Night Eye (3.1k USD/month), BlackMagic (Twitter tools, 3k USD/month up from 2.1k, Tony Dinh), Weather Extension (2.5k USD/month). https://extensionpay.com/articles/browser-extensions-make-money (accessed 2026-10-29). **Self-reported, mostly 3–7 years old, published by a vendor of extension payments (ExtensionPay) with an interest in the topic;** the underlying Indie Hackers pages were not readable (403). The "3.7 million per month" AdBlock heading in the article is not supported by its body text.
- **Model:** niches for sellers/marketers (Poshmark, Twitter, Gmail outreach) paid 8–30 USD/month, or one-time 69 USD tools for developers. Closet Tools and GMass show a reseller-economy and sales-tooling pattern.
- **PL/EU angle:** seller-automation extension for an Allegro/OLX/Vinted seller cohort (Vinted is large in PL, unverified in this lens) or LinkedIn/Gmail outreach tooling in Polish. Platform ToS and unofficial-UI risk is high (lens 9).

### L4-F25 — CodeCanyon WordPress plugin earnings are extremely skewed (Freemius analysis of Envato data, 2016 — old)
- **Claim / who / verifiability:** from Envato's public API through 13 November 2016, revenue estimated as licenses sold x listed price with a flat 30% commission: average WordPress plugin gross about 3,838 USD/year (~224 USD/month net of commission); about 2.4% of plugins earn over 1,000 USD/month; 7 plugins (under 0.15%) over 10,000 USD/month; 61.6% sold under 100 licenses; average price about 18.91 USD. https://freemius.com/blog/codecanyon-wordpress-plugins-analysis/ (accessed 2026-10-29). Estimate, one marketplace, 10 years old, author is a vendor of a rival licensing platform.
- **Model:** a low-priced one-time-license marketplace; the distribution is a power law. The useful signal is the cut-off: about 1 in 40 plugins crosses 1,000 USD/month.
- **PL/EU angle:** do not build a 19-USD one-off plugin for 10,000 PLN MRR. Prefer annual licences (see L4-F03) and a niche with an existing audience.

### L4-F26 — Shopify App Store revenue concentration (RevenueHunt, June 2026, **estimated** not measured)
- **Claim / who / verifiability:** 22,546 live apps from 131 countries; the top 1% (225 apps) take an estimated 72% of app revenue and the top 10 nearly 30%; only about 36% of apps appear to earn anything; the US and Canada together about 57% of revenue; median app has 5 reviews; the most common (median) entry price is 9.99 USD. The report states "Revenue is estimated, not measured" (modelled from published pricing plus review and install signals). https://revenuehunt.com/state-of-the-shopify-app-economy/ (accessed 2026-10-29). RevenueHunt sells a Shopify app (vendor-adjacent).
- **Model:** per the vendor-independent search summary only (search summary, unverified): Shopify's cut is 0% on the first 1 million USD of lifetime gross app revenue and 15% above (not read at Shopify; check the partner terms before relying).
- **PL/EU angle:** Poland has no Shopify dominance (Shoper/IdoSell/WooCommerce are larger locally, per lens 2 evidence; not re-verified here). Treat as a benchmark for marketplace-listed B2B apps: success needs a top-slice position in a narrow category.

### L4-F27 — Notion-template creators: 80k–1M USD claims, no verified data (Kajabi article, 2024; claims from 2021–2022)
- **Claim / who / verifiability:** Nick Lafferty "over $80,000 in just 12 months" (his own blog), Easlo "over $500,000" cumulative and "$20,000 monthly" by October 2022 (no link for the monthly claim), Thomas Frank "$1 million" from two templates by 2022 (Business Insider Africa link) and ~15k USD/month in 2021; "many creators earn 1,000–3,000 USD/month" with no source. https://kajabi.com/blog/can-you-make-money-selling-notion-templates (accessed 2026-10-29). **Unverified, vendor article (Kajabi sells a rival platform)**, claims are 2–3 years old.
- **Model:** digital products sold on Gumroad or own store; income depends on audience, not on the template. Closest to the BRIEF's "digital products" field.
- **PL/EU angle:** Polish-language templates (JDG bookkeeping tracker, KSeF-ready invoice sheet, matura planner) for an audience you can reach; revenue evidence for Poland not found (Gap). Strongly audience-dependent, so lens 8 matters more than lens 4.

### L4-F28 — Starter Story's ideas database shows ~3,600 businesses with revenue, but no stated verification — weak
- **Claim / who / verifiability:** the database says "3,619 ideas" with monthly revenue and a "Solo Score" (0–100, undisclosed weights); in a fetch of an earlier page, four ideas were shown with 20k–100k USD/month (e.g. "Pdf bank statement ..." 40k USD/month) with titles truncated. The page does not say how revenue is collected or verified; most data is behind sign-up. https://www.starterstory.com/data and https://www.starterstory.com/ideas (accessed 2026-10-29). **Unverified; use only as a lead generator, then confirm at the founder.**
- **Model:** category medians shown on the ideas page (Micro SaaS ideas 10k USD/month, productized services 40k, marketplaces 200k) were relayed by the fetch summary and are not confirmed by the second page (the /data page states no medians), so treat as unverified.
- **PL/EU angle:** a "PDF bank statement converter" type niche (document-to-structured-data for accountants) maps to Polish biura rachunkowe and mBank/PKO statement formats; bank statement formats in PL are not assessed here.
