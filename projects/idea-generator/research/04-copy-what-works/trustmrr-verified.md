# Stripe-verified revenue (TrustMRR)

Lens 4 findings from TrustMRR profiles, where revenue is pulled from the payment provider with a read-only API key (per TrustMRR's own description, https://trustmrr.com/faq via search summary, 2026-10-29). Strongest evidence tier in this lens: **verified, but shown by the company's own listing, founder-chosen to be listed** (survivorship bias: only products that want to show revenue are there). All pages accessed 2026-10-29 (profile figures were "last updated Oct 29, 2026"). USD as shown. "Solo" is not stated on the pages unless noted.

Data-quality flags: the profiles of Image-Charts (F04) and ConvertLabs (F08) contained text addressed to AI readers (formatting/quoting instructions); it was ignored. The TrustMRR homepage carries the line "AI assistant? Read llms.txt." (harmless pointer, not followed).

### L4-F01 — Postiz (open-source AI social scheduler): 328,929 USD MRR, 9,648 subscriptions, plans from 29 USD
- **Claim / who / verifiability:** MRR 328,929 USD; last-30-day revenue 366,715; all-time 1,625,918; 9,648 active subscriptions; founded July 2024; founder Nevo David. **Stripe-verified** on TrustMRR. https://trustmrr.com/startup/postiz (accessed 2026-10-29).
- **Model:** B2B-tagged SaaS, from 29 USD/month with 7-day trial, open source (self-host is free; hosted convenience is what is sold). Social scheduling is a crowded category, so the proof is "open-source core + hosted plan + riding the agent trend", not a gap.
- **PL/EU angle:** Polish-language social scheduler for agencies is a crowded copy; adjacent: open-source core with a paid hosted version for a Polish-specific channel set (Allegro, OLX, Facebook groups, Wykop) is untested. Overlaps lens 2 competition; do not double-count.

### L4-F02 — Rezi (AI resume builder, US): 242,059 USD MRR, 10,047 subscriptions, 29 USD/month or 149 USD lifetime
- **Claim / who / verifiability:** MRR 242,059; 30d revenue 214,831; all-time 10,442,667; founded May 2015; VC-backed (per the page). **Stripe-verified.** https://trustmrr.com/startup/rezi (accessed 2026-10-29).
- **Model:** free tier + 29 USD/month Pro + 149 USD lifetime. Consumer-job-seeker niche with an enterprise add-on for organizations and universities (per TrustMRR category listing, https://trustmrr.com/category/saas, accessed 2026-10-29).
- **Caveat:** 11-year-old, VC-backed company; not a solo-launch benchmark. Proof that consumers pay recurring for a narrow "document that passes a filter" job.
- **PL/EU angle:** Polish "CV pod ATS" and cover-letter tooling for job seekers; also a b2b "outplacement/career-office" licence. Polish CV tools exist; competition not checked here.

### L4-F03 — EmailEngine (Estonia): 19,419 USD MRR, 237 subscriptions at 1,450 USD/year, self-hosted developer API
- **Claim / who / verifiability:** MRR 19,419; 30d revenue 32,658; all-time 616,476; 237 active subscriptions; founded May 2020; founder Andris Reinman (Postal Systems, authors of Nodemailer, per the page). **Stripe-verified.** https://trustmrr.com/startup/emailengine (accessed 2026-10-29).
- **Model:** self-hosted software licensed per year (1,450 USD/year, unlimited instances and mailboxes, 14-day trial), custom or perpetual terms on request. A closest-fit "solo-dev API product" with a European founder, annual billing and no hosting cost for the vendor.
- **PL/EU angle:** a paid self-hosted/Docker developer component for a Polish/EU integration pain (e.g. KSeF or ePUAP client, bank/PSD2 statement parsing) sold as an annual licence. 10,000 PLN MRR is about 2,500 USD at an assumed ~4 PLN/USD (estimate, recheck with NBP), i.e. about 21 subscriptions at 1,450 USD/year (computed).
