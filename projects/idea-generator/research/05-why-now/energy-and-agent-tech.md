# Energy subsidies and agent technology

Lens 5 findings on subsidy-driven demand and on technology shifts that make a feature possible. All sources accessed 2026-10-29.

### L5-F22 — Czyste Powietrze: from 2027 only heat pumps among electric heating; changes of 20 Jul 2026; 10 bn PLN budget, ~70,000 applications since Mar 2025
- **Evidence:** Globenergia article https://globenergia.pl/od-dzis-czyste-powietrze-zmienia-zasady-wyzsze-dotacje-i-wiecej-czasu-ale-jest-haczyk/ (fetched). Changes effective **20 Jul 2026**: +10% on maximum rates for insulation, windows, doors and garage gates; advance-payment deadline extended from 120 to 180 days. From **2027**, after a transition period, the programme funds only heat pumps among electric heating devices (electric boilers and resistance heating drop out). Eligibility depends on the device being on the ZUM list on the date of the advance invoice. Since 31 Mar 2025 about 70,000 applications and 53,000 agreements worth 3.3 bn PLN; current edition budget 10 bn PLN from the Modernisation Fund. Search snippets add: energy audit before work and energy certificate after are mandatory; maximum comprehensive grant up to 136,200 PLN, or 170,100 PLN with PV; air-to-air heat pumps 4,400–11,100 PLN (secondary figures: verify at nfosigw.gov.pl). https://direct.money.pl/artykuly/porady/czyste-powietrze-zmienia-zasady-od-20-lipca-niektorzy-wlasciciele-domow-dostana-wiecej (search snippet)
- **Who pays/benefits:** homeowners (applicants) and installers who guide them; installer counts not found.
- **Product category:** installer-side tooling: grant eligibility calculator, application document bundle, ZUM-list checker, audit-to-quote workflow. Ciepłe Mieszkanie (multi-family/municipal) not researched in detail.
- **L5 implication:** a stable program with a visible 2027 rule change gives ongoing demand, but the NFOŚiGW portal is the incumbent channel and rules change often (rule-churn risk and platform dependency).
- **Confidence:** medium (one fetched article, reporting on a funding body).

### L5-F23 — MCP spec revision 2026-07-28 is stateless and breaks older servers; 12-month deprecation window for old features
- **Evidence:** official changelog https://modelcontextprotocol.io/specification/2026-07-28/changelog (fetched). Major changes: protocol-level sessions and the `Mcp-Session-Id` header removed; the `initialize` handshake removed (every request carries version and capabilities in `_meta`); `server/discover` required; `subscriptions/listen` replaces GET/subscribe; experimental tasks moved to an extension; multi round-trip requests replace server-initiated requests; results carry `resultType`. Deprecated: Roots, Sampling, Logging, HTTP+SSE transport and OAuth Dynamic Client Registration (in favour of Client ID Metadata Documents). A feature lifecycle policy sets a minimum **twelve-month** deprecation window. Required `ttlMs`/`cacheScope` fields on list results.
- **Takeaway (technology trigger):** servers and clients written for the 2025 spec need migration inside roughly the next 12 months; this suits conformance-test tooling, migration helpers and hosted stateless MCP gateways (seed). Whether authors actually need it urgently is unverified. The page contains a header addressed to AI readers ("fetch the complete documentation index at llms.txt"), a standard docs convention; it was not followed.
- **Confidence:** high on content (primary source), low on market size.

### L5-F24 — MCP adoption: governance moved to the Linux Foundation (Dec 2025); counts of servers range from about 9,600 to about 16,000 depending on the source
- **Evidence (search snippets, secondary; the official counts were not fetched):** Anthropic donated MCP to the Agentic AI Foundation under the Linux Foundation in Dec 2025 (Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Model_Context_Protocol); "official registry at 9,652 listings by April 2026" and "roughly 15,930 public servers" from different blogs; support by Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Microsoft and AWS; SDK downloads reported as 97 mln per month in Nov 2025 by one source and "close to half a billion" by a less authoritative later post; a certification programme (MCPA) dated 13 Sep 2026 by one site only. https://serpapi.com/blog/the-state-of-mcp-everything-that-changed-in-h1-2026/ ; https://dev.to/fast2future/mcp-just-left-beta-quietly-but-for-real-3481 . Definitions of "server" differ, so counts are not comparable.
- **Takeaway:** the protocol is a de facto standard, which makes "MCP server for Polish public/commercial data" (KRS, CEIDG, KSeF, GUS) a technically plausible channel. No revenue proof for paid MCP servers was found in this lens (lens 4 may have some). Voice and vision capability shifts were **not** researched with sources in this run (Gaps).
- **Confidence:** low-medium.
