# Invoicing and tax reporting: KSeF, DE, FR, ViDA, DAC8, e-Doręczenia

Lens 5 findings on e-invoicing and tax-reporting triggers. All sources accessed 2026-10-29 (today is 2026-10-29, so dates before it are already in force). "Fetched" = page read in full by the fetch tool; "search snippet" = only the search summary was seen, treat as lower confidence. Related price evidence: [L3-F26](../03-monetization/willingness-to-pay-and-market-size.md#l3-f26--ksef-connectivity-is-bundled-free-by-the-mainstream-invoicing-tools-counter-finding-not-a-standalone-price-point).

### L5-F01 — KSeF is mandatory in three waves: 1 Feb 2026, 1 Apr 2026, 1 Jan 2027
- **Trigger:** national e-invoicing system KSeF 2.0. Large taxpayers (2024 gross sales above 200 mln PLN) from **1 Feb 2026**; all other taxpayers, VAT-registered or exempt, from **1 Apr 2026**; "digitally excluded" micro-taxpayers (invoiced sales up to 10,000 PLN gross per month) join **1 Jan 2027**. From 1 Jan 2027 also: cash-register invoices stop counting as invoices and the KSeF number must appear in transfers between active VAT payers.
- **Evidence:** official stage page https://ksef.podatki.gov.pl/etapy-wdrozenia-ksef/ (fetched; gives the three dates and the 10,000 PLN exclusion, not the 200 mln threshold). The 200 mln / 2024 base, the 2027 side-effects and the offline certificates (available Nov 2025) come from https://fakturownia.pl/kalendarz-ksef (vendor page, fetched) and https://kpmg.com/pl/pl/home/insights/2025/08/tax-alert-wprowadzenie-krajowego-systemu-e-faktur-jest-juz-pewne.html (fetched; act signed 27 Aug 2025, buyers must give NIP).
- **Who:** every Polish taxpayer issuing B2B invoices. Count: see F03.
- **Product category created:** integrations (ERP/shop/POS to KSeF), offline-mode handling, archive/reconciliation, accountant workflow tools, KSeF-number-in-transfer checks for bank/payment software. Existing solutions: all mainstream invoicing tools bundle it free (L3-F26), so a standalone invoice tool is a counter-signal.
- **L5 implication:** the 1 Jan 2027 wave (about 2 months away) is a dated, binding trigger for the smallest firms, but it hits exactly the segment with the lowest willingness to pay.
- **Confidence:** high for dates (official page + two secondary sources agree).

### L5-F02 — KSeF penalties: legally provided, but deferred; sources conflict on how long
- **Trigger:** penalties for invoicing errors/not using KSeF. KPMG (Aug 2025) writes that the act postpones imposing penalties for KSeF invoicing errors until the **end of 2026**. Fakturownia says penalties are not imposed in 2026 and that the Ministry of Finance "announced a postponement to 2028", explicitly an announcement and not a legal change. An Infor column by Prof. Witold Modzelewski (11 Jun 2026) says there are no administrative fines for not issuing a structured invoice and that none are expected in 2027 (announced officially, body and date not stated in the article; opinion piece).
- **Evidence:** https://kpmg.com/pl/pl/home/insights/2025/08/tax-alert-wprowadzenie-krajowego-systemu-e-faktur-jest-juz-pewne.html ; https://fakturownia.pl/kalendarz-ksef ; https://ksiegowosc.infor.pl/wiadomosci/7574858,ksef-2026-unikamy-katastrofy-bo-faktury-wystawiamy-po-staremu-nie-bedzie-kar-za-niewystawianie-faktur-ustrukturyzowanych-takze-w-2027-roku.html (all fetched). Penalty **amounts** were not found in any of them: unverified.
- **Takeaway:** "the deadline has no teeth until 2027/2028" weakens urgency; the real driver is buyers who only accept KSeF invoices. Check ISAP (act amending the VAT Act, Dz.U. 2025) for the penalty clause before using this in a write-up.
- **Confidence:** medium (conflicting secondary sources).

### L5-F03 — KSeF audience: about 1.75 million active VAT payers; voluntary KSeF reached only 5,230 firms
- **Evidence:** Sejm interpellation no. 10257 to the Minister of Finance, received 10 Jun 2025: "ok. 1,75 mln podatników VAT czynnych"; voluntary KSeF used by 5,230 companies from 1 Jan 2022 to 5 Mar 2025, about 3 per mille (the user count is taken from PIT.pl, not verified by the author). https://api.sejm.gov.pl/sejm/term10/interpellations/10257/body (fetched). Non-VAT-registered and exempt firms are also in scope from 1 Apr 2026 (F01), so the true count is above 1.75 million (not quantified; unverified).
- **Behaviour after launch:** Modzelewski (opinion, 11 Jun 2026) says most taxpayers kept issuing paper/ordinary electronic invoices and few did both; no official count of KSeF invoices was found (searches returned none).
- **Takeaway:** a market of ~1.75 million firms where nobody opted in voluntarily: the demand is purely compliance-driven, and was therefore concentrated at the deadlines (Feb/Apr 2026, Jan 2027).
- **Confidence:** medium (interpellation text, secondary count).

### L5-F04 — Germany: E-Rechnung receiving since 1 Jan 2025, issuing from 1 Jan 2027 (large) and 1 Jan 2028 (all)
- **Trigger:** since **1 Jan 2025** every German business must be able to receive B2B e-invoices; issuing is mandatory from **1 Jan 2027** for businesses with prior-year turnover above 800,000 EUR and from **1 Jan 2028** for the rest. Permitted formats: XRechnung and ZUGFeRD 2.0.1+ (not MINIMUM/BASIC-WL), others if EN 16931-compatible (Factur-X, Peppol BIS named). EDI in non-e-invoice formats allowed until 31 Dec 2027. Exceptions: small-amount invoices up to 250 EUR, tickets, B2C (search snippet).
- **Evidence:** PwC newsletter on the BMF letter of 15 Oct 2024 https://newsletter.pwc.de/umsatzsteuer/nl0029-2024-10-29/bmf-e-rechnung (fetched; its own wording on thresholds is "large companies" 2027 / up to 800,000 EUR 2028 — the 800,000 EUR cut is the search-snippet figure, not on the PwC page). Search snippet also noted a second BMF letter of 15 Oct 2025 and that ZDH asked to postpone the 2027 step, with no sign of a legislative process (https://www.boerse-express.com/news/articles/e-rechnung-pflicht-ab-januar-2027-fuer-unternehmen-ueber-800000-euro-929362, not fetched).
- **Who/count:** German businesses with B2B turnover (count not found: unverified). Compare German craft-firm counts in lens 3.
- **Product category:** formats/validation (XRechnung/ZUGFeRD), inbox and archive, ERP/Excel generators for SMEs. Existing: Lexware/sevDesk-type tools (prices in L3-F09, L3-F10).
- **Why it matters for a Polish founder:** a second mandated market, 12–14 months out, where Polish KSeF know-how (structured XML, validation, archive) is portable. German-language sales and support are the cost (estimate).
- **Confidence:** medium-high on dates; the 800,000 EUR threshold rests on search snippets.

### L5-F05 — France: e-invoice reception for all from 1 Sep 2026, issuing for large/mid firms 1 Sep 2026 and SMEs/micro 1 Sep 2027
- **Evidence:** official service-public.gouv.fr page https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/actualites/A15683 (fetched): issuing for large companies and ETIs from 1 Sep 2026, for PME and micro-enterprises from 1 Sep 2027; receiving for all from 1 Sep 2026; new mandatory invoice mentions (client SIREN, delivery address, goods/services, VAT on debits); e-reporting follows the same calendar; use of a State-approved platform ("plateforme agréée"). Penalties (search snippet, Moneyvox, not fetched, from the 2026 finance law): 500 EUR then 1,000 EUR per quarter for no approved platform, 50 EUR per missing invoice capped at 15,000 EUR per year and entity; about 149 approved platforms already validated (press snippet). https://www.moneyvox.fr/banque-pro/facturation-electronique.php
- **Takeaway:** this is a platform-certification model (PA/PDP) that a solo developer cannot join cheaply; the open space is tools around it (SME onboarding, format conversion).
- **Confidence:** high for dates (official page), low for penalty numbers.

### L5-F06 — EU-wide ViDA: mandatory intra-EU B2B e-invoicing and digital reporting from 1 July 2030
- **Trigger:** Directive (EU) 2025/516 (ViDA), in force 14 Apr 2025 per the search snippet. Intra-EU B2B transactions must use electronic invoices in the EN 16931 format with per-transaction reporting from **1 Jul 2030**; Member States may now mandate domestic e-invoicing without asking the Commission for a derogation. Platform-economy "deemed supplier" rules: sources conflict (1 Jul 2028 optional start with postponement to 1 Jan 2030 per RSM; other dates per other sources): unverified. Legacy national reporting must align by 1 Jan 2035.
- **Evidence (search snippet; pages not fetched):** https://vatit.com/blog/vat-in-the-digital-age-vida-the-complete-guide-to-the-eus-vat-reform/ ; https://lookuptax.com/docs/explainers/vida ; https://www.rsm.global/netherlands/en/node/1193 . The EUR-Lex text was not read.
- **L5 implication:** 2030 is beyond the 18-month window in the rubric. It supports "the country-by-country mandates keep coming" (PL 2026/27, DE 2027/28, FR 2026/27), not a trigger by itself.
- **Confidence:** low-medium (secondary, conflicting on platform rules).

### L5-F07 — DAC8 (crypto-asset reporting) in Poland: first reports for 2026 transactions; DAC7 was implemented almost a year late
- **Trigger:** DAC8 requires crypto-asset service providers to report user data from 2026. MDDP (17 Jun 2025, fetched) says the EU deadline for national law was 31 Dec 2025, first reporting year 2026, EU-level first report 31 Jan 2027, and that Poland was expected to be late as with DAC7 (which Poland implemented almost a year late). Later search snippets say Poland enacted its law (adopted 13 Feb 2026, in force 18 Mar 2026) and set **30 Jun 2027** for the first report on 2026, with registration with the head of KAS by 31 Mar of the first reporting year; the sources differ on dates: unverified.
- **Evidence:** https://www.mddp.pl/crypto-transaction-reporting-from-2026-what-should-you-know-about-dac8/ (fetched); https://www.dudkowiak.com/fintech-in-poland/dac8-directive-in-poland (search snippet, not fetched). Penalties: the directive only demands "effective, proportionate and dissuasive" sanctions; no Polish amounts found.
- **Who:** crypto exchanges, wallet providers, brokers (RCASPs), also non-EU providers serving EU residents. Count not found. The broker side needs MiCA/KNF licence context, which the founder profile excludes; a tooling/reporting API vendor is a different matter.
- **Not found:** DAC7 platform-reporting (marketplaces, deadline 31 Jan each year) specifics for Poland were not read in this run (see Gaps).
- **Confidence:** low-medium.

### L5-F08 — e-Doręczenia: sole traders in CEIDG had to open an e-delivery address by 1 Oct 2026 (already passed)
- **Trigger:** companies in KRS registered before 1 Jan 2025 had to have an active address for e-Doręczenia by 1 Apr 2025; sole traders in CEIDG registered before 2025 by **1 Oct 2026** (one source says the application by 30 Sep 2026). The address must also be activated, then it is entered in the electronic address database (BAE).
- **Evidence (search snippets; pages not fetched):** https://www.ifirma.pl/blog/czy-to-koniec-awizo-z-urzedow-nowe-zasady-doreczen-od-1-stycznia-2026/ ; https://poradnikprzedsiebiorcy.pl/-e-doreczenia-obowiazkowe-rowniez-dla-przedsiebiorcow ; https://mikroporady.pl/porady/kto-kiedy-od-kiedy-i-jak-musi-stosowac-edoreczenia . The statute (ustawa o doręczeniach elektronicznych) was not read. No 2027 obligation was found; penalties unverified.
- **Takeaway:** the deadline passed 4 weeks ago; late adopters are the live audience. A narrow, low-value trigger (free official channel, no software need); recorded as a counter-example of a deadline that creates no paid product.
- **Confidence:** medium-low.
