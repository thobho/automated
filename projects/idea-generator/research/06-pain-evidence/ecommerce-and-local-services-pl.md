# E-commerce sellers, salons and trades in Poland

Lens 6 findings on Allegro/API platform risk, Booksy Biz reviews, salon-software migration, and the (thin) demand signals for quoting tools and invoicing choices. All sources accessed 2026-10-29. Reddit and Facebook were unreachable and the Allegro community forum timed out (README Coverage). IDs `L6-Fnn` are stable anchors.

### L6-F08 — Booksy Biz (salon software): 130 of 300 sampled reviews are 1–2★; 44 mention commission / "Boost" and clients stolen via links
- **Evidence:** App Store listing https://apps.apple.com/pl/app/booksy-biz-booking-payments/id725335996 — 3.97/5 from 3,892 ratings (iTunes lookup API). The 300 most recent Polish-store reviews (RSS feed, 2024-01-17 to 2026-10-27): 1★ 113, 2★ 17, 3★ 16, 4★ 27, 5★ 127. In my regex counts 44 reviews mention commission/Boost (prowizj*/boost), 10 notifications, 9 the Versum migration (F09).
- **Quotes (PL, short):** "Klientów z polecenia i znajomych kwalifikuje jako boost przez co ściąga prowizję" (1★, 2026-10-12); "Booksy blokuje Boost, oskarżając przedsiębiorców o celowe zaniżanie prowizji" (1★, 2026-10-12); "po wyłączeniu przez miesiąc ściąga 50% z usługi" (1★, 2026-08-18); "umówione wizyty znikają bez żadnego powiadomienia" (1★, 2026-10-06); a Ukrainian-language review in the sample complains of "almost 200 zł per month" plus "more than 40%" from each client (2★, 2026-09-07).
- **Workarounds / money:** reviewers say they take the fee into prices or give up Boost; one wants a calendar view the app does not offer. No named alternative appears in the sample; one 1★ review (2025-02-23) says "Gdyby istniała inna aplikacja do rezerwacji tak popularna jak Booksy, to chętnie bym zmieniła".
- **Repeated or one-off:** repeated, 44 of 300 mentions across 2025–2026.
- **Counter-evidence:** 127 of 300 are 5★ and the listing sits at 3.97 from 3,892 ratings, so most users are content; the 5★ reviews are not read in detail here. The complaints are about price and a marketplace commission, which a flat-fee tool addresses only if salons accept losing the Booksy client-discovery marketplace.
- **Product category:** flat-fee salon booking without first-visit commission ([L2-F05](../02-weak-competition/allegro-and-ecommerce-tools.md#l2-f05--booksy-145-złmonth-plus-45-first-visit-commission-a-recurring-salon-pain), lens 3 seed 9).
- **Confidence:** medium-high (large sample, one store).

### L6-F09 — Forced Versum → Booksy migration produced a pocket of loud, specific complaints
- **Evidence:** 9 of the 300 reviews in F08 mention Versum, all but one negative, 2024-05 to 2026-10. Quotes: "Jesteśmy po przymusowym przeniesieniu z Versum do Booksy" (1★, 2025-07-23); "Przez ponad 10 lat korzystaliśmy z Versum i nigdy nie mieliśmy problemów" (1★, 2026-10-23, says Chrome barely works); "brakuje wielu bardzo ważnych funkcji, które były dostępne w Versum. Między innymi... raportów dla poszczególnych pracowników" (1★, 2024-11-30). Background: Booksy and Versum merged in Dec 2020 (money.pl via search result, not fetched: https://www.money.pl/gielda/booksy-i-versum-sfinalizowali-fuzje-spolek-6586110707234433a.html ).
- **Takeaway:** a segment of larger multi-employee salons (the Versum base, per the merger statement that Versum served mostly larger salons) feels trapped by migration and wants reports and permissions. Whether they would switch is unproven; a user in 2025 says there is no alternative with comparable reach.
- **Repeated or one-off:** repeated but small (9 of 300).
- **Confidence:** medium.

### L6-F10 — Allegro tightened REST API terms on 23 Sep 2025; third-party tool users and developers filled GitHub with compliance questions
- **Evidence:** the official tracker https://github.com/allegro/allegro-api shows 1,610 issues created after 2025-10-29 minus earlier ones — more exactly 2,543 issues created since 2025-06-01 (GitHub search API, 2026-10-29). Top threads by comments since June 2025: "Pytanie o zgodność z nowym regulaminem REST API (zmiany od 23.09.2025)" 44 comments https://github.com/allegro/allegro-api/issues/12146 ; "Regulamin API" 47 comments — "Jako kolejny użytkownik dostałem @ z ostrzeżeniem" https://github.com/allegro/allegro-api/issues/12327 ; "Czy korzystanie z aplikacji Maxapp (MaxSote) jest zgodne z regulaminem" 58 comments (an app that imports Allegro offers to EmpikPlace; seller afraid of "usunięcie kluczy API lub kary umowne") https://github.com/allegro/allegro-api/issues/12129 ; "Losowe blokady captcha podczas komunikacji z API produkcyjnym" 41 comments https://github.com/allegro/allegro-api/issues/12463 ; a notice requiring an own User-Agent identifier by end of June 2026, 47 comments https://github.com/allegro/allegro-api/issues/13126 .
- **Reading:** the pain is two-sided. Sellers who use cross-marketplace tools get warnings; tool vendors face rule changes and captcha-style blocks.
- **Counter-evidence for building on Allegro:** this is **platform risk evidence**. Seed ideas that depend on the Allegro API (SDK, price monitor) can be switched off by a terms change. It is also evidence that sellers need compliant tooling, which argues for Allegro-certified integrations.
- **Repeated or one-off:** repeated, several threads with 40–60 comments.
- **Confidence:** high on the activity, medium on interpretation (issue bodies only skimmed).

- **Repeated or one-off:** repeated sentiment, zero tool requests.
- **Confidence:** low (snippets, dates uncertain).

### L6-F13 — Counter-evidence: JDG owners asking "which invoicing app?" get cheap answers (inFakt, Fakturownia) and rarely complain
- **Evidence:** Wykop thread 2025-07-21 "Jaki program do fakturowania wybrać?" — 2 upvotes, six answers within hours naming fakturowo.pl, inFakt ("5zł miesięcznie" per commenters, unverified), Fakturownia https://wykop.pl/wpis/82426943/jaki-program-do-fakturowania-wybrac-macie-cos-godn ; thread about "Jaką aplikację do księgowości wybrać dla JDG?" (about 11 months before Oct 2026): a bookkeeper raising from 160 to 230 zł, alternatives named Systim, Mała Księgowość (~100 zł/year per commenter), inFakt https://wykop.pl/wpis/83688105/jaka-aplikacje-do-ksiegowosci-wybrac-dla-jdg-https . App Store: inFakt 4.81/5 from 9,348 ratings, Fakturownia 4.63/5 from 2,818 ([L6-F01](ksef-and-accounting-pain-pl.md#l6-f01--official-ksef-20-app-203%2F5-from-645-ios-ratings-12-of-51-sampled-reviews-ask-for-push-notifications-of-new-purchase-invoices) lists the lookups; checked via iTunes API 2026-10-29).
- **Reading:** generic invoicing for JDG is a solved market with strong incumbents. Pain sits in narrow, regulated edge cases (corrections, foreign buyers, accounting-office workflows) rather than in "easy invoicing".
- **Confidence:** medium.
