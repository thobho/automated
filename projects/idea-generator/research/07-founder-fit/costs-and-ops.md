# Costs and operating burden: money, API churn, outages, support

Lens 7 findings on running costs and maintenance load. All sources accessed 2026-10-29. Cost arithmetic is mine and labelled "estimate"; no exchange rate is applied (prices stay in USD). IDs `L7-Fnn` are stable anchors.

### L7-F16 — BaseLinker's API is self-serve: the customer generates the token, 100 requests/minute, no approval step documented
- **Evidence:** BaseLinker API documentation: the token is created by the user in the panel ("Account & other → My account → API"), sent in the `X-BLToken` header; limit 100 requests per minute; some methods return empty responses unless enabled in the account's API settings; the page describes no approval or partner-programme requirement (the "Base Connect" section was not explained): https://api.baselinker.com/ (accessed 2026-10-29, read via a summarising fetch).
- **Reading:** contrast with Allegro ([L7-F01](api-access.md#l7-f01--allegro-rest-api-every-application-is-reviewed-refusal-needs-no-reason-key-must-be-used-within-30-days)): a tool for BaseLinker users (order automation, reports, label or invoice helpers) can be built and sold with customer-pasted tokens, no gatekeeper, plus one aggregator in place of many marketplace APIs. Risk: BaseLinker can build the feature itself or change terms; whether its terms of service restrict commercial add-ons was **not read** (unverified).
- **Confidence:** medium.

### L7-F18 — Selling SaaS across the EU: a merchant of record (Paddle) takes 5% + USD 0.50 per checkout and handles VAT and invoices; Shopify takes 0% on the first USD 1M
- **Evidence:** Paddle pricing page: pay-as-you-go 5% + 50¢ per Checkout transaction; Paddle is merchant of record, collects the applicable tax, tracks filing deadlines and submits returns: https://www.paddle.com/pricing (accessed 2026-10-29). Shopify: 100% of the first USD 1,000,000 kept, 2.9% processing fee ([L7-F05](api-access.md#l7-f05--shopify-app-store-public-apps-need-a-review-with-a-screencast-test-credentials-lighthouse-impact-10-points-and-shopify-billing-revenue-share-is-0-on-the-first-usd-1000000)). Stripe fee levels and Polish VAT/OSS rules were **not** checked in this lens.
- **Reading:** for a ~29–99 PLN/month product the fixed 50¢ is a visible cut (estimate; exchange rate not applied here). The saving is that the founder, with about 40 h/month of non-code time, does no VAT filing for cross-border consumer and small-business sales. Polish-only B2B invoicing through the founder's own invoicing tool is the alternative (lens 9 owns margin).
- **Confidence:** medium.

### L7-F19 — Any product that *issues* KSeF invoices must handle planned downtime, announced failures and a voluntary offline24 mode; receive-only products avoid this
- **Evidence:** Polish trade-press explanation (Infor): modes are planned unavailability (offline invoices sent by the next business day after it ends), a failure announced by the Ministry of Finance (invoices must reach KSeF within 7 business days after it ends), total failure (no later submission needed) and voluntary offline24 (any time, e.g. own internet down); offline modes after a failure and offline24 need a KSeF certificate "type 2"; offline invoices need two QR codes (OFFLINE and CERTYFIKAT): https://ksiegowosc.infor.pl/ksef/7507741,tryb-offline-i-certyfikat-typu-2-jak-fakturowac-w-czasie-awarii-ksef-lub-braku-dostepu-do-sieci.html . Another site (kluczesoft.pl) states a different deadline and an outage on 7 Mar 2026; it conflicts with the above and is unverified, and no official MF notice of a 2026 outage was found. Vendors also announce maintenance windows (Symfonia: 26–31 Jan 2026 for the KSeF 2.0 roll-out, per search snippet).
- **Reading:** issuing-side KSeF products need offline queues, QR rendering, certificate management and retry logic: the part of a KSeF project that is hardest to test automatically and most likely to generate support tickets. Read-only products (fetch, archive, notify, match) skip all of this. For the founder's "little support, easy to test" bar, the receive side is clearly cheaper than the issue side. This is inference from the mode list.
- **Confidence:** medium (trade press, article not cross-checked against the VAT act).

### L7-F20 — Integrations on KSeF carry visible churn: v1 API incompatible with v2, shared test data, production-only secrets, docs revised through Sep 2026
- **Evidence:** the Ministry's notice of 29 Jan 2026: only keys, certificates and tokens from production apply on production; test credentials cause technical errors; encrypt tokens and symmetric keys only with the production public key: https://ksef.podatki.gov.pl/wyjasnienia/przypominamy-o-zasadach-poprawnego-korzystania-ze-srodowiska-produkcyjnego-systemu-ksef-20-w-zakresie-kluczy-kryptograficznych-oraz-certyfikatow-i-tokenow-ksef/ . Environments doc: cyclic maintenance on test systems from 1 Oct 2025, TEST data shared between integrators (16.03.2026 revision); limits doc revised 14.09.2026 (primary; see [L7-F02](api-access.md#l7-f02--ksef-20-api-public-no-integrator-certification-or-approval-free-test-and-demo-environments-the-cost-is-protocol-complexity-and-rate-limits) and [L7-F03](api-access.md#l7-f03--ksef-rate-limits-are-small-and-keyed-by-taxpayer-context-ip-bulk-sync-must-use-package-export)). Trade press: API 1.0 is not compatible with API 2.0 (taken from the headline/URL slug of an Infor article on the test environment; article body not read): https://ksiegowosc.infor.pl/wiadomosci/7046951,srodowisko-testowe-ksef-20-dot-api-od-30-wrzesnia-modul-certyfikatow-i-uprawnien-do-ksef-20-od-1-listopada-2025-r-mf-api-ksef-10-nie-jest-kompatybilne-z-api-ksef-20.html .
- **Reading:** budget recurring maintenance for any KSeF product (spec drift, FA(2)→FA(3) schema, token-to-certificate switch). Allegro shows the same pattern ([L7-F01](api-access.md#l7-f01--allegro-rest-api-every-application-is-reviewed-refusal-needs-no-reason-key-must-be-used-within-30-days)). Heavy on-call is not implied, but monthly spec-watching is.
- **Confidence:** medium.

### L7-F26 — LLM inference prices (OpenRouter, 29 Oct 2026): from USD 0.07–0.28 per million tokens for small open models to USD 2/10 for Claude Sonnet 5.5
- **Evidence:** OpenRouter public model list (https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/models, queried 2026-10-29), USD per million input/output tokens: anthropic/claude-haiku-5.5 0.10/0.50 (batch 0.05/0.25); anthropic/claude-sonnet-5.5 2.00/10.00 (batch 1.00/5.00); anthropic/claude-sonnet-4.6 3.00/15.00; deepseek/deepseek-v3.2 0.28/0.42; qwen/qwen3-coder-next 0.12/0.80; openai/gpt-5-mini 0.25/2.00; google/gemini-2.5-flash 0.30/2.50; google/gemini-2.5-flash-lite 0.10/0.40; mistralai/mistral-small-2603 0.15/0.60; qwen/qwen3-coder-30b-a3b-instruct 0.07/0.28. 457 models listed. List prices; they change.
- **Reading:** inference cost is not the constraint for document-level tasks; see F27.
- **Confidence:** high (primary API), perishable.

### L7-F27 — Estimate: LLM cost of extracting a structured record from one document is about USD 0.001 with Claude Haiku 5.5 and about USD 0.02 with Sonnet 5.5
- **Evidence:** arithmetic on the F26 prices, **estimate**, assuming 4,000 input and 1,000 output tokens per document (my assumption): Haiku 5.5: 4,000 × 0.10/1e6 + 1,000 × 0.50/1e6 = USD 0.0009; Sonnet 5.5: 4,000 × 2/1e6 + 1,000 × 10/1e6 = USD 0.018; DeepSeek V3.2: ≈ USD 0.0015. So 1,000 documents a month cost about USD 0.9 (Haiku) to USD 18 (Sonnet); the batch variants halve this.
- **Reading:** an LLM step per invoice, email or listing does not break the 500 PLN/month budget at hundreds of customers, provided calls per customer are bounded (agent loops with many tool calls would multiply this; not estimated). Quality and error handling, not cost, are the feasibility question for LLM features, and AI-Act labelling applies ([L7-F12](licensing-and-regulation.md#l7-f12--eu-ai-act-article-50-transparency-duties-applied-from-2-aug-2026-and-were-not-delayed-by-the-ai-omnibus-fines-up-to-eur-15-million-or-3-of-turnover)).
- **Confidence:** medium (estimate).
