# Lens 9 — Platform and supplier risk

Part of [lens 9](README.md). All sources accessed 2026-10-29. Complaint volumes are in lens 6 ([L6-F10](../06-pain-evidence/ecommerce-and-local-services-pl.md)); platform access rules in lens 7 ([L7-F01 onward](../07-founder-fit/api-access.md)). This file collects **cases where a platform or supplier changed terms, prices or APIs and customers had to react**, which is what the L9 "platform risk" criterion is about.

## L9-F17 — Allegro: new REST API terms from 23 Sep 2025 forbid sharing keys, showing Allegro offers on competitor sites and importing offers to rival marketplaces; the API surface also keeps being withdrawn (multipack discounts end 12 Jan 2027)
Allegro Community thread "10.09.2025 nowy regulamin REST API": a moderator says changes apply from 23 September 2025; sellers may not share REST API keys with third parties, use them to present offers on competitors' sites, or reuse Allegro materials on competitor sites; importing offers to competing platforms "co do zasady" violates the terms; external integrators are allowed if they use their own key and sellers connect by OAuth. Penalties are not specified in the thread (one user quotes "wezwania do zapłaty po 50 tysięcy", unconfirmed forum claim). Users disagreed whether BaseLinker-style cross-marketplace sync is affected; no moderator ruling. Source: https://spolecznosc.allegro.pl/t5/zaawansowani-sprzedawcy/10-09-2025-nowy-regulamin-rest-api/td-p/1078488 (accessed 2026-10-29). I did not find the official terms text or news post (developer.allegro.pl search returned only the forum). Allegro Developer news (October 2026): creation of multipack quantity discounts withdrawn effective 12 January 2027 (date moved from 30 November 2026), turnover-discount resources removed 2 November 2026, new seller-ratings structure 2 November 2026, required `categoryId` in POST /ingredients/parse from 13 November 2026. Source: https://developer.allegro.pl/news (accessed 2026-10-29). GitHub issue counts and the User-Agent requirement: [L6-F10](../06-pain-evidence/ecommerce-and-local-services-pl.md). Incumbent price power: BaseLinker price changes in [L2-F01, F02](../02-weak-competition/allegro-and-ecommerce-tools.md).
- **Reading:** an Allegro-dependent product is one terms change or one deprecated endpoint away from rework; the platform has already banned a whole use case (cross-marketplace offer export) once. A tool that serves **one seller's own Allegro sales through OAuth with its own app key** is the compliant pattern. A product that sells the same sellers something Allegro itself wants to own (pricing, ratings, shipping) is most exposed.
- **Confidence:** medium (forum moderator, not the legal text).

## L9-F18 — Reddit API pricing (2023): USD 12,000 per 50 million requests ended Apollo (about 7 billion requests a month, estimated USD 2M/month); Twitter/X did the same to third-party clients
Betakit: Reddit's price was USD 12,000 per 50 million API requests; Apollo's developer Christian Selig reported about 7 billion requests in the previous month and estimated he would owe about USD 2 million per month, over USD 20 million a year; Apollo shut down on 30 June 2023. Source: https://betakit.com/reddits-new-api-pricing-kills-canadian-app-apollo/ (accessed 2026-10-29). A search-result summary adds that Twitter priced out many third-party apps and later added a USD 5,000-per-month API tier (not fetched; unverified).
- **Reading:** the worst case for a thin client on a platform API: the platform sets a per-call price after the client has built its user base, with weeks of notice. Any idea built as "better front end for X's API" needs a plan for a price shock, and ideally data the platform does not own.
- **Confidence:** high for the Apollo numbers (founder's figures reported by press).

## L9-F19 — Shopify: checkout.liquid, additional scripts and script tags sunset on the Thank-you/Order-status pages (28 Aug 2025 for Plus; 26 Aug 2026 for non-Plus script tags), forcing every app that touched checkout to rebuild as an extension
Shopify.dev checkout-layout page: for the Information, Shipping and Payment steps checkout.liquid is no longer supported; on Thank-you and Order-status pages checkout.liquid and additional scripts were sunset on 28 August 2025, script tags on Plus stores on 28 August 2025 and on non-Plus stores on 26 August 2026; merchants must move to Shopify "Extensions in Checkout". The page does not cover Shopify Scripts (a search summary gave 30 June 2026; unverified). Source: https://shopify.dev/tutorials/develop-theme-layouts-checkout (accessed 2026-10-29). Fees: [L7-F05](../07-founder-fit/api-access.md), [L8-F03](../08-distribution/marketplaces-and-directories.md).
- **Reading:** Shopify gives long, dated notice (about 2–3 years of overlap), but each sunset is unpaid rework for app vendors and a reason for merchants to re-evaluate their apps. Ongoing maintenance hours (not infra cost) are the hidden margin item for any app inside a platform that ships breaking changes yearly; a solo founder with an agent stack can absorb it but it eats the 40 h/month non-code budget when reviews and support arrive.
- **Confidence:** medium-high (primary page, partial scope).

## L9-F20 — LLM supplier risk is bounded but real: Anthropic gives at least 60 days' notice before retiring a public model and has retired six model families in 14 months; the sampling parameters temperature/top_p/top_k return a 400 error from API version 2026-10-21
Anthropic's model-deprecation page: "at least 60 days' notice before model retirement for publicly released models"; Claude Haiku 3.5 deprecated 19 Dec 2025, retired 19 Feb 2026; Sonnet 3.7 deprecated 28 Oct 2025, retired 19 Feb 2026; Haiku 3 deprecated 19 Feb 2026, retired 20 Apr 2026; Sonnet 4 and Opus 4 deprecated 14 Apr 2026, retired 15 Jun 2026; Opus 4.1 deprecated 5 Jun 2026, retired 5 Aug 2026; Sonnet 4.5 deprecated 30 Sep 2026 and retiring 30 Nov 2026. Parameters temperature, top_p, top_k were removed in API version 2026-10-21 (400 error). Source: https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/model-deprecations (accessed 2026-10-29). Price levels: [L7-F26](../07-founder-fit/costs-and-ops.md).
- **Reading:** a pipeline built on one model must be re-tested roughly every 6–12 months (retirement of any given model), so the product needs a regression set and a model switch (OpenRouter-style abstraction). Other vendors' deprecation policies were not checked. LLM price and quality are an input shared by every competitor, so they erode differentiation as well as cost.
- **Confidence:** high (primary page, a supplier's own record).

## L9-F21 — WhatsApp Business: per-message pricing since 1 Jul 2025; replies inside the 24-hour window that were free are charged from 1 Oct 2026; Poland became its own rate-card market from 1 Jul 2026
Meta developer docs: from 1 July 2025 messages are charged per delivered template message (marketing, utility, authentication); utility messages sent in response to a user were free from 1 July 2025; "as of October 1, 2026" they are charged (pricing type moves from `free_customer_service` to `regular`); free entry-point windows (Click-to-WhatsApp ads, up to 7 days) stay free; Poland became a standalone rate-card market with lower rates effective 1 July 2026; the page shows no zloty amounts (rate cards not read). Source: https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing (accessed 2026-10-29).
- **Reading:** WhatsApp is the obvious SMS replacement for reminders (compare SMSAPI 0.14–0.17 PLN, [L9-F06](costs-and-fees.md); Fresha 0.15–0.90 PLN per WhatsApp message, [L9-F13](switching-and-lockin.md)), but its price and categories are set by Meta and moved twice in 15 months. Unit cost for notification-heavy products is a moving target; pass-through pricing is the safe design.
- **Confidence:** medium (zloty rates not verified).

## L9-F22 — Official free tools compete directly: the Ministry of Finance provides free KSeF and JPK tools (Aplikacja Podatnika KSeF, KSeF mobile app, e-mikrofirma), rated 2.0–2.9 in the stores
The free tools are described in [L8-F17](../08-distribution/search-communities-ads-and-stories.md) (free web app with login via login.gov.pl or qualified certificate; mobile app; e-mikrofirma for sole traders) and rated in [L2-F12](../02-weak-competition/reviews-and-app-stores.md) (Google Play 2.9, about 1,020 reviews; App Store 2.0, 645 ratings). A search summary of Polish press (pro.rp.pl, pit.pl, 2019–2020) says e-mikrofirma lets sole traders issue invoices, keep the VAT register and send JPK_V7M/V7K/FA for free, needs Windows and Java, and that the Ministry was working on a fuller version; current availability and version not checked on podatki.gov.pl (unverified). Sources: https://pro.rp.pl/podatki/art444121-aplikacja-do-jpk-dla-malych-firm and https://pro.rp.pl/ksiegowosc/art45331531-darmowa-e-ksiega-i-jpk-dla-malych-firm-ministerstwo-finansow-obiecuje (search summary only; paywalled, accessed 2026-10-29). German analogue: free federal-state E-Rechnung validators and viewers dominate search ([L8-F18](../08-distribution/search-communities-ads-and-stories.md)).
- **Reading:** compliance tools that merely **do what the state tool does** compete with a free, official, improving product. The state's gaps (login friction, notifications, foreign currency, bad ratings) are the available opening, and they can be closed by an update; a paid tool needs a job the ministry will not do (multi-client view for an office, archive, matching to bank/ERP, reminders). Market-size effect on customers: the low ratings suggest unmet demand and the free alternative caps the price.
- **Confidence:** medium (assembled from lenses 2, 8 and secondary press).

## L9-F23 — Google Maps Platform replaced its USD 200 monthly credit with per-SKU free thresholds on 1 Mar 2025 and extended volume discounts
Google's change note for 1 March 2025: the USD 200 monthly credit became a free usage threshold per Core Services SKU; automatic volume discounts now scale to 5,000,000+ billable events (from 100,000+); Google's example: 2,000,000 Geocoding requests a month would have cost USD 7,900 before and about USD 5,050 after; some services moved to Legacy status; customers with negotiated agreements kept their terms. Source: https://developers.google.com/maps/billing-and-pricing/march-2025 (via search summary; page not fetched; accessed 2026-10-29).
- **Reading:** map/geocoding data products have a variable cost that is cheap at tiny volume (free thresholds) and large at scale (thousands of USD per million calls). A data product that geocodes, maps or enriches per customer should price per volume or use open data (OSM/Nominatim, Polish BDL/PRG) instead (alternatives not costed here).
- **Confidence:** medium (summary, example is Google's own).
