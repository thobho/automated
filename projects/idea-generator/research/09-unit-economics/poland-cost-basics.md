# Lens 9 — Polish JDG cost basics that matter for margin

Part of [lens 9](README.md). Scope: only the fixed costs of running the business that sit **under** product margin. Product margin is in [costs-and-fees.md](costs-and-fees.md). Both findings come from secondary Polish tax-advice pages found through search summaries; none was checked against ZUS or podatki.gov.pl. Verify with an accountant before use; not tax advice.

## L9-F24 — ZUS for a JDG in 2026: full ("duży") ZUS about PLN 1,927 a month plus a health contribution of at least PLN 432.54; preferential and "Mały ZUS Plus" bases lower the social part to roughly PLN 456–1,927
Per iFirma's 2026 guide and Firmove (via search summary): full social contributions PLN 1,926.76 a month incl. voluntary sickness insurance, on a base of 60% of the forecast average wage (PLN 5,652 of PLN 9,420); preferential base at least 30% of minimum wage (PLN 1,441.80); minimum health contribution PLN 432.54 from February 2026 (100% of the minimum wage of PLN 4,806); Mały ZUS Plus for turnover up to PLN 120,000 for 36 months in each 60-month period. The summary flags that iFirma's Mały ZUS Plus example amounts look unchanged since 2024 and may be out of date. Sources: https://www.ifirma.pl/blog/skladki-zus-2026-ile-wynosza-aktualne-skladki-zus-dla-przedsiebiorcow/ and https://www.firmove.pl/aktualnosci/finanse/zus/wysokosc-skladek-zus (via search summary, not fetched; accessed 2026-10-29).
- **Reading (estimate):** if the product is run through a JDG, 10,000 PLN MRR carries at least ~2,400 PLN a month of social + health contributions at full ZUS, before income tax; whether the founder already pays these from other activity (an employment contract, a B2B contract) is unknown, so lens 9 treats ZUS as a founder-level fixed cost, not as product margin.
- **Confidence:** low-medium (secondary; unverified against ZUS).

## L9-F25 — Ryczałt for software-related services is 12% (not 8.5%), according to NSA-cited tax commentary; SaaS subscription revenue classification was not checked
Search results (podatki.biz, poradnikprzedsiebiorcy.pl, ifirma.pl, infor.pl) agree that the 12% ryczałt rate applies to software-related services (PKWiU ex 62.01.1) and IT hardware advice (62.02.10.0), that an NSA ruling extends it beyond writing code, and that a 2023 interpretation put analysis and process modelling under 12%; 8.5% is for other services. No result classified **subscription SaaS** specifically, and no official podatki.gov.pl page was read. Sources: https://www.ifirma.pl/blog/jaki-ryczalt-dla-programisty-85-czy-12-stawka-ryczaltu-dla-informatykow/ and https://www.podatki.biz/artykuly/ryczalt-85-proc-w-it-nizsza-stawka-tylko-gdy-nie-jestes-programista_34_63591.htm (via search summary, not fetched; accessed 2026-10-29).
- **Reading (estimate):** ryczałt takes the tax on revenue, not profit, so a 12% rate on gross subscription revenue is a hard ceiling on net margin regardless of cloud cost: 10,000 PLN MRR leaves at most ~8,800 PLN before ZUS (the Stripe and VAT effects come on top). A cost-heavy product (LLM, SMS) would be better on the scale of income tax (liniowy 19%) where costs deduct; choosing the form is an accountant question for week 2, not Phase 1.
- **Confidence:** low-medium.
