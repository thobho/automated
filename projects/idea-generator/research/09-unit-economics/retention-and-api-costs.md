# Lens 9 — Retention by price level and API/infra cost per request

Part of [lens 9](README.md). All sources accessed 2026-10-29. Complements [churn.md](churn.md) and [costs-and-fees.md](costs-and-fees.md).

## L9-F26 — Retention rises with price: SaaS Capital's 2025 private-company survey finds higher net retention at higher ACV (median NRR 102% at USD 25–50K ACV); the claimed "vertical SaaS retains better" gap is disputed
SaaS Capital (published 18 Sep 2025, the 2025 survey): "higher net retention correlates with higher ACVs", and companies with the highest ACVs have the highest median gross retention; at USD 25–50K ACV median NRR is 102% (top quartile 111%, bottom 97%); among companies above USD 1M ARR those with NRR of at least 110% grew faster than the 24% median. The values for ACV below USD 10K (the band a 29–99 PLN/month product occupies) appear only in charts, not in the text. Source: https://www.saas-capital.com/research/saas-retention-benchmarks-for-private-b2b-companies/ (accessed 2026-10-29). Vertical vs horizontal: a search summary says SaaS Capital's 2020 brief had vertical gross retention 90% vs horizontal 89% and its 2022 brief found the gap closed; a Tidemark-2025 figure of about 91% (vertical) vs 78–85% (horizontal, small business) came via a secondary blog and could not be verified; Bessemer's vertical benchmarks (gross retention above 90%, gross margin above 80%) were quoted from a secondary source (FE International). Sources: https://www.saas-capital.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/2020_B2B_SaaS_Retention_Benchmarks.pdf and https://www.feinternational.com/blog/vertical-saas-vs-horizontal-saas-multiples-which-sells-for-more (search summary only; PDF not machine-readable here).
- **Reading:** supports the decision rule: a higher price per customer and a narrow vertical are the two levers that move a product toward the sticky end; the data does not prove that "vertical" alone creates retention. For this founder, an accounting-office or compliance tool at 150–500 PLN/month has better odds than a 29 PLN consumer-grade tool (estimate, direction supported by F09, F11).
- **Confidence:** medium for direction, low for vertical-vs-horizontal magnitude.

## L9-F27 — Compute for an API product is negligible: AWS Lambda USD 0.20 per million requests plus USD 0.0000166667 per GB-second, API Gateway HTTP APIs USD 1.00 per million calls
AWS Lambda pricing: USD 0.20 per 1M requests, USD 0.0000166667 per GB-second (x86), free tier 1M requests and 400,000 GB-seconds a month (Arm price not extracted). Source: https://aws.amazon.com/lambda/pricing/ (accessed 2026-10-29). API Gateway: HTTP APIs USD 1.00 per million requests, REST APIs USD 3.50 per million, 1M free calls a month for 12 months. Source: https://aws.amazon.com/api-gateway/pricing/ (accessed 2026-10-29).
- **Reading (estimate):** a request that runs 200 ms at 512 MB uses 0.1 GB-s = USD 0.0000017; with API Gateway HTTP the full cost is about USD 1.20 per million requests. Even 10 million requests a month is about USD 12–15, i.e. 50–60 PLN at an assumed 3.7 PLN per USD (not looked up). For a developer API, **egress data, database and third-party data-source fees (e.g. LLM calls, Google Maps, F23)** are the real cost; the hosting is not. No lock-in follows from the cheap infra: a competitor's costs are equally low.
- **Confidence:** high for list prices, low for the workload assumption.
