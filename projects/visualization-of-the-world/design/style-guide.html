Design system and style guide

Tokens and components for the data stories about Poland: population, cities and housing. Calm, data-first, phone-first, light and dark. Everything here is driven by tokens.css (generated from tokens.json); the rules are in README.md.

Three directions

One is built completely; the other two are token-only alternatives on the same components. Data colours are shared by all three, so a chart means the same thing in every look.

Colour

Swatches show the active direction and theme. The ratio is measured live against the page background; the full check is scripts/check_contrast.py.

Neutral scale

Cool grey with a hint of the accent hue. Neutrals carry all text, borders and chart chrome.

Surfaces and text

Accent

One accent, for interaction only: links, selected state, primary button, focus ring, slider thumb. Never used as a data colour.

Semantic

For messages and badges, always with an icon and words. Text variants pass 4.5:1 on every background and on their tint.

Data colour

Each colour does one job: identity (categorical), magnitude (sequential and map), direction of change (diverging) or state (status). Validated with the dataviz method: lightness band, chroma floor, colour-blind separation (Machado 2009) and contrast, for light and dark.

Categorical: 8 hues, fixed order

Assign in order, never cycle. A ninth series goes into "Inne" or small multiples. Slots 3, 4 and 5 are below 3:1 on white, so charts using them always carry direct labels or a table view.

Place type

The three place types are slots 1-3, the only three that pass the colour-blind check on all pairs, so they work on maps and scatter plots too. The colour follows the place type everywhere, in every chart.

Sequential

One hue, light to dark. Blue is the default; red is the second sequential context and the warm arm of the diverging scale.

Blue 100-700

Red 100-700

Diverging

Decrease (red) to increase (blue) around a neutral grey midpoint. Equal lightness on both arms.

Map (choropleth)

Seven classes of the blue ramp. In dark mode the ramp flips so low values sink into the background. Missing data is hatched grey, never a ramp colour.

brak danych

Status (reserved)

Only for state (good, warning, serious, critical), never for "series 4". Always with icon and label.

Typography

Open-licence variable fonts (SIL OFL 1.1), self-hosted as woff2: latin plus latin-ext, so every Polish letter comes from the font, not a fallback. Display face for headings and big numbers, text face for everything else. Numbers in tables, axes and tooltips use tabular figures.

Space, radius, shadow, motion

Spacing (4 px base)

Inside components: 1-4. Between components: 4-6. Between sections: 7-9.

Radius

Small and square-ish. Controls 6 px, cards 10 px, data marks 4 px on the value end only. No pill shapes.

Shadow

Only for things that float over content: tooltip (md), mobile menu and popovers (lg). Cards use a hairline border, not a shadow.

Motion

Short and functional: state changes 140 ms, panels 220 ms, chart transitions 360 ms, story steps 600 ms. With prefers-reduced-motion: reduce every duration token becomes 0 ms.

Layout

Phone first: 4 columns, then 8 from 600 px and 12 from 960 px. Max content width 75 rem (1200 px). Reading text stays at 38 rem (about 70 characters) even on wide screens; charts may break out to the full grid.

Charts

All numbers below are made up (sample/sample-data.json) to show the styling. They are not GUS data.

Rules

  1. Title says the finding, subtitle says the measure and unit ("% ludności, 2000-2024").
  2. Axes: one value axis only (no dual axes). Tick labels 12 px in chart-text, tabular figures; a solid baseline at zero; no axis line on the value side.
  3. Gridlines: 1 px chart-grid hairlines on the value axis only, 3-5 of them. Never vertical gridlines on time.
  4. Direct labels at line ends for up to 4 series (the legend stays too). Label text is ink, not the series colour; a small mark carries the colour.
  5. Values: label only what the story needs (max, min, highlighted). Everything else in the tooltip and table view.
  6. Annotations: dashed 1 px rule plus a short text in chart-annotation ink, e.g. a census year or method change.
  7. Marks: lines 2 px; bars with a 2 px gap and 4 px rounding on the value end only; markers 8 px with a surface ring.
  8. Every chart, map and table that shows data ends with the "Źródło:" footer: publisher, dataset or table code as a link, date accessed. No source, no chart.
  9. Hover and focus: crosshair and tooltip on line charts, per-mark tooltip on bars and map regions; the chart can be focused and stepped with arrow keys; a table view exists for every chart.

Accessibility rules

  • Focus ring: 2 px focus-ring colour, 2 px offset, on :focus-visible only. Never removed.
  • Touch size: 44 × 44 px minimum for anything tappable, including chart hit areas (bigger than the mark). 44
  • Keyboard: Tab through controls in reading order; ← → in tabs, sliders and focused charts; Space toggles place types and play; Esc closes the menu and tooltips. A skip link is the first focusable element.
  • Never colour alone: legends and direct labels name every series; status has icon plus words; selected toggles get a filled swatch and a check.
  • Contrast: text 4.5:1, large text and UI 3:1, data marks 3:1 or labelled (exceptions listed in tokens.json).
  • Motion: reduced-motion users get no transitions, no shimmer, no smooth scrolling.
  • Charts: SVG with role="img" and a text summary in aria-label; table view for every chart.
