How to read the housing numbers

GUS publishes two different splits of completed dwellings. The direct series (var 748247, houses = single-family, flats = multi-family) starts in 2009. Before 2009 only a proxy exists: individual construction (var 430, mostly houses, but also private dwellings for sale or rent) against the direct total (var 748601). This page shows the proxy years in a hatched band and the direct years on their own; a chart that needs one consistent basis uses 2009-2025 only.
